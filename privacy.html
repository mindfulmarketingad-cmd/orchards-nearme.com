Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orchards Near Me ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit orchards-nearme.com (the "Site"). We are committed to keeping things simple and respecting your privacy.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the Site. Information may be handled in the following ways:

  • ZIP code searches: When you search by ZIP code, that ZIP code is used to look up an approximate location so we can show nearby listings. We do not store your searches.
  • Contact form: If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide (such as your name, email address, and message) so we can respond.
  • Log and usage data: Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically collect standard technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited for security and performance.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve the Site and its search features.
  • To respond to your inquiries and suggestions.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the Site.

Third-party services

The Site uses third-party services to function, including map tiles from OpenStreetMap, a ZIP-code lookup service, and web fonts. These providers may receive your IP address as a normal part of delivering their services. Listing data is sourced from public mapping data. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

The Site aims to keep cookie use to a minimum. Any cookies used are for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general, family audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You may use the Site without submitting any personal information. If you have contacted us and would like your information removed, please reach out and we will honor your request where reasonably possible.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Questions about this policy? Please contact us.
