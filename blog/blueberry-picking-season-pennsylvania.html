Best Blueberry Varieties in Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Duke, Bluecrop, and Blueray. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania blueberry farms, including operations in the Pocono region, peak in July; call ahead to confirm picking hours since these are generally smaller operations than the state's famous apple orchards.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
