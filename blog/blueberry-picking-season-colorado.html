Where to Pick Blueberries in Colorado

Colorado's high altitude and alkaline soil make commercial blueberry growing genuinely difficult, and the handful of growers near Denver who do attempt it amend their soil heavily and choose the hardiest available cultivars. It remains a small-scale, dedicated pursuit rather than an industry.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Colorado

Colorado orchards and farms commonly grow a small number of cold-hardy highbush varieties grown in heavily amended, acidic soil. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Colorado

Blueberry picking near Denver is a niche pursuit, so call ahead to confirm a farm has a crop before making the trip — amended-soil operations can have unpredictable yields. Expect a much smaller selection than in major blueberry states.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
