Blueberry Picking Season North Dakota

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Blueberry picking isn't commercially available in North Dakota — due to the state's severe winters and alkaline soil.

When Is Blueberry Picking Season in North Dakota?

North Dakota has no meaningful commercial blueberry season — the local climate and soil don't support reliable production, so pick-your-own blueberry farms aren't part of the landscape here.

Where to Pick Blueberries in North Dakota

North Dakota's severe winters and alkaline soil rule out commercial blueberry farming almost entirely, and what little blueberry growing happens near Bismarck is limited to a handful of dedicated home gardeners using raised, amended beds.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in North Dakota

There's essentially no commercial blueberry picking near Bismarck — North Dakota's winters and soil are both working against the crop. Cold-hardy apple orchards are a more realistic pick-your-own option here.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
