Blueberry Picking Season Hawaii

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Blueberry picking isn't commercially available in Hawaii — the climate doesn't provide the winter chill the bushes require.

When Is Blueberry Picking Season in Hawaii?

Hawaii has no meaningful commercial blueberry season — the local climate and soil don't support reliable production, so pick-your-own blueberry farms aren't part of the landscape here.

Where to Pick Blueberries in Hawaii

Hawaii's tropical climate doesn't provide the winter chill blueberry bushes need to set fruit properly, so the islands have no commercial blueberry industry. Blueberries sold in Honolulu are shipped in from the mainland.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Hawaii

Hawaii doesn't support commercial blueberry growing — the climate doesn't provide the winter chill the bushes require.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Hawaii

There's no local blueberry picking option in Hawaii — buy fresh blueberries at a farmers market or grocery store instead, since all of it is shipped in from the mainland.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
