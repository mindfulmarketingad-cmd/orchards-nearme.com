Cherry Picking Season Alaska

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Cherry picking season in Alaska is july through August for the sour cherry varieties that have been adapted for Alaska's extreme climate, with the season varying significantly by location and year.

Typical season: July through August for the sour cherry varieties that have been adapted for Alaska's extreme climate, with the season varying significantly by location and year. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Cherries in Alaska

Alaska's extreme climate limits what's possible in cherry orchards, but the state's long summer days and some protected growing sites have enabled a handful of orchards to grow sour cherry varieties that are remarkably productive in good years. Cherry picking in Alaska is a genuine novelty, and what exists tends to be small-scale and community-focused, making it all the more memorable when you find it.

Use the map on our cherry picking near Juneau, Alaska page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Cherry Varieties in Alaska

Alaska orchards and farms commonly grow sweet cherries such as Bing, Rainier, Chelan, and Lapins, grown at commercial scale in some of the best cherry country in the world. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Cherry Picking in Alaska

Alaskan cherry picking, where available, is best pursued by connecting with local growers through farmers markets and community boards rather than expecting commercial pick-your-own infrastructure. The small scale of what's possible here is a feature rather than a limitation—it's agricultural curiosity and community connection in its purest form.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
