Pear Picking Season Utah

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Pear picking season in Utah runs late August through October, with high-elevation orchards often running later than lower valleys.

Where to Pick Pears in Utah

Near Salt Lake City, the mountain west's high-elevation valleys — Colorado's Western Slope in particular — grow excellent pears using the same intense sunshine and cool nights that produce the region's well-regarded apples and peaches.

Best Pear Varieties in Utah

Utah orchards and farms commonly grow Bartlett and Anjou pears, grown at elevation alongside the region's better-known stone fruit and apple crops. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Pear Picking in Utah

Mountain orchards often combine pear picking with their more famous peach or apple programs, so ask what else is in season during your visit.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
