Where to Pick Cherries in Nebraska

Cherry picking options near Lincoln are limited but not entirely absent, with orchards in eastern Nebraska growing tart cherry varieties harvested in late June. The cherry season in Nebraska is brief, and the farms that participate are typically small family operations that appreciate a phone call before you arrive. Best Cherry Varieties in Nebraska

Nebraska orchards and farms commonly grow tart cherry varieties such as Montmorency, especially in the Great Lakes region, alongside a smaller sweet cherry crop. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Cherry Picking in Nebraska

Nebraska's limited cherry picking options reward persistence—find the farms through local agriculture listings or farmers market connections in late June and plan to go when they report readiness. The experience of picking fresh cherries in the plains states is a small but genuine pleasure, and the farms that make it possible are typically happy to have visitors who seek them out.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
