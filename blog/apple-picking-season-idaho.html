Apple Picking Season Idaho

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Apple picking season in Idaho runs from August through October, with the Emmett Valley in Gem County — known as the Gem of the Hills — producing some of the most commercially significant apple crops in the Pacific Northwest.

When Is Apple Picking Season in Idaho?

Idaho is a serious apple-producing state whose output often goes unrecognized because much of the crop is processed rather than sold fresh. The Emmett Valley, just 30 miles north of Boise, has a long orchard tradition, with the town itself celebrating its Cherry Festival (and adjacent orchard season) each June. By August, apple picking opens in earnest across the valley floor and lower foothills, running through October as heavier varieties come in.

Where to Pick Apples in Idaho

The best apple picking in Idaho is concentrated in the Emmett Valley in Gem County, the Treasure Valley near Boise, and the Teton Valley in eastern Idaho. These areas combine the climate, soil, and agricultural tradition that makes pick-your-own orchards viable and worthwhile as a destination.

Best Apple Varieties in Idaho

Idaho orchards commonly grow Fuji, Honeycrisp, Gala, Jonagold, and Braeburn. Availability varies by farm and time of season — earlier varieties like Gala and Zestar kick things off, while Honeycrisp, Fuji, and later-ripening types extend the season toward its close. When you arrive, ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Apple Picking in Idaho

The drive from Boise to Emmett along State Highway 16 passes through orchard country — you can often spot u-pick signs from the road in August and September.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring a bag or use the farm's containers, wear closed-toe shoes (fallen apples make the ground soft and uneven), dress in layers since orchard mornings can be cool even in September, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
