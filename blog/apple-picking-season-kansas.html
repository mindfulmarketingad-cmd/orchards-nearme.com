Apple Picking Season Kansas

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Apple picking season in Kansas is concentrated in September, with a limited but dedicated group of orchards operating across the state's varied terrain.

When Is Apple Picking Season in Kansas?

Kansas is not prime apple country — the combination of summer heat, spring hail storms, and highly variable temperatures makes consistent apple production challenging. However, a number of determined growers have established successful orchards, particularly on the east side of the state where rainfall is higher and temperatures slightly more moderate. Look for u-pick operations primarily in Shawnee, Douglas, and Butler counties.

Where to Pick Apples in Kansas

The best apple picking in Kansas is concentrated in the Wichita area in south-central Kansas, Shawnee County near Topeka, and the Flint Hills region. These areas combine the climate, soil, and agricultural tradition that makes pick-your-own orchards viable and worthwhile as a destination.

Use the map on our apple picking near Topeka, Kansas page to find specific orchards by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Apple Varieties in Kansas

Kansas orchards commonly grow Gala, Fuji, Honeycrisp, and Jonathan. Availability varies by farm and time of season — earlier varieties like Gala and Zestar kick things off, while Honeycrisp, Fuji, and later-ripening types extend the season toward its close. When you arrive, ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Apple Picking in Kansas

Kansas orchards often run a short season — check listings carefully and call ahead because the window can be compressed by summer heat or early frost.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring a bag or use the farm's containers, wear closed-toe shoes (fallen apples make the ground soft and uneven), dress in layers since orchard mornings can be cool even in September, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
