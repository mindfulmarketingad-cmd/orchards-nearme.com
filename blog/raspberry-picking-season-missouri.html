Raspberry picking season in Missouri runs July through September, with a first flush in midsummer and a second everbearing crop into early fall.

Where to Pick Raspberries in Missouri

Near Jefferson City, the Midwest's river valleys and glacially-formed soils support a strong tradition of small, family-run raspberry farms, with Michigan and the Great Lakes states particularly well suited to the crop.

Use the map on our raspberry picking near Jefferson City, Missouri page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Raspberry Varieties in Missouri

Missouri orchards and farms commonly grow summer-bearing varieties like Latham and Nova alongside everbearing Heritage and Caroline types. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Raspberry Picking in Missouri

Many Midwest raspberry farms combine picking with other summer produce, so ask what else is ready — sweet corn and tomatoes often overlap with peak raspberry season.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
