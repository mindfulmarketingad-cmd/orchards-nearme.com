Blueberry Picking Season Alabama

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Blueberry picking season in Alabama is late May through July, with rabbiteye varieties in Baldwin and Covington counties typically peaking in June.

Typical season: Late May through July, with rabbiteye varieties in Baldwin and Covington counties typically peaking in June. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Blueberries in Alabama

South Alabama's sandy, acidic soil is well suited to rabbiteye blueberries, and the farms clustered around Baldwin and Covington counties, a couple hours south of Montgomery, anchor the state's commercial blueberry industry. Several of these family operations open rows to pick-your-own visitors each early summer.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Montgomery, Alabama page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Alabama

Alabama orchards and farms commonly grow rabbiteye varieties like Tifblue and Climax, along with southern highbush cultivars bred for warm winters. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Alabama

Call ahead before driving to Baldwin or Covington counties, since rabbiteye blueberries ripen quickly in Alabama's early summer heat and the picking window can shift fast. Go in the cooler morning hours both for comfort and because berries hold up better off the bush before the day heats up.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
