Best Raspberry Varieties in North Dakota

North Dakota orchards and farms commonly grow summer-bearing varieties like Latham and Nova alongside everbearing Heritage and Caroline types. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Raspberry Picking in North Dakota

Many Midwest raspberry farms combine picking with other summer produce, so ask what else is ready — sweet corn and tomatoes often overlap with peak raspberry season.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
