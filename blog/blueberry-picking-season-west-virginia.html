Where to Pick Blueberries in West Virginia

West Virginia's mountainous terrain limits large-scale blueberry farming, but the state's naturally acidic soil supports a number of small highbush farms in the hills surrounding Charleston.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Charleston, West Virginia page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in West Virginia

West Virginia orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Duke, Bluecrop, and Blueray. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in West Virginia

West Virginia's small blueberry farms in the hills around Charleston are worth calling ahead to, since they don't produce at the scale of flatter-land operations elsewhere in the country.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
