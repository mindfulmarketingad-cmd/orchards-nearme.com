Where to Pick Oranges in Alabama

Near Montgomery, Alabama's small citrus industry is concentrated along the Gulf Coast in Baldwin County, where the water's moderating effect on temperature allows cold-hardy satsuma oranges to be grown commercially this far north.

Best Orange Varieties in Alabama

Alabama orchards and farms commonly grow cold-hardy satsuma mandarins, the backbone of the small Gulf Coast citrus industry, along with a limited number of sweet orange plantings. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Orange Picking in Alabama

Gulf Coast satsuma season is short and weather-dependent, so calling ahead before the drive to Baldwin County is essential.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
