Where to Pick Blueberries in Tennessee

Middle Tennessee's blueberry farms, grown on family operations within driving distance of Nashville, take advantage of the region's moderate climate and reasonably acidic soil to produce a respectable highbush crop each summer.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Nashville, Tennessee page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Tennessee

Tennessee orchards and farms commonly grow rabbiteye varieties like Tifblue and Climax, along with southern highbush cultivars bred for warm winters. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Tennessee

Middle Tennessee blueberry farms near Nashville peak in summer; call ahead to confirm availability, since these tend to be smaller family operations than the region's apple farms.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
