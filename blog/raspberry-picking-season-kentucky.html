Best Raspberry Varieties in Kentucky

Kentucky orchards and farms commonly grow primocane (everbearing) varieties bred for heat tolerance, such as Caroline and Autumn Bliss, grown mostly at higher elevations. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Raspberry Picking in Kentucky

Go early in the day during Southern raspberry season, since lowland heat and humidity can make afternoon picking uncomfortable and softens the berries fast.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
