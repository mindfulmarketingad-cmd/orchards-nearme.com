Where to Pick Raspberries in Utah

Near Salt Lake City, the mountain west's cooler, high-elevation valleys suit raspberry canes well, with the intense sunshine and cool nights concentrating flavor. Small u-pick farms are scattered through irrigated valleys in Colorado, Idaho, and Utah in particular.

Use the map on our raspberry picking near Salt Lake City, Utah page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Raspberry Varieties in Utah

Utah orchards and farms commonly grow cold-hardy varieties such as Boyne and Killarney, along with everbearing Heritage and Caroline types suited to short high-elevation growing seasons. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Raspberry Picking in Utah

Mountain-grown raspberries ripen unevenly through the season, so calling ahead to check which rows are currently producing saves a wasted trip.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
