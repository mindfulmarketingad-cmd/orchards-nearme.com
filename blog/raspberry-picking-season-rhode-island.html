Best Raspberry Varieties in Rhode Island

Rhode Island orchards and farms commonly grow a mix of summer-bearing varieties such as Nova and Killarney and everbearing types like Heritage and Caroline. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Raspberry Picking in Rhode Island

Raspberries bruise easily, so bring shallow containers rather than deep buckets to avoid crushing the berries at the bottom.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
