Where to Pick Blueberries in Illinois

Illinois blueberry growers cluster in the sandier soils of the southern part of the state, with several u-pick farms within range of Springfield that have amended their ground specifically to lower the pH blueberry bushes demand.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Springfield, Illinois page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Illinois

Illinois orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Bluecrop, Jersey, and Patriot. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Illinois

Southern Illinois blueberry farms tend to have more reliable crops than those further north, so it may be worth the extra drive from Springfield. Call ahead, since these are smaller operations than the Midwest's major apple orchards.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
