Cherry Picking Season Delaware

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Cherry picking season in Delaware is late May through June for the limited cherry operations near the Pennsylvania border, with the season aligning closely with neighboring Maryland's.

Typical season: Late May through June for the limited cherry operations near the Pennsylvania border, with the season aligning closely with neighboring Maryland's. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Cherries in Delaware

Delaware's orchard scene includes a modest amount of cherry production, particularly in the northern tier of the state near the Pennsylvania border where the climate is slightly cooler and more amenable to stone fruits. Cherry picking options are limited compared to neighboring states, but the orchards that do grow cherries typically offer the experience alongside their larger apple operations, making it easy to combine both in a single visit.

Use the map on our cherry picking near Dover, Delaware page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Cherry Varieties in Delaware

Delaware orchards and farms commonly grow sweet cherries like Bing and Rainier alongside sour pie cherries such as Montmorency. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Cherry Picking in Delaware

Delaware's cherry picking options require some research to find, but the farms in northern Delaware that grow cherries tend to communicate picking days through local social media and farm websites. Going when they announce readiness rather than planning too far ahead ensures you find cherries at peak ripeness rather than past their best.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
