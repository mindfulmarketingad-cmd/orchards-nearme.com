Best Blueberry Varieties in Delaware

Delaware orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Duke, Bluecrop, and Blueray. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Delaware

Delmarva blueberry farms near Dover peak in July; visit on a weekday morning for the best selection before weekend crowds pick through the ripest bushes. Bring a cooler, since blueberries soften in the heat faster than people expect.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
