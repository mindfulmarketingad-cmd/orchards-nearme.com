Typical season: May through July, with the peak typically falling in June at the state's major rabbiteye farms. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Blueberries in Georgia

Georgia is one of the nation's leading blueberry producers, with rabbiteye varieties thriving in the sandy soils of South Georgia, and the orchards within a couple hours of Atlanta produce some of the most celebrated blueberries in the Southeast each June.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Atlanta, Georgia page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Georgia

Georgia orchards and farms commonly grow rabbiteye varieties like Tifblue and Climax, along with southern highbush cultivars bred for warm winters. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Georgia

Georgia blueberry season peaks in June; call ahead since intense summer heat can accelerate ripening beyond what the calendar suggests. South Georgia farms a couple hours from Atlanta tend to have the largest commercial operations.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
