Where to Pick Blueberries in Nevada

Nevada's arid climate and alkaline soil make blueberry growing a real challenge, and the small number of growers in irrigated valleys near Carson City who attempt it do so on a limited, carefully managed scale.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Nevada

Nevada orchards and farms commonly grow a small number of cold-hardy highbush varieties grown in heavily amended, acidic soil. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Nevada

Blueberry picking near Carson City is a niche, limited option — call ahead to confirm availability, since Nevada's arid climate makes for unpredictable small-scale yields.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
