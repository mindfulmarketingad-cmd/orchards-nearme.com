Blueberry Picking Season Maryland

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Blueberry picking season in Maryland is late June through July on the Eastern Shore, continuing the Delmarva Peninsula's berry-growing tradition.

Typical season: Late June through July on the Eastern Shore, continuing the Delmarva Peninsula's berry-growing tradition. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Blueberries in Maryland

Maryland's Eastern Shore, part of the same Delmarva Peninsula peach-and-berry belt that runs through Delaware, supports a number of highbush blueberry farms within range of Annapolis that have grown the fruit commercially for generations.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Annapolis, Maryland page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Maryland

Maryland orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Duke, Bluecrop, and Blueray. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Maryland

Eastern Shore blueberry farms near Annapolis peak in July; cross the Bay Bridge on a weekday morning to beat both traffic and weekend crowds. These farms have grown blueberries commercially for generations.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
