Where to Pick Blueberries in Missouri

Missouri's blueberry farms cluster in the Ozark foothills, where better drainage and slightly more acidic soil give the bushes an edge, and several u-pick operations within range of Jefferson City have built a loyal local following.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Jefferson City, Missouri page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Missouri

Missouri orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Bluecrop, Jersey, and Patriot. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Missouri

Ozark foothill blueberry farms near Jefferson City tend to have better drainage and more reliable crops than flatter parts of the state, so they're worth seeking out specifically.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
