Typical season: May through June for the limited tart cherry options in the northern mountain counties, significantly earlier than the apple season that follows in late summer. Exact timing shifts a week or two earlier or later depending on the year's weather.

Where to Pick Cherries in Alabama

Cherry picking near Montgomery is a limited prospect, as Alabama's warm and humid climate is outside the range that sweet cherry trees prefer for reliable commercial production. Those determined to find cherry picking will have the best luck heading north toward the Tennessee border or into the mountain communities of northeastern Alabama, where small orchards occasionally grow tart cherry varieties in the cooler summer months.

Best Cherry Varieties in Alabama

Alabama orchards and farms commonly grow a limited selection of tart cherry varieties suited to the region's mild winters, where reliable sweet cherry production is uncommon. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Cherry Picking in Alabama

For Alabama residents seeking cherry picking, the most practical approach is to monitor the orchard listings north of the state and plan a trip specifically timed to late May when tart cherry varieties in the mountain foothills come ripe. The reward for the planning and the drive is fresh cherries that no grocery store can match.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
