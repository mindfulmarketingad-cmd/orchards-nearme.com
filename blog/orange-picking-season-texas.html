Orange Picking Season Texas

July 1, 2025 · Orchards Near Me

Orange picking season in Texas runs October through March, with the Rio Grande Valley's subtropical climate producing one of the earliest harvests in the country.

Where to Pick Oranges in Texas

Near Austin, the Rio Grande Valley in the southernmost tip of Texas is the state's citrus belt, with groves around McAllen and Mission producing oranges and grapefruit in a climate that rarely sees a hard freeze.

Best Orange Varieties in Texas

Texas orchards and farms commonly grow Navel and Valencia oranges, often grown alongside the region's famous Ruby Red grapefruit. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Orange Picking in Texas

The Rio Grande Valley citrus season can be affected by occasional winter cold snaps, so calling ahead to confirm availability is worthwhile before making the drive south.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
