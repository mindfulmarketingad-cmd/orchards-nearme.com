Where to Pick Pears in New York

Near Albany, mid-Atlantic orchards have grown pears alongside apples and peaches for generations, with the same Appalachian foothill and piedmont farms that produce the region's famous apple crop also growing a smaller but genuine pear harvest.

Best Pear Varieties in New York

New York orchards and farms commonly grow Bartlett and Bosc pears, along with some heirloom varieties preserved by smaller family orchards. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Pear Picking in New York

Mid-Atlantic pear season is brief compared to apples, so ask the orchard directly which weeks their pears are ready rather than assuming it matches the fall apple rush.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
