Where to Pick Blueberries in Michigan

Michigan is one of the top blueberry-producing states in the country, and the famous fruit belt along Lake Michigan's eastern shore, a few hours from Lansing, has built its identity around the crop for over a century thanks to the lake's moderating effect on the climate.

Use the map on our blueberry picking near Lansing, Michigan page to find specific farms by ZIP code and read visitor reviews before you go.

Best Blueberry Varieties in Michigan

Michigan orchards and farms commonly grow northern highbush varieties such as Bluecrop, Jersey, and Patriot. Availability varies by farm and time of season, so ask the farm staff which rows are currently at peak — that guidance is worth more than any printed list.

Tips for Blueberry Picking in Michigan

Michigan's fruit belt along Lake Michigan, a few hours from Lansing, is genuinely worth the drive during blueberry season — the lake's moderating effect produces some of the best blueberries in the country. Late July is typically peak season.

General tips that apply everywhere: bring your own containers or use the farm's, wear clothes you don't mind staining, dress in layers since summer mornings can start cool, and bring cash — many smaller farms are not set up for card payments at the stand.
