About Orchards Near Me

We help families find the freshest orchards, pick-your-own farms, and garden centers in their corner of the country - so every weekend can be a fun day in the fresh air.

Orchards Near Me is a free, easy-to-use directory built for apple lovers, cherry pickers, weekend gardeners, and anyone who enjoys getting outside with the people they love. From the first blossoms of spring to the apple cider donuts of fall, there is always a reason to visit a local orchard.

We have gathered thousands of orchards, farms, and garden centers across all 50 states and placed them on one friendly, interactive map. Just pop in your ZIP code, filter by what you are looking for, and start exploring the green spaces near you.

Orchards

Apple, cherry, peach, and berry orchards where you can pick your own fruit straight from the tree.

Farms

Pick-your-own farms, farm stands, and family-run growers with seasonal produce and good old-fashioned fun.

Garden Centers

Local nurseries and garden centers to help your own backyard bloom, all season long.

How it works

Listing information - including business names, addresses, ratings, and visitor reviews - is sourced from public mapping data and refreshed periodically. We focus on the basics that help you plan a visit, and we always recommend calling ahead or checking a location's website for current hours, pricing, and what is in season.

Our mission

To make it simple and joyful for families to discover the orchards and green spaces in their community, support local growers, and spend a little more time outdoors together.
