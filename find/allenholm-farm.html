★★★★★Zach Benedicto2 years ago

Great place for apple picking! Family friendly and fun! A short wagon ride to the orchard. $13 for a small bag and all you can eat apples while picking them. Very pure creamy creemee with maple sprinkles and delicious! Apple cider was good as well. Unlimited pictures with the goats! Plenty of games for the kids and adults alike. Make sure to check out the country store and don’t forget to get a creemee!
