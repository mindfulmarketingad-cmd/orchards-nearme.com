★★★★★Hailey Stalter3 months ago

My husband and I have been driving past this place for a couple years now and finally decided to swing by and see what there was to see this last week. I was flabbergasted at the number of roses available. There were quite a few with rose beetles, so I didn't take any home, but I imagine once the first flush of blooms subsides the beetles will too and I'll be able to swing back by and pick something nice out for Mother's Day. It was pretty easy to navigate the center with the carts provided, and we loved the kitschy vibe around the little ponds in the front; everything felt very family friendly. Prices were expected for a smaller nursery, but it didn't put us in the poor house and I got a few vegetable starts, herbs and perennials. I was especially glad to see and purchase one of the white sage plants, as those can be somewhat difficult to locate elsewhere, especially at the large box stores. The staff were very friendly and helpful, too. We definitely plan to return in the future!
