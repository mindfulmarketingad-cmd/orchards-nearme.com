If I could give this place 6 stars I would. It is by far the most family friendly winery I have ever been to. Our boys (3 and 5) had hours of fun while we as parents got to enjoy some wine. We stopped for some lunch and it was delicious. I highly suggest the roasted corn- so sweet and crisp. We bought lots of pumpkins, corn stalks and autumn decorations.

★★★★★Rashid Ahmed4 years ago

It’s a huge farm with a lot of activities. If you are looking forward for a nice family day then this could be on the recommendation list. There are a lot of fun stuff for kids.
