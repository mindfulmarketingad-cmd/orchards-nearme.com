★★★★★Seth Eden9 months ago

I love this place! Their staff are friendly, kind, courteous, and down to earth honest! Their store location is kept clean with plenty of space to wander around. The available produce is in season and filled with flavor!! I love the peaches, and the peanuts are perfect for making boiled peanuts in large batches! Their cider looks amazing but I cannot drink this because of the sugar. They have a lovely space for families to hang out and take pictures by some old tractor and the kids to swing on a swing set. You can wander around the farm some in back, just don't go to far. They have rows and rows of persimon trees, and a whole plot just for flowers which is food for their honey bees. This farm is doing it right!! Their location is handicap accessible, just be aware their parking lot is gravel, so take that into consideration.
