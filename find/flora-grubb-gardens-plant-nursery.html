★★★★★Guillaume Bourcy8 months ago

Green? Super-green I’d say! This has become my favorite nursery on the Westside! Conveniently located in Marina del Rey right by the 90 entrance/exit, it’s set on a spacious lot with an impressive selection of both California native and American plants. The native section is particularly strong, but you’ll also find a great variety of other species to explore. The outdoor layout makes you feel like you’re wandering through a little jungle in the heart of L.A. — peaceful, lush, and inspiring. Prices are very fair — not the cheapest in town, but definitely competitive for the Westside given the quality and selection. Little extra that makes this place unique is the coffee cart located on the parking lot. Coffee and plants, a great combo. If you’re in the area, stop by, pick up a plant, and do a small favor for Mother Nature!
