★★★★★Mitch Johnson2 years ago

We have had trouble growing things in black clay soil. They were really helpful and said to add 4 bags of peat moss and 4 bags of sheep manure for 20' x 30' garden. They don't have bulk sand available, but black dirt, mulch, and class 5 gravel is available. Also a good note was potatoes like sandy soil. 🙂
