Small local orchard with no bells and whistles, but they have a nice selection of different types of apples. Plenty of parking. Pick your own or grab from pre-picked bushels. They also carry a variety of locally produced products like maple syrup, honey, etc. This past weekend I bought Honey Crisp, McIntosh, Cortland, Gala...

★★★★★Brandon Witterman3 years ago

Apple picking in Maine every fall is a family tradition for us and Maine-ly Apples has been great to us over the years. The variety, the scenery, and the store are all part of the perfect fall experience!

★★★★★Heather Ireland8 months ago

So refreshing to be able to go to a small family owned apple farm an not have to fight the crowds! Plenty of parking, the workers were so friendly an helpful! Kids loved being able to pick our own apples an have the selection to pick from! Price was reasonable, an small gift shop was fun to shop. Popcorn an a carmel apple for the ride home! Told family about this location an they went an checked it out as well.

★★★★★Lillian Johnson8 months ago

Went with family after trying a much busier and crazier place and it was a beautiful change of pace. The owners were helpful, kind and so friendly. They explained the different varieties and sent us off into the beautiful and very well maintained rows of apples. We really could not stop talking about how well taken care of the trees looked. (We are nerdy farm people!) 100% will be going back! Was about 45 minutes from Bangor and 30 from Belfast, well worth the drive out!
