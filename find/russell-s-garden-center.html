★★★★★RoadTrip NewEngland6 months ago

Russell’s Garden Center sits along Route 20 in Wayland, a longtime stop for gardeners and home growers across MetroWest. What looks like a modern retail space today is actually part of a business with roots stretching deep into Massachusetts agricultural history. Founded in 1876, Russell’s began as a family-run farm and gradually evolved into one of New England’s best-known garden centers. Over generations, it expanded from produce and nursery stock into a full-service destination offering annuals, perennials, trees, shrubs, houseplants, and seasonal displays. Beyond plants, Russell’s reflects how traditional New England farms adapted to suburban growth while staying locally owned. Its scale, plant selection, and year-round operation make it both a working nursery and a regional landmark for gardeners who return season after season. #russellsgardencenter #waylandma #shoplocal #newenglandgardens #gardenhistory #massachusettslife
