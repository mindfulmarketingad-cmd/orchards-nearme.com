★★★★★Sicily Moon8 months ago

Overall a great experience! We visited in the fall and got lots of squash, pumpkins, and little goodies from the shop. There were animals to feed and pet, a hayride, u-pick orchards, live music, fall drinks and snacks, and activities for everyone, what more could you want?

★★★★☆Brandi Beer8 months ago

U pick is great and a lot of varieties of squash and gourds. Animals here and hayrides as well. You can pick apples too. Some things pricey but a nice all in one spot. Has dahlia picking at entrance, but pricey for a bouquet in my opinion. Plenty of parking.

★★★★★Justin Brummage11 months ago

This farm is truly a gem, offering a wide range of activities for everyone to enjoy. You can visit the petting zoo, where you'll meet adorable pigs, rabbits, ducks, and various breeds of goats and chickens. The expansive U-pick fields are perfect for harvesting an assortment of apples, cherries, squash, and more. Don’t forget to check out the generously stocked gift shop, featuring an impressive selection of spices, unique wares, delicious candy, and thoughtful gifts. We had the pleasure of staying the night through Harvest Host and parking is ample making it a fantastic spot for a memorable stay!

★★★☆☆Tay8 months ago

A cute little farm but not much to do. Many of the pumpkins were smashed and many were not round, but that’s nature haha. The food is closed during the week I believe or there was nobody to work in the booth which was a bummer really. The hay rides also don’t run often so time your visits if you come during the week. As for the apple trees, you want to be taller or be lucky and find a ladder. They have a press your own cider station and it’s cool! Their cider is super good and their pumpkin bread.
