This store had a large selection of apples to choose from. In addition to the apples there is a bakery with delicious Fried Apple Pies and many more sweets. ★★★★★Jessica Johanneson11 months ago

Stopped on our way to see some waterfalls! I can't stop thinking about the peach cake. OH MY, how delicious. I can't wait to go back and get more! Kids had strawberry smoothies and ice cream cones, and we got a loaf of apple bread that was topped with apples, apple cake and peach cake. It was so soft and fresh!
