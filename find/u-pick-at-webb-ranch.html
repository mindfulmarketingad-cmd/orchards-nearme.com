★★★★★Sophie Tayler6 years ago

Just what we were looking for! Not every row was 'equal' - some were not doing so well, where other rows were booming. The red raspberries weren't doing so well, but the golden ones had comparable flavour. The blackberries were very well maintained, all canes off the ground and nicely shady in the morning, and picked up very quickly. They had plenty of parking, plenty of signs to direct people through the ranch, boxes provided for picking. A couple bathrooms too. Bringing a wagon definitely makes sense if you've got small kids, or are looking to pick a lot of berries. Rather than passing through the narrow archways (lovely for photos), you'll see pros heading to the left or right to avoid the traffic jam.

★★★★★Rashmi pali4 years ago

One last family outing before the school starts? Head over to Webb ranch, the blackberries are in abundance now (Aug 2021); our kids enjoyed picking and eating at the same time! More than 40 rows of berry goodness!! Tracie was the person in charge and looks like she and her boys did an amazing job of maintaining the berry fields. Webb Ranch is one family leased ranch, the 3 daughters of the ranch take great pride in their family business and it shows in how lovingly the ranch is maintained; how everyone greets and welcomes one another and are always ready to help their guests! Take a left from the U pick exit and you will reach the Stables where there are horse riding classes and beautiful horses, well maintained grounds!
