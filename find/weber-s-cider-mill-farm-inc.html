★★★★★Jess T8 months ago

Really cute farm to take kids to enjoy everything fall! Or, like myself, go as an adult to also enjoy everything fall! The cider and donuts are fantastic and as fresh as can be. The fresh picked apple selection is the best around. Prices are fair. Pumpkin selection is great! There's a small tractor ride and other things for kids to enjoy. Food truck on site. Overall a great place to stop by on a crisp fall afternoon for food and apples! Oh, and cant forget the kettle corn!!

★★★★★Jessica A.3 years ago

We went on a rainy fall day, but they had a ton of pumpkins under a sturdy canopy and the market store was open. The store had SO MUCH stuff! The baked goods were delicious and there were ciders, cheeses, pies, cider donuts, dried pastas, and so much more. Lots of local producers and local produce. I really enjoyed my cider donuts! The Halloween decor section was cute, but everything I saw was mass produced. It would have been cool to see some local artisan goods there as well. But the selection was wide and everything was cool. I was really impressed with how accessible the market was. Lots of ramps and easy areas to move around. My only disappointment was that all of the caramel apples had nuts on them. I would have loved one without nuts!

★★★★★Ashton Menefee3 years ago

Awesome place! Take the kids, go as adults, it’s all good. Be patient with the parking and it goes quick. They are in a residential neighborhood so small streets but their staff is out there directing traffic and parking. Once there, lots of cool stuff, get an Apple Cider slushy and Apple Cider donut. They have a great bakery and produce selection. The lines also move fast. For kids there is a petting zoo and they had food trucks there on a busy fall Saturday. Nice people, nice place, and a great way to spend some time.
