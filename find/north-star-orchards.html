Reviews

★★★★★Caitlin Studley Potter6 years ago

I love this place! I have fond memories of visiting it as a child with my family, and recently brought my husband with me to see the view and pick up some apples. They have a wonderfully scenic orchard, with a spot in the back for picnics or just a thoughtful moment to enjoy the view. The owners are incredibly kind, and they have a wonderful little gift shop when you pull in you can shop for local gifts including dishware, candles, candy, all things apple and more. You can purchase bags of apples and cider there as well, or you can walk around and pick your own based on what’s available that day. If you’re looking for a quintessential New England fall activity, this is it.
