★★★★☆Ronak Patel2 months ago

We recently visited Country Farms with our family, and it turned out to be a fantastic experience. The location is very family-friendly, with plenty of activities and play areas designed specifically for children. It made it easy to keep our toddler entertained and engaged throughout the visit. One of the highlights for us was the chance to see the farm animals up close. Our toddler absolutely loved it, and it was great to see how interactive and accessible everything was. A fun surprise was spotting a peacock on the property, which added a unique and memorable touch to the visit. The overall atmosphere was relaxed and enjoyable, making it a great outing for families looking to spend quality time together outdoors. There were a lot of bees around, which might concern some visitors, but they didn’t bother us at all and didn’t interfere with our experience. All in all, we had a great time and would definitely recommend this place to families with young kids. It’s a wonderful spot for a casual, fun-filled day, and we’ll certainly be coming back again.
