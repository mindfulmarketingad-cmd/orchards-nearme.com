We went to Earl May yesterday, Dec. 19, to find a tree for Christmas, and purchased an absolutely beautiful tree from their lot that is still fresh and fragrant! ★★★★★Prickles Hoggington5 years ago

The staff are very friendly. I like how knowledgeable they are, too. They were able to help me find some creeping phlox for my garden. The checkout line was a little long, but I blame that more on everybody buying a huge cart of plants than on the employees. The selection is great. They have many different annuals, perennials, trees, herbs, and vegetables. They usually have several varieties to choose from, too, and not just one type like Walmart or Lowe's. I really appreciate that, especally for annual flowers. I like variety and not just the generic mass produced seedlings you see everywhere. The plants are at an okay price. I would wait for a sale if I were buying more than a few plants, and I wouldn't buy their mini-succulents at $5 each when I can find similar plants at other places for $2 or $3. Overall, it is a great place to shop. Will be back again when I have more time to garden.
