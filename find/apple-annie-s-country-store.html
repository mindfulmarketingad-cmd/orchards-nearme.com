★★★★☆Marsha Ambrose2 weeks ago

We arrived at nine am as the store was just opening. The gal inside was warm and welcoming, and we took our time looking at everything they had for sale. We settled on taking home two pies. The apple crumb is a take and bake pie, the pecan was ready to eat. The pies were VERY reasonably priced at under 20 dollars each, they were filled with quality ingredients, and were so much better than a grocery store pie. They tasted as great as they looked! We also bought some fudge, two flavors, and it was fine, nothing outstanding, but just good fudge. We also purchased a beautiful wooden spoon for my kitchen, as they had all sorts of cool things there like that. Looking for a last minute gift of food? Bring someone a pie! Apple Annie's is definitely worth the stop, for a slice a la mode, or a whole pie!
