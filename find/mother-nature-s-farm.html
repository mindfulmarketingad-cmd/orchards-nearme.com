I’m a fairly new wheelchair user and Mother Nature’s Farm was the most ADA-friendly family spot we’ve found in Gilbert so far! The grounds were spotless and the walkway dirt was well-packed so I didn’t have to miss out on any of the fun with the littles. All the people were super friendly and I’m only sad we hadn’t visited sooner. We will certainly be back!

★★★★★Madison Browna year ago

Mother Nature’s Farm has so much to offer! We took our kids to visit the pumpkin patch and animals on a Friday and had such a blast! There are many things to do to keep the little ones entertained and lots of rest areas for when you need a break. We enjoyed feeding the animals, racing the pedal go-karts, the corn pits (those were new this year!), and the bouncy houses. You get a free pumpkin from the patch inside the farm with each paid entry ticket. Highly recommend this farm for fall festivities. This is our 3rd year visiting and we will continue to come back every year! Also I very much recommend getting there right when the farm opens at 9am so it’s not too hot by the time you leave.

★★★★★Neela Armfield2 years ago

We've been coming to this pumpkin patch since my teenagers were babies, and we still love it so much! The price is the best in town, it's great that it includes all the things plus a small pumpkin. There are countless photo spots and variety of things to see and do, all spread out over a great area of land. Their favorite this year was the corn bin, the slides, and the different animals. I recommend planning for a few hours to see and do everything. The food and drinks are reasonably priced. I also love that this is an animal rescue/sanctuary.
