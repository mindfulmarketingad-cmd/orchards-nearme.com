★★★★★Melee D3 years ago

Cute place and lots of tasty apples. I've never gone apple picking in my life but since it's a thing here I wanted to try it before there were none left this year. My family enjoyed it! The orchard is right next to their store, which was nice as I'm not able to walk far at the moment. We got two 1/2 pecks so the kids could each have their own bag to carry. We got lots of fuji, gala, and golden delicious apples. My 3 year old in particular was in heaven picking and snacking on his apples, but everyone got into the treasure hunt of finding a nice looking apple. They also have local meats, maple syrup and apple cider for sale but we just stuck to apples today. We'll be back again next season!
