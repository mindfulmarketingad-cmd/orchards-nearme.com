It’s important what we put in our bodies. Quality meat is the basis for our diet and luckily in researching healthy pork suppliers, we found Open Barn Farm. They raise premium, pasture-grown, heritage hogs. Because we live in Florida and the farm is near a Fredericksburg, VA, we decided to go Whole Hog! We arranged with Peter and Emma to pick it up on our return from a trip to Maryland. We stopped by the farm along the Rappahannock River (Beautiful!). After a quick tour of where the pigs were raised (in the open near a corn field), we picked up the packed 120quart cooler which Peter had stuffed with sausages, ribs, shoulder roasts and some of the most delicious thick-cut pork chops! The 10 hour drive home was no problem and the goods remained frozen. Definitely returning for my next one!
