Reviews

★★★★★Kristi Tannera month ago

The Flower Bin is one of those places that makes you wish it were located in your hometown instead of across the country. What started as a quick visit turned into an experience I could have easily spent hours enjoying. The selection of plants and flowers was impressive, but what truly stood out was the atmosphere. Throughout the property you'll find beautiful displays, unique planters, wind chimes, water features, and garden décor that make it feel more like a destination than a garden center. My favorite area was the rose greenhouse. I spent nearly 30 minutes wandering among the different varieties, stopping to admire and smell the blooms. The fragrance alone was worth the visit, and it was fascinating to experience how different each rose variety smelled. Whether you're an experienced gardener or simply enjoy beautiful plants and peaceful surroundings, The Flower Bin is a wonderful place to explore. Would I return? Without hesitation. In fact, I only wish it were closer to home so I could visit regularly.

★★★★★Ruthie Bohemian Writer10 months ago

By far, this is the best place to purchase any type of plant, including bushes and trees. They do a lot of their own growing and the greenhouses that they do buy from, that other places also buy from, are priced lower than the other places. Their selection is huge and is varied in terms of kinds of plants and colors. When looking for perennials, this is the place to go to! I’ve not seen anywhere that has such an amazing selection of colorful perennials. The staff is also knowledgeable and will help you with any questions you may have. They also have an amazing selection of pots, live fish swimming in a small pond, garden tools, greeting cards, seed packets, artful garden pieces, birdbaths and gift items. It’s a glorious place to walk around and feel gratitude and joy for the loveliness of nature.
