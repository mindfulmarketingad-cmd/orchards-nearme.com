It's go time. I purchased a beautiful tree on March 7 and on March 10, my landscaper planted it in the garden. By March 30th the tree lost its leaves from the bottom up and by April 11th a DNR order was issued. I did everything by the book to keep it alive. Finally I had to admit it had passed on to where all good trees go. I took my receipt, and album of pictures, from planting to death, along with the tree tag to the Special Services Desk. They have a dedicated customer for life!!! Did I forget to mention this tree cost almost $500.00? Thank you Patuxent Nursery for you quality service. I will sing your praises wherever I go!
