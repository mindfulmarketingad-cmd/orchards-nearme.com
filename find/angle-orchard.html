Reviews

★★★★★Scott12 months ago

Great local orchard, located partway up Mt. Graham. Extremely scenic location. The orchard is well-tended and very popular locally. They have buckets for picking. Check their website for ripening schedule. We went the 4th of July weekend this time, when Saturn Peaches were in season. They have ripe fruit throughout the summer and early fall. Prices are a little more than the grocery store. Our peaches were $2/lb, and we picked 19 lbs in less than an hour. Remember you're not just buying fruit. You're buying an experience and memories. You're buying local. And any you pick that are half-eaten by bugs, you'll probably put the good half in your mouth instead of on the ground or in your bucket. There is nothing like tree-ripened fruit that's still warm from the sun!
