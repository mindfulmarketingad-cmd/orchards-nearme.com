My wife and I had so much fun picking strawberries. We got a box with 6 containers it ended up costing 18.00. Good price for as much as we got. Lots of families and kids having a good time. They offer parking.

★★★★★RmJa year ago

We visited Columbia Farms U-pick every year without fail to pick strawberries and Peony flowers. The Hoods strawberries are absolutely sweet and delicious compared to the other two varieties. Only catch is that you need to freeze it or consume it within 24 hrs. The price is cheap for the quality and is 10x better than any supermarket. The staff is friendly and helpful. Their Peonies are a delight to walk among the beautiful blossoms of Peonies. They come in 3-4 vibrant colors and have a pleasant fragrance. If you have never been here, you are definitely missing out on a "must-do" family activity in Sauvie Island, Portland.
