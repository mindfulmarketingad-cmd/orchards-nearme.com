Reviews

★★★★★Melissa D'Angelo5 months ago

We tried to go here on a weekend during the fall and it was mobbed. We were directed to park somewhere down the road and walk so we decided to come back during the week. So happy we did. This farm is perfect for any small children you have in your life. The donkeys are the sweetest and you can get up close and personal with the chicken and the horses too. The farm stand has so many different kinds of apples and apple products. We walked away with a jug of apple cider and THE BEST apple cider donuts we have EVER had. If you want to go apple picking they sell out for the entire season very quickly so plan ahead.

★★★★★Leilani6 months ago

This was my first time ever apple picking and it was a blast! The apples are small but delicious. All the apple products in the store are fantastic and the apple cider donuts can't be missed.

★★★★★Katie Martin (Katie Martin Studios)8 months ago

Such a cute orchard to visit!! We went later in the season so the orchard picking was closed. But we enjoyed the animals and the farm stand! Came home with apples, pears, cider (get a bottle it’s so good), and DOUGHNUTS! Don’t pass those up, best I’ve ever had!!!

★★★★☆Angela Seitz9 months ago

I went here with my daughter (3yrs) and my mom and we really enjoyed our visit. The farm itself is really cute we loved petting Dolly and Dude, the donkeys, and seeing the huge horses. There was background info on how they came to be at Ya Ya which was fun to read. The website said $3/person for Apple picking but it was actually $4 plus $16 for the Apple bag. I thought that was a little expensive but it supports the farm and animals. The apple orchard has numerous varieties of apples, I think they could be labeled a bit better, we were unsure about which variety we were picking. The apples were quite small but it was fun tasting all the different varieties and picking them ourself. Ya Ya doesn't use any pesticides so many of the apples were being enjoyed by bugs but we filled our bag with some that were untouched. Overall the farm is really cute with nice views. The general store was a bit expensive but the donuts and small pies were delicious!! Apple picking slots sell out so if you're interested make sure to get a reservation in the spring!
