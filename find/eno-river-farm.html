★★★★★Reviewer of Things2 years ago

Eno River Farm is a really relaxing afternoon. They have a small but well stocked nursery, ice cream and drinks bar, locally sourced items for sale, and usually a few different things to pick, today for example they had blackberries and flowers (sunflowers and another type). Earlier this year they had raspberries and tulips. They have a lot of shaded seating, including seating for large groups. They don't allow pets on site. It would be a beautiful place for a picnic. Hopefully they also start doing events in the future, like concerts. It's a beautiful spot.
