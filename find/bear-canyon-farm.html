★★★★★Bonnie McCauley2 years ago

Driving to Bozeman for a yard sale item, my daughter and I noticed a little road side shed with a sign that said you pick, now open. So after our yard sale pick up we headed back to find the you pick stand. There's a little shed full of fresh picked vegetables and fresh fruit 🍓🫐🍅🌽🧅🥒🥬🥕🥕🫑🍆🧄🥔 , and fresh flowers 🌺 everything is bundled up , bagged or in containers for you. There's signs with prices and pay on an honor system !! They were out of raspberries and strawberries, a sign had a number to call if they were out. So we called and they said use the containers, open the gate and pick what you need !! So we got a few containers opened the gate to an Absolutely beautiful garden, spread far and wide, with everything you could ask for. We gathered a few more containers of fresh mouth watering strawberries and some yellow tomatoes, to go with some cucumbers from the stand. We were running out of time, so we're hoping to stop again this week for some more goodies !! It's you pick S,M,T,F 9 am - 7 pm !! Bear Canyon Farm provides employment and hands on learning for students at Mount Ellis Academy !! How great is that !! Such a beautiful blessing to offer this, thank you and God Bless !! 10/10
