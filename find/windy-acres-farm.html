★★★★☆stephanie justice9 months ago

Needed to run an errand in Geneva so I decided to make it a day trip with my 22 month old and check out Windy Acres. It was a quiet day there, maybe only 4 or 5 other families. I paid our admission which it was nice that 2 yrs and younger are free. We explored the different photo ops, which there were plenty. Most of the kid friendly stuff was appropriate for my little one, slightly seemed run down though. We got a cup of "feed" for the animals which I felt was well worth the under $5 price tag. The Goats, Llama, and Geese were eager for the treats. My son loved the Corn Bin, Giant Slide, and the Animals. I would have loved to take the train ride but maybe its only for weekends. I also felt like everything seemed "not open and ready" I especially was disappointed that not even the bubble station was set up. Overall it was nice to have most of the place to ourselves, the Apple Cider Doughnuts were tasty, and we had a lot of fun.

★★★★☆EJ Ignacio2 years ago

It was my first time in this amazing space and despite knowing from what I can see what I expected to experience, I found it quiet enjoyable. They have this really kid friendly space with a lot of activities, some of which adults can enjoy. We loved watching our little niece run around trying all the little attractions. We especially enjoyed playing shooting hoops, touring the animal farm, seeing a llama for the first time etc. The food there and drinks are delicious too!. Tried their apple cider slush which was awesome! Both donuts we sampled were delicious even the pumpkin flavored one (which was surprising!). Next time will try their cider donuts lol. The vibe was lively even with the chilly weather. It was nice to see people and their children going around and enjoying themselves. Mind you this was and still is just a field though with some little attractions. Kind of hit me that despite how modern our world is right now, there are simple joys to be found in places like this. Corny but true. 10/10 would come back again next year!
