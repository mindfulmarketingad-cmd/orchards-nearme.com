★★★☆☆Kittena week ago

Title: Great produce and solid flavors, but bring your wallet and check the hours! I visited the market side of Reeves Peach Farm at 9:00 AM, but be aware that the rest of the farm activities and the restaurant don't open until later in the day, which felt a bit overhyped for an early morning trip. If you are bringing the whole family to eat and shop, be prepared: you can easily drop over $100 here. The market is definitely on the expensive side. I grabbed 4 lbs of peaches, a good-sized watermelon ($10), a glass bottle of Fanta, and two 17 oz jars of specialty goods ($9 a piece!), putting my quick trip in the $40 range. I put the $9 jars to the test to see if they were worth the premium price tag. Jalapeño Bacon Jam: Very thick consistency and smells like real bacon immediately. It’s delightful and very savory—it would be a fantastic addition to baked chicken or a vegetable stir-fry. Habanero Peach Salsa: Smells like basic salsa, but the taste is a fun bait-and-switch. You get a quick second of peach sweetness before the habanero heat immediately takes over. If you don't like spicy food, skip this. If you do, it’s delicious. The Verdict: Are these 17 oz jars good products? Yes, you can't deny that they are solid choices. But are they the best things I've ever tasted to justify $9 a pop for regular use? No. They are absolutely a "rare occasion" treat rather than a weekly staple. Skip the basic "good food" reviews from others—if you’re coming here, just make sure you know exactly what you’re paying for!
