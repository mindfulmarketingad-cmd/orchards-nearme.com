★☆☆☆☆Taylor Camarena4 months ago

I’m not usually one to leave negative reviews, but I was genuinely disappointed by the lack of phone etiquette I experienced. I called to inquire about hydrangeas—specifically whether they were in stock and what the pricing looked like. I was told, “It depends on the hydrangeas, they are listed from $30–$40.” I then asked what varieties were available or if it would be more beneficial for me to come in and look in person. The response I received was, “Uh, well there’s nothing grown, so?” The tone felt dismissive, but I chose to continue the conversation. I asked whether they had other mature flowers ready to be potted, and the response, accompanied by a slight laugh, was, “Ha, what do you mean? Like we have roses…” At that point, I politely ended the call and let them know I would be taking my business elsewhere. It’s unfortunate, as I was genuinely looking forward to working with this company while relandscaping my yard. Customer service—especially over the phone—makes a lasting impression, and this experience fell short of expectations.
