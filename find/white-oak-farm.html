★★★★★Mary Ryan11 months ago

We have been frequenting this beautiful farm for 4 or 5 years —-we go as many times as we can in the window of opportunity so as to fill our freezer. The berries last us all winter and are just wonderful on yogurt. We also enjoy the fresh produce —squash and corn is what we have tried personally—delish! There are fresh beautiful flowers that are sometimes available as bouquets or as pick your own. My favorite part of picking the berries are all the wonderful sounds: cows mooing, sheep naaaying…sweet Rio cat has sometimes accompanied me down a row. It’s a delightful way to spend a couple of hours.

★★★★★Richard Noggin3 years ago

Every year for 10 years we come here to pick blueberries. The farm is clean with good parking. The berries bushes are well maintained and very easy to get to, super easy for young and old. This year three of us picked 22# in about an hour. 2022 Update, still the best place to pick in the area. 5 of us picked 52# in 90 minutes, I don't think we picked off of more than 8 bushes total.
