★★★★★Barbellza year ago

Found such a great plant shop! 🌿 I wasn’t even planning to stop in, but I’m so glad I did. They have an amazing selection, especially if you're looking for annuals, but they also carry a nice variety of perennials, herbs, and even a few harder-to-find plants. Everything looked healthy, well-cared for, and thoughtfully organized, which made browsing really enjoyable. The staff were absolutely wonderful — super friendly, approachable, and incredibly knowledgeable. They took the time to answer all my questions, from how big certain plants would eventually grow to how much sunlight and water they would need. It felt so refreshing to get genuine advice from people who are truly passionate about what they do, instead of just reading a tag or guessing based on vague instructions. One of the best surprises? The prices were very reasonable — almost identical to what you’d find at a big box store — but the experience was so much better. Helpful staff, a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere, and no sense of being rushed or overwhelmed. You could really take your time and enjoy choosing the right plants. I left with a few new additions to my garden and a huge smile on my face. Definitely a place I’ll be coming back to — and recommending to friends!
