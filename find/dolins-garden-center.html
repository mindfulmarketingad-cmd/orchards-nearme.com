★★☆☆☆Rachel Schaub3 months ago

I loved coming here but I will not return. They advertise that they have been in business for 45 years and I assumed I could lean on them for knowledge about plants- with that amount of time in the business. I purchased tropical milkweed from them in the month of January because they advertised it as a host plant for monarch. The caterpillars are all sick. The monarchs come out of chrysalis too weak to take flight. I have researched and found out these tropical milkweed are bad for them and they are supposed to be cut back from October-March in Florida. They are a category 2 invasive species for Florida. I am highly upset a local nursery would advertise this in the month of January. I have had to keep disposing of dead caterpillars and monarchs. I will attach an image of the sign they promoted in January. I cannot believe a local shop would do this. I expect from Lowe’s or Home Depot- Do you understand monarchs are in decline? This plant is spreading a parasite called OE.
