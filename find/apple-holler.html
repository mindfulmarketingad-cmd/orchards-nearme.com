★★★★★Rye3 months ago

Came here with my partner a few months back to try apple picking for the first time coming from Chicago. We got there around noon on a Saturday and the place was busy but they had plenty of parking along the road in addition to the full lot. The price to entry is pretty fair, and you choose a size to how big of a bag you want for collecting. Once in, this place is huge. They have plenty of food/drinks, apple picking, a petting zoo area, a corn maze and lots of other kid friendly activities. We were there for the apple picking and it was such a great time! They give you a map with all the in-season varieties and where you can find them in the orchard. There is also shuttle rides that drive you around the orchard and drop off at some points if you want to minimize walking since its a large field. Even though the place was crowded, the orchards had plenty of room to walk around and pick apples with your group. There are sooo many apples and it is overwhelming at first all the different kinds. The great part is you can try the them before deciding if you want to take them. Make sure to come hungry, we ate a ton of apples. After picking, we went to the cider house and got a cold cider. They were delicious and refreshing! It was a lovely evening and i’d recommend it for anyone looking to go apple picking!
