★★★★★Nicholas Lefebre2 years ago

One of the finest experiences I have had at any establishment. Brought our daughter out to the farm on a sunny July afternoon for some raspberry and dark cherry picking, and left with the fruits of our labor, a ton of wonderful goodies from the farm shop, and treasured memories. The farm experience is truly special, the people are wonderfully hospitable, kind, helpful, and genuinely passionate about what they do. You will not round a corner in the orchard/patch without a smiling face willing to help. We have been going to King Orchards for years, in every season, and the experience is getting better. Furthermore, the addition of hard ciders after a day of picking was a splendid ending to a great day on the farm. We sat under an old maple tree enjoying a crisp, refreshing, and well made cider, while our daughter played in the playground set up near by. Bliss. Do yourself a favor and head on down to the farm. You will not be disappointed.

★★★★★Mary Jean Meyerson2 years ago

Far and away our favorite place to pick and buy fruit in northern Michigan. We prefer to go to the main farm off M88 to pick - cherries (sweet and tart), apples, raspberries, and strawberries, but if we just want a quick stop to grab the freshest fruit, we will stop at the farm stand on 31. For picking, you pay by the container. Honestly, it’s not that much of a savings, but it sure is fun. EVERYONE should pick cherries at least once. Plus, they will pit both sweet and tart cherries for you (for $10), and it is super fun to watch.
