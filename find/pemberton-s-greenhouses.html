★★★★★Mary Beth Pecco-Cummins2 years ago

Such a gem! We absolutely love visiting Pemberton's. Such a unique experience unlike any other greenhouse in central KY...trust me, we visit Lots of greenhouses. Lots of varieties and options of annuals, perennials, rare tropicals, veggies, etc. Open year around. We have visited in all seasons and never been disappointed. The plants are extremely healthy and well taken care of, but the best part is the helpful and knowledgeable staff. It's a family owned business and it truly shows in all aspects. Even my 10 year old loves visits to Pemberton's. Take the time, make the visit, you won't be disappointed. The most recent visit was Nov 2023 and the selection of poinsettia was incredible!
