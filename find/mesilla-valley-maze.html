★★★★★Eric Davisa year ago

3rd year in arrow here. Love this place. Feel like every year gets better. The roasted corn is a must try. The flowers picking wasn’t that much this year but the maze was amazing. Great options on food. Parking can get full so plan accordingly. The last 2 weeks pumpkins go on reduce prices which was awesome. Staff very friendly.
