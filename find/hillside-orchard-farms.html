★★★★★Kenneth Davis8 months ago

There a lot to see and do here. Selections of apples that we have never heard of were available. The staff will be glad to let you sample a slice before you buy a basket. All sorts of preserves and jams as well. Again, some that I've never heard of. Too many selections! If you have a sweet tooth check out their bakery, perhaps go for a peach or apple fried pie like I did. Warning, one fried pie is enough to share. Plenty of parking. Easy to find. While you are there be sure to get your Farm Passport book stamped. If you don't know about this program be sure to ask the staff for a book.

Low key orchard. Away from the crowds. Great apples & tomatoes. Just about any kind of jelly, jam or honey you could hope for. Corn maze, hay rides and feed the goats too. Perfect on a fall afternoon.

★★★★★Kimberly Smith9 months ago

We loved our experience. They are open for U-pick apples during the week. The orchard was accessible for my daughter who uses a walker. I recommend it for anyone who needs stroller or wheelchair accessible experiences. The orchard was well maintained, flat and easy to navigate.

★★★★★Thomas Simpson8 months ago

Hillside orchard is a beautiful place nestled in the mountains of northern Georgia close to Talluah falls state park. The farm used to make sorghum molasses in the old days. That also have a gemstone mining area for kids. They are vet kid friendly and handicap and wheelchair accessible. They have lots of jellies, syrups, candies, and bakery goods. They have restrooms available and have handicap parking along with plenty of regular parking. We ate a sweet potato fritter. It was very yummy! This is one of the farms on the Farm Bureau farmers market tour. You get a passport and have them stamp it to get different prizes. We have visited 10 farms so far. This farm is in the Blue Ridge Mountains region.
