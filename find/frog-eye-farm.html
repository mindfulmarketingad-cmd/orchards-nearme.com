★★★★★Pia Boje3 years ago

Wow, what an amazing place to pick blueberries. I read about the farm in one of the travel brochures you can pick up in visitor centers and started following them on Facebook last year. When I saw it was picking time my family and I made the trip there on a Sunday in early July. The farm was easy to find with navigation but definitely easy to miss if you don't know where it is. The opening hours at that point were 7am to 12pm. We got there 10:30ish. There were some cars parked but it wasn't over crowded. And there's enough room for everyone to spread out. The surrounding area is beautiful. We picked up 2 little buckets and started picking. The owners were very nice and said there are 20 different kind of blueberries growing and they encourage you to taste the berries to see which ones you like best. They also offered free bottles of water. It was super fun to walk through all the rows and try all the berries. At the end they weighed the berries and you had to pay. Be prepared to pay cash, venmo or by check. There is a portable potty on the farm. All in all the experience was nice, nothing fancy about the farm, just old fashioned blueberry picking which we enjoyed as a family. If you are looking for delicious blueberries and a family outing look no further. I hope to pick more berries next year!
