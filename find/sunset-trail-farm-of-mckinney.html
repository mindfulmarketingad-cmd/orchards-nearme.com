★★★★☆R Morretti8 months ago

A delightful fall escape at Sunset Trail Farm! This little farm in McKinney is absolutely charming. We spent an afternoon exploring their fall events, hay maze, pumpkin selecting, crafts, fun games, and of course sipping their standout blackberry lemonade. The kid activities are plentiful, from duck races to photo ops and small petting animals. Even though my toddler had a meltdown (hey, it happens!), the atmosphere was warm, friendly, and welcoming, and it made me want to come back and do the parts we missed (animal feeding, face painting) next time. The farm is family-owned and leans into sustainable, regenerative practices, especially during their berry seasons. They grow thornless blackberries, limit attendance so the experience isn’t overcrowded, and schedule events like “Date Night at the Farm” and “Mommy & Me” tours to deepen the connection. Some things to keep in mind: buy your tickets ahead of time (they often limit walk-ups), wear appropriate shoes (it’s a working farm), and arrive prepared for narrow roads and a bit of farm logistics. Pets are not allowed, and some attractions may depend on staffing or scheduling. But overall? If you’re looking for that sweet, local, family-friendly farm vibe with beautiful autumn touches and fun for kids, this place is a winner. We’ll be returning next fall for sure.
