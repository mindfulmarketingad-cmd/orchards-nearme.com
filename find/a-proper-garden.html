★★★★★Sunny2 years ago

This establishment transcends the typical nursery or garden; it's a beloved local gem where one can immerse themselves in a world of botanical wonders. Here, you'll discover a wide array of plants, flowers, and decorative items that will captivate your senses and elevate your surroundings. Recently, I had the pleasure of hosting a book club meeting at their cafe, a delightful surprise as I was previously unaware of their culinary offerings. The menu they offer for brunch is truly outstanding, featuring a delectable selection of dishes that cater to diverse tastes. Among the highlights were the scrumptious Four Cheese Quiche and the mouthwatering Harvest Quiche, which I had the pleasure of savoring. The Grilled Vegetable Flatbread and Risotto were equally impressive, showcasing their commitment to fresh and flavorful ingredients. To top off the culinary journey, their seasonal plated dessert, featuring a delightful of sorbet and cheesecake, was a heavenly finale. It's a place where you can savor both the beauty of nature and the pleasures of gastronomy. This place is my favorite!
