★★★★★Koria week ago

If you are looking for the perfect family-friendly summer activity in Michigan, you have to visit Felzke Farms in Dewitt! We spent a beautiful morning out in their strawberry patches, and it was an incredible experience from start to finish. The fields were absolutely loaded with gorgeous strawberries. There is seriously nothing quite like the taste of a sun-ripened, fresh Michigan strawberry—they are incredibly sweet and juicy! It was a wonderful environment for kids of all ages. Our baby had the absolute best time sitting right in the rows, helping us pick (and doing plenty of taste-testing along the way!). The farm is clean, easy to navigate, and the perfect spot to teach kids where their food comes from while supporting an awesome local business. We walked away with a massive haul of the best berries we've had all year. If you haven't been out yet, don't miss out—there's still time left in the season! We will definitely be making this an annual family tradition. 🍓☀️🧺
