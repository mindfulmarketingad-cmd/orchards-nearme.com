★★★★★Cheri Doane2 months ago

I have used a different garden center in past years, but this year I decided to make the trip to Grimes and I’m glad I did. This is a locally-owned business, and the owner is involved in the day-to-day operation. I looked at the large selection and wanted something slightly different, so Rita helped me on the spot. She went above and beyond to create beautiful pots that were just what I wanted. I was also assisted by another staff named Stacia, who answered questions with a smile. I even had time for a brief chat with the owner, Kyle. All in all, I was able to find what I wanted, have two custom pots created, ask questions, and be on my way within 30 or 40 minutes. I learned some helpful information about the flowers and greens that I had chosen, and now I realize why my lobelia from a different business stopped blooming mid-summer. I will definitely go to Canoyer Garden Center again!
