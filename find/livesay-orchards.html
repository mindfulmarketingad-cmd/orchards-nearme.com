My friend and I had a great fall fun day at this orchard. They had a pumpkin patch and apple orchards to pick from with a hayride. We did the corn maze, looked at the sunflower fields, shopped around the store, and had fun in the family fun zone. I am recommending this place to my friends with families as it is a great outing for kids, as well! There is also food onsite with delicious fall donuts, and good facilities. Helpful staff were posted around, which I appreciated. It was $2/lb for apples, so about $30 for a full basket- but it’s local, direct, and the apples were actually very good to eat!

★★★★★Andria Manquen8 months ago

Livesay Orchards is such a wonderful place for kids of all ages! There’s so much to do and explore — our kids had a blast playing in the corn pit, kicking the soccer ball, milking the fake cow, enjoying the playground, and especially using the Apple Blaster! The hayride out to the field was a big hit too, and they loved getting to pick their own pumpkins and apples straight from the trees. The orchard is well maintained and has plenty of parking, which makes visits easy and stress-free. It can get a little expensive if you’re bringing a large group, but the experience is worth it. One thing to note — it’s not very stroller friendly, so if you have a stroller with small tires, you might want to plan on baby-wearing instead. Overall, it’s a fun, educational, and memorable place for families, and we’re so grateful to have it close by!
