Reviews

★★★★★J Choi11 months ago

Such an adorable place! I originally went to pick strawberries, but since it was the end of the season, I ended up picking lots of raspberries and blueberries instead. One thing I didn’t realize beforehand is that this place also serves as a wedding venue! It didn’t seem like a wedding was set up that day, but the whole farm was still absolutely beautiful. The u-pick process is simple — check in at the farm stand, grab a basket, and head out to the fields. After picking, you return to weigh your harvest and pay. They do accept cards, but bringing some cash might be a good idea just in case. I was hoping to buy duck eggs and regular eggs after picking, but sadly they were already sold out by the time I got back. So, if you're interested in getting eggs, I’d recommend buying them first before heading out to pick berries!
