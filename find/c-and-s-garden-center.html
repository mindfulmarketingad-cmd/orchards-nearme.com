★★★★★Brent Meagliaa year ago

This is a great nursery located in central Hawthorne that has a decent selection of just about everything you may need. Whether you grow tomatoes, blueberries, fuchsia, sunflowers, or cannabis this place has everything you need. This is true of many places in the area, but what separates this nursery is the attitude of the employees and the owner himself. You know who owns the place just by the way he acts. You will also know who takes care of the plants because of how enthusiastic and knowledgeable he is. I help a disabled person get to and from El Camino so I often stop there with him so we can walk around and he can enjoy the plants and walk my dog. I waited until I had been there five times before leaving this review just to make sure it wasn’t a one time thing. I had bought several plants, nutrients and soil, and if I need anything that they don’t carry they offer to order it for me. The prices are not low, but they definitely are not high either. For example I think I paid $-10-$12 for a medium bag of Ocean Forest and $12-$13 for a couple feet of organic acid mix. All their plants are source locally and guaranteed to be appropriate for our environment. That’s not the case with Lowe’s or Home Depot. This is a gardener’s nursery and I recommend it to everyone I know that enjoys gardening. Stop by and support your local nursery!
