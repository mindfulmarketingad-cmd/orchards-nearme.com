Waldoch Farm has a good selection of produce in good condition. All of it seemed fresh and lasted plenty long enough for me to enjoy. The folks working there were also very kind and helpful. The place was clean and had a lot of parking available. Overall, Waldoch farm is a good spot to stop at if you're looking for some good, fresh produce at reasonable prices.

★★★★★TinaEh VangLee8 months ago

Went on Saturday afternoon. There are a lot of activities to do here and its quite big! We did not expect to stay here for 3 hours and it was an excellent experience for families and especially for kids. Activities ranges from: Corn Maze, Corn Pit, Tractor Rides, Pumpkin Patch field, Farm animal feeding, photo spots, kids play areas, zip lining, and a few others. The animal feeding is a decent deal, $5 for 2 small cups to feed the farm animals. the food here is alright, its more on the expensive side but it does the job to keep you filled up. Look at pictures to see what they offer. Overall, an excellent place to visit. WELL worth the entrance fee as other farms charge for each activity. Pay one time entrance fee and most of the activities are free apart from buying other items from certain activities; food for animal feeding, buying Pumpkin. we had an excellent time and experience. its definitely one farm that's close enough to the cities to really enjoy!

★★★★★Mae Bangtan8 months ago

We went on a Sunday afternoon around 4pm. It was not crowded. There was enough space and activities for everyone to roam around and enjoy. My favorite was probably the animals, particularly the horse and MINI PONY. 😍 One time entrance fee and all activities are included, except purchase of pumpkins, animal feed and food. There was so much activities to do, we lost track of time and missed the hayride to the pumpkin patch because they cut the rides 45 minutes to closing. We'll definitely be back next year to spend the whole day there. Season pass is also an option bc they have activities all summer til fall. Worth the season pass if you're coming for the events/activities.

★★★★★Amy Workman3 years ago

The Joyer Adventure Farm is our favorite pumpkin patch experience in the TC area. Well worth the price of admission. We were there for 4 hours and maybe did 2/3 of everything there was to do and that was hustling. Best part is the staff. It's easy to feel like cattle at these places sometimes, but not here. Every person we came across was focused on making sure you were having a good time. There was always someone to ask for directions or clarification, but you weren't bugged by them either. We may need to go a second time this year. The food options were much better than expected. Sauerkraut dogs, mac and cheese dogs, chili dogs, tots, great curds, great hot cider, and funnel cake fries. They take cards at all the concessions and for things like the apple blaster (so fun). Our only regret is not getting the bucket of mini donuts and chickening out and just getting the bag. Best. Ever. So homemade. They put the state fair donuts to shame. We won't make that mistake again.
