Reviews

★★★★★Mohammad Alshoula week ago

Indian Creek Farm is a wonderful place to visit. The scenery is beautiful, the atmosphere feels peaceful, and the staff is very friendly and welcoming. The restrooms were shining clean, which really stood out. The donuts were absolutely delicious and fresh, and the strawberry-picking experience was great. We also saw pears, peaches, raspberries, and apples, which made the visit even more enjoyable. It is also a great place for photo sessions, with plenty of nice spots for pictures. Overall, it was a very pleasant experience and definitely a place worth visiting again.
