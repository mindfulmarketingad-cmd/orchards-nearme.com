★★★★☆Rosemary Ramireza year ago

Overall a really lovely place to visit and I hope I get to come back soon. Good selection and friendly, helpful staff. I picked up two small false cypress shrubs, a tassel fern and some sword ferns on my trip. The Fernspray cypress, tassel fern and sword ferns were all very healthy and I’m thrilled with them (first three photos). The Soft Serve cypress, though, was incredibly root bound and noticeably more sparse and yellow (fourth photo) than the same-size Soft Serve cypress I picked up from another nursery earlier this month (fifth photo). It really needed to have been potted up at some point. I had to prune off a lot of matted, encircling roots and I hope it makes a recovery this spring.
