★★☆☆☆Cloe Beauty8 months ago

The roasted corn was honestly the best corn I’ve ever had in my life, perfectly sweet and delicious! Their Apple Cider Smash was really good too, and the apple cider donuts were warm and yummy. Now for the not-so-great parts: the lines were extremely long, easily over an hour wait just to get food or do any activity. Some of the vendors felt sketchy, and one BBQ food stand charged me extra “tax” fees that weren’t listed anywhere — no other vendor did that. The food was also very overpriced for the small portions. The website is misleading because it says general admission includes hayrides, but when you get there, it’s actually an extra $5. Pretty much every single activity or item costs extra, so your ticket only covers entry and a free scoop of cheesecake. To top it off, they only accept cash, and the on-site ATM hits you with a $4 fee, which is ridiculous. Overall, it could’ve been a much better experience with more transparency and organization.
