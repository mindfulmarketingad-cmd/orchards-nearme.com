We had a great morning at Chapple Farms with Farmer Tracy! The birthday girl was more timid than the younger girls so Tracy went out of her way for Lily to feel included and ease her fears. You generally can feed capybaras outside but when my daughter started scream crying from fear of emus (irrationally, they weren’t doing anything wrong), Tracy saved the day by allowing us to feed the inside capybaras instead of the outside capybaras and then once Lily was calmed down, she even fed the emus from the other side of the fence with her new found braveness- we were so proud and owe it to Tracy for going the extra step to help her feel at ease. Her ability to converse at the kid’s level and educate them about the animals and farm experience. The visit included much more than I expected. The bathrooms were clean, they offered ice water to the girls when we were done, and should we have brought our handicapped son, the footing was flat and easy to navigate. There were moments the girls were a little over the top with excitement and Tracy redirected gently when appropriate OR let them play even if that wasn’t part of the tour. It’s obvious that she truly loves her employer’s farm, her job, and teaching about the animals. Overall, a 5 star experience for sure!
