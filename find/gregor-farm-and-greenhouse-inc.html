★☆☆☆☆Hup Cala year ago

Stopped in today at about 8:45 am. Usually nurseries open early in the summer. I walked in and there was a sign welcoming customers. The checkout stand wasn’t open but I assumed that was because they hadn’t had any customers wanting to check out yet. So I walked around a bit until I heard a woman say “we do open at 9 am”. I said, “I’m sorry, I assumed you opened at 8”. To which her response was “the sign isn’t illuminated and all the sites say 9. I’m going to keep watering and I’ll try not to spray you”. I immediately headed out the door. On my way out, I looked for the sign she mentioned and found a neon sign in direct sunlight, making it impossible to see if it was on or not. And since I had been there before, I didn’t need to look at their website or get directions from google. If her goal was to get me to leave and never come back, congratulations, mission accomplished. Gardening should be fun and one shouldn’t be threatened to get hosed down for showing up 15 minutes before they open.
