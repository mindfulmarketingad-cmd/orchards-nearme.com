★★★★★Maggie Thompson7 months ago

Very cute farm in Maine with a small cafe/restaurant inside with lots of bagels, sandwiches, bakery items, and drinks to choose from. There's also some merch and local products available for purchase too. They had a small pumpkin patch outside and a few animals you could see from outside. They also had tables outside overlooking the fields which was really beautiful in the fall. Good amount of free parking and the staff was very friendly. Would definitely recommend checking this place out if you're in the area! Support local!

★★★★★April Ritter5 months ago

This little local gem is truly something special. It’s a charming farm stand with a tiny café serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner, along with a thoughtfully curated selection of local goods, wine, and more. I stopped in this morning for a sandwich and latte, and it genuinely made my whole day. Absolutely delicious, quaint and welcoming. It’s also clear how much the family behind this place cares about their community. Even after a recent house fire, they worked hard to reopen so they could continue supporting their staff, which says so much about who they are. Well worth going out of your way to visit and support. I highly recommend the Farmer’s Breakfast Wrap!

★★★★★Christina Millera year ago

Popped into this super cute spot for the first time since the new owners took over, and I’m so glad I did! The menu is packed with options—whether you're in the mood for a breakfast sandwich, a hot latte, fresh bagels, pizza, a burger, or even a salad. Plus, they’ve got all sorts of fun pantry goodies, gifts, beer, wine, fresh eggs, and some chilled and frozen items. While you wait for your food, you can wander outside to say hey to the chickens, goats, donkey, and pig! We decided to hang out and had lunch at the big table that fits 4-6 people. We tried a little bit of everything: breakfast sandwich, pizza, burger, and falafel wrap. Everything was soooo tasty! There are also a couple of bar seats if you want to grab a quick bite. Can’t wait to come back!

★★★★★humminghorse2 years ago

Really enjoyed staying here overnight in my camper and sampling some yummy meals and also enjoyed watching families enjoy immersing themselves in the experience of apple picking and friendly encounters with farm animals. Also, they are decorated for Halloween and have beautiful pumpkins and marigolds for sale. The farm is lovely and right on the Sebago to sea greenway which I took advantage of with my dog. My dog is good with farm animals but still, I kept her up because of the food service & young families ( There is a playground and lawn games in the picnic area too). The staff work hard and are friendly and helpful. Market has an abundant variety of homegrown fresh foods and treats!
