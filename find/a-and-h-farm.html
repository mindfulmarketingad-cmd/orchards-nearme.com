★★★★★JustAnMHKGirl A3 months ago

A&H Farm is hands down one of the best spots in the Manhattan area for a family day out, and it’s a place that kids absolutely love. There is so much to keep them entertained, from the petting zoo to the grain bins, but the real star of the show has to be the giant slide. It’s always a huge hit and provides endless fun for the little ones while the parents enjoy the fresh air. During the fall, their pumpkin patch is top-tier; it’s the perfect place to wander around and find that ideal pumpkin while taking in the beautiful scenery. The experience doesn’t end with the outdoor activities, though. I always make sure to stop by the attached store before heading home. It is packed with incredible local goods, from fresh produce and honey to unique jarred preserves and seasonal decor. The staff is always so friendly and helpful, making the whole visit feel like a true community experience. Whether you’re there for the high-energy kids' activities or just to stock up on some local treats, it’s a fantastic way to spend an afternoon.
