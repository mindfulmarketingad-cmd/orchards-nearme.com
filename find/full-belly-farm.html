★★★★☆Alexey Subach9 months ago

It’s a nice beautiful place but it felt more like a small shop than a farm when we visited at the end of September. They didn’t have any ice cream / creamy with strawberry as the machine “closes off” around Labour Day. They sold a lot of pumpkins and some other stuff from local producers (cheese, maple syrup etc) but nothing you can’t find in small towns. It may be interesting to pop by if you are really close but otherwise I wouldn’t bother with more than 10 min ride
