Reviews

★★★★★James Thomas4 months ago

Great selection of fruit and vegetables, both grown there and semi-locally imported, including some more are varieties such as Blue Hubbard squash. Wonderful orchard for apple picking, and their apple cider donuts are to die for.

★★★★★Paul Nakazawa2 years ago

Certainly a seasonal favorite for apple picking, pumpkins, gourds, and fall goodies (cider donuts, apple cider, assortment of baked and frozen pies, etc.) Farm store and cafe on one side of the road and pick-your-own apple orchard, pumpkin/gourd patch on the other. Great for families. One caveat for the orchard — not accessible for physically disabled persons or elderly folks where walking up/down hills is difficult or there is possibility of falling. The farm store is accessible and has parking in front.

★★★★★Linda Poirier8 months ago

Best apple cider donuts ever! Their Wilson’s Farm chicken pot pies are truly delicious. The employees are friendly and helpful (especially Clarissa). Thoroughly enjoy the whole place!

★★★★★Susanne E.4 years ago

Delicious breads and baked goods, excellent farm fresh produce, gourds, fantastic fresh mums, cider donuts, and let's not forget the take out window for hot apple dumplings! Love that the farm stand is separate from the apple picking for ease of making a quick(ish) put stop. Is cash only but there is an ATM inside. Have been coming here for 30 yrs easily..since I was a kid. Never disappoints.

★★★★★Ralph Stan3 years ago

First time there, really good selection of pre-made food like pies, etc.. The apple dumplings and apple cider donuts are made constantly and absolutely delicious. You can even get ice-cream for the apple dumplings! You pick up the dumplings and donuts in the side window. There are picnic tables there to eat if you want to add all. Our friends say the pies are amazing, we will be going back to get some 😁
