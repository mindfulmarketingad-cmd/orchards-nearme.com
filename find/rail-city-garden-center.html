I did not move my dog toward the cat, I noticed the cat's aggressive/defensive behavior and was actively trying to get away from the situation. ★★★★★Cathryn McGregora month ago

I had no idea this place was here until today. And I honestly don't want to share this review because this place was hidden jem! I wanna keep it to myself! This place deserves 5 stars. My friend had warned me that the prices were high, but I didn't find it to be so. I found the prices to be comparable with those of other big chain plant centers. The variety of plants to choose from was more varied than the big chains. There was a lovely ambiance here that you won't find at other places. There are two water features to look at. One had giant coy and turtles swimming in. The turtles are available for purchase and the whole thing was just lovely. There was also a large tree house with a winding slide coming out from it. Im not sure, but I think children would be allowed to utilize it. There was an area by check out that had complimentary coffee, water and fresh popped pop corn. We were also offered assistance right away and enjoyed chit chat with a happy employee. It was an all around enjoyable experience. I highly recommend. Oh, and they had a lot of other things one would need when landscaping or building a garden, but I didn't check the prices on those. I did check on the prices of their saplings and I was surprised to find they were less than the big chain store prices.
