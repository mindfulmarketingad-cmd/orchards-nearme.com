Reviews

★★★★★Clairise Caspera year ago

Huge cherry trees. Light and dark cherries. Both so juicy and delicious! We were kind of confused at first. When you pull in, you park literally 10 feet from the cheery trees. Ladders are available. Bring your own bag. Price per pound. Pay up around the front of the house.

★★★★★Aub Lawa year ago

We had the most fun with our adult daughter picking cherries at this beautiful orchard. Several varieties and they were delicious and at a fraction of the price cherries are going for at places like Costco. We saw several young families and I couldn’t wait to tell my sisters about it so they could take their grandkids. Definitely a unique experience that gets you out of the house and grounded with nature.

★★★★★Raji Thangaraj6 years ago

Loved the farm. Huge cherry farm and great variety. The cherries are the best and loved the taste. The farm is well maintained and the owners are very friendly. Will visit again next year. 🙂

★★★★★Ashley Regel6 years ago

Super easy to pick, friendly people. Ladders are available, bring your own buckets. They accept cash,check or venmo. We took (3) 4 year olds and a 2 year old. Lot of branches low enough for them to pick from. Port-a-potty was available, not sure if it usually is as it was our first trip.
