★★★★☆Sreekanth Kancharla7 months ago

Nice Orchard for Apple, Pumpkin picking. They have vegetables also for picking. Variety of vegetables such as Tomatoes, Zucchini, Peppers, Egg plant. Flowers etc. Each apple variety is clearly labeled and easy to pick. Wagons are available at the entrance. If we can't hold the basket once they get filled with apples, it is good to take the wagon and keep the baskets. No fee for entry. Buckets can be taken at the entrance for free of close and fill them with either apples or veggies. Apples and veggies can't be together as they will be weighed for a different price. They have play area where all kind of ages can play small games. Very nice place for families or groups. Can spend an hour or 2 here.

★★★★★Felicia Otero9 months ago

Family friendly, with TONS of things for kids of all ages to do. They have a small playground, a corn dig play area (instead of sand) mini rope maze, 3 diggers, a bike course, ring toss, pumpkin tik tac toe, and a few more games to enjoy. We have come here twice now for the fall season and they have not disappointed us yet! I highly recommended trying their in house made fruit slushies! I got the peach one in their tumbler it was perfect, no sugar added makes it 10x better then any other slushy around. Their apple cider is probably the best apple cider around, we bought 2 gallons and turned them into our own slushies at home (Add some caramel vodka and holy smokes). They make apple cider donuts using their apple cider, on point. You can also make your own caramel apples.
