Farms in New Mexico: What You Need to Know

New Mexico is home to a range of spots for visiting u-pick farms, spread across the state's different growing regions. This page rounds up every farms location listed in New Mexico so you can compare distance, ratings, and reviews before deciding where to go.

You Pick Farms in the Mountain West

the Mountain West supports a genuine year-round pick-your-own calendar, starting with strawberries in June-adjacent spring weeks and continuing through summer berries, stone fruit, and a long fall run of apples, pumpkins, and other harvest crops. Farms growing in well-drained, high-elevation soil across the region often diversify across several of these crops, which is why the same farm can be worth visiting more than once a year.

Best Time for Farms in New Mexico

Pick-your-own season in New Mexico runs from spring strawberries through summer berries and stone fruit, into a fall peak of apples and pumpkins that usually draws the largest crowds.

Tips for Farms in New Mexico

Call ahead or check a location's website before visiting, since hours and availability for visiting u-pick farms can change with the weather and time of year. Weekday visits are usually less crowded than weekends.
