★★★★★Mahlon White5 years ago

Welcome to Des Moines's best kept secret!!! I stumbled across this gift shop almost by accident and I am glad I did. Who knew there was a lavender farm and gift shop so close to Des Moines! If you love lavender this place is for you. From the moment you open the front door you are welcomed by the warm smell of lavender, the scent is intoxicating. The owner of the farm is working behind the counter she is a very sweet woman and knowledgeable about all things lavender. I was very surprised by all of the different items they sell, soap, lotions, and candles to name a few. The best thing is you are able to walk through their lavender fields and pick your own or choose from the already harvested lavender in the gift shop. Such a wonderful place and with the trip!
