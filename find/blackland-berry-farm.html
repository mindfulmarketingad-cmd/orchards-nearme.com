★★★★★Jared Guynesa year ago

VERY SPECIAL AND UNKNOWN - Deep in Rockwall County Texas on a parcel of property known as “Blackland Berry Farm” is perhaps the most unique snowcone stand I’ve ever come across. It does not even have a name, other than a flag that declares SNOW CONES at the entrance to the small gravel lot. This is a place that makes 100% natural snow cones with no artificial ingredients or sugars. They are not bright or neon colors, as the stuff they use is all real. The flavors are light, nothing overwhelming or sticky. Even the whipped cream is somehow all natural! They are so dedicated to making cheap, natural, high quality snow cones they forgot to do any kind of marketing. They don’t have a website, they don’t even have hours stated online. What presence they have is all 100% on facebook, but I did ask the owner the hours to report back to you all.
