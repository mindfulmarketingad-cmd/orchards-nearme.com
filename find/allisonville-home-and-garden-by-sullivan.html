I’ve been shopping at this nursery regularly since spring and have probably spent several hundred dollars here over the past few months. Most of the employees have always been very kind and helpful, which is why today’s experience stood out so much to me. While waiting in line this morning, I was asked by the manager to move and stand separately to the side, even though there was no one else there and the customers behind me continued waiting in the original line without being asked to move. It immediately felt uncomfortable and unnecessary. Later, I found a beautiful snake plant that had clearly been sitting dry for quite some time, with visible dehydration wrinkles on the leaves. I politely asked whether there might be any discount due to the condition. The employee helping me was very kind and even offered to ask the manager. When the manager came over, he stared at me, repeatedly rubbed his hand roughly across the snake plant leaves, and asked, “Where is the damage?” I pointed out the visible dehydration wrinkling on the leaves. He then picked up the plant and said, “There’s no discount. You can not buy it,” before walking away. I completely understand stores having pricing policies, and I would have respected a simple “no.” What disappointed me was the unnecessarily rude and dismissive attitude throughout the interaction. I still purchased the plant because I genuinely loved it, but the experience left me feeling uncomfortable and unwelcome. ————————————————————- ******Update on 5/20/26****** Thank you to Jenny for reaching out and taking this seriously. I will continue supporting this nursery, it’s a special local garden shop for our community.
