Reviews

★★★★★Rachel Iverson10 months ago

I think this is the best greenhouse in the region. Gorgeous plant selection with some unique rare options. All the plants I’ve purchased here have been healthy. I love wandering around and seeing new plants. The little shop is cute too, some different fun options. Definitely grab your wallet and a card you’re going to need both! If you go in late June they have great deals on perennials. The succulent greenhouse is a thing of dreams, I could live in there. The little koi pond and the owner’s orchid collection is absolutely beautiful and envy inducing. If you love plants you absolutely have to stop here and probably stop here 2-3 more times again. I had. I had a great time putting together my succulent planters with the large selection they have here. Once had to ride out a suddenly thunderstorm in the shop area!
