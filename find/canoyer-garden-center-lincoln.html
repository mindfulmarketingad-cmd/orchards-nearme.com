Reviews

★★★★★Paul Whitea month ago

first time checking the place out. They had a sale on 4-in vegetable plants including tomatoes, cucumbers. Just about everything you would plant in your seasonal vegetable garden. They were $4.99 each plus a BOGO sale meaning you effectively get two plants for five bucks. Some of the tomato plants were already showing yellow flowers on them to give you a sense of how mature they were. Huge inventory. This place is way bigger than any other garden center in town. Lots of staff members asking if they can help you find anything. The prices here were way lower then anywhere else in town. The greenhouse open air concept and polished concrete floors felt very clean and upscale. the quality of the plants was top notch. If you're looking to buy some new plants and haven't been here before, definitely go here first, you're going to be blown away.
