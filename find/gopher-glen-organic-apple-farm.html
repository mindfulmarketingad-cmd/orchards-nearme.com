★★★★★Innovative Constructions3 years ago

This apple farm is worth driving to. This is a store full of products made mostly by them. Of course they have lots a different types of apples that you can buy in paper bags and enjoy. We got 4 different types of apples and they were all very fresh as though they were just picked. We also bought honey in a bottle also great fresh honey. Your kids will also enjoy seeing some chicken in a cage kinda fun for them. The drive up to the apple farm is also nice and we saw a fee deer on our drive up. Overall, a great place to visit if you’re driving by on a trip.
