★★★★★Miliria Crawford3 years ago

Took my girls after school today (10/19/22). When we got there wasn't sure if they were open cause the gate had a different schedule posted than on google. I called and they welcomed us right in. The lady(didn't get a name) which I assume was an owner was really nice. I paid $20 for myself and 3 kids as entry fee. She offered us a tractor ride, which we accepted and even offered to blow the bouncy houses back up, but I declined due to the wind. She did warn us that the pumpkin patch had pretty much been picked but let us pick from the display pumpkins. We picked 6 of the smaller sizes and after weight we paid about $15. I felt a little bad cause we showed up at about 4pm and we were going to leave but she encouraged me to let the girls stay and play for a while in the sand box and ride the little trikes (which helped tremendously cause it's not even 7:30p and they are showered, fed and in the bed sleep). I came last year on a weekend and had a great time! I will return every year, as it's in town and love supporting the local businesses!!! I've seen the negative reviews, but the fun is what you make it! I got to relax a little and allow my kids to have some after school fun outdoors. Thank you to the owner for staying when I'm sure you had a long day! You are very appreciated!
