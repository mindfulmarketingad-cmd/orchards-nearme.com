I’ve been a customer since this shop was located in Chelsea, and my family has been coming here for over 15 years because of my rooftop garden. During a recent move, many of my plants struggled after going from a west-facing setup to a full-sun south-facing apartment, so I stopped by around 6:40 PM to ask for advice on plants better suited to the new conditions. Unfortunately, I was told, “We are not a nursery.” While checking out with a dahlia, I also asked about some indoor plants I had purchased there in the fall that had since died from too much sun, both indoors and outdoors. The cashier told me they were closed and could not answer questions, even though it was still before 7 PM. When I mentioned the posted closing time, I was told, “We are closed now.” I understand staff need time to close up, but if customer assistance ends 20–30 minutes before closing, the posted hours should reflect that. It was disappointing after being a loyal customer for so many years.
