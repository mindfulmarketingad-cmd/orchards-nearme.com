Reviews

★★★★★Sean Clayton10 months ago

What a hidden gem! Hartland Orchard offers an amazing variety of fresh, seasonal fruits — from pumpkins and strawberries to blueberries, apples, and peaches. Everything is incredibly fresh and easy to pick, making the experience fun and relaxing for all ages. If you’re not in the mood to pick, no worries — they offer pre-packaged bags for $20 (one size only), and you simply pay when you're done. Just a heads-up: they only accept cash or check, so come prepared! Beyond the fruit, their vendor items are a real treat — the honey, jams, preserves, pies, and donuts are all top-notch. I was just passing through, but after this visit, I’ll definitely be coming back! A perfect stop for anyone who loves local, quality produce and homemade goodies.

★★★★★Khanh Armstronga year ago

We came here with our kids on a misty morning and had an amazing time! The staff were so nice and the apples were delicious. There were so many kinds to pick! We were a little thrown by how far into the orchard we could drive, but it was super convenient to get to the different types of apples. Our minivan was parked on the grass right next to the apple trees we picked from. That saved a lot of time and energy lugging heavy bags of apple back to our car with the toddlers! Every tree was laden with apples, and they provided pole grabbers so we can reach for the big ones! Afterwards we stopped by their shop, and got the kids on a little tractor ride through the fields that was pretty sweet. Very scenic and peaceful. Highly recommend!
