★★★★★victoria annea month ago

I originally chose this nursery because of a review I read- but my experience ended up being the complete opposite. From the moment I walked in, I was helped right away. Not just pointed in a direction, but actually walked around (by Dad) and guided through the process of choosing plants. I know absolutely nothing about gardening, and they still took the time to explain how everything works- what to plant, how it will grow, and how to take care of it in my specific setup. The pricing was very reasonable, the service was excellent, and even the other customers were friendly and helpful. It honestly made the whole experience enjoyable instead of overwhelming, and I actually left feeling confident about what I was doing. I’ll definitely be coming back and would recommend this place to anyone- especially beginners like me who need a little extra guidance.
