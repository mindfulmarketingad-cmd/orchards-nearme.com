★★★★☆Ed C4 years ago

The garden center is really big. I enjoyed it has different sections for everything. The garden decor is probably my favorite. They have so much variety and so much to see. I can spend a good amount of time here and not see everything. The staff was very friendly and I had multiple team members ask if I needed any help. The facility was well organized and everything seemed in a very organized manner. They had everything from annuals to perennials to surprising a rare collection that was available to customers. I have never seen that before from any garden center so I thought that was pretty unique of them. My reason for four stars and not five stars is because they are quite pricey when you compare them to others. Especially the garden Decour. Be prepared to pay premium pricing here. Overall I’ll continue to shop here and I would definitely recommend it to my friends and family.
