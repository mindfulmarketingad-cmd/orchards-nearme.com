Reviews

★★★★★Avinash Patil2 years ago

This is a fantastic family-owned farm that offers farm-fresh fruits and vegetables, along with an impressive on-site bakery. Their apples are particularly outstanding. It's an excellent place to visit during apple and pumpkin season. You can enjoy not only fresh apples but also a variety of delicious baked goods, including caramel apple pie, donuts, and fried pies, all of which are highly recommended. The staff is friendly, polite, and informative, making your visit enjoyable. This farm is a favorite spot for fruit picking, fall activities, or simply indulging in apple donuts. Additionally, they welcome dogs, allowing them to join in on the hayrides and apple picking fun.
