Reviews

★★★★★Brian Conklin8 months ago

We stayed overnight at Miller Orchards while visiting Leavenworth and loved it! It’s only about 10 miles east of town, right next to the fruit stand. The stand offers fresh produce, homemade jams (including sugar-free), and works mostly on the honor system with easy payment by cash, Venmo, or PayPal. There’s some highway noise from US-97, but the spot is clean, easy to access, and full of local charm. We’ll definitely stay again!

★★★★★Seth wendzel8 years ago

Free samples of anything at the fruit stand and great seasonal selections of beans vegetables fruits gourds and snacks for the road. Speaking with the Miller family they said it's about 25 acres with a variety of apples pears cherries peaches nectarines and the cutest golden retrievers.

★★★★★Aine McDonalda year ago

I live in Seattle and every year drive to this little roadside stand to get boxes of their beautiful peaches pears or apples. This is a real family farm, and I'm happy to drive the 2 hours there to support a business where my money goes directly to the farmer! The owners are out working on the stand and can tell you about the different varieties of produce to help you find just what you need. In it autumn, it is beautiful with the prettiest display of every kind of pumpkin you can imagine! Gone are the days of just orange pumpkins. The variety is astonishing. Dont miss the gem!
