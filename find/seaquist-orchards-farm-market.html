★★★★★Puppers Policettia year ago

This is a wonderful market surrounded by beautiful orchards of apple and cherry trees. It is a great place to stop by to grab some fresh apples and cherries, jams, juices, and preserves. They also have pie fillings of different fruits, berries, and berry combinations. Among their products are a variety of coffee, tea, and jerky. If one were so inclined, stop by their baked goods section and peruse their delicious freshly made pies, pastries, and donuts from their bakery. Among their other items are candles and quaint decorations for the inside of one's humble abode. They have a refrigerated section with bratwurst, bacon, and sausages as well as fresh cherry and apple juice, some of which are frozen. There are also different kinds of frozen pies that one can purchase and store in the freezer for later use. Their staff is mostly friendly and accommodating and willing to lend a helping hand if you're in need of some assistance. They do have public restrooms that are handicapped accessible. Parking is ample and available in their lot. There is outdoor seating right outside of their store front and off to the right are picnic tables set next to a playground for the little ones to explore. There is also a food truck set next to the picnic tables. So come one and all and check out their fresh, high-quality selections and really take your time and have a look-see because I guarantee you that there is so much to explore and discover here that you will not leave empty-handed. Cheers!

★★★★★Deborah Mallek3 years ago

Seaquist Orchards is one of the places we make a point to stop while we are in Door Co. Make sure that you have a lot of time when you stop to be able to spend time exploring, seeing all Seaquist orchards has to offer. We were here in the fall, and the outside was decorated in a way that welcomed the season. There were lots of pumpkins, gourds and everything fall. As soon as you walk in the door, you get the feeling of homemade, authentic goodness. Everything cherry you can imagine with lots of apple and other local produce preserves. There’s also fresh popped popcorn for the aroma! Also, we had a cherry almond smoothie that was so filling and tasty. Don’t miss out on the variety of homemade fudge, and so many homemade bakery items, including; turnovers, pies, cookies, breads, etc. There’s a lot of variety of canned goods, jellies, jams, marmalade‘s, sauces, and dips, salad dressings, soups, and sausages. If you’re needing a unique gift or souvenirs, you’re in luck, there are all kinds of options for all kinds of age groups. Another bonus, depending on staffing, is they offer samples of some of the items. When we were there, they had several kinds of apples because it was fall harvest time, so we got to sample and then choose our favorite to purchase. There is also a freezer department that stores, frozen cherries, either sweetened, or unsweetened, frozen cherry juice, apples, pies, and all kinds of other things. Seaquist is smart with their checkout line because you’ll find all kinds of other goodies to purchase. This place is a must stop when you’re in Door County.
