Reviews

★★★★★Mark Savoca9 months ago

Mood Farm is a great place to pick your own produce or simply stop in for fresh-picked fruits and vegetables. We visited during the Apple Festival, which included a hayride pulled by an antique tractor, three pounds of pick-your-own apples, a cider donut, and a small bottle of apple cider. It was a lot of fun for both kids and adults. Parking was plentiful and convenient on the Bridgeton Pike (Route 77) side.

★★★★★Brianna Ballinger9 months ago

We love picking our own at Mood's. Raspberries to peaches, blueberries to apples, grapes to pumpkins and everything in between all are great choices for PYO. Prices are good and produce is fresh. My kids and husband love the fresh cider donuts, too. Lots of farm fresh options during the summer and early autumn months.

★★★★★Natasha Vitale2 years ago

Love it here! They have excellent apple cider donuts which can be ordered hot & fresh if they’re actively making them. We also enjoy all their slushies and apple cider. Convenient spot to grab fresh produce in the summer too. We also go every year for their fall activities. It is NOT an overcrowded, overproduced headache like other South Jersey spots. This is the real deal - it’s a farm first and the simplicity of everything is what makes it so special and actually enjoyable. The kids love it too and the corn maze is our favorite!
