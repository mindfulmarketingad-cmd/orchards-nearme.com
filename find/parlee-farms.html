★★★★☆Laheri k9 months ago

Lovely Farm Experience! 🌸🍎 We visited this farm last weekend for apple picking, and it was such a fun family outing! My kids especially enjoyed the hayride that took us out to the orchard to pick apples. The apple-picking bags cost $41 for 6 people or $30 for 4 people—definitely worth it for the experience. The farm is beautiful, with so much to enjoy all around. They sell non-GMO corn, have a food truck, and you can treat yourself to ice creams or their delicious apple crisp bowl with apple cider donuts right by the farm stand. Honestly, the apple crisp bowl was so good I wanted to go back for another one! 🍏✨ The kids loved visiting the farm animals—goats, chickens, roosters, and sheep. You can even feed them by putting 25¢ into a machine for a small portion of feed, which makes it extra fun for little ones. I can’t wait to return for pumpkin season—it’s the perfect place to enjoy pumpkins, fall flowers, and capture beautiful photos with the colorful, flowery background. 🎃🌼 Highly recommend for families looking for a fun and relaxing day outdoors!
