★★★★★Alec Panagakis9 months ago

Best apple orchard ever!!! I would drive two hours to come here again! Perfect date night! I came on a Thursday and they had delicious wood-fired pizza and trivia! They also had corn hole, tic tac toe, connect 4, pumpkins, corn pit, goats, chickens, and slides! So many fun activities to keep you entertained for hours! They had apple trees and a little pumpkin patch which was perfect for pictures! You smell a campfire the second you get out of your car! Everything was reasonably priced, we were in and out for about $20 which is a lot less than expected. They have a farmers market (with their OWN original products). If I could give this place 6 stars I would. I will be sending every the rest of my family their way. Great for couples. Great for little kids.

★★★★★Samantha Harrell8 months ago

Oh my goodness! What a fun little place! We visited a few years ago and came back this year during fall break. I was so excited to see the parking lot absolutely full. We got some donuts and a cinnamon roll and headed outside. The backyard is full of fun activities for kids, and there is plenty of room for adults to sit and chat. The see-through Beehives on the bee walk are SUCH a fun addition. I can’t wait to come back for one of the evening events and try out their cider and mead! This place is Just what Wilson County needs. Truly a hidden gem.

★★★★★Donald Shepard8 months ago

We visited the orchard today with our grandkids and everyone loved it. We took them to let them run and play a little while. I think we visited just about everything on the farm and the girls loved it. A small fee of 5 dollars (at our time of visit) per person, gave us access to explore the animals (feeding them a little snack) for a few bucks, ride a couple of good slides, paint on an old truck, play in a corn crib, make some bubbles, visit a bee hive and watch the bees actually working flower beds close by. If you are looking for something to do with your kids on a Saturday this fall, I highly recommend taking a little time to let them enjoy playing at the orchard. On your way out, make sure you stop and get a few apples, a fried pie, a jug of cider or two lol, and the apple cider slushies were the bomb. We will definitely be back. Excellent place and very friendly staff.
