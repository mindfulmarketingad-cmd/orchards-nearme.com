★★★★★Jonathan Rodriguez7 years ago

We went to the pumpkin patch 🎃 and had the best time! I have two young boys and they loved walking through the thousands of pumpkins there. We spent over an hour there just looking at everything. They put straw down over the dirty to walk on which is a brilliant way of keeping things a little cleaner. They had every single variety of pumpkin you can think of. They had a variety I hadn’t seen that were a gray green that were eerie and unique. If you want some cute pictures of your kids in a pumpkin patch overflowing with beautiful pumpkins if every shape and size check them out. They also have events and shows going on every single night that are super entertaining.
