Awesome farm. Came from cincy and loved the fresh air. I’m an adult but I rode all the little rides and slides. And considering today’s market the food prices were very reasonable especially when you factor in that it’s fresh and homemade!

★★★★★Lindsey Lewisa year ago

We had so much fun! So many activities, for a $20 entry fee, it is very much worth it! There are lots of beautiful flowers and animals in addition to fun rides and “haunted houses.” The animatronics and everything look handmade which is charming if a bit cheesy. I was especially grateful to everyone who was so kind and welcoming to my dog, he loved the hayride and was in love with the sheep and goats who seem to have loved him too! They followed him all the way down the fence line when we left! He is old, blind, and deaf but he had the time of his life being a farm dog! (I did put my dog in a diaper for the visit because I did not want him peeing on the pumpkins or flowers but this is not required!). The animals there were well cared for, from what I saw, but a bunny did escape as we were leaving. The food is a bit overpriced so I would just eat somewhere else and bring a water bottle! Play equipment was mostly new and sturdy, there was some older equipment that could do with an update but there was so much to do, you could make a whole day of it. The baby’s favorite part was the animals and the pirate show, but he was also a big fan of the ball corral and sandboxes. My favorite part was the animal farm and the people rollers(?)
