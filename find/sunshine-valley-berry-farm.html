★★★★★Mia M3 years ago

Visited in early July while in the area and bought jam, honey, raspberries, and syrup. While the berry picking wasn’t open at the time (the berries weren’t ready yet) we were offered freshly picked raspberries and samples of different syrups, jams and honey. The jam and syrup were delicious. I wasn’t able to actually eat the raspberries we bought though, because one member of my family decided to eat all of them and said that they were very good. The farm is also beautiful and the couple managing it were kind and helpful. Would definitely visit again!
