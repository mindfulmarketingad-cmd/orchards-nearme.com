★★★★★Kelli Thuya2 months ago

Probably my favorite nursery in the entire treasure valley! Huge selection of indoor plants, trees, veggie/fruit plants, shrubs, flowers and everything in between. I get about 90% of my veggie starts here every year and have always experienced next level quality. They also have unique varieties that I’ve never seen anywhere else. Entrance can be slightly confusing your first time visiting. Parking lot is dirt and does have a couple larger potholes that might be a concern for those with mobility requirements.
