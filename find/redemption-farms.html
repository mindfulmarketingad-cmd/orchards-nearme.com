Reviews

★★★★★Visitor 247 - Georgi7 months ago

Our family has loved visiting over the past couple of years. Last year we picked strawberries in their beautiful patch, and this year we came back for apples, pumpkins, squash, and sweet potatoes. We unanimously agreed they were the best sweet potatoes we’ve ever tasted. There’s just nothing like the joy of harvesting your own produce without all the planting and tending… we get to do the fun part! Their apple cider is incredible, and the prices for both produce and U-Pick are always fair. This is exactly the kind of family-owned, local business we love to support. I appreciate that they give generously to those in need and openly share their Christian faith, often connecting Bible verses to their farming and life experiences. It’s a meaningful way to feel closer to nature, and the food we bring home to our table. Highly recommend visiting—Redemption Farms is a blessing to our community!
