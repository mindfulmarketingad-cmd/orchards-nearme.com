★★★★★Ade C2 years ago

Unforgettable Farm Camping Experience I recently took my entire class of 24 to the farm for an overnight camping adventure, and it was truly exceptional. This field trip was by far the best I have ever experienced. The organization of stations was outstanding, and the staff's knowledge shared with the students was incredibly enriching. The students had a hands-on experience at each station, which made the visit truly memorable. Here are some highlights of our visit: Bees and Hive Functionality: Students learned about bees, their hive structure, and their role on the farm. Livestock Study: They interacted with sheep, goats, chickens, and rabbits, understanding how these animals contribute to the farm's success. Wood Carving and Wood Shop: Students learned to carve wood into bowls and each made their own to keep. Fungi Exploration: The students went into the woods to learn about fungi and its benefits to the farm. Greenhouse Visit: They explored the greenhouse and land where crops are grown each spring. Pizza Making: The students enjoyed making their own pizza using fresh dough and veggies - a real hit among them! Blacksmith Shop: They even visited the blacksmith shop to see the craft up close. Hiking Adventure: An evening hike left the students in awe as the sun set, creating a magical experience. Not to forget the amazing staff - Tom, Susie, and the team. Their wealth of knowledge and warm hospitality made the trip even more special. The experience was beyond measure, and their genuine and down-to-earth nature left a lasting impression on all of us. I highly recommend taking part in their summer camps to experience the magic of Calypso for yourself. You wouldn't want to miss such a wonderful opportunity! 10 out of 10 would HIGHLY recommend!!
