★★★★☆Kelli Pool (Kelli Pool Real Estate)6 months ago

Heartland Nursery and Garden Center Lee's Summit is a convenient and well organized destination for gardeners looking for quality and unique plant options. Located right at 470 higway & View High Drive, it is easy to access with great parking, excellent availability of carts, and a smooth checkout process. The variety here is impressive, especially when it comes to hard to find plants and unique filler options that are not always available at other garden centers. The plants are consistently healthy and well cared for, which makes them ideal for gift giving or easy, successful plantings at home. The nursery is consistently well stocked and clearly laid out, making it easy to browse even during busy times. Staff members are plentiful, knowledgeable, and helpful, which adds to the overall positive experience. Prices do tend to run higher, often 25 to 40 percent more than other garden centers, and while the reward program is a nice perk, it does not always fully offset the higher cost. That said, the quality of the plants and the availability of unique selections often make it worth the visit. Despite the crowds, shopping here is always enjoyable, and it remains a go to spot for dependable quality and distinctive plants.
