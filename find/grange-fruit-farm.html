Reviews

★★★★★DaVideo & Photography11 months ago

Nice, locally-owned and home-grown, fruits and vegetables shop, with a wide assortment of fresh-picked food items available, as well as some canned or jar items. You can also pick your own fruits from the orchards in back when the the seasons are right. Prices are reasonable and the quality is very good. The entrance is a one-way gravel path from 13-Mile road, and the exit will take you to a side street so you don't have to pull out into fast traffic. Plenty of parking on the gravel lot. The is a designated handicapped parking area, and entrance into the store is easily accessible.

Came here looking for blueberries to make freezer jam and also to freeze for smoothies later. They had lots of 10 pound boxes, unfortunately not the variety we were looking for. The berries were large and juicy, but not as sweet as we like. Lots of other produce available. Good crowd here picking fruit.
