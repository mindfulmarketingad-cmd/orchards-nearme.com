★★★★★Joe Haupta year ago

It's wonderful to have Baughers nearby to where we live. They are open every day except Wednesday. During the season, the fruits and vegetables available depend on what is ripening locally. Today we picked up concord grapes, plums, Asian pears, corn, potatoes, onions, figs and special small pears. The white and yellow peaches also looked great; however, they will have to wait until our next visit. We won't be visiting any supermarket produce section until Baugher's closes for the season around Halloween.
