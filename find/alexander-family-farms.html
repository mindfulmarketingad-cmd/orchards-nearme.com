★★★☆☆creative_ thimble2 months ago

Lots of signs make it easy to get there. Process was straightforward- stop and prepay for a bucket, head out to the fields. Seasoned pickets will know to walk to the far end of the rows and back part of the field, which proved true for me on a Friday afternoon. Plenty of berries, but I was disappointed by two major things. 1) Many berries had split from the bottom up, something I’d never seen before. This left the inside of the berries open to bugs and other stuff. 2) Most disappointing, the berries were tasteless. I’d tasted a couple in the field and figured my tastebuds were off due to allergy congestion. Ate a few on the way home, then took some to work where my coworkers wouldn’t even eat them after a few took a bite. If I lived closer I’d return the berries and ask for a refund. Hopefully the berries will be better with a couple more weeks of sunshine since this looks like a great place!!!!
