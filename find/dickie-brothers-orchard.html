★★★★★Meg Ca year ago

Tl;ldr: if you're looking for a laid-back and authentic feeling pick-your-own apple adventure, this is the place. I'd rate it 100/10 if I could! I can't believe we hadn't found this place until now! A few minutes more out of the way but absolutely worth it. This isn't like other orchards where you're climbing over crowds of people trying to reach the one apple left on the tree. It was a really relaxing and enjoyable experience! There might not be food trucks or music, but there's apple cider slushies (delicious btw!), apple fritters, and cider donuts instead. Plus really affordable pumpkins, jams, and pre-picked apples of all kinds as well! The drive out is beautiful, the people are very nice, and there's even a sweet dog named Benson waiting for a pat when you pull up to the pick-your-own apple field. Our Pink Lady apples were perfectly ripe and delicious. I can't wait to come back next year!
