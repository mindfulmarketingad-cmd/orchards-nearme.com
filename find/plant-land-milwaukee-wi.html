Reviews

★★★★★Caitlin Joestgena month ago

Plant Land is truly a local community treasure. From the moment we arrived, we were welcomed with such genuine warmth and hospitality. Within seconds of getting out of the car, we were greeted by one of their adorable four-legged "employees” with happy tail wags and friendly sniffs, which immediately put huge smiles on my children’s faces. Their nursery stock is incredibly impressive and very well cared for. I could easily spend hours wandering through the aisles admiring the beautiful selection of plants, vegetables, perennials, and annuals — they truly have something for everyone. What stood out most was the kindness of the owner. At one point, my toddler pinched a flower off a plant, and instead of being upset, the owner kindly gave us a small starter pot and some soil free of charge so she could continue to “grow” her flower at home. It was such a thoughtful gesture that meant a lot to us. My son was also determined to find Nicki the Kitty, and the owner happily helped us track her down so we could give her some well-deserved pets and snuggles. Plant Land is a rare gem of a business — welcoming, family-friendly, and full of heart. We left with beautiful plants and even better memories. You will not regret visiting!
