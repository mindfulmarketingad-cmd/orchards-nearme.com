★☆☆☆☆Sandy T7 months ago

I know how much a one‑star review can hurt a small business, so it pains me to write this. Still, I feel I was taken advantage of—though I admit part of it may be due to my own lack of knowledge about pricing. I’m not a fan of grass, but I wanted something to help keep weeds down in my backyard. I decided to try Mondo grass, thinking it might be a good option. At Glenn’s, I paid $50 for a tray. Later, I discovered the exact same tray at Home Depot for $25. To make matters worse, Glenn’s did not provide me with a receipt, which makes it difficult to properly account for the purchase. I understand that small businesses don’t have the same buying power as big box stores, and I want to support local shops. But a 200% markup feels excessive, especially in today’s economic climate. That said, I take responsibility for not researching prices beforehand. This was a hard lesson for me, and I share it here so others can make a more informed decision.
