★★★★★Beth L2 years ago

I love everything about this farm! They have the freshest prepared goods (seasonal applesauce, salsa, jam) and vegetables! I make sure I always stop at their pop ups and visit their farm store when I can! I love that it’s family run and family focused. They also write beautiful books for kids and adults! It’s a must visit!

★★★★★Patricia Wheeler2 years ago

Honestly, the best berries and cukes we’ve ever had. The black raspberries were just out of this world, and my partner can’t stop talking about the cucumbers! Also incredible maple syrup. Added to our list of farms to visit on our annual family trip to the Adirondacks (worth the drive to VT!)
