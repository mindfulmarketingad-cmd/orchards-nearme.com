Amazing experience overall. Yes the mini golf is a little rundown, yet the cornfield was short but fun. The animals were a blast to visit. If you have young kids there is definitely plenty to keep then entertained in the form of bounce houses and playground fun. They carbonated apple cider is delicious. The frozen apple cider was extremely good! Plus they have many other baked goodies and apple goodies to buy!

★★★★★abiracmd4 years ago

I'm from out of town and I'm blown away on how amazing this disc golf course was. The main building has all sorts of items for purchase all the way from maple syrup to disc golf discs. The staff was super nice as well. Large parking lot next to building. $7 bucks to play disc golf. The course was way beyond my expectations. Good signage. I think a total of 23 total holes to play. Really enjoyed the beauty of the course, variety of holes, upkeep, and overall difficulty. Tee pads at most holes. Nice baskets. There are several water hazards (including a cranberry field for staff only)so be careful not to lose a disc. Thank you to whomever designed and installed this course.

★★★★☆danielleforta year ago

We drove 1.5 hours one way to check out this Orchard. I was surprised how big it was and all the variety of apples they had to pick along with blueberries and grapes. We missed out on the grapes but managed to get some blueberries being the end of the season for them. We didn’t have small children to enjoy the activities but there was a lot for small kids to do. We watched one of the free juggling shows which was entertaining and went to the cider house for some sampling. We should have bought donuts when we first got there because by afternoon, the store was absolute chaos. So many people standing in line made it impossible to actually walk around to see what else was being sold. Only made two flavored donuts at that time. The girl was walking the line taking donut orders in advance to speed up the process but we still missed out on the pumpkin donuts we ordered as they stopped making them. I would suggest having a cashier outside for those who want to buy pumpkins and other outdoor things so we don’t have to stand in line in the store for 1/2 hour holding plants and pumpkins. I would have bought outdoor stuff but didn’t want to go back through the line carrying plants and pumpkins. We did buy some pumpkins at the Apple orchard as they had wagons for use which was nice.
