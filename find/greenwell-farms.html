★★★★★andre mirakyan6 months ago

We liked and bought coffee from Greenwell farms since we first visited in 2010, so we came again when revisiting the Big Island 15 years later; coffee is still great and the free tour actually got much better. They now grow a lot more different things on the property and make it almost a botanical garden tour experience, so you get to learn about many things ranging from tulip tree to black pepper to cacao, aside from coffee itself. Nate was a really knowledgeable guide who kept everyone in the group engaged and having fun- from seasoned plant geeks to reluctant teenagers dragged in by parents- and was especially awesome with little kids. My husband hires private tour guides often on his birding trips, but even he was impressed by his knowledge and positive energy. I also loved the unique vibrant artwork at the gift shop, done by a local artist. Thank you Greenwell farms for going the extra mile for your fans!
