★★★★★Holly P2 years ago

INCREDIBLE SELECTION & QUALITY! Low prices! Friendly staff that’s family owned! Supporting the local community! Fresh produce, fruits, vegetables, seasonal fall harvest items like corn, mums, and pumpkins in the fall, strawberries, blueberries, blackberries in the summer! Lots of squash, lettuce, potatoes, tomatoes, fresh homemade apple cider, homemade jams, tomato sauce, pickled beets, honey & more! Homemade ice cream and treats! Super friendly and helpful owners! They are also down at the Chestertown Farmers Market on Saturdays in town 8-Noon! They also have a fun corn maze for kids! In the summer you can pick your own berries! Fun for the whole family! Great for kids!

★★★★★kelly vaughn4 years ago

So thankful we found this Gem!! What a Beautiful farm. The fall decorations were arranged perfectly. We were able to get so many cute family pictures. There was no entrance free and all the activities were free. The corn maze was awesome!! Thick stalks making it fun to adventure through. The pumpkin prices were very cheap. We got 6 huge pumpkins for $10 a piece. The pumpkins were all in excellent shape for easy carving. Almost all had a nice stem. Their homemade signature ice cream tasted like Grandma's and again very cheap!! My family and I had such a nice day visiting and we will absolutely be back. I highly recommend.
