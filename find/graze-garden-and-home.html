Great selection that has increased slightly in variety with new ownership. The store has been refreshed but some of the same staff from Twigs are still around which is nice to have familiar faces. Prices are definitely on the top end but the quality also reflects this. If you want cheaper you can drive much further, but the convenience is what brings me back each time.
