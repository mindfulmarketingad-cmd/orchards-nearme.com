★★☆☆☆Ily Austin8 months ago

My boyfriend and I arrived and accidentally ended up attending a fall festival of sorts. We were told it was ten dollars per person to get in, and we'd get included events like feeding animals, making flower bouquets, and things like that. None of these things ended up being included, however, and I couldn't find where the animal feeding was- I guess our "tour guide" we were told would come didn't know he was supposed to help? Overall, it honestly felt like we were intruding the entire time, seemed more like a neighborhood event than something "outsiders" we welcome to, and reeked of weed throughout too. Two dogs were walking around that seemed severely neglected; one had matted fur, and the other was oddly skinny. I will say we were disappointed by not being able to pick apples or even see the trees, but the ones from the farmers' market were fairly good, including their apple bread.
