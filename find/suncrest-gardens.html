★★★★★A S7 months ago

This place is worth a visit just to window shop. They have really nice pots - smaller sizes inside, with a massive selection of larger ones outside in any color you could imagine. The greenhouse has a huge variety of plants, and some that aren't for sale but are a marvel to see. In the nicer months they have a great selection of trees and shrubs. The inner gift shop area has a lot of nice knicknacks all year or ornaments around Christmas time. The employees are also very nice.

They have plants and colors/ variations that I've never seen before, definitely with the trip just to look around. The staff and customer service were beyond helpful!
