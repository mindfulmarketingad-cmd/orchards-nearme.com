Reviews

★★★★★Frederick The Planespotter3 months ago

I visited the Applewood Orchard twice last fall and had a great time both times. I really like the chill atmosphere with several lanes for each type of apple, never felt crowded or busy. They have a great selection of different types of apples and pastries available. Both times I went, I got suckered into purchasing some baked goods by the check out line and I’m glad I did since they’re so good. There is enough parking to get by, but some parked on the side of the dirt road which you must take to get to the orchard. The folks working there are also very kind and helpful and there is some other cool stuff to do such as a hay ride or corn maze. Overall, Applewood Orchard is a very nice apple orchard and worth the ride from the Twin Cities area.
