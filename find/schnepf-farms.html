★★★★★Addie Rausch3 months ago

Not-so-hidden gem in Queen Creek! 🌟 This is a fantastic weekend spot to enjoy breakfast on the patio while the kids explore the new ropes course, feed the goats, and admire the peacocks and peahens. There are also cows, chickens, and bouncy pillows in the shaded areas all free to enjoy (breakfast is extra, of course!). The food is amazing biscuits and gravy, bacon and eggs, and homemade cinnamon rolls it feels like visiting a grandma who lives on a farm. Every visit is truly magical. The owners pour their hearts into creating a peaceful sanctuary for busy city dwellers. Keep an eye out for concerts, special events especially at Christmas and outdoor markets when planning your trip here. Highly recommend!
