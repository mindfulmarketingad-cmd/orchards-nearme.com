★★★★★Ashby12 months ago

What sets Mackintosh apart is the full experience they offer. The orchard’s dwarf trees make fruit picking super accessible, especially for kids, and there are handy picking tools and wagons to make hauling produce easy and fun. After you’re finished picking, the friendly staff help weigh and bag your fruit, ensuring an stress-free experience. Plus, the farm is pet-friendly and even has resident dogs and cats roaming the property, which gives it such a wholesome touch. The farm market and café elevate the visit. We indulged in a scrumptious chicken pot pie and an apple panini, both highlight-worthy, along with crispy apple cider donuts that were simply divine. The café also offers rotating menu specials, local cheeses, farm-fresh eggs, jugs of cider, pastries, and seasonal treats, you truly feel immersed in farm-to-table goodness. In short: Mackintosh Fruit Farm delivers seasonal freshness, engaging experiences for all ages (and pets), and a superb farm-market café. It’s a perfect destination for a full day of relaxing, delicious family fun. A heartfelt 5-star recommendation! 🍎
