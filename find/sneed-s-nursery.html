★★★★★Rich Goglia2 months ago

Since moving to Richmond in 2022, Sneed's has been my go-to for everything that makes my yard come alive — plants, shrubs, flowers, and so much more. I love supporting local businesses, and after passing Sneed's so many times, I finally decided to stop in. I could not have been happier. The selection is incredible — so many colors, so many options — and I genuinely love walking around to see what's new each visit. But what makes Sneed's truly special is the staff. Over the years I've gotten to know many of their "green thumb" experts, and I consider them friends as much as yard gurus. Team members like Amber, Ashley, and Melissa have been invaluable in guiding me toward beautiful and often unique plants, shrubs, and trees that have made my landscape vibrant and colorful. Their knowledge is second to none, and whether you have a question or just need a little direction, they are always there with an answer and a great suggestion. Oh, and I absolutely love the "Charlie Brown" section!! If you are looking for a wonderful local nursery with an amazing selection and a staff that truly cares, Sneed's is your place. Fair warning though — every time you go, you will find something else to fall in love with, and the only challenge becomes figuring out where to fit it all in your yard!

★★★★★Ash Barnesa year ago

This is one of the most nicely curated nurseries I've ever visited. The prices might seem a little higher than usual, but the plants are large, healthy, and expertly tended. Each one is a specimen plant: what you're getting is something that will immediately thrive in your garden, not a seedling that's going to need constant looking after to get a good start. I found everything here. Natives! Diverse perennials! Gorgeous and affordable annuals. Various trees, bushes, and shrubs that are hard to find elsewhere: special redbud cultivars, for instance, and ninebark cultivars I haven't seen anywhere except here. The staff were super attentive without being stifling. They were able to answer questions about ultra specific plants, and didn't hesitate to tell me if they didn't have something in stock, not to mention where else I could get it if they couldn't order it. I call that service. This is a great place. I will look forward to future visits, if only to admire the vast array of gorgeous foliage.
