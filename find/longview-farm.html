I’ve been going to this farm as far back as I can remember. The produce is phenomenal, prices are great. It’s a beautiful place to get your veggies, local honey, and local maple syrup. ★★★★★Madelyn Albright3 years ago

Longview Farm has the best farm stand in the region. Great variety of fresh vegetables and produce all at a reasonable prices. They have pick your own flowers ($10/lb) which is just an absolute delight! On summer weekends there is usually a food truck parked out front with amazing food and picnic tables. We stop here every time when returning from climbing in Rumney. Absolutely recommend!
