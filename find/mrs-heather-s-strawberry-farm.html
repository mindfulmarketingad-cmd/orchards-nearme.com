My experience at Mrs. Heather's Pumpkin & Strawberry Patch was thoroughly underwhelming. A thorough search of the strawberry fields yielded nothing but rotten and bug-eaten berries. The play equipment and farm facilities appear to be in disrepair, indicating a lack of maintenance and updates. The strawberry irrigation system is outdated, and some rows lack connection to the system altogether. The farm's overall appearance suggests neglect. To maintain a successful business, regular maintenance and upgrades are crucial. When I shared our disappointing experience with the owner, highlighting the subpar quality of the berries, she seemed dismissive, inquiring about the children's experience on the playground. I clarified that we had only just arrived and spent our time searching for suitable berries, which proved unsuccessful. Despite our disappointment and the poor condition of the berries, the owner declined to provide a refund, even with two upset children and a paltry harvest of five mediocre berries. The visit was an unfortunate waste of time and money. I would recommend exploring alternative options, such as Liuzza Land or Landry Poche Farm in Holden. In retrospect, purchasing a $25 flat of berries from a roadside stand in Ponchatoula would have been a more satisfactory choice for my family.
