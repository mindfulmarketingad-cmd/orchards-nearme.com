★★★★★Suzanne K.8 months ago

By far one of the cutest pumpkin patches I’ve been to! And you can pick your own pumpkin right off the vine (if you want to!) and there are plenty of cute picture perfect moments! And my cute little 5 pound pumpkin I found (and picked myself!) was only $2.75! What a steal!

★★★★★Katie Fiola Jonesa year ago

Great pumpkin patch. I like this place because it is beautiful and simple. They don't have all the crazy carnival stuff for kids and I appreciate that. This place is relaxed and nice to hang out at. They have lots of parking, food trucks and a coffee truck, and plenty of pumpkins that are reasonably priced. You can pick out pumpkins right at the barn or you can take a trek out to the field to find your favorite one. There's a pretty pond and lots of great areas for photo oops. They also have hay rides. No entrance fee to just hang out. We brought our own wagon but they do have wheelbarrows to use for free. And apparently they have Christmas trees in the winter available

★★★★☆hannah nichols5 years ago

Really great pumpkin patch! Had a great variety of already picked pumpkins to choose from. Pumpkins of all colors and sizes placed around with different decorations. Super cute photo opportunities. They also have a patch to go out into and pick your own if you want. They also had corn stocks, Indian corn, and squashes. The only thing is they're a bit more expensive than some places, they do price by the pound. Overall recommend.
