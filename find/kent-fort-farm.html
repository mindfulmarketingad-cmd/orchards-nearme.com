★★★★★Arielle Bazulka4 years ago

Amazing hidden gem at the end of the island. This place comes with everything traditional about Holloween without the hassle of larger commercial farms. The owner is friendly and personable. They had a pumpkin patch, hay ride, petting zoo, and corn maze. I have 3 kiddos, ages 4, 7, and 11 and they absolutely loved this place! We will be coming back every year.

★★☆☆☆Chris Taylor3 years ago

Bring cash. Even walking through the corn maze isn't free. We were hoping to pick our own pumpkin from a patch. These were loose pumpkins in a lawn. Fine if you're a local, but if you're from further away you can do better.
