★★★★★Angela Marchesea month ago

This will be my future 2026 peach stand visit on 290 W on the right side. After my visit to Burg's Corner I come here. As stated clinged peaches come out Mid- May. It has an unpaved parking lot. I go on a weekday as I'm visiting several peach stands. I park in front of the green building or along its side. Their orchards are in the back. There are many peach types here. Here on a long front table, the attendant offers peach wedges of this or that to help you decide on your purchase ! Separate from this is a metal stand with shelves where one may purchase farm vegetables like squash, onions, etc. Also there's a small shop to the left. As its door opens one sees many canned and bottled items, jams, relishes, etc. There are also pecans for sale. I like to purchase several varieties here. Last year I asked the attendant if she could write on the paper bags what I bought as I would be sharing with others ? And she did. I like coming here for the clinged varieties. And I make a later summer trip for the freestone peaches. Homegrown peaches ! YUM ! May 8, 2026 10 P.M. News announces " Due to drought, the 2026 peach crop isn't good. Stands will have only 30% to 40% of their crop. And peaches may come from other places." I had heard that peach stands have a Co- op with other States and when the peaches aren't good, peaches come from these States. It's too bad the 2026 crop isn't good because it means fewer peaches and higher prices. Check with this Stand.
