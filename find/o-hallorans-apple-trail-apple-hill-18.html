★★★★★Mackenzie Shelton9 months ago

O’Hallorans was lovely! We’d never been to Apple Hill before and this farm was our first stop of the trip. After visiting 3 others, my boyfriend and I both expressed that we wished we’d spent more time here. It was very uncrowded for a Saturday and the most peaceful, serene setting. The U-pick apples are delicious (and very fairly priced), and the animals were super cute—bunnies, goats, chickens, turkeys, and peacocks oh my!! There’s also a very sweet farm dog named Pixie. Take a walk along the grounds, enjoy a pause for a picnic, and be sure to snack on some fresh apples. This place is worth a long stop and I look forward to coming back.
