High marks because I’ve loved this place since I was a kid which is why this bummed me out so badly. We are a low income family and when we can afford it we splurge on these shortcakes we get the large size every time because it’s a must. This Juneteenth weekend we came to enjoy time at the shortcake stand and paid for two large strawberry shortcakes. No modifications. My partner and I noticed halfway through that we were shorted a whole ice cream scoop. That’s literally one of the best parts. Being careful with our budget I am always noticing when we are being shorted based on what we paid. The stand says a large shortcake consists of two ice cream scoops. Mine was one scoop and an overcompensation of berries to hide the missing scoop it seemed. The line was way too long and busy to try and cut in front or wait an hour to complain. And I couldn’t find who to reach out to about my concern. I went to their social media posts to share my experience and it was unanswered. I’m not sure how to complain about the girls there shorting me and possibly more people there that day a scoop of ice cream when we paid for two so I’m just gonna leave this review here.
