Reviews

★★★★★Melissa Christensen2 years ago

Very nice place with a huge variety of hanging baskets and flats for planting. They have plants and flowers I’ve never seen before. Prices are fair and staff is friendly. My only recommendation for improvement would be to make the aisles between plants and flowers larger as if you get in an aisle with someone else in it who is browsing there is no room to go around them. Not a big deal but somewhat inconvenient. I would highly recommend buying plants and flowers here and we will definitely be back. Plenty of parking on the premises.

Not only is this where I get my funky Hoyas and Cacti, it’s a peaceful/therapeutic place to bring my grandma with Alzheimer’s. She recently had to move and give up her cats..she also loves plants…it’s a lovely paring of the two. Sometimes we just go for the vibes 🖤 Brian and staff are always welcoming.
