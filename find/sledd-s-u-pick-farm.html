Reviews

★★★★★Niasha Roberts2 years ago

I visited with my husband and two year old son and we all had a great time! Someone suggested it for my family and gave us some details. She recommended you check the Facebook page (or Google) to make sure they are open as the hours may vary. I agree with this. My family drove about an hour and we would have been disappointed if we got there and it was not open. Once you get to Mims to get to the farm it is a little bit out of the way and down a dirt / unpaved road. Just drive with caution. There was a porta-potty on premises; I found it to be cleaner than I expected. There was a small trailer with a very inviting woman. We brought our own scissors (as it was suggested) but she did offer us scissors which I thought was great. They also had sunflower seeds and honey for sale. The farm itself is a maze style and you can just wander through and take pictures. You can cut any and as many sunflowers as you want and you pay when you exit. My son had the best time running around and picking out sunflowers that we later put on our dining room table. I highly recommend as a place to take pictures, to go and support a nice local small business and even a different activity for a couple or a family or even friends! It is cash only!

★★★★☆Anne Claire7 years ago

Super cute place. It was nice to be outside and walk around the flowers. There are bees of course, but they weren’t really bothersome. We brought some gloves which were nice to have. Make sure to bring something to cut the flowers. Careful wearing white, you will get pollen on you...lesson learned. We bought honey there as well, which is delicious. It’s a nice activity to do with family, friends, etc.
