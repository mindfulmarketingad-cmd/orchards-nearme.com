★★★★★Lisa K.3 years ago

We've returned to Hidden Cove Orchard annually since before we were married (in their orchard!) 16 years ago. Their fall festival is always a wonderful experience. The staff is attentive to insure their trees are protected and produce a quality apple each year. (So don't ask to pick apples or allow children to climb trees or pick apples.) The store is a small, cozy experience, with high quality items you would be pressed to find elsewhere. Their seaonal decor always inspires me to "up my game" in my own decor. The products, including the food items, are all boutique quality. Come for the experience, go home with a (ready to bake) pie and fresh apples to savor the experience.
