★★★★★Amy Coney3 years ago

This place is great to visit. You can walk the orchard and pick the different varieties of apples at the time they are ripened. Their website is very informative about the different types of apples, what they’re good for and when they will be ready to pick. They have apples for everything, but you might have to go at a certain time (or more than once) to get the specific variety you need since they only let you pick when the apples are ready… you know you’re getting good apples at the right time. They make great apple cider. Their peaches are amazing!!! The staff was very helpful and friendly. The orchard was clean and easy to navigate. They also have prepackaged fruits if you don’t want to do u-pick. There is something for everyone.
