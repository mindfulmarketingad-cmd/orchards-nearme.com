We picked blackberries and pluots! The experience was great and the owners were super friendly and pointed out the best trees to pick from! Would definitely go again and highly recommend. The only confusing thing is the parking but they have instructions on their website and are super responsive if you call as well!

★★★★★Jessica Taillieu5 years ago

Absolutely no doubt in my mind I will be coming back here. The owners were super friendly and helpful. The produce was GREAT, best berries we've ever had and all of it was an extremely reasonable price. It was very quiet, small place, we had lots of space to ourselves (practically had the whole farm to ourselves since there was only other couple there and they left soon after we arrived - what a VIP experience!). We were able to take our time picking strawberries, blackberries, pluots and corn and it was so much fun! Tip to any fellow millennials: bring cash. I also brought a small pair of scissors to cut the strawberries and was glad I did. It's a real farm so you probably want to wearing camping pants/shoes or something you don't mind getting a little dirt on. They provided the buckets and paper bags we used but you can bring your own as well. There's a small sign that has the name of the farm out front - don't be thrown off by the fact that the place just looks like a house. It is a house, and there are only a couple of parking spaces, you are in the right spot :)
