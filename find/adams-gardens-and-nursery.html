Reviews

★★★★★Hannah Manna month ago

I'm so glad we found Adams Nursery! We had gotten trees from another local nursery in the past, but the prices at the other one had gotten shockingly high and their service wasn't good anyway. We stopped by Adams one day and found the staff to be very helpful and the prices to be totally fair and expected. I appreciate that the grounds are a manageable size and we could easily look around at all of their options — but they really do have a *great* selection! We had a couple trees in mind that I didn't think we'd find easily, but Adams actually had them. We paid for delivery on our trees, and it was going to take a few days for them to get dropped off, which was expected. However, they called later that same day and said they could actually bring them over already, which was great. The man who delivered them also looked at one of our existing trees that we have had issues with and helped diagnose the problem. He was super kind and helpful. All in all, a very positive experience — we'll be back next time we need more!
