★★★★★Rachel Drummonda year ago

**An absolute gem of a place!** Bramble's Edge Farm is a *dream come true*—whether you’re looking for the perfect photography backdrop or a cozy little market full of locally made treasures! The scenery is breathtaking with its rustic charm and natural beauty, making it a *photographer’s paradise*. Every corner feels like it was made to be captured. The market is such a treat—*fresh produce, handmade bath salts, sugar scrubs, and more seasonings than I could count*! Everything smells, looks, and tastes incredible. You can tell it's all made with care and love. And speaking of love—Robert and his wife are some of the **kindest, most welcoming people** you’ll ever meet. They made us feel right at home and even introduced us to their adorable silkie chicken, *Gretchen*! I mean… come on, how sweet is that?! I’m already looking forward to going back. Whether you’re stopping by to shop or shoot, **Bramble's Edge Farm is a must-visit**. Highly, highly recommend!
