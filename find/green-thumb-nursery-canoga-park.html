I have become a huge fan of his work; I proudly display his bonsai on my balcony and receive compliments from everyone who sees them. His knowledge and passion for these trees are evident. I already plan to be back very soon for another batch! To the owner: Bonsai is such a beautiful, almost forgotten art form. I truly believe many people would pay good money to take lessons from someone as knowledgeable as Mr. Fidel right here at the nursery. It’s just an idea… I look forward to coming here many, many more times. I am so grateful I was recommended to this shop—from what I can tell, there is no better place in the San Fernando Valley to find high-quality bonsai that are actually cared for by an expert. Thank you, Green Thumb and Mr. Fidel!
