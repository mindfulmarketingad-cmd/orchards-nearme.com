Facebook page: Check their latest updates on their page for what's ripening and to book your appointment. Appointment is required to come pick fruits. You provide the boxes or bags to transport the fruit home. U-Pick seasons vary depending on fruit, season, and weather. So always check their Facebook page for updates. "Citrus fruits, such as navel oranges and tangerines, are available between October and December. Tangelos and grapefruit are available between December and April. Juice oranges are available between March and May. Oriental persimmons are available between late August and October. Peaches and blackberries are available between April and June." U-Pick Fruit Prices: quoted from their page 10/26/23 Citrus fruit are $8-$10 per 1/4 bushel bag. Pomelos are $1.50 per pound. Oriental persimmons are $4 per pound and blackberries are $5 per pound. Peaches are $4 per pound. Passion fruit 50 cents each (if and when available). 10/24/23: My review Highlights This is a small family-owned five acres of fruit farm. The family grows Fuyu, Christmas, and other persimmon varieties. They also grow citrus fruits. U-pick season varies depending on weather, season, and fruit variety. You must check their business page on Facebook for their posting of what fruit is available and message or call them to book an appointment. Appointment time is required to come pick fruits. I took my kids here and my son enjoyed harvesting his favorite Fuyu persimmon variety. Fuyu is the only non-astringent persimmon variety they grow. The other varieties are astringent which means you will have to wait for the fruits to really soften up or else the taste will scar your memory and taste buds for a lifetime. 😂 I learned that from experience. Thanks James for your time and talk. I learned a chestnut tree needs a partner for pollination. The owner James is very informative and he looks like Albert Einstein. James also has a turkey that tries to act like a chicken. The kids enjoyed watching the turkey. 😆 Oh we also saw a mockingbird nest as shown in photo.
