I had a blast at the apple orchard! It’s a fantastic spot for families, with so much to do and see. It’s not just about apples; they’ve got pumpkins, a mini farmers market, and more. One thing to note is that the seating is a bit far from the main parking area. So, it might be tricky for those with mobility issues due to the gravel paths and uneven ground. But they’ve got several booths selling snacks and trinkets, which is pretty cool. Plus, there’s a giant sandbox for the kids and a fun area with hay bales to climb on. Overall, it’s a great place to spend the day! 🍎🎃🛍️
