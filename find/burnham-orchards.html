★★★★☆Williama year ago

Great selection of produce and a very tasty bakery!! The apple cinnamon donuts are ridiculously good and they have such a ni e assortment of apples. My wife, mother in law, and I went apple picking this past weekend. They have several kinds of apples to pick from. The reason for marking down a star was the lack of apples available to pick from the trees. It was very challenging to find apples that weren't rotted already or gone bad. It took over an hour of searching to gather a reasonable amount of apples to feel we may have gotten our money's worth. We would have been better off buying from the market, which I'm guessing the trees were empty because the good apples were in the market. My recommendation that would have made this a 5 star review would be the staff issuing a warning to those buying bags for apple picking that the trees are/were picked through already and a lot have started rotting. Then they'd know to buy from the market if apple quality is what they want. If they want the apple picking experience, then they can still get that.
