Reviews

★★★★★Bridger Roberson9 months ago

I was surprised at how fun this place was. So many different kinds of trees. Not sure the prime time to come but we came the second week of the Fall semester and there were still tons of apples around. Don’t expect to get a perfect apple. Be willing to pick apples that you’ll have to cut bruises off of. The apples are 1$ per lb and it’s an honesty thing. Just scan the QR code in the apple museum shed and pay that way. Super fun and would be great for a date or family activity.
