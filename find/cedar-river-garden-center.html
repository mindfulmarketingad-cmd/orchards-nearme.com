★★★★☆Ronald Oliver3 years ago

They have a great staff, and a great selection of plants. There’s shredded topsoil and compost can’t be beat for making a garden bed. But you need to be careful if you buy a tree that has been overwintered. We got those river birch at half price and paid to have them planted since the pot was so bad. We are disappointed that they sold a tree that only had leaves on one trunk. I would wait until the trees have leaves on them before buying next time. They do not warranty over winter trees. I just wish they would make sure they had leaves before the winter begin.
