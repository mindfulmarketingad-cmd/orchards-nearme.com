Reviews

★★★★★Patrick Cody2 years ago

Nourse Farm in Westboro is a true hidden gem that offers a delightful escape from the hustle and bustle of daily life. As soon as you step onto the property, you can feel the welcoming atmosphere. The pick-your-own vegetable options are an interactive and enjoyable experience that appeals to both young and old. For those less inclined to DIY, their farm store is stocked with fresh, high-quality produce. The strawberry rhubarb pie is an absolute must-try; it's a heavenly blend of sweet and tart that will leave you craving more. Their four goats—Ben and Jerry and Bert and Ernie—are charming additions to the farm that kids and animal lovers will adore. Don't forget to say hello to their majestic white horse as well! The scenic trails provide a peaceful setting for family walks, making it the perfect venue for spending quality time with loved ones. Last but not least, the Veg Out food truck serves up delicious fare during the summer, adding yet another layer of appeal to this charming locale. Overall, Nourse Farm is a fabulous place that's well worth a visit for its unique offerings and warm, community-centric vibe.

★★★★★Paul Nakazawa3 years ago

The Nourse Farm just celebrated its 300th anniversary, owned and operated by many generations of the Nurse (Nourse) family. The farm stand offers a variety of seasonal fruits and vegetables grown on the farm, honey, jams/preserves, homemade pies, cut flowers, and a refrigerated section for delicate items like fresh-picked raspberries, frozen pies, eggs, etc. Also there are pick-your-own fields that are open during the relevant time periods. An on site food truck offers smoothies, sandwiches and other items made largely from produce grown on the farm. Plenty of outdoor seating (covered for protection from direct sun). Definitely a place to come back to.

★★★★☆Lindsay LeBoeuf3 years ago

Nice farm with a small farm stand store to buy their goods. We did the pick your own raspberries in late August for $5/lb. You have to walk up a rocky hill to get to one of the two raspberry sections to pick from so don't wear sandals. There were plenty of raspberries to pick and they pop off the plant very easily so young kids should have a problem. To note, there were a bunch of bees (mostly honey bees) which is nice to see but they also loved the raspberries so just keep an eye out when picking. We picked for about 30 minutes and filled about a large yogurt container which came to a bit over 1lb. Raspberries have stayed fresh for the last week in the fridge and still taste great!
