We had an a amazing strawberry picking at the farm. Unlike most u pick farms we’ve been to, jones family farm is extremely organized. Farm staff was along the way to guide you where to go, and I really appreciated that the strawberry field was clean, dry and most importantly very minimal wasted strawberries. After having been to many apple picking places where the orchards can stink of rotten apples, jones family farm impressed me and I can say it’s definitely a much more pleasant experience. ★★★★☆Andrew Izzo2 years ago
