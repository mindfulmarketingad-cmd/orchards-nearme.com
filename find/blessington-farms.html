Reviews

★★★★★MsDarBay3 months ago

The best and most fun farm near Houston! Unlike other farms around the city, it never feels overcrowded, which makes the experience so much more enjoyable. Everything is very well organized, and there are plenty of activities to keep kids entertained. Also, the tickets price is low for what you get. You can feed the animals and visit the aviary, which has a huge number of beautiful birds you can also feed—such a unique experience! During Easter, they host a very well-organized egg hunt where kids don’t have to compete; each child simply collects 10 eggs, which makes it stress-free and fun for everyone. There are lots of slides, bike rides, and trampolines to keep kids active and happy. The restrooms are always clean, which is a big plus. On top of that, the prices for food and gifts are very reasonable. I definitely recommend this farm for a fun family outing!

Good times!! Great place to pick blueberries and blackberries. Both were HUGE this year. We were able to purchase plants (blue and black berry) as well as some very delicious local honey. Everyone that helped us (ladies by the blueberries today) were fun, professional and friendly. This is also a great place to bring the kids- with lots of other activities.

★★★★☆Stephanie Lemke3 months ago

We visited Blessington Farm and had so much fun! The entire place had a great atmosphere and all the staff we interacted with were upbeat and genuinely looked happy to be there, which made the experience even better. There is so much to do for kids and families — from photo ops, giant slides, sand dunes, animals, tractor rides, and rope courses. It really feels like a full day of activities and our kids had a blast the whole time. There’s something around every corner and we can’t wait to come back again. The only thing to be aware of is that once you pay admission, several activities cost extra. Things like animal feed tickets, concessions, rock mining, strawberry picking, fishing, and face painting all require additional purchases. It’s all fun, but those extras can add up pretty quickly, so just plan for that when you visit. Overall, it was a great experience and a really fun family day! 🌾🚜🐐
