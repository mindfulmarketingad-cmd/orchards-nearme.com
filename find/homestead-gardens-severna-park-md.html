Reviews

★★★★★Kara Kane2 months ago

The cutest garden center! They were the only garden center in the area currently carrying large meyer lemon trees, so I came for that (and got one that looks just lovely). I was not expecting all the work from local artisans and unique garden supplies. The price on the meyer lemon tree was consistent with other garden centers I checked. Some of their gardening supplies are a bit on the pricier side but they're also so unique and handmade. Look at this cool weeder with lots of curved tines, I've never seen one like it before! I also saw a lovely purple metal watering can and a lot of unique arbors and metal garden decor. The employees were kind and friendly, answered all my questions, and helped me load my new tree into my car. I will definitely be back in the future!
