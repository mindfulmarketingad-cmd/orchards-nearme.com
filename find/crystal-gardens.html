★★★★★Kate Murphy2 years ago

Great variety of healthy plants - annuals (including the MOST spectacular petunia selections) and perennials (including an amazing variety of hardy succulents) & pond plants; specimen shrubs and trees. Great selection of planters and pots, bird baths and lawn ornaments. Gorgeous hanging planters; also an entire hoop house of succulents and cactus, another of shade plants containing the most spectacular foliage plants anywhere locally. Go early in the season for best selection and leave plenty of time for your visit. There’s also a fun antique/gift store on premises and a lovely rock shop. Both are a tad pricey but offer unique merchandise. I highly recommend the whole experience. Plant prices are fairly competitive, especially considering that many of their selections aren’t available anywhere else in the area.
