★★★★★Ariel Gonzalez10 months ago

Totally amazing experience! My son and I came upon a man driving a small utility vehicle while we were wandering in the orchard looking for ripe peaches to pick He greeted us, so I asked him if we were going in the right direction. He offered to drive us to the section with ripe peaches. After a few minutes of picking peaches he returns to us and offers to drive us up to another section with even more ripe peaches. It was an exceptionally welcoming experience! Later on, while purchasing some peach wood for my smoker, the cashier went above and beyond in ensuring that we received the credit we earned from their loyalty program. On a side note, we saw a picture inside the farmers market of the man that drove us around the orchard. Apparently he was the owner. We will definitely be returning!!!
