★★★★★S Ta year ago

They sell native/drought hardy grass seeds by the lbs, in bins with a scoop like you see at some grocery stores, so you can choose the amount you get rather than buying a pre-bagged amount. I havent seen such a setup at any of our other local nurseries, so I thought that was cool. The place has a more compact/organized feel to it, which makes it easy to browse their selection in a timely fashion. Like every nursery here, there are certain species and plant varieties they carry that other places dont. It was very well stocked when I visited compared to the other nurseries I had been to that day. Also had some cute watering cans like a duck watering can and a fish watering can. And a watering can painted with a character from a webcomic I read, so that was quite a surprise to see!
