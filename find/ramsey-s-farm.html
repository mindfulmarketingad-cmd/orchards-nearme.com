★★★★★mj Fahrman (MJ)a year ago

BEFORE HALLOWEEN! VISITED ON OCTOBER 26, 2024 My family and I couldn’t have picked a more lovely day to visit this farm for the first time. I work at a college and they decided to take students to visit a few weekends ago. I looked it up on Google Maps and was definitely curious to check out as well. Since COVID, my family and I have been going to random farms in October prior to Halloween. It gives us to spend time together as our lives get busier and our daughter gets older. We had a sunny, breezy (more windy) day. It’s a big farm with plenty activities. Corn maze, hay rides, play area for little ones, sunflower farm, purchase a variety of pumpkins straight off the fields. You can purchase baked good, beverages. There was a food truck too. A great place to visit as a couple or with children. Limitless photo opportunities. We definitely had a great time! Verdict: MUST CHECK out next year!
