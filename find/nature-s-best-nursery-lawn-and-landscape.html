We waited for 10 mins, and then my daughter went to look for someone - 5 min later she returned, no one found. She then started calling the nursery business line - no answer. A gentleman got in line behind us, by this time we'd been at the checkout for almost 25 mins. I mentioned to him, we'd been waiting a little while and could not find an employee. After about 5 min he went to search for someone. By this time another customer got in line - my daughter is still calling & no answer. Our total wait time was nearly 45 min when the gentleman returned to say "someone is coming". The worst part of all of this is after waiting patiently all of this time, the check out person kept trying to over-charge me for the "pull apart herbs". I told her the multiple signs at every station says - 2" pull aparts 3.99 & 4" pots 6.99. She told me she fixed it but after I left I discovered she neglected to fix 3 of the 2" pots and charged me 9.99 each for the 2x2 pull aparts instead of 3.99. She also overcharged me for a clematis by another 5.00 - That's 23.00 too much! I doubt I'll go back there but if I do give them a second chance I will double check my receipt before I go to the car. I would have done it this time but was trying to be courteous of the people in line behind me who were saying just before the employee arrived, they were just going to take the plants and leave. The checkout is right next to the parking lot and no one would have known the difference except those of us who were honest.
