Garden Centers Across New England

New England's garden centers operate within a tightly defined growing season bracketed by a real winter on both ends, which gives the region's nurseries a clear and predictable annual rhythm: spring annuals and vegetable starts, a full summer of perennials and flowering shrubs, and a beloved fall season built around mums, pumpkins, and the trees and shrubs chosen specifically for their autumn color.
