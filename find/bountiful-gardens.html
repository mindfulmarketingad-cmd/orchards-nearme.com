★★★★★Keith Morrisona month ago

Bountiful Gardens in Hillsborough was recommended to me by a friend. When I took the recommendation, I was primarily concerned that the nursery have a wide variety of plants to choose from. As it turns out, the selection at Bountiful Gardens is huge. Just as importantly, I wanted advise about redoing the small garden plot in front of my townhome. And I am pleased to report that the customer service at Bountiful Gardens is every bit as good as their selection of plants. From the moment I started talking to Dan Ilkow, their Landscape Designer, I knew I was in good hands. Although modest in terms of size, I wanted a total makeover of the garden. Once I spelled out my needs, I received the time and attention to detail that resulted in a very successful project. Dan's education in horticulture allowed him to provided sound advice regarding the choice of plants, the layout of the garden and the various options available to me. Throughout the process he was excellent in communicating the steps involved and their timing. The team they sent to do the work arrived on time, worked efficiently and before planting anything verified the layout. As a bonus, Dan actually checked in with the crew remotely during the work and had them send photos to make sure that things were being done correctly. The result was exactly what I wanted. At a time when the rule of thumb is that good customer service is in very short supply, Bountiful Gardens is a wonderful exception to that rule. I recommend them without hesitation and will be using them for my own gardening needs in the future.
