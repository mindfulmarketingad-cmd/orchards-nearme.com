★★★★★Anuhea Jai8 months ago

I loved everything about this place! My boyfriend took me here because he knows how much i love sunflowers but the flowers aren’t where the sights end. ★★★★★Hitomi Otagiri8 months ago

We went to the Waimanalo Country Farms Fall Fest on a Wednesday, and it was such a great experience! Everything was decorated so cute and festive. When we arrived, a friendly boy guided us to the check-in desk where we paid the entrance fee, and from the start, you could tell everyone there was having a great time. We were hungry, so we grabbed food first : the seafood boil (sausage, potato, corn, and shrimp) for $15, a li hing lemonade for $5, plus pumpkin pie and cornbread. Everything was delicious! We even went back for more lemonade. The cornbread is always amazing, and their pumpkin pie might be one of the best I’ve ever had. After eating, we walked around the pumpkin patch, took a bunch of photos, and picked two big pumpkins to take home, both for only $16 total! We also visited the farm animal area where you can feed them, which was such a cute touch. It was a fun and wholesome experience overall. I always love coming here, everyone is so friendly and welcoming. Definitely worth a visit if you’re looking for some fall fun on Oahu!
