★★★★★Gem G.4 years ago

Went here for the first time today and we sure had a blast! There's tons of persimmon trees you won't even know where to start picking. They're huge and really sweet! The owners are really nice, we called them right when we got there and they met us at the back. They let you borrow shears, buckets and baskets you can borrow when you pick, plus the wagon. The owners even helped us load our car with the persimmons that we bought! Even offered us water which was a really nice thought! If you're looking for a place to have fun with your kids away from the city, this is the place to be! They said they are open from September until 1st or 2nd week of November.
