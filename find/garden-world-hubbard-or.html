★★★★★Rosemary Ramireza year ago

Best place in all of northwest Oregon to shop for Japanese Maples (from someone who has purchased 25+ trees from at least five different local nurseries and three online specialty vendors). Incredible selection and very good pricing on both the trees and delivery (which is in a covered box truck, so trees don’t get dried out during transport). Pictured are my Nishiki Gawa in peak fall color; my beautiful big Seiryu; Japanese Sunrise; and Shidava Gold maples, all from Garden World. Can’t wait to come back!

★★★★★James Baez4 years ago

Went to GW yesterday to pick up some blueberry plants for the house and it was our first time visiting. Cindy greeted us when we arrived and was super helpful in directing us on our adventure. Over the course of our afternoon shopping and looking at all the wonderful plants we came across Antonio numerous times. You could tell that he took exceptional care of all the plants on the grounds and he had a very personal touch to each one. Keep in mind there are hundreds of different varieties but you could sense that each one had a personal place in his heart. We definitely will be back to purchase our tees and I am still deciding on which Ornamental grass we should add at the homestead. Friendly, helpful, and a fantastic experience.

★★★★★Kathaleen Parker2 years ago

A fun place to get landscaping at good prices! Easy freeway exit and lots of parking. No lawn tools, no garden decor / pots, and no fertilizer, just lots and lots of plants for your garden. Highly Recommend!
