A Wonderful local farm. An excellent choice for a day of family fun. 🙌 I got tickets through Groupon for the Pumpkin Patch Experience, a five-pack for $16 that alone is worth 5-stars for such a great deal! Cost includes everything except for pony rides, food/drinks, and the price for pumpkins. Pumpkins were roughly $8 for medium and $20 for large. My family was able to spend the day roaming the farm. Truly a hidden gem. 📸Wandering the farm and taking pictures. 🌽Walking through the corn maze. 🎃Searching for pumpkins. 🏰Playing in the hay castle. 🐐🐔🦃🐷🐎Admiring the billy goats, chickens, turkeys, pigs, horses. 🚜Hayride. 🐴Pony rides for $6. 🍩🥤There was a food truck that had yummy donuts and a pop-up with tasty specialty drinks.

★★★★☆Elysia Garbade3 years ago

Brought my 2 year old there in late sept for fall pumpkin fun. Just the right festivity, and things to do for 3 hrs. Tractor ride, petting goats, seeing pigs, chickens, horses and cows. Great for her. Also climbed a hay mountain—and perhaps her favorite was sitting in a big wooden box of tried corn kernels. Cool sensory experience for kids. The day started out chilly, which might have scared some folks off—but it was a nice thing to do on a kind of wet, fall morning. I should have brought cash, but the still took card.
