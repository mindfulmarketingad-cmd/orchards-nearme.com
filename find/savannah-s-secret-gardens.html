After a long hunt for large camellias, SSG was the place! The main store ladies were very friendly & helpful in directing me to the best place. When needing to go to their larger nursery, it is typically by appointment only, but they were extremely flexible and kind. There’s more there than meets the eye as there’s two sides to their larger plot. A gentleman, Mark, was extremely kind and helpful. He had so much patience with me and was so grateful and courteous.
