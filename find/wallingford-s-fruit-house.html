★★★★★Sophia Adams7 months ago

We love this place and visit several times/yr. I do find their prices a tad-bit high but the experience balances it out. I used to take my children here and now we take our grandchildren. The children love the Barnyard with toys, animals and farm equipment to climb on. This year we participated in their Nightmare walk($25/person). We wanted to purchase sweatshirts but they were all out of the sizes we needed so we bought t-shirts instead. Great family fun, great apples, great experiences.

★★★★★Trisha8 months ago

Always a great time here! Even when it is packed with people the customer service is great and they have the best warm sugar donuts along with their apple cider slush! It’s always a good time apple picking, the kids can play on the playground, there was a craft “fair” going on as well. Definitely recommend for anyone looking for a place to pick apples, they have pumpkins as well, mums, a corn maze, tractor ride and at night they have “Nightmare on the Ridge” which I believe is a haunted walk! Great place all around!
