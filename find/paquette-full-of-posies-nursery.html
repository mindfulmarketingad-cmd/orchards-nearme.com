We go way out of our way to go to this Nursery because they are absolutely amazing! Every Memorial Weekend we make our trip here to plan our veggie gardens. They not only have an excellent variety and selection of veggies, berries, fruit trees, ect. (We got our first tree, a Cherry tree, here!) but they cultivate just about everything themselves. Most, if not all, of their veggies are cultivated there, so you won't be buying retailed plants from who knows where. Their staff is extremely knowledgeable and helpful, not to mention great to chat with. Other than their own cultivated plants, they support other local businesses and local horticulture to Vermont. We love this place and we're lifetime patrons. They're also dog friendly!
