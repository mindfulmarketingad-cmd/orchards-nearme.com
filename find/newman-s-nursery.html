★☆☆☆☆Ginger Ernestina2 months ago

I purchased a plant from Newman's Nursery because they were the only place in town that had that particular type of plant. There were two different prices on the plants. There was a sign in front of the plants that said they were $79.00 but all of the plants on that pallet had a tag that said they were $39.00. I did the honest thing and asked a sales person how much they were. He said they were $79.00 but that they often make mistakes in pricing and that I could have the plant for the price on the tag. I thanked him told him at that I actually needed two plants but could only afford one at the higher price so because they are a lower price I would get two. He did not have a problem with that. I also suggested that they correct the price tags on the plants. When I got to the cash register someone else came up and asked me if these were my plants. I said yes and he told me that the plants are $79.00 not $39.00 and he acted like I was trying to price switch the tags. I explained to him what the first person had told me. He said he couldn't honor that so I told him that is fine and that I would purchase one of the plants. When I got up to the cashier she told me, "You didn't get away with it today." I feel like I was treated very rudely. I understand that this is a small business and unlike a large box store they may not be able to honor their mistakes in pricing. I also understand that there is a lot of theft going on in our city right now but to treat a customer that inquired about the price the way that they did is just rude.
