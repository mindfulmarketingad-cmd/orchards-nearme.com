★★★★★Sonjaa year ago

Great place! This is my third year picking here. The family is kind and flexible. If you want giant berries, head to the grocery store. But if you want sweet, delectable, fresh berries go pick here. It's $20 for an entire tray and $3 for the tray, but if you bring it back the next time you pick you don't get charged the $3 again. There are weeds, but that means my berries are full of nasty herbicides, so I'm willing to hunt! I definitely recommend this place! Check their website for picking times. Family friendly.
