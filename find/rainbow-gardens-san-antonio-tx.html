★★★★★Maggie Floyd4 weeks ago

As we head into the part of the South Texas gardening season where the sun begins acting like a relentless vengeful supervillain, I can honestly say I’ve been very pleased with the plants I bought from Rainbow Gardens this spring. My tomatoes, basil, mint, oregano, and several other garden residents all got their start there. Not everything will survive the summer, because this is South Texas and some plants simply aren’t built for this heat. But I’ve already harvested enough basil and mint to dry for later, and I’ve gotten so much enjoyment out of the garden along the way. One of the things I appreciate most about Rainbow Gardens is that they don’t just sell plants. They help people learn how to garden successfully in our challenging climate. Their classes, events, and online resources have taught me a lot and helped me adapt when Mother Nature decides to start playing on legendary mode. And thanks to their Bonus Bucks program, I’ve got an August shopping trip on the calendar and a completely unsubstantiated belief that I still have room for more plants. Overall five stars for the great plant selection, knowledgeable staff, and a genuine commitment to helping local gardeners succeed.
