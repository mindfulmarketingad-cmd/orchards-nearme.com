Great experience. We love that there are so many attractions for one price. Galleria is worth the trip. There are so many choices you likely won't fit everything in. This is especially true with young children, but you will have a great time. Plenty of food truck options, lots of bathrooms. Even the port-a-poties are clean and well maintained. There are too many options to lost them all. We love the train rides, tractor ride to pumpkin picking, the pillow jumpers, obstacle course, and the corn pit. Parking is well laid out and organized. We also visit other local favorites such as Huber's and Mulberry Farms. However, I think Gallerin is the most bang for your buck and our favorite of the bunch in the fall season.
