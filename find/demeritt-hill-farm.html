★★★★★Bridget Rounds4 years ago

Hands down the best apple cider donut I've ever had. The farm was stunning, the employees were wonderfully pleasant, and the prices were so reasonable. DeMeritt Hill Farm reminds you that not all farms are there to price-gouge! The hayride was $2 per person, bouncy house was free. Also, the grounds were so well maintained. The apple orchard was very large and beautiful. We also discovered some walking trails, a few ponds, and some Christmas trees off in the distance! I can tell this is a well-loved farm - owners, employees, and public. We will be making this farm a Fall tradition!
