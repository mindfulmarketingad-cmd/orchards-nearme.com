★★★★★Lisa Rice8 years ago

Always have fun picking blueberries here. Been coming here for years. Very fair prices, you pick then pay by the pound. They have different varieties to choose from. It is nice to be out in nature and hear the birds chirping. Go in the morning before it gets too hot. Everybody that goes shows respect and leaves no trash behind. Bring drinking water to stay hydrated. Bug repellent and comfortable clothing a must. Always meet friendly fellow blueberry pickers here. Have fun and loose track of time. Very highest recommendation!!! Beautiful!!!
