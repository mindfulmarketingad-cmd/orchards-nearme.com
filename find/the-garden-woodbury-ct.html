★★★★★Noah Oliveira3 years ago

The Garden is hands down the best plant nursery around. As an amateur tropical houseplant collector, The Garden has the most amazing selection of houseplants I have ever seen at any nursery in CT. They range from classic common houseplants, an AWESOME collection of cacti and succulents, to beautiful rarer specimens of all different types of philodendrons, Alocasia, monstera, and more!! I feel like a kid in a candy shop every single time I walk into the greenhouse here! The employees are all super friendly and all seem to be passionate about the work they’re doing, and happily answer any questions you’ll have. If you’re in the market for houseplants, wether you’re a novice, or especially a collector, look no further as I can tell you you’ve already found your new favorite spot! (The Garden also has all the outdoor perennials, annuals, shrubs and trees you’ll ever need!! I personally don’t plant outdoor plants so I can’t speak much on those, but the greenhouse is beautiful and full all spring/summer!)
