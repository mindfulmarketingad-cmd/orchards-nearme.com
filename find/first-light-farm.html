Reviews

★★★★★Taylor Myers5 years ago

My favorite local farm! Very friendly faces! We had fun meeting the chickens and taking a tractor ride around the farm with our son! We picked out pumpkins and bought some amazing looking veggies and a bar of local organic soap as well. We were offered Tea and snacks while we took quick shelter from the rain under one of their tents. This place is amazing and everyone should at least visit once! You won't regret it! Also: Delicious local HONEY and a pre mixed bag of veggies for a stew! I can't wait to eat this stuff up

★★★★★Stephanie Kitchen3 years ago

We were in Carnation visiting a friend and went by the farm and learning center. It is wonderful, they have amazing produce. We spoke with Jane at length regarding local honey (we got 2 jars). She was great at engaging my teenagers educating them about the produce and the farm. A wonderful place to visit while you are in the area.

★★★★☆Jacob Jaeggli8 years ago

A CSA and fruit stand run by the nicest folks. I stopped by the farm to see what they had, and was treated to a small tour of the fields. They're just getting started in a new field this year and had some limited availability of certain things so that they could make sure to have enough for their CSA customers, but they made sure we were well taken care of and left with a good bundle of greens, bok choy, beans, squash, and cucumbers.
