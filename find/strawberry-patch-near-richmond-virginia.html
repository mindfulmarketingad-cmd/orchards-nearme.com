Strawberry Patchs Near Richmond: What You Need to Know

Richmond sits within the Mid-Atlantic, giving visitors easy access to strawberry patchs that reflect the area's growing conditions and picking calendar. Mid-Atlantic Strawberry Patches

The mid-Atlantic's strawberry season runs from mid-May into June across New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, and West Virginia, aided by the region's sandy coastal-plain soils that many growers favor for berries. New Jersey in particular has a long commercial strawberry-growing tradition that supports plenty of u-pick options each spring.

Best Time to Visit a Strawberry Patch Near Richmond

Mid-spring into early summer, generally mid-May through June in the region's sandy coastal-plain soils.

Tips for Your Richmond Strawberry Patch Visit

Weekday mornings tend to be quieter than weekend afternoons at popular mid-Atlantic patches. Look low in the plant for fully red berries, since the reddest fruit is often hidden beneath the leaves rather than sitting on top.
