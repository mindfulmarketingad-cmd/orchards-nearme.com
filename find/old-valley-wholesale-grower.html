I can’t believe I ever bought a single plant anywhere else! Keep in mind that the photos I posted show the selection on labor day weekend (the very end of the season). My housemate and I were having trouble finding perennial grasses anywhere this late in the season. Somebody suggested Old Vally Farms to me so I went out and had a look. The selection was Incredible, and not just grasses, everything. The marked prices were the best I had seen all year and I was pleased to find that everything was half off right now! ★★★★★Lonnie Schott5 years ago

Was recently told of this nursery and went out almost immediately because I read on the website that they have the largest selection of day lillies. Well, not only did they have an overwhelming selection of day lillies but other plants also. They have an incredible selection of shade plants including hostas. Overall, their selection of shrubs, perennials and other yard plants is very good...so are their prices. Everything is locally grown which is a real plus when it comes to plant survival rates. The staff is exceptionally knowledgeable, very friendly and helpful. If you're a gardener you have to check them out.
