★★☆☆☆Jackie M3 weeks ago

Visited Wilmore Nursery intending to purchase several plants and garden starts, but unfortunately the customer service fell far short of expectations. As novice gardeners, we came looking for guidance and were prepared to spend money to make the right choices. We asked three staff members for assistance, and aside from one very kind and helpful teenage employee, the adult staff members were unfriendly, dismissive, and uninterested in helping. The checkout experience was equally disappointing. The cashier seemed focused on getting customers through the line as quickly as possible rather than providing service. We had to ask about the plant warranty, and the explanation felt rushed. When we expressed interest in purchasing mulch, she told us, “Since the line is so long, you can always come back.” At that point, there were only a few people waiting and another cashier was already working. I had barely spent 30 seconds looking at the mulch samples before making my selection. What stood out most was the lack of engagement. We weren’t just browsing, we were customers actively looking to buy and seeking expert advice. For a local nursery, this felt like a significant missed opportunity. Better staffing, training, and a stronger focus on customer service would go a long way toward improving the experience.
