★★★★★CA McNeil5 years ago

Wonderful juicy berries. Run by the nicest women: very helpful & down-to-earth. They set you up with a small berry bucket and a generous length of rope to tie it around your waist. Then they'll direct you to the ripest berries of the day. There's a little bit of a walk to get to the bushes, whose high-bush berries start about 4&1/2' up from the ground. Your diligence is highly rewarded in no time with a bucketful of very blue plump berries., which you walk back to the open garage to get weighed as you return your gear. I assume it's cash only, as it's a very small family-run business.
