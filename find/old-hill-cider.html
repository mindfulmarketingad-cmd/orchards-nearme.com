We found this gem driving to West Virginia and it was absolutely worth the stop! The staff was so friendly and knowledgeable about the ciders and were happy to tell us stories of the origins of their ciders. The drinks were delicious and crisp. Go around back for a beautiful view of the town at the bottom of the hill. Worst part about this place was I couldn’t take it home with me to New York!!

★★★★★Jody Thomas4 years ago

We stayed here with Harvest Hosts last night and thoroughly enjoyed it! We arrived and got our camper set up then went up to the greenhouse and did a tasting of all of their ciders. They were so delicious! Our favorites were Virginia Radler, Off The Press and Seasons Finish, we took home a case and 3 bottles to keep enjoying until we can make a trip back. The server did a wonderful job of keeping our tasting running smoothly while also helping other customers. The views here are incredible! We were here on a windy day but sitting inside the greenhouse with all of the windows we were still able to enjoy the stunning views. This place is definitely worth a trip!!
