★★★★★Noah Henyon3 years ago

4/15/2023, Sat: Prospect Hill Orchards sells at the Abingdon Square Park Greenmarket on Saturdays from 8am-2pm most Saturdays of the year, except less often in the winter. The orchard is family owned from the Hudson Valley area of NY State a few hours north of NYC. The Orchard is unique in that they sell NY State organic apples grown with natural fertilizer, and many of the berries such as raspberries, smaller wild type blueberries, currants, and gooseberries are no-spray. The fruit is always very fresh, and there are selections of baked goods that are without any added sugar (sugar-free). You can find selections of jams, granola, cider doughnuts, apple sauce, apple cider, peaches, pears and many other selections from this orchard. It is my favorite orchard in all of the Greenmarkets.
