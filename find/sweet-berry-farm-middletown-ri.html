Reviews

★★★★★Laura Lentza month ago

We stumbled upon this charming farm while looking for a spot to grab a quick lunch, and what a gem we found!! Sweet Berry Farm is a wonderful spot with a farmers market and cafe. The selection of delicious fresh local food is unreal! Pop in for a coffee or tea, breakfast sandwiches, fresh lunch sandwiches, desserts and so much more! There’s indoor and outdoor seating, with the sweetest kid playhouse set up like a farm stand outside to keep the littles entertained. Outside is also a small stand alone shed featuring local artisans jewelry, and crafts. If you are in the area, this is a must visit to put on the list, especially on a beautiful day! Lentz_explore

★★★☆☆Hannah Lee9 months ago

Ambiance: The farm feels more like a charming little shop than a traditional farm. They sell plenty of name-brand foods alongside items made right on the farm, plus lots of cute home decor for purchase. Apple Cider Donuts: Only served on weekends, Apple Cider: Served over ice at the café. Refreshing and sweet, but honestly tasted a lot like grocery store apple juice. Death by Chocolate Cake: $7 and absolutely worth it! A generous slice, super moist despite being pre-sliced from the fridge. The thin layers of buttercream-style chocolate frosting made it rich but not overwhelming. SO YUMMY!!
