Long gravel driveway takes you up the hill to the Peach house on the left. Beautiful place for rustic Peach and blackberry Orchard picking on rolling hills. Great place for kids to come see where their produce comes from.

Lots of fun doing you pick. They provide boxes and explain everything. Fruit is great. They sell pre- picked if you don't want to do the you pick. Location is beautiful. We went in early July. Cash only. Off road drive out to grove may be difficult in a sedan.

★★★★★Christiana Odum12 years ago

I went here after being turned away at Breeden's Orchard because I had a child with me. Pratt's was WONDERFUL! They were friendly and warm to all of us, including the child, and we got some beautiful peaches. This was my young nephew's first experience at an orchard and he loved it! He didn't want to leave!
