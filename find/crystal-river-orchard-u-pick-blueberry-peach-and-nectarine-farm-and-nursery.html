★★★★★Kathryn Alkirea year ago

A Hidden Gem for Berry and Fruit Lovers – Beautiful, Peaceful, and Perfectly Maintained! As an avid u-pick fruit picker across the state, I can confidently say that Crystal River Orchard is one of the best farms I have ever visited. The blueberries were absolutely stunning. They are plump, vibrant, perfectly round, and bursting with flavor. You can immediately tell how much care and attention the owners put into the property. Everything is immaculately maintained, from the healthy, abundant fruit to the well-maintained picking rows. One thing that really stood out to me was how accessible and family-friendly the orchard is. There is no tall grass to wade through or uneven ground to trip over. The fruit is conveniently located close to the parking area, with bushes spaced closely together, making it easy to access without a lot of walking. The wide, stroller-friendly aisles would make it easy for families with young children to enjoy the experience. And to top it off, there were no bugs or ant hills to deal with, which made the picking even more pleasant. After spending time in the Florida sun, it was such a treat to step into their small indoor, air-conditioned space to briefly cool off and check out their selection of homemade seasonal jams and spreads. Everything is clearly made with love and care, and I can’t wait to try the blueberry jam I picked up. Overall, Crystal River Orchard is the kind of small business I love to support when passing through town. The folks there are passion-driven, welcoming, and offer an exceptional product and experience. A must-visit during berry season!
