★★★★☆Brian Dible10 months ago

This was an interesting stop while out road tripping the US. We drove past and decided to turn around to check out the place out. It is definitely unique for sure. We enjoy seeing the place even though we wasn't interested in making a purchase. Did notice that the veggies weren't exactly the best looking and the prices were extravagant. They wanted $2.79 per pound for zucchini, and $1.25 for a cucumber.

Reasonable prices, lots of variety, and even the buildings were super cool to look at. Added bonus chickens wondering around.
