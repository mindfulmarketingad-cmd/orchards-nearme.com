Reviews

★★★★★Christopher Johnson8 months ago

Found this old school farmstand by searching for "apple picking" in Google Maps after an accident blocked our road to Baugher's. Great recommendation ... we enjoyed our stop on the way back to Baltimore. We knew there wouldn't be any apple picking, but Carolyn's had a wide variety of fresh produce including our other two fall day trip objectives: beautiful mums (buy 2 large pots for $15) and plump pumpkins. Both were likely grown nearby as there is an active small farm a few hundred feet behind the farmstand. That's where I got a nice view of a healthy pumpkin patch and a batch of potted mums remaining on a harvested flowerbed.
