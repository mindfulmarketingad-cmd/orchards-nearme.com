★★★★☆Eric Boesen3 months ago

Hyline Orchard is a small, charming spot that quietly does its thing. The fruit is fresh, the pies are homemade, and the farm market has plenty of local goodies—even if nothing jumps out as life-changing. Staff are friendly without being overbearing, and the orchard itself is pleasant to stroll through. It’s not flashy, but it has a low-key charm that makes it worth a visit if you’re in Door County. Pick some apples, grab a pie, and enjoy the relaxed pace—it’s the kind of place that quietly grows on you.
