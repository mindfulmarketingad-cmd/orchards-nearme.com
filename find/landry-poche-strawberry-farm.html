★★★★★Lauren Waggoner3 years ago

What a wonderful afternoon we had with the kids!! They have plenty of picnic tables where you can bring a lunch, and some play equipment. We tried going at about 11:45 am, and it was still packed with a school group. So we went and got subway and brought it back about 12:30, and we pretty much had the place to ourselves. So go in the afternoon if you want to avoid school crowds, or call and see if there's any scheduled for that day. The u-pick field is not treated with pesticides, so we were comfortable eating while we picked! (We probably ate as much as we picked between the five of us, they were delicious!!) A wooden tray of 8 pints was $20, or a bucket (6 pint I think) was $13, which we thought was very reasonable. They also had some canned products for sale, we got some beautiful dark honey and strawberry jam! Mrs. Rhonda was SO kind and helpful! It was definitely a "mom & pop" place, and we are happy to support their business! This seemed like a much more enjoyable experience and a better value than the other place up in Albany (we actually planned to go there first, but turned around and left as soon as we saw how insanely crowded it was) 10/10, highly recommend!! 😁🍓
