★★★★☆Stephanie (The Scentarian)2 months ago

Watson’s Nursery has been a place I’ve returned to for many years. It’s truly a beautiful nursery lush, well stocked, and full of inspiration for anyone who loves plants. I can usually find most of what I’m looking for, and walking through the space always feels like a peaceful escape. That said, I’m giving 4 stars due to a few frustrating experiences. The prices do run on the higher side, and I recently had an online order canceled for the Arborose Climber ‘Quicksilver,’ even though it still showed as available online. When I asked about it, I received two different explanations from staff, which was disappointing and confusing. It’s also stating it’s still available online which will lead to more frustrated customers . I’ve also noticed that while some employees are incredibly knowledgeable and helpful, others seem less certain. There have been times I’ve been given incorrect information about plant care, especially when there wasn’t a care sheet available. When you’re trying to make the right decision for your plants, consistency really matters. Overall, Watson’s is a gorgeous nursery with a great selection, and I’ll continue to visit but I do hope to see improvements in communication and consistency moving forward.
