★★★★★Andres M8 months ago

I went to this farm last weekend. It’s a nice place to spend a different kind of day, especially on a Sunday. They have animals, and different sizes of pumpkins to see and buy (a bit expensive around $25 for a big one). The small pumpkins are really cute to look at, and it’s fun to walk around the long fields full of mini pumpkins. There’s also a cafeteria with a variety of food, another area for kids’ activities, and even a tractor ride. Honestly, there isn’t much to do if you go without kids, but overall, the place is very well organized, parking is easy, and entry is only $5 per car!

★★★★☆Vinay Patela year ago

We went to Berry Patch Farms today and found it to be very affordable and fun experience. The entrance fee was just $5 which includes haywagon ride around the farm. Plenty of free parking available. They had goats, pigs and chickens in the coop. A small play area for kids with swing and a slide. Couple of shops selling boiled peanuts ($5), pumpkin flavored ice cream ($5) and a hotdog ($3) stand and a lady doing face tattoo ($3) for kids. They also have a horse wagon carousel ($3) and corn maze ($2) which was okay with the kido had fun. They had a large selection of pumpkin available for picking and for all shapes and sizes. I felt like you can find a pumpkin for cheaper at Wal-Mart or Costco thou. Mini pumpkin were just $2. Its a good experience visiting this place and I highly recommend this to anyone looking to spend couple of hours T the pumpkin patch.
