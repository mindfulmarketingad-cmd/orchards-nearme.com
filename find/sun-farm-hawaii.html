Our visit for the farm tour was a mixed experience. The food and beverages were very flavorful and well-prepared; however, we felt the overall presentation and variety were quite minimal, as the meal was served in paper plates and bowls. Regarding the tour, we had hoped to learn more about the farm's agricultural and regenerative practices. The tour primarily functioned as a walkthrough identifying plants currently growing on the property, which was less detailed than what we had hoped. While we understand that farm offerings can change based on the season, our experience didn't align with the imagery presented on the website and social media. We think the experience would benefit from more alignment between the promotional marketing and the actual visit.
