Reviews

★★★★★Kavika Faleumu2 years ago

What a wonderful orchard! We went during their fall festival. There were plenty of games and things to do. One of my favorites was the apple sling. They had a small menu of lunch items and drinks outside. They also had a larger selection of goods inside - we got some donuts of different flavors; some apple cider and some pumpkin 😋 We wrapped up with a good bunch of apples. You pay by the pound. Great weather today too! Would recommend, especially if you have small children.

★★★★☆Amber J.2 years ago

Plenty of Apples and a cute little orchard overall. They had delicious Carmel apple shakes and fantastic whiskey ciders. I do wish the items in their store were sourced from the farm but most items were from Wisconsin so they pass the vibe check. Also the only food ready to eat on site wasn’t very good. They had frozen pizza and popcorn. Neither were outstanding. I’m used to orchards having fresh pie slices and apples with Carmel drizzle as the least of their food offerings and this place could easily turn that the other direction. That being said it was the beginning of the season. I’d give them another go. Would love to see some fresh pie slices and maybe some warm soup on the menu. They do have a rather cute kiddie area and word on the street is they provide some fun weekend entertainment.
