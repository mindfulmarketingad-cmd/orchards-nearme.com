★★★★★Hope Martza month ago

So cute and welcoming to families. Lots of space to play and be a kid but plenty of fields so you don’t feel overwhelmed by other kids or patrons. We went the weekend after their strawberry festival and there were still SO MANY strawberries! Pricing is also done by the size of your bucket rather than by weight, so there’s no surprises about what you pay. The staff in the barn store did a great job of explaining how to identify the best strawberries, pick them properly, and find the best location to pick in the fields. So beginner and family friendly and made a great strawberry shortcake later!
