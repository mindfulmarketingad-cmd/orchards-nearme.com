Great local nursery with excellent knowledge. Not 5 stars because once again they do everything possible to take shortcuts with Caras bucks and coupons. Spent $117 and used a $20 coupon. Got 3 Cara’s bucks, not 9. Last year I used Caras bucks and spent $180 using 30 Cara’s bucks to make it $150. Returned a $17 item and they gave me back $10 saying Cara’s bucks were used. Love this place, but they’re already more expensive than box stores. We try to always support local, but not sure it’s worth spending more $$ for everything to constantly be short changed at every corner.

★★★☆☆Andrew Harbor5 years ago

They have everything under the sun here, but it’s all so, so expensive. I’d say that quality is worth the price but there were quite a few tragically neglected plants. It seems like they need more staff for their enormous collection of plants, many of which require special care. I did discover that the “jade plant” I’d been gifted was actually a Portulacaria afra aka spekboom aka elephant bush. A lot of plants are mislabeled, though, which led to my very first houseplant death 😢. Oh well.
