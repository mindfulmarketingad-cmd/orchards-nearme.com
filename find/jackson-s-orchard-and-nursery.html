★★★★★solmaza year ago

We visited Jackson’s Orchard & Nursery for a fun day of apple picking, and overall, it was a great experience! We paid $3 each for a hayride, which added a nice touch to our outing. The farm features a corn maze, pumpkin patch, and plenty of apple trees. Unfortunately, when we went, the apple picking wasn't as fruitful as we hoped, as many trees were picked over. However, we did buy a bag of apples for $5, which was a fair price. Despite the apple situation, the kids had a blast exploring the farm, and it was a wonderful family day out. There were lots of activities and various items for sale, making it a great spot to spend time together. I’d recommend it for families looking for a fun day in the outdoors!
