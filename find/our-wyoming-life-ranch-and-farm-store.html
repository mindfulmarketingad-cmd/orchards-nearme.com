Reviews

★★★★★Davida year ago

Through HarvestHost we were able to secure the reservation. Jeff was the ranch hand who received us and provided us an adapter cord to supply our 30a Rv with electricity. Their RV park didn’t appear to have a water hookup but we didn’t need it. The shop is a small and homey. Items sold were root veggies, honey, honey wax, dressings, many kinds of jerky, many kinds of frozen soup, some O.W.L. apparel, and frozen pork and beef of all sorts. We purchased two steaks and a pack of bratwurst. These three items totaled to around $45. Kindly note the prices are market-based. Thank you Jeff for all your help. I am certain the O.W.L. recognizes your hard work to keep the ranch and farm running smooth throughout the wintertime. God Bless You!
