Reviews

★★★★★Charbee Cappsa month ago

The perfect weekend road trip destination. Pretty country roads carry you to this quaint farmer's market/ice cream stand where you can admire beautiful views while you relax in their bench swing gazebo and enjoy your delicious ice cream and the fresh country air. We found lots of cool stuff in the gift shop. I don't usually buy stuff in places like this, but everything was priced right, so we splurged a little. The building and grounds were well kept and immaculate, both inside and out. Each of the strawberries from the bunch we bought looked and tasted story-book perfect. All in all, we had a wonderful visit.
