★★★★★Kris Hand4 years ago

I finally had the chance to take my kiddos here and it was such a fun experience! It’s only a short drive (maybe 20 min) from Lebanon or Camdenton, Missouri. GPS found it easily. The owners/staff are super nice and helpful. There’s snacks/drinks on site along with a public restroom. I love that they are open once early in the day and then they open back up once school lets out. It makes this an easy field trip for me to take my kiddos on after 3pm. These are by far, the juiciest and most flavorful strawberries I’ve ever had in my life. If I had known how good they were going to be, I would’ve been here long ago!! We bought over 11lbs of strawberries and I still don’t think it was enough for my kids!!🤣 Thank you IronGate for such a great experience.
