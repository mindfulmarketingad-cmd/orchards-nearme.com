★★★★★highflyingnote2 years ago

I went to Shady Hill on April 29th, 2024, with my wife to look for some lettuce plants and seed packets. After parking, we were greeted by a Fairyland like appearance ... the place was beautiful! It was easily obvious that someone had worked their tails off to make this place look so nice; I felt like I was at someone's house! There were so many beautiful flowers, arranged by color and styles. We were there for lettuce, and at the back of the property, on tables, we saw six-packs of young lettuce plants, beautifully arranged for sale. We grabbed a six-pack, then went to a small shed like house for our seed packets. Man, this place has everything! My wife, who is the shopper, said that the prices beat the other nursery in Manchester. There were plenty of friendly and helpful staff on hand, although we didn't ask for any assistance. I did tell someone I saw in one of the green houses what an amazing job they did! We will be back at some point to buy other plants for our garden. I just wanted to throw in my two cents on my experience at Shady Hill Nursery and Gardens!
