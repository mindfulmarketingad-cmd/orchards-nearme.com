★★★★☆Jessi Mithun8 months ago

I think kids enjoy it. But...you pay $9 to get in, then $5 to hit golf balls into a pond, $8 for laser tag, $5 for mini golf, $5 to pull a paintball trigger at a target, $5 to launch mini pumpkins at targets, $3 for a hayride, then 50 cents per pound for a pumpkin. So you're looking at nearly $50 per person. There are some free activities tho. Mini zipline, large barrels to walk in, pedal cart track, oversize chess/checkers, corn kernel pit, large slides, corn maze.
