Reviews

★★★★★Bethanny Lahey (Fig House)5 months ago

Wonderful picking experience. Call ahead if the weather has been cold and then arrive EARLY for the best picking. Berries are large and very sweet. Walking and picking are easy with wide rows and provided carrying pails. Great thing to do with 6+ year old kids. Younger needs supervision to not trample fruit. Plenty of parking and a large seating area under oak trees (bring a folding chair or blanket) if the group needs to be split. Only word of caution is Sumter county rd 104. If your vehicle doesn’t have a lot of ground clearance it may scrape on the raised railroad crossing next to 35. Mustangs beware.

★★★★★Casual Explorer2 months ago

Pick your own blueberries, strawberries, and blackberries in incredibly manicured fields. Very friendly and helpful staff. Reasonable prices for easy picking strawberries , blueberries, and blackberries. Also strawberry onions are sold and grown on site. Check their website for hours and in season berry availability. A small selection of berry related products (such as jellies and jams) are also available. A great place!

★★★★☆Kim Bell5 years ago

Layered out very nicely for picking. The berries were big, beautiful and plentiful. My only complaint is the berries themselves, they don't have that really deep, rich, strawberry taste. It's a fun day to pick your own and I would do it again next season.
