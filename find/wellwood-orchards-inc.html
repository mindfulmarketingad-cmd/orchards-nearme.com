★★★★★Lisa from NHa year ago

I’m a sucker for anything family-oriented, and this place is just that! Apple picking season is such a special event here in New England, and Wellwood Orchards offers not only apple picking but also a petting zoo, a small store that serves fresh-cooked donuts, and hand-pressed cider. In addition to apples, the orchards also offer peaches, and they often have strawberries available as well. They frequently host fun little festivities, so it’s a good idea to check their website every so often, especially during the summer. It really is a special place with plenty of parking, making it very easy to navigate. They even provide tractor rides to and from your car, taking you to the tops of the orchards’ hills. There’s no fee to park, and during busy times, there are staff members available to help you navigate the parking lot. There are also great photo opportunities with cutouts and pumpkins during the fall. Almost every time I’ve been there, they’ve had a food truck or two. My grandchildren have always enjoyed this area!
