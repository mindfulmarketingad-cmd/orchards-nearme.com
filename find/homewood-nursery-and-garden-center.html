★★★★★Shanah Bella year ago

We've been talking about checking this nursery out for awhile, as it keeps coming up in conversation. So we finally made it out there and are so happy we did. This place is HUGE! It's actually a bit overwhelming, to be honest. So it's a good thing they have plentiful staff wandering around to assist when requested. They have a wide selection of basically everything in the plant kingdom. They also have all of the extra things you could need to fully incorporate your plants into your living space. The prices are a little bit higher than some of the other nurseries we've visited, but it just depends upon which product, as it's not across the board. Overall, a good find and we'll definitely be back.

★★★★★Hollya year ago

It's a beautiful nursery with a ton of different things to see. The outside section is vast and well labeled so it's easy to find what you're looking for, and there's multiple different rooms inside to explore. We forgot her chair so it was a challenge. The greenhouse was so hot that she got overheated and we had to leave very quickly, so I didn't get to experience as much as I would have liked.
