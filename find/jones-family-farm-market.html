★★★★★LadySaint7 months ago

Jones Family Farm – Edgewood, Maryland Jones Family Farm at 2107 Philadelphia Road in Edgewood, Maryland,21040 is truly a local treasure. This quaint, family-run farm has been serving the community since 1951, and you can feel that legacy the moment you arrive. The atmosphere is warm, the staff is friendly, and the entire place has that charming, down-to-earth feel that makes you want to stay awhile. They offer a wide variety of products—fresh meats, beef, eggs, and so much more. During the holiday season, the farm turns into a festive wonderland filled with Christmas trees, wreaths, ornaments, and beautiful holiday décor. Everything is high-quality and thoughtfully arranged. I genuinely love it here and will absolutely be back to support this local gem. If you’re in the Edgewood area, definitely check them out! Definitely Lady Saint Approved 🫡😇
