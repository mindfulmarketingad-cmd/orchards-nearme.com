★★★★☆Shannon Baldwin4 years ago

Great blueberry picking. It's a bit in the middle of nowhere but certainly a fun drive to enjoy. There is plenty of parking and 2 different areas to pick at. Each area has 2 variety of blueberries. The blue berry patches are covered in netting which makes the expierence very nice. There was no bugs or birds and the bushes were well organized and easy to pick. The only part that was confusing was where to pay. So there is a large barn next to the main patch. The spot to pay is in the barn and the opening is on the opposite side of the blueberry patch. In there there are buckets you can use to pick and return once your done. The blue berries where 3.50 a pound. Overall the picking was easy and setup really nice will certainly return to pick more berries before the season is over.

★★★★★Angela Barthauer2 years ago

Great place for pick your own blueberries. In my research, they are the best price per pound. It's very rural, expect a drive, but if you check their Facebook page, you'll know hours of operation and see photos of the best time to go. The owners are helpful and friendly and spoke of a desire to keep their prices reasonable. July 2023, they were $4/lb for U-pick when other farms were charging $7/lb. Again, follow their FB page for current pricing. I went early in the season and it took two of us about an hour to pick 4.5 lbs. If you go at the peak of the season, the bushes are so loaded, you could probably cut that time in half. I'll definitely be going back.
