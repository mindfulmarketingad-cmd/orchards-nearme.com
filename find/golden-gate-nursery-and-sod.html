★☆☆☆☆V N3 months ago

I’ve been going to this place for a long time. On several occasions there were problems with the plants—diseased or infestation. They don’t warrant their plants so no returns or refunds. They deferred to the pesticide section where I am welcome to buy the product to treat the infestation issue. Unfortunately, I keep coming back because it is right in Naples and is very convenient. However, I stopped by last week and took a look at the nursery as a whole—not just the the plant I’m specifically looking for, but rather browser around. Couldn’t help but notice how rough many of the plants were. They looked diseased or dried out with splotchy leaves, black spot, yellowing, etc. One of the aisles had sprinklers scheduled, couldn’t help but notice the hoses were not setup for water to go into the pipes as designed. No wonder a lot of the plants were malnourished and drying out. Such oversight could be likened to mistake or maintenance needed; however, it is important to note, this is a nursery and it’s their specialty to care for the plants. If you end up going here for whatever reason, I recommend you look at the plant and see if it’s in good health, such as vibrant green leaves, not yellowing or signs of unwanted insects. I was too trusting. I was tired of settling and finally ventured out. I am so glad I drove the extra 30 minutes to Estero or Bonita Springs nurseries for better selection and service. The other nurseries were also able to do custom orders in case they don’t have it in stock. Thats what I’m used to, but Golden Gate Nursery does not. Farewell.
