Reviews

★★★★★Diane Shupp4 years ago

Best blueberry farm in NJ! We drive over an hour to pick blueberries here twice a year. Besides having great prices, the staff are incredibly friendly. They also sell blueberry related products, such as honey and jam. They provide the buckets for you and will drive you in a wagon to the blueberry rows. If you don’t want to pick your own, blueberries are also available in pints or 10 pound boxes. We like supporting a local farm and we love blueberries!

★★★★★Mark Melin3 years ago

Awesome U-pick blueberry farm!!! Available for about three weeks at the end of June/early July. Duke, Blue Crown and Elisabeth varieties all available. Cute store for honey, syrup, etc. Porta-pottys on site. It’s a real farm, not an agri—tourist trap. Though they do take you out on a wagon! Been coming here happily since 2007.

★★★★★Karen Allen2 years ago

Always wonderful time. Bushes are laden with berries. They provide buckets,bags, tractor rides out and back. The staff are sweet and helpful. Always a delight to meet new friends while picking, and share recipes!!
