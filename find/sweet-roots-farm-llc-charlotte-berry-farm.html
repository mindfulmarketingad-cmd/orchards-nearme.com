Sweet Roots is my favorite farm stand in the area. They have an excellent selection and EXCELLENT HOURS! You could stop there and actually get everything you need to make a meal as they stock produce, breads, dairy, meats, dry goods, etc. Obviously places like this are expensive but they keep it within reason (Philo ridge is currently charging 53 for a whole chicken 😳) I could go on and on, I love this place.

★★★★★Charlotte A.a year ago

A year round farm-stand with a fabulous selection of locally made cheeses, yogurts and specialty foods. The produce selection is broad even in depths of winter, Farmer and Owner Jane does an amazing job making every visit to the store a treasure hunt for good food. Summer blueberries and seasonal creemees 🍦from the berry crop are the icing on the Sweet Roots cake. Friendly, welcoming store and a community treasure.

★★★★★Ddful Full3 years ago

This is the best farm stand we have visited on our tour of the Burlington-Charlotte, Vermont area. We live in Northern California where the farm stands can also be vastly different with excellent ones standing out head and torso above the rest. Sweet Roots has an incredible array of goat cheeses, including one with saffron called Fool's Gold as well as some of the best local jams we've found. We missed berry season but will definitely try to come back to pick blues before this lifetime ends.

★★★★★Dan Putler4 years ago

Sweet Roots is a great place for both locally grown produce and for local information for "flat-landers" like my wife and I. We shopped there multiple times in our 6 weeks in the Champlain Valley, and were always pleased with there produce. One of the staff there also pointed us to the Back Door Bakery. This combination, along with a few other things we learned allowed us to discover a true "farm to fork" Vermont experience.
