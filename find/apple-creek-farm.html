★★★★★Jonathan O'Konek8 months ago

A cute and cozy cottage getaway right outside of Bismarck, North Dakota! My family and I were fortunate enough to stay at this wonderful place, which sits on a 40 acre alpaca farm. It was great fun watching, feeding, and petting the alpacas—who were not only very friendly, but also very entertaining. The cottage had nice amenities (kitchen, living room, TV with all kinds of channels, etc.) and was stocked with fun games and books for my kids to play and read. And the owner and the people who work on the farm are extremely kind, accommodating, and friendly. I highly recommend that you schedule a visit and take time to browse their alpaca store, which contains neat alpaca apparel!
