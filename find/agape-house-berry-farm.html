★★★★★SoyKibble L9 years ago

Great place! I was able to take my little dog with me after I called ahead. The owner has a larger dog on the farm that free roams so if you're gonna bring a dog with you, call them ahead of time. The berries themselves were pretty good. Most of the smaller, ripe ones were very sweet with just a little bit of tartness. The larger berries tended to be a bit more sour. However, there is a HUGE difference in flavor between fresh picked strawberries and the ones you buy in the store. Store bought ones tend to taste like solidified lemon juice. These strawberries were flavorful and a mix of sweet and tart. It was $6 per pound of strawberries. The blackberries, I was told, aren't going to be ready until Early June. The field itself was about a dozen rows of strawberry plants. I was able to through each row and pick the berries that I wanted. I was given a bucket and a plastic bag to store them in.
