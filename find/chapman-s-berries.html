★★★★★Patricia Huynha month ago

First year picking blueberry was so much fun! Great activity for whole family. We even convinced my 26 yrs son to join us. The three of us each got a bucket and started picking in separate rows. After 45 minutes the morning sun was beating down on us so we stopped. When compared buckets my husband did way better with larger berries and more fruits. We ended up with a slightly less than a bucket. At $4/lb we paid $18 for a lot of blueberry. See you next season!!

★★★★★Joel Jongco5 years ago

This was so much laid back fun. You get a little black 1/2 gallon bucket and you pick as many blueberries as you want. A full bucket is $20, half is $10, and a quarter bucket is $5. We picked a little over a quarter and not quite a half so we were only charged $6. On the way out, we noticed they also sold assorted lawn ornaments and they were available painted or unfinished so you can paint them yourself. They also sold fresh eggs from a friend’s farm.

Definitely a little gem of a place in Saint cloud. Highly recommend trying out with family and friends.
