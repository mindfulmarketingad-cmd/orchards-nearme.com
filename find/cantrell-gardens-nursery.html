★★★★★Cristina Araujo2 months ago

This place has one of the best selections I've seen in Little Rock! They have 75 varieties of roses and this was the only place I was able to find the variety of hydrangeas I was looking for. Staff was super helpful and knowledgeable. While prices might be a bit higher than big box stores, the quality is far superior. I bought some sweet alyssum recently that smell like heaven and then saw some at a big box store that were almost the same price and had very little scent. If you're someone who wants a specific variety of plant or who is looking for something interesting and needs a lot of options that are high quality, I'd highly recommend stopping by.
