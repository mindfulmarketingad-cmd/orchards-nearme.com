★★★★★C Sherman3 years ago

Courier Orchards is a family-owned Orchard that has been in Merrimack for decades. It's simple, nothing fancy, good prices, and a fun time. They have wagons for families with children. When we visited they were picking McIntosh, honeycrisp, and local Merrimack grown peaches! They have a small store that offers apple butter, some jams, pumpkins, and fresh vegetables and picked fruit for those who don't want to pick your own. The apple cider is great. There is plenty of parking, and the walk to the Orchards is a short 5 minute walk. They accept credit cards.

Those children now adults many with their own children and are returning to Currier Orchards to enjoy the same experience they had.
