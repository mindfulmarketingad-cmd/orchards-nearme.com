Reviews

★★★★★Allie Rogne (Allie in Wanderland)8 months ago

Millstone Creek Orchards absolutely blew my expectations out of the water — and it was well worth the hour drive! This place has something for everyone — adults and children alike — with so many fun seasonal activities, from picking fruit and exploring the grounds to taking part in their Autumn Adventure featuring the most adorable Highland cows 🐮🍂. Their Cider House is another must-do! They serve a great mix of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, including refreshing mocktails. I loved the Peach Moscato frozen drink — it was the perfect treat for one of those fall days that still feel like summer! 😅 Their food menu was solid, but the real showstopper? The apple cider donuts — warm, fluffy perfection. 🍩 I’m already planning a return trip just to try their apple cider float one day! If you’re looking for a cozy, family-friendly destination with small-town charm and big-flavor treats, this is the spot. Definitely a hidden gem for your fall bucket list! 🍁
