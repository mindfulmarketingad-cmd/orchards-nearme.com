Reviews

★★★★★Emma Ryan8 months ago

Our new favorite fall spot! I took my kids here on a Sunday morning in October and was so impressed with all the attractions! It was incredibly clean and well planned. Endless entertainment for a 2 and 5 year old. What really set it apart for me is the care that went into providing shaded areas, the cleanliness and the staff. There are staff at every activity monitoring and multiple offered to take pictures of me with my kids! The staff was genuinely proud of Waterman's farm and excited to make sure families enjoyed it too. There's plenty of space to run and climb. Some of the activities to give you an idea are sandboxes, various playgrounds, a hay bale mountain, corn box (sandbox filled with loose corn) petting zoo, pony rides, tractor tricycles, ball pit, slides, a large dinosaur that eats pumpkins, pumpkin painting, carnival food stands and a hayride out to a pumpkin patch. My kids especially loved the colorful plastic dinosaurs, cows and horses they could sit on. There was an entire carnival ride section that we didn't venture out to. Plan to spend an entire day!

★★★★★Morgan Hitea year ago

We came here to attend the “Big Bounce America 2024” for the world record largest bounce house!! So cool that this farm held such a huge event. So many people and parking was no issue whatsoever. I will say it was very hot this day and there is no shade but it was worth it. This farm is a hidden gem right off the interstate! Would love to go back for future activities they might have.
