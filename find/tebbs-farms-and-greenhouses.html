Reviews

★★★☆☆Megan Roloson2 weeks ago

I visited this greenhouse twice this season while working on our yard, and unfortunately I don’t think I’ll be returning. The first time I stopped in, I was looking for something specific and didn’t have the best experience. The staff felt pretty dismissive when I asked about it, and I got the impression that it was somehow my fault for not coming earlier in the season. This was frustrating, especially considering we still had a hard frost in May this year, so many people were understandably waiting to plant. The selection was okay — not huge, but given the size and location, that part was understandable. I also found the prices to be about 10–15% higher than other locations I checked for the same types of items I was looking for. About a month later, I decided to give them another chance. I wasn’t looking for anything specific this time, just hoping to maybe find a few end-of-season deals. The greenhouse was packed, parking was chaotic, and the inside felt extremely hectic. Their hanging baskets were beautiful, but not really what I was looking for. While the staff wasn’t dismissive on the second visit, they were clearly overwhelmed and rushing around. I do appreciate the effort to keep lines moving, but both visits left me feeling like there really wasn’t much customer service or help available. Overall, between the higher pricing, limited selection, and lack of customer service, I’ll likely choose one of the many other greenhouse options in the Williamsport, Montoursville, and Muncy area in the future.
