Reviews

★★★★★Sam Zizzi7 years ago

One of our family's favorite days of the year is when we can visit this organic blueberry farm. The kids love to wander and pick their own. The vibe is mellow and family oriented. They have a restroom and a shaded area with cool water. Plenty of parking too. Friendly staff that will provide baskets to use ($1 to keep). Bring your own container to save a few bucks. The berries aren't cheap but they taste fantastic. Check their Facebook page for their opening and closing days and times.
