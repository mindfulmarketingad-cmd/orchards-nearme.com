A nice, quaint little farm shop. Prices were mostly reasonable; I got all of this produce for just over $10. They had a nice pick your own deal where one could go out in the garden and pick a good variety of food -tomatoes, different kinds of peppers and squash, eggplant, pumpkins, Swiss chard, cabbage,- for a dollar a pound.
