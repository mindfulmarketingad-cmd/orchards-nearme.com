★★★★★Judy Hinterlong2 months ago

They sell many selections of native plants recommended by our local amazing Zoo. I've read reviews of people saying their prices are too high- well of course they don't have the purchasing power advantage of a gigantic chain like Home Depot or Lowe's. Their plants are always in tip top shape, they've spacious beautiful greenhouses. For the guy that posted plants are free obviously hasn't understood how much potting soil, plastic pots, and diesel fuel to deliver the plants cost. The costs to water, fertilize and inspect for diseases, as well as costs involved in harvesting seeds, cleaning them, or making cuttings and potting up are not cheap either. Their staff is reasonably informed about plants and what's suitable for home gardens. I like to go there because I like supporting local businesses as much as possible. I'd highly recommend this place to anyone wishing to buy beautiful, healthy and hardy plants.
