★★★★★Barry Mckinney3 weeks ago

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ So much bigger than it looks!Don't let the exterior fool you—Applacres is huge once you step inside! It has the ultimate nostalgic charm, right down to the old refrigerators they use to display and sell their fresh produce. The aromas of baked goods and fruits are amazing, and the free popcorn greeting is such a sweet touch. They have a great selection of jams, jellies, and treats, and they even share free produce by the door so nothing goes to waste. We love this place and will absolutely be returning!

★★★★★Richa year ago

This a really neat local place. They have year round produce and seasonal rotating specialties. We ate their Carmel apples, preserves, popcorn, Carmel corn, apples. It was great home made stuff. The staff was very happy to answer our questions about the products, taking time out of what they were doing to talk about how the stuff was made and what went into the food they had on the shelves. They don't do online ordering so you are going to have to come here in person. The community is worth the trip in though.

★★★★★Bonnie Morgana year ago

I go every summer to pick their gorgeous blackberries. Way better than anything found at the store, and better prices. My son asks to get cherry cider shakes on a regular basis, and they have carried the best cantaloupes I have ever eaten. The owner has even been kind enough to give me advice on caring for my fruit trees at home. I love this place and hope it never changes!
