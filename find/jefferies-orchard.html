★★★★★Matt Wilhite3 years ago

While camping at a nearby State Park, I decided to try to find a nearby orchard to get some seasonal apples and other produce. I am so pleased that my search brought me to the Jeffries Orchard. As I pulled into the parking lot of the orchard, I was immediately taken back to my childhood, visiting the family-owned orchard near my hometown. The first thing I noticed was the absence of pretentiousness and artificial trappings of the season. Then, as I entered the cozy store, I was welcomed by both of the people working in the customer area. The store had a good selection of seasonal produce and other locally made products. Most importantly, however, their apple selection was great. The lady working at the register was very helpful, answering my couple questions and making a couple recommendations. I decided to get a mixed half-peck of "eating apples," (as opposed to cooking apples). All three of the types of apples I picked up were excellent. The most interesting of the apples was a type they called a Russet. I have never run into it in any of the orchards I have visited in the past. It is crisp and has a bold sweet-and-tart flavor that put a real smile on my face. The shopping experience and resulting produce I received were absolutely fantastic. The friendly, helpful staff were very attentive without being intrusive. If you are in the Springfield area, do yourself a favor and checkout Jeffries Orchard. You will not be sorry.
