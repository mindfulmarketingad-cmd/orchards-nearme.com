★★★★★Eric Lopotosky10 months ago

Watson's Nursery in Olympia is a true gem for garden enthusiasts and casual visitors alike. Nestled in a beautifully landscaped setting, the nursery offers a peaceful, welcoming environment that makes you want to linger and explore. The grounds are immaculately maintained, with winding pathways, charming displays, and a sense of serenity that makes every visit feel like a retreat. One of the standout features of Watson’s is its impressive selection of healthy plants, shrubs, and trees. Whether you're looking for colorful annuals, hardy perennials, fruit trees, or native evergreens, you'll find an abundance of well-cared-for specimens. Their greenhouse is equally impressive, stocked with lush houseplants and succulents, while the outdoor areas showcase everything from ornamental grasses to flowering shrubs. In addition to plants, Watson’s offers a wide range of gardening accessories, pottery, tools, and tasteful outdoor décor. The layout is intuitive and inspiring, making it easy to envision how items will look in your own garden. What truly sets Watson's apart, however, is the staff. Friendly, approachable, and incredibly knowledgeable, they are always eager to offer advice or answer questions. Whether you're a seasoned gardener or just starting out, you'll feel right at home at Watson’s Nursery. Highly recommended for quality, beauty, and expert care.
