★★★★★Leslie Smith5 years ago

Oh my, the fantastic Thunder Muffins! I had them first at the Best of Missouri Market in St. Louis years ago, and made it a point while traveling through Southwestern Missouri recently to stop at the farm and get them direct. They are just the most perfect decadent, incredible, tasty, moist, beautiful thing! They also have many other items at their store (in addition, you can pick berries during berry season), and I got some blackberry lemonade, which was delicious, and some of the honey that their own bees made! They had freeze dried blueberries, coffees, jams and jellies, mushroom items, but, for me, it was all about the muffins. It was an undeniably beautiful drive down some country roads to get there. If you are anywhere close to Branson or Springfield, make it a point to pop in, especially in June when I believe the berries are at their peak (I was here and early May, so the berry vines were just flowering).
