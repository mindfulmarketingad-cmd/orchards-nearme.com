Reviews

★★★★★Shane Millera month ago

Great, family-friendly place. There's the market and small playground, where you wait to get picked up for the fruit picking. It can be a bit pricey, but it's a full experience and well worth it. Down the hill from the establishment is a grassy park area with more parking and a petting zoo with goats. They have a culvert pipe slide that children love too. I've been coming here for years and have always enjoyed this orchard.

★★★★★Myer “Mymy”8 months ago

First time visiting this farm and my family and I enjoyed ourselves. They have a petting zoo, multiple playgrounds for the kids, vendors, farmers market and face painting. Everything at the market and entrance to the fields are nicely priced. Strollers, bags, and wagons are not allowed in the field and they offer an area to leave them. You definitely will need that stroller for your little one when you return . These apples were so delicious. I’m never buying them from the grocery store again. The food at the vendor trucks was actually good. Can’t wait to go back when it gets warmer to pick additional fruit.

★★★★☆Joan Dawson7 months ago

Took a friend for a day trip from the DC suburbs. We both had half cheese steak sub, which was tasty. I didn't realize it until the bill came but the fries were extra. She had onion rings (3 of them in the serving). Fries were real good. Had banana pudding ice cream for dessert - best ice cream I ever had! Went to the market on site. Great prices on produce. Drove down to the petting zoo at the farm, which was a very pleasant experience. Would suggest better signage for the zoo and handicap parking. Otherwise, we had a real nice outing.
