This place is AMAZING! Apparently it’s been around since the 80’s, but I only just heard about them a few weeks ago! My husband, our son, and I visited them Saturday morning and I left with 5 new plants, a philodendron white princess, a philodendron micans, an Adansonii, and TWO monstera deliciosa’s. They were all priced spectacularly! Way better prices than your typical big box store! The staff/management was incredible as well! Very helpful and kind. I can’t wait to go back in a few weeks to pick up some new babies! 💚

So if you are looking for a landscaping contractor who knows about plants this is your place. The new parts of Longfellows showcases the hard scapes they can do. I miss the variety of plants, some plants were hard to grow in Missouri, but some of us like the challenge. Two ADA bathrooms. Lovely place to listen to the birds and enjoy the surroundings.
