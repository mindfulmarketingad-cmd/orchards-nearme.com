I had an alright experienc at Parsons Farm with my family. It was nice seeing a wide amount of produce and other things available to purchase. I also like how the little one can see and pet different animals that normally you can't see often. My one complaint and question is why your horse, Fiona, is so skinny. I understand that winter was just over, and it's harder to keep weight on animals during the colder months. I just don't understand why all the other animals had good weight on them while I can see her ribs and hips. Besides this, I saw her in a small paddock with little grass. I do not know if you rotate her and the cow into the larger paddock. But she did not have any water or hay that I could see. This was disappointing in care and allowing visitors who know little about horses to think this is okay and normal to see.
