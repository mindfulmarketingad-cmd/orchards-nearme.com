★☆☆☆☆Petera year ago

Don't assume, like I did, that the landscapers at Faltz know what they're doing. My yard had a twenty-five-year-old timber retaining wall that had reached its expected end of life. I planned to replace it with something more durable, like concrete blocks. A landscape architect from Faltz recommended a stacked stone wall, and I agreed. A month and thousands of dollars later I had a beautiful wall, for awhile. With each freeze thaw cycle the wall moved. Five years later it was clearly failing. When I contacted Faltz, the owner and the landscape architect looked at the wall and essentially said that failures occasionally occur. They speculated that adding drainage might help. Faltz didn't offer to repair it. I hired a civil/structural engineer to give an opinion. He cited several construction mistakes (for example, stones that are too small, lack of gravel below and behind the wall, absence of geotextile fabric, and absence of drainage tubing). I got estimates from other landscape contractors, and they cited many of the same design mistakes. Now I have to pay to have a five-year-old stacked stone wall removed, and pay to build a new wall.
