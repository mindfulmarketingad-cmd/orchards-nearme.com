Mahoney's in Winchester is huge. Excellent selection of houseplants. They were a little light on alocasias and anthuriums but fully stocked on everything else. I love that they had tiny plants for $4, so you can have a lot of little unique plants for not much money. The price for houseplants was not bad and they are very healthy plants. Many were also different from the houseplants we saw at their Falmouth and Oserville stores, too. They had a great clearance section, too. They also had a huge selection of pots.
