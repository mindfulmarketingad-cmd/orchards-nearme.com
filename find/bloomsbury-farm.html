Reviews

★★★★★Christina Zigliotto3 months ago

Bloomsbury Farm is such a special place located between Franklin and Nashville and has truly become my go-to for fresh, organic, thoughtfully grown food. It’s not always easy to find organic options in this area, and the quality of what they grow really stands out. Everything feels seasonal, local, and genuinely connected to the land. I especially love their Friday Farm Days, like all the farm's events, they're so fun... and such a beautiful way to experience the property while picking up produce, flowers, and other farm offerings. Their CSA program is also incredible and makes it easy to stay stocked with fresh, nutrient-dense ingredients throughout the growing season. What makes Bloomsbury even more meaningful is that it’s family-owned and you can truly feel the heart behind what they’ve created. It’s clear they’ve built more than just a farm — they’ve built a real community. From the welcoming atmosphere to the thoughtful events they host throughout the season, everything feels warm, inviting, and memorable. The property itself is absolutely dreamy, with open green space and beautiful flower fields that make it an ideal setting for gatherings. It would be the perfect backdrop for a wellness event, celebration, or any thoughtfully planned experience of your own. I also love that they’ve expanded into educational offerings like their farm school, creating opportunities for people of all ages to connect with nature and local agriculture. Bloomsbury Farm is such a gem in the Nashville area and a place I always look forward to returning to.
