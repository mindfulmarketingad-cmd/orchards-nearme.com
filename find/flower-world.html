★★★★★Zeynep M (Z.M.)4 weeks ago

Flower World is a really fun place to visit, especially if you love plants. They have so many options, it can honestly feel a little overwhelming in the best way. I recommend grabbing one of the maps they have in different sections, especially if you are looking for something specific or just want to understand where everything is. Give yourself at least two hours because there is a lot to walk through. Also, don’t miss the park right next to it — it’s a nice little extra stop. The staff was very friendly and helpful, and make sure to enroll in their discount program if you shop there. Also, stop by Maltby Produce Market while you’re there!
