★★★★★Peter J. DiRoccoa year ago

We’ve been coming regularly to Frankie’s Nursery for years just for their fresh fruit, (and occasionally to buy fruit trees, a lemon, lime, starfruit, chico) and it never disappoints! They offer an incredible selection of high-quality tropical fruit, along with rare and grafted fruit trees for those looking to grow their own. Now, they’ve made things even more exciting by selling their own amazing patented pineapple plant! The staff is always friendly, knowledgeable, and happy to help. Whether you’re a longtime grower or just looking for delicious fruit, this nursery is a fantastic place to visit.
