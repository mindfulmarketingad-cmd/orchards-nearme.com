★★★☆☆Makayla Vincenta year ago

My family recently visited Pippin Orchard. The cost was $25 for a large bag of pick-your-own assorted apples. About half of the trees had fruit on them, but none of the trees were labeled. So we weren't sure which kinds of apples we found. The trees that had apples were in pretty good condition. The location itself was large enough, so visitors has room to space out and weren't on top of each other. Their country-style store didn't have a whole lot. They had different fresh produce offerings, some dairy products, a variety of meat, some homemade pies. Nothing was really appealing to get for kids, which was the reason for the family outing. They were hoping to see things like caramel, chocolate or candied apples, seasonal muffins/doughnuts, candies or little sweet treats. That was a bit disappointing. They did have a small fenced off area with some animals. That was pretty cute. They looked well loved and cared for, which was a great highlight. Overall, it wasn't a bad experience. It was just okay.
