Reviews

★★★★★Noah Henyon2 years ago

Prospect Hill Orchards comes to the GrowNYC Tribeca Greenmarket 8am-2pm on Saturdays year round with fresh berries including blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries, many of which are no-spray in the summer as well as peaches and cherries. The orchard is family owned for over 200 years and known for their apples, and one of the few orchards coming to NYC farmer's markets with organic NY State apples that use clay as a growing practice and natural fertilizer. Prospect Hill also sells granola with organic oats, cider donuts and baked goods including a sugar-free apple hand pie and sugar-free oatmeal raisin cookie.
