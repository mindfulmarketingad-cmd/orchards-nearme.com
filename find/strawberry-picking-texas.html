Strawberry Picking in Texas: What You Need to Know

Texas is home to a range of spots for strawberry picking, spread across the state's different growing regions. This page rounds up every strawberry picking location listed in Texas so you can compare distance, ratings, and reviews before deciding where to go.

Strawberry Picking in the South Central states

Strawberry farms across the South Central states grow in warm, sandy soil, which sets the pace for a season that typically runs April through May. The region's milder spring temperatures push the harvest ahead of most of the country, making it one of the earliest strawberry destinations nationally. Pick-your-own rows tend to open on short notice once the fruit is ready, so checking a farm's current status before visiting is worth the extra step.

Best Time for Strawberry Picking in Texas

April through May, with the earliest berries in South Central states typically leading the harvest and the window closing quickly once temperatures rise.

Tips for Strawberry Picking in Texas

Call ahead or check a location's website before visiting, since hours and availability for strawberry picking can change with the weather and time of year. Weekday visits are usually less crowded than weekends.
