★★★★★William Cain (Old Medic Doc)a year ago

Came to visit and must say you can’t miss the water tower entrance sign. Love it. Drive along the gravel driveway and park. Parking seems limited. Entered the store and was amazed at how much was packed into this little store. Upon entry to the store your hit with comforting cool air and an aroma. There is something for everyone in this store, including an impressive Case knife collection and sales and a coffee shop up front. Staff was friendly and helpful. Went outside and to the right and visited the angora goats and pigs. Was a great visit for our family.
