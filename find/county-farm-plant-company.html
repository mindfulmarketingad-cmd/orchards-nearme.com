★★★★★Jim Johnson3 months ago

County Farm Plant Company is a big, family-run garden center in Baxley, Georgia. They've got over 50 greenhouses and tons of different plants. They specialize in awesome vegetable starts like tomatoes, peppers, and eggplant, plus flowering plants and hanging baskets (they make about 80,000 every year!). You'll also find a huge variety of annuals and perennials there. For garden decor, they have a massive selection of concrete statues, pottery from all over the world, wrought iron stuff, and outdoor furniture. They also offer fruit trees, Boston ferns, palms, and seasonal favorites like mums and poinsettias. Since the place is so big, they even have golf carts for customers to cruise around the greenhouses. And sometimes there are even food trucks on-site!
