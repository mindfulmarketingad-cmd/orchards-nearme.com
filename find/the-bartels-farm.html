★★★★★Alisha Ramireza year ago

Hands down my favorite pumpkin patch in NoCo! I can honestly say that they continually try to improve and add attractions to their pumpkin patch each year. I love how they have the “How Tall This Fall” sign, as my son has taken a picture with it each year and it’s so fun to look back and see how much he’s grown! They have food, snacks and drinks and this year even added an ice cream truck which I thought was great because it can always get so hot especially in early October when we like to go. I like how they have an area for already picked pumpkins or give you the option to go pick your own and the prices are amazing!!! I have went to other patches that charge by the pound and spent 80$ for 3 pumpkins and bartels and I got a couple medium size pumpkins and some tiny pumpkins for 30 bucks! This year at one of their side shops they sold these adorable crochet ghosts and my sons and I loved! Absolutely recommend over and over 100 times 🎃🧡
