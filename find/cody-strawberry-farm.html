★★★★★Jody2 months ago

I visited Cody Strawberry Farm in Richfield, NC with my grandsons, and it was a highlight of our spring. This has become our favorite annual tradition for U-pick strawberries. The boys had a blast picking fresh strawberries, running around the playground, and cooling off with a strawberry slushie. I also made sure to grab a jar of their homemade strawberry jelly—it’s delicious! The staff was incredibly friendly, the parking was easy and accommodating, and the prices are very affordable for families. If you are looking for a great family outing or a fun farm experience in Stanly County, this is it. We will definitely be back for more local strawberries and farm fun soon!
