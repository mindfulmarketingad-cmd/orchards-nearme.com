★★★★★Sanjeeta Paia year ago

This was our first year going to the new location at 4721 Summit Plantation Rd, Hollywood, SC 29449. We have always gone to their farm in their previous location for blueberry picking and have loved it. The new place in Meggett didn't disappoint us. They don't have blue berries yet and won't probably for a few more years but flowers there are gorgeous. We saw sunflowers, zinnias, celosia, gomphrena. We took our own clippers and picked them. We paid by cash. We went a bit late outside of morning house but the owner opened the gates for us. Please make sure you check their hours. Currently 8 am - 10 am and then 5-8 pm in the evenings. Such a beautiful location and very friendly owner.
