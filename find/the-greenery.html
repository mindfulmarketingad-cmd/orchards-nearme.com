★★★★★Lance Ga year ago

The Greenery is as nice a garden center as any you'll find around North Alabama. Sure, Atlanta, Nashville or Birmingham have really good ones, but The Greenery should be considered if you're anywhere in the Tennessee Valley and Huntsville area. There's always plenty of staff who are busy working the expansive grounds, and they're always very knowledgeable and helpful. Staying aware and catching their sales saves my wife and I time going off somewhere to save a buck or two, and that's part of the appeal of how convenient to our area The Greenery is. We highly recommend this awesome plant and garden center. Don't forget their free selection and super nice gift shop.
