★★★☆☆Alissa Elles2 years ago

Never been here I til today, brought a 7 year old & 2 year old. Lots of parking. I would suggest a sign to direct people after parking on where to start. We stood waiting in line at the red barn only to find out that was checkout not the start. Don't recommend strollers or wagons. If walking is difficult for you don't recommend risking the fall. Isles uneven and only wide enough for single file walking. Great experience for children 7 or older, difficult to keep the attention of younger kids. Recommended a bag with hand wipes and something to kneel on. We had so much fun!!! The crop is absolutely wonderful and the juiciest strawberries I've had in a long time.
