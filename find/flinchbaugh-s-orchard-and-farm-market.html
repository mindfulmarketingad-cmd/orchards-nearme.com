★★★★★Beth Schrack7 months ago

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ A Must-Visit Gem! If you’ve never been to Flinchbaugh’s Orchard & Farm Market, you are seriously missing out! This place is an absolute treasure. They have everything—from fresh produce to delicious baked goods to some of the most unique gifts you’ll ever find in a farm market. We picked up a little bit of everything: sugar-free applesauce for my diabetic husband and oil-free salad dressings for my daughter with an oil allergy (love that they have options for everyone!). And yes, a few treats may have slipped into our basket too… apple cider donuts and mini whoopie pies are simply impossible to resist! The chow chow and garlic-stuffed olives didn’t even make it past the front door before getting opened. If you’re on the hunt for a fun or unique gift, this is the place—so many great items you could easily turn into a cute gift bag. And let’s talk about what originally brought me in: glitter drink mixers. I mean… hello?! How fun is that! They’re as adorable and festive as they sound. To top it all off, the owner and staff are incredibly friendly and make the whole experience even better. I can’t recommend this place enough—an absolute 5-star visit from start to finish!

★★★★★Maria Barrera2 months ago

If you are looking for a way to truly embrace spring, Flinchbaugh’s Orchard and Farm Market in Hellam is a must-visit. I recently went for their Tulip Experience, and it was one of the most therapeutic and beautiful days I’ve had in a long time. ​What makes it special: ​The Fields: They have over 200,000 tulips in every color imaginable. Walking through the rows of vibrant reds, purples, and bi-color blooms feels like stepping into a painting. ​The Photo Ops: They have done an amazing job setting up "Instagram-mable" spots that don't feel forced. The butterfly bench is a highlight, and the rustic heart-shaped cutout provides a perfect frame for a souvenir photo. ​Pick-Your-Own: There is something so satisfying about choosing your own bouquet. They even give you tips on how to pull the stems to get the most length for your vase at home! ​The Atmosphere: Even on a busy day, the farm feels open and peaceful. It’s a wonderful spot for a surprise date or a quiet morning of reflection. ​Pro-Tip: Don’t just stop at the flowers! Their farm market and bakery are incredible. I highly recommend picking up some local goodies to take home along with your fresh-cut bouquet. ​Whether you’re a local or driving in from the Reading area like I did, it is worth every mile. It’s more than just a farm; it’s a place to recharge and find a little bit of joy.

★★★★★Carla Peaco8 months ago

Fall is our favorite season! One of our favorite fall activities is visiting local pumpkin patches/corn mazes/apple picking/Fall festivals. We just moved to York a few months ago from south of Baltimore, and our previous farms are a bit far. We searched for local farms offering our favorite activities, and Finchbaugh's came up. We decided to check them out. Boy are we glad we did! Hubby enjoyed the corn maze. We both had fun searching the pumpkin patch for the perfect pumpkins. We followed that up with a lovely wagon ride to their extensive apple orchard, where we picked close to 15 pounds of apples(maybe more). After that, we had to visit the store. So many offerings-pies, cookies, meats, fruits/veggies, juices and ciders, to name a few. Today they had food trucks available, as well as a brewery, a coffee stand and a sweets stand with a delicious cider float! Lots of fun activities for the kiddos. We think we may have found our new Fall fun location!
