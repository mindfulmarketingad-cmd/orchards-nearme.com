★★★★★Chef Matt10 months ago

I’ve been using Lull farm for at home and as a professional chef for over a decade. The Milford farm stand deserves more than just a shout out. We are lucky to have such a well stocked, professionally run, and surprisingly reasonably priced stand in our town. Not only are the produce, breads, jams, pickles, ect phenomenal, but the people working there make the store all the better. The staff has been consistently so kind and welcoming over the years.
