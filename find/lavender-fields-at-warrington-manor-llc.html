★★★★★Arin Kluchnik10 months ago

A visit to Lavender Fields always makes my day better. The property offers a variety scenic backdrops for photography at any skill level. With all due respect if you can’t manage to take a decent picture while there it’s your own dang fault. I am an amateur at best but I have found Lavender Fields to be a selfie WONDERLAND as well as a perfect place to snap adorable photos of my children. Around Easter time I was literally blurting out such things as “peak aesthetics” and “total perfection” as I watched the sweet children frolic amongst the lavender hedgerows and play on the ivory bounce castle with sprawling vines and a mystical labyrinth in the background. My children have learned about nature; specifically entomology and botany while admiring their beautiful gardens. My son ADORES the feathered and furry friends there, he especially loves the goats!!! I must say the staff is always extremely kind and helpful! We are an autism family and we feel so safe and welcome there because of their inclusivity. The Wild Flowers Cafe is quaint. I always feel like I’m being a little fancy when I am treating myself to one of their fresh and flavorful menu items. It’s been a super hot summer and I recently quenched my thirst with a refreshingly floral lavender lemonade while I watched butterflies flitting about the gardens. My daughter enjoyed the cupcakes and she is an extremely fussy and picky eater. Everyone in my home appreciates the lavender hand sanitizer that I always pick up from their gift shop, it smells so good!!! One of these days I am going to treat myself to a tea party that they sometimes host there, or one of their other special events because I always have a ball just visiting on a regular day. I can’t even imagine how delightful a relaxing tea party would be on top of all of the wonderful attributes and offerings at Lavender Fields! I’m definitely a very happy repeat customer.
