Delicious variety of apples and very kind family! We called ahead and they said would be ok to bring our dog along too. Looking forward to see how they grow in the future, we’ll be back!

★★★★☆dipika4 years ago

Reddington's is a small, family run farm with maybe 40 rows of a wide variety apples and a small sunflower patch. They don't use any pesticides which means the apples are fresh and unique, but the orchards are quite buggy and often eaten through. At $3/lb picking here isn't more economical than a grocery store but it is a fun activity and lets you try unique apple varietals. The family who runs this farm is quite friendly and had dreams to open a cidery here which I hope can happen!
