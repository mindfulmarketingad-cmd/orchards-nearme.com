★★★★★Davida year ago

Went here on with some friends and my lady, and we had a wonderful time. You can build your own terrarium from top to bottom. You can choose your container, sand, rocks, plants, decorations, and isopods. Then you place it all yourself, designing it how you like, being guided, and having any questions answered along the way. There's a good selection of different plants, rocks, and sand of various colors, as well as tools to make sure you get the perfect look you want. Austin is super knowledgeable, friendly, and happy to help. It was a great time and definitely something fun to do with a group.
