★★★★★Rachel Dillon4 years ago

We went during the week so there were less crowds and it was great bc our toddler could just run around. They have a rope maze, a HUGE slide, goats, chickens, roosters and these cute pedal cars to ride on and it was all free! On the weekends they have a bouncy house (which I think you have to pay for) and apple picking. They have a cute little general store with a few items of fresh produce, homemade jelly’s and salsas (we tried the fine cut hot salsa which was gone in a day) apple cider (and an apple cider slushy which I highly recommend trying!) wonderful pumpkin patch! Will be back next year!
