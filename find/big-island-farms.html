We really enjoyed seeing what is growing at Big Island Farms! The tour was about an hour and we really enjoyed seeing and learning about the food forests along with all the herbs. Well worth the time if you are on the Big Island

★★★★★H. Robinson6 months ago

Such a great experience! The walking tour showed not only how quickly plants can grow in this region, but shared the extraordinary diversity of edible plants and fruit-bearing plants on the island. Plus we learned how to select the ripest and most tasty fruits for our next visit to the local farmers market. It’s wet in this part of the island! Recommend wearing durable waterproof (hiking) shoes/boots and bringing a rain jacket.

★★★★★Shane Krukow2 years ago

We did a 1 hour tour with Sarah. She was most delightful and knowledgeable about editable plants and sustainable organic practices. My wife and I enjoyed our time with her very much! She explained different plants as we sampled them. We purchased macadamia nuts, tea and baked goods (organic and gluten free) that were wonderful! Thank you!

★★★★★Annelise Miller11 months ago

We had a wonderful time on the Big Island Farm Tour. We appreciated learning about the plant life on the island and being able to ask questions. It was a great start to our trip because the tour highlighted things we began to notice throughout our stay on the Big Island. Our kids loved trying the various edible fruit and plants and it was fun to see how things grow. My only regret is that we couldn't see other parts of the farm. ★★★★★Christina Magers4 years ago

SO THANKFUL for my time at Big Island Farms this summer! I learned so much about organic agriculture, sustainable farming, the vegan lifestyle, permaculture, and caring for the environment and other human beings in healthy, sustainable ways. I do recognize that there were some minor organizational issues and a lot more time in the kitchen than I was expecting to spend there, but I would honestly say that the entire experience was well-worth it and I know that this place has lots of potential to offer for whoever goes there next! :)
