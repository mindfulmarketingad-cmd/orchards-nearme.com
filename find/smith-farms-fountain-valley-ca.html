★★★★★Susan Aumaier9 months ago

A rare gem of kindness and community spirit! I stopped by Smith Farms this evening hoping to grab some cherry tomatoes, only to realize they had already closed. I figured I’d missed my chance—but to my surprise, they opened the doors just for me. My total came to $6.50, and with their $10 minimum for card purchases and no cash on hand, I was ready to walk away empty-handed. Instead, they handed me the tomatoes and refused to charge me this evening (I will go back tomorrow and pay them). In a world where generosity feels increasingly rare, Smith Farms reminded me what true neighborly kindness looks like. Their produce is always fresh, but it’s their heart that makes this place truly special. Thank you for going above and beyond—you’ve earned a lifelong customer.

★★★★★jolene sloane2 years ago

I've been a friend of the family and a regular customer at their farmers markets for about 10 years. Their quality is impeccable. The prices are a bit on the high side, but it's worth it. Great organic strawberries, and a little later on in the summer their melons are outstanding. The Tuscan cantaloupes are amazing! Wonderful collection of other organic fruits and vegetables. Can't recommend them enough.
