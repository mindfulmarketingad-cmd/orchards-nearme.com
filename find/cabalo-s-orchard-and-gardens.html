★★★★★Lauren Benda2 years ago

We had a wonderful time visiting Cabalo's Orchard 💕 Upon arrival we were promptly greeted by a friendly worker who guided us to the types of apples we were looking for based on what we were going to make (apple sauce & apple pies). Walking around the orchard was a dreamy experience; the rows of trees are so picturesque, with such sweet floral smells, and mountains off in the background. We also loved winding our way through the pumpkin patch and picking pumpkins right off the vines. They had plenty of apple picking tools and wagons to make the experience even easier for their guests. 🍎🍏🌳 All of the baked goods we made with our apples were sooo delicious! We even had enough apples leftover to make apple butter! Will highly recommend this place to our family and friends! 🥧
