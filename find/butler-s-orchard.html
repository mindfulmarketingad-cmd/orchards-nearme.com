Reviews

★☆☆☆☆Hao Xua month ago

We visited this farm for strawberry picking and had a very disappointing experience. Most of the field appeared poorly maintained, with large areas covered by weeds and very few quality strawberries available for picking. Many of the berries we found were either extremely small, overripe, or already rotting on the plants. After spending time searching, it became clear that there simply were not enough good strawberries left in the field to justify the admission fee. What made the experience even worse was the staff's attitude. When we raised concerns about the condition of the fields, the response was essentially indifference. No one seemed interested in helping or addressing the issue. We understand that weather and growing conditions can affect a harvest, but customers should be informed when picking conditions are poor rather than being charged full price for fields that are largely picked over and poorly maintained. This felt less like a pick-your-own farm experience and more like paying to search through weeds for a handful of usable strawberries.
