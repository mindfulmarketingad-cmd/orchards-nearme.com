★★★★★Scott D. Shaker8 months ago

Awesome destination for the family! Lots of free activities for families including hay and corn mazes, corn pool, slides, forest area, play store and more. Lots of good food choices to eat. A farm store offering pasture raised eggs, pork products,, jams and pickled products. Pumpkins and mums for sale. You can also pay for a nice long hayride. They have a space for rent for large parties. You won't want to miss this spot!

★★★★★Stacey Paulick2 years ago

The kids always have so much fun at Cuff's. There are free activities for all age levels. There is also hot cider, & other beverage & food to purchase. There was also a coffee cafe truck to purchase various drinks. You take a hay ride out to the pumpkin fields & pick your allotted stash. I feel like you used to get more pumpkins & gourds for the exact same price. Still fun.

★★★★★Allyce Z6 years ago

Wonderful family outing. Pick strawberries in Spring and pumpkins in Fall. It cost me $8 to get in. That includes a hay ride, pumpkin, 4 awesome gourds, 2 ears of colorful corn. Great and large playground area with unique things to do. Pigs were by the fence but not part of the farm. They have wagons and fabric bags for you to use while you are there.

★★★★★Allyce Zimmer6 years ago

We had a great time at Cuff's Farm. It is neat that you get 4 gourds, 2 ears of colorful corn, and a big pumpkin for each admission. I love that they gave reusable bags and had wagons to transport the pumpkins. The play area is huge and fun. Pretty good price for all that you get.
