Our Absolute Favorite Spot in Greenville! Plant and See Nursery is hands down our most favorite place in the Greenville area, and we visit almost every week — that should tell you everything! This place is truly special. First, the sheer size of it is impressive. It feels like walking through a botanical garden, with greenhouse tunnels and wide open sections filled with an enormous collection of plants. Whether you’re looking for indoor plants, outdoor flowers, succulents, herbs, or trees, they have it all — and everything looks healthy and well cared for. It’s also one of the best spots for photography in the area. The lush greenery, colorful blooms, and beautiful layouts make it a paradise for plant lovers and photographers alike. What really keeps us coming back, aside from the incredible selection, is how reasonable the prices are. You get so much value without breaking the bank. Highly recommend to everyone!
