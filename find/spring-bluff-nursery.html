★★★★★Kendall Peaka year ago

I have driven past this nursery for many moons. I never really noticed it to be honest. I noticed it this year because they had colorful flags and an OPEN sign in front. I had no intentions of stopping there, but I was curious as to how big this place was and what they had to offer as at that time it was late April and "tis the season"! I was surprised by the volume of plant varieties and trees. I ended up buying a dinosaur kale plant that first visit. It was not my last visit. I've been back 3 times and I've since bought a beautiful rose bush and my most recent visit, today, I bought some English lavender to place alongside my roses because they grow well together, and they're beautiful together. I'm brand new to gardening and landscaping, but every question I had they could answer. They have a cute shop where you check out, and they sell seeds, books, butterfly houses, local honey, and a lot more! I want to go here and sit down and crochet among the plants sometime this summer. They have a gazebo with chairs and tables there. I need to ask if that's a place I could simply just hang out. If you haven't been to this place yet and you're curious about it, I recommend stopping by. Prices are very reasonable too. Photo is of one of the rose bushes I got from there along with my new English Lavender plant. Cannot wait to make Lavender Lemonade this summer! YUM!
