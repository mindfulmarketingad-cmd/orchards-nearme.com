★★☆☆☆Bridget R2 years ago

We've been visiting Smith Farms in June for 5 years. Sunday was year 6. It was NOT the same. We were not greeted with the usual warm smile and welcoming greeting. Instead, an older woman (presumably the owner) sat at the cart eating & didn't even look up at us. We had to ask to be handed a container. I then asked if there was any strawberry jam this year & her response was, "Do you see any this year?" I was taken aback & didn't respond. We typically get 10lbs, but there wasn't many left & most were 1/2 their normal size. We stopped at a little over 5lbs. Even upon checkout, the gentleman in front of me commented on all of the deer tracks he'd seen. "Yup," was all she could be bothered to mutter. She said nothing to us other than the price & went back to take her seat. We brought extra family this year & it was an embarrassing experience. We will find another spot in future years. We pay more for the experience. If we wanted rude, we'd go buy our strawberries at Walmart.
