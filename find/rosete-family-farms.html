★★★★★Samuel Paris2 years ago

As the old adage goes, "You get what you pay for." Truly worth every penny to be out here at this beautiful venue for an extended weekend. We hosted our daughter's 1 Year Birthday out of the event center and then stayed in the farmhouse. The decorations are well placed, the atmosphere invites you to relax and just enjoy. There is a hot tub, fire pit, and dining table on the backside of the barn in case you want to spend the cool evenings outside.the showers are gorgeous, and the small touches of a wine bottle and waters in the fridge just said, "Welcome" in the most memorable way. Our hosts were kind and made every effort to ensure the event center was set up to our liking, as well as ensuring we had no questions or needs throughout our stay. Their son was super polite and ensured our trash was removed promptly after the gift opening and party (thanks so much for that!). If you are looking for a place to get away, enjoy a wedding, party, retreat, this is the place to do it. We drove from Denver, CO to be here this weekend, and it did not disappoint in the slightest. Thanks, Rosete Family Farms for letting us share in the most meaningful of memories.
