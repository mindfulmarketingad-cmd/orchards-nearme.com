★★★★★Christina Whitford9 months ago

We went on a field trip to the orchard last week. It was a lot of fun and they had plenty of things for the families to do. The kids really enjoyed the big bins of corn. They could have played in those all day long. There is a large variety of types of apples. More than I even knew existed. The staff is very knowledgeable about all the varieties of apples and what is best for what you are planning to do with them. I ended the day by having an apple slushy which was so good. I purchased some bbq beef sticks that I planned to try and take home. They were so good I ate the entire package for lunch and bought another. I highly recommend both items as they were both so delicious. I may go back just to get more. The people there were so kind, informative and helpful. I highly recommend this to all families and plan to take my family. There were also plenty of places to sit in the shade. Wagon rides to the orchard run every 30 minutes so you were not stuck waiting long for a ride to or from the field.
