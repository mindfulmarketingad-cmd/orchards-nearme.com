★★★★★Betsy Muehlhausena year ago

Each season brings a new reason to love Benson's. The healthiest, best priced bedding plants and oodles of ready to go planted containers all spring and summer. Then, my favorite, the pumpkins, gourds, corn stalks show up for fall decorating. Finally, produce as fresh as from your own garden is ready . I'm currently enjoying Benson's beets and carrots, but their cabbage, squash, onions, peppers all made it to my kitchen. Benson's is probably most famous for their sweet corn and once you enjoy an ear or two, you'll know why. Benson's has been a treasure to many generations in Missoula and may they continue for many more.
