★★★★★Christine Ha year ago

The owner Joe and his staff were so very helpful when we were purchasing plantings for our new flower beds. We had a drawing and list of what we wanted/thought we wanted. Joe answered all of our questions and made recommendations on a few changes we were making due to some plants not being deer resistant. He is very knowledgeable. His team member (I didn’t get her name) happily assisted in pulling all the plants and trees and helped bring them to our truck. Joe also helped with potting soil for indoor plants. We had a great experience and our new flower beds look great. We will be shopping there again when we’re ready for some annual filler plants as we continue to work on the beds. They also have a very cute, well stocked store/gift shop and the cashier in there was prompt and friendly. Highly recommend. Thanks Joe & Staff
