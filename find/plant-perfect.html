★★★★★Anjel Voigt3 months ago

The absolute BEST place to come for your plant addiction.. at least mine anyway 😂. During the summer they have a huge variety of outdoor plants from perennials, annual, basically any kind of tree even fruit. They sell inside and outside gardening supplies. The indoor plant section is ridiculously addictive! They have spider plants, monstera, snake plants, cactus, bamboo, aloe Vera, arrow head plants, money trees, ponytail palm, succulents, Venus fly traps, along with other carnivore plants and MANY MANY more plants to see and choose from. They sell flower pots, items to hang them with, plant stands, plant decor, bird houses, gardening tools, a variety of bug sprays, and other plant sprays, potting soil, and many many more items. They have a rewards program where when you spend so much you earn so much back. I absolutely love this place and have been a member for well over 6 years. Come stop in!
