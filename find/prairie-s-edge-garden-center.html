Prairie's Edge Garden Center is a neat place showcasing garden and country items. In the autumn the center opens a corn maze and pumpkin patch for children and adults that enjoy those activities. The items featured in the gift shop are priced slightly higher than other locations but the quality is good. I've always had a good experience at this place. (08-20-22): neat selection of tables, mats and signs that can be changed out seasonally.

★★★★★Delayna Adams5 years ago

This place was a treat to stop in. Such a huge selection for your garden and very VERY fair pricing. A fraction of what places in STL would sell for. I loaded up on a variety of pumpkins for the porch... every color from orange to white to light green to multi, fun shapes..."ugly" pumpkins.. I was so happy to find the unglazed gray clay pots I've been looking all over for. They had a great selection of mums, pots and indoor/ outdoor decor, perennials and annuals. Everything you need to make your porch and garden look amazing. Loved it.
