★★★★★The Shenanigan2 weeks ago

I went looking for snake plants. They have a small selection that I'm sure they switch out with what they get in. Lots of succulents and even air plants. I didn't look much outside because there was SO MUCH to look at! Everything was very reasonably priced as well.

★★★★★Catherine Tolbert5 years ago

As I was planning my trip to Charleston I asked a local group which garden centers to visit. Abide a while garden center was the number one suggestion and it just so happened that it was on the way to my brother's home. I was blown away by their selection of plants that are seemingly rare in Greenville, yet so plentiful and yet reasonably priced here. They had a tie constellation on display that is absolutely stunning. Of course, it's not for sale, but one can only hope. They have a generous selection of Hoyas and even rare sansevieras. I definitely plan to visit again before journeying back home. So happy to have stumbled upon this gem and met all the wonderfully crazy plant people.

I really love this place. They don’t have the biggest selection of plants in the city, but the ones they get are unique and healthy. They have the biggest air plants I have EVER seen. The boutique part inside is also really nice. I got some Christmas ornaments during the holiday sale. Great place for gifts.
