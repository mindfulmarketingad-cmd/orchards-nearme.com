★★★★☆David Parajona year ago

This place has an impressive selection of trees, vegetables, flowers, and cacti—something for every type of gardener. The staff is friendly, knowledgeable, and eager to help. They also carry a wide range of pots in different sizes and styles. If you are looking for something specific, be sure to call ahead of time to ask staff if they have it. One thing to note: there’s a second part of the store in the backlot, located right next to the rear parking area. I’d suggest parking there so you’re conveniently situated between both sections for easier browsing and loading.

A cute local nursery with a surprisingly diverse selection of plants and fruit trees. Staff is super friendly and never makes you feel like you're inconveniencing them with questions or requests.
