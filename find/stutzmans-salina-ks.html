Wonderful place! Every time I go visit my family I try to stop by but it’s either hit or miss (on my account) Well this time they weren’t were they usually are & my dad said they moved but didn’t say where. So I had to leave early one morning & get gas & the only station that had my type of fuel was by the mall. So as I’m heading over there I see the plants & the sign. It’s 6:30 a.m. & the gates are open & a lady blowing debris about with the leaf blower. The sign says open at 8 am, well I proceeded to go in & asked the lady, in which I startled her unintentionally. She said well why not since she was there that I could look around. Well what a wonderful selection of plants at very good prices! I particularly enjoyed the rarer & unique plants that you don’t find at your every day box store places. I ended up with some great hanging baskets and couple of other items. She offered me a box & some plastic to use in my car which was greatly appreciated especially since I was in a rental. I thanked her for letting me in early. Great place, highly recommend & definitely will return!
