What a lovely and beautiful place to get plants and wreaths This is a family owned business that started in 2014. It is a Garden center along the south side of Constitution Highway between Charlottesville and Frederickburg. As we were driving by we saw the wreaths outside along the wall of the building. We ended up getting two beautiful wreaths that looked fresh and handmade at a very reasonable price. Overall, this is a great place to stop if you need food for your animals, some plants, or want to get some nice gifts.
