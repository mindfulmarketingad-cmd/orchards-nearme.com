★★★★★Nicole Schmidt3 weeks ago

Absolutely incredible experience for families! We came out for the Strawberry Festival and it was top-tier from start to finish. The strawberry patches are well-maintained and perfect for little ones to pick, the farm tractor photo-ops are adorable. The absolute highlight for our toddler was the Fun Farm area—especially the little play town stores where he could pretend to run the shops! It kept him completely entertained and engaged. Clean, organized, delicious berries, and infinite joy for the kids. We will definitely be making this a regular family tradition! Highly recommend.

★★★★★Mandy Silva3 weeks ago

We had a great time strawberry picking at Johnson's Farm Produce! We came home with tons of strawberries, and they are incredibly sweet and delicious. The kids loved picking their own berries, and it was a fun family activity. We also spent time in the fun area, and the kids had an absolute blast. There was plenty for them to do, and it really added to the experience. The only thing I wouldn't do again is the train ride. We bought extra tickets for it, and honestly, it sounded like it was about to fall apart. It was super loud, very bouncy, and not something we'd spend the extra money on again. The kids still enjoyed it, but the adults were holding on for dear life. 😂 Overall, we had a wonderful day, brought home lots of amazing strawberries, and made some great family memories. We'd definitely come back for the strawberry picking and the fun area!
