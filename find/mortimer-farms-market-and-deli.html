★★★★★A Z4 months ago

Nice store that is well worth it stop at. There's a huge gravel lot with parking, with paved accessible parking spots up front. Walking up, you get a nice view of their expansive farm and whimsical play areas. Near the entrance area, there's outdoor seating with cute lighting. The deli/market had assorted ice cream flavors, candies, and even fresh produce from the farm. They also have a nice selection of pickled veggies too- and their pickles are the best I've ever had- crispy and delicious! Great service and very welcoming employees.
