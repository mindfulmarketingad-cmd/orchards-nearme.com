★★★★★Ty Millera year ago

They have many trees! Local and not local, spruce, birch, cherry, big and in the ground or smaller and in pots. I got 4 black hill spruce in pots, each about 4 feet tall. Very green and healthy, smells like spruce! Their staff are very knowledgeable and experienced with trees. The owner is an Arborist and the company goes beyond planting trees, they also maintain trees, and they offer a warranty if they have to install the tree. They have their own equipment to move big trees from their yard or you can come get them yourself. They have big trees for $1000+ or baby trees for less than $100. Was about $400/tree for my ~4 foot trees in pots. I worked with Adam, he is very good at his job. He is like the tree whisperer, he just knows everything about trees and can answer any questions I had. I totally recommend this company for trees! The whole experience was easy and it was like Christmas picking out our new trees!
