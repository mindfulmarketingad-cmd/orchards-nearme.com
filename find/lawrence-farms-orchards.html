★★★★★Let's Go Celtics10 months ago

Lawrence Farm is simply the best pick-your-own farm that I’ve ever been to! They had a wide variety of vegetables, fruits, starches, and other produce. My two personal favorite fruits and veggies that I picked were the sweet corn and the plums. The corn was so juicy and sweet, and was amazing when eaten raw, or cooked (as I did later for dinner), and the plums were juicy and slightly tart, and perfectly ripe! The grounds are beautiful and well-kept, and the tractor rides they offer are not only a good way to get around the farm, but also offer an amazing view of the orchards. In addition, their farm store is well-organized and stocked with many locally produced organic goods, like honey, maple syrup, and my personal favorite, apple cider. Plus, they offer many homemade treats too, including frozen apple cider slushes, and warm apple cider donuts. Overall, it is a great place to come with friends or family to pick delicious fruits, and have lots of fun!
