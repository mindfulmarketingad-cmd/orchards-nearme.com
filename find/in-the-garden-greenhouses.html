I don’t normally post negative reviews but in this case, I feel that I have been treated so badly that I have to. Purchased almost(191.00) $200.00 worth of plants from In The Garden, mostly vegetables and a hanging basket of flowers. We are reasonably experienced gardeners. When we got home, we hung the basket and transplanted the veggies and protected them from low temperatures and rainy weather but they developed a powdery substance on the leaves, turned brown and about 50% died. I called the owners of In The Garden to let them know about this and was responded to with immediately elevated emotions, excuses, anger, interruptions lots of blaming and ultimately very, very poor customer service. I tried to have a calm , productive conversation. But got only anger, recrimination and excuses from both of the business owners. I even gave them a chance to calm down for a while. The husband of the couple who own In The Garden called back but not to reason but to continue his emotional outburst and blaming. I ended to the call politely but did hang up while he was still talking. He offered after all of the drama, to replace some of the dead plants and lost money. I asked, instead that he use this experience to remind him to think of how he would like to be treated when the shoe is on the other foot. It is not lost on me what difficulties and pressure are placed on small business owners, especially in the agricultural arena. In the end, I hope that In The Garden thrives and that the people who own it are well and happy.
