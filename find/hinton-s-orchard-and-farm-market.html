★★★★★Trina D.2 months ago

This is truly one of the best spots to find everything you need for your flower garden or to start planting your own vegetables. The Hintons are such a wonderful family, and I always look forward to visiting the Orchard and browsing their E-Town Market for fresh veggies, baked goods, delicious fruits, local dairy and meat products, farm-fresh eggs, garden supplies, and more. Their apple butter and ciders are simply some of the best I've ever tasted! All these things make visiting the farm a delightful experience, but my favorite time of year is fall, especially when they host their wonderful Halloween events. It's always a fun, family-friendly celebration filled with activities that keep kids enjoying themselves all day long. The jumping pillow, petting zoo, corn maze, silo, paintball, and so many other fun attractions make every visit memorable!

★★★★★Herbert Sharp5 years ago

We visited last Saturday afternoon on a beautiful day. This was advertised as their Strawberry Festival but I really didn't see what would normally look like a festival. It is a great little Farm Market offering you-pick strawberries and already picked available in a small store. Their strawberry ice cream is a nice treat especially during this summer-like day. The shop has a variety of preserves and similar items. A nice variety of farm gift items. Rocking chairs in front of shop to relax with your ice cream cone or bowl (reasonably priced). Greenhouses offer a variety of flowers and plants. I also spotted a pavilion with picnic tables and a kids play area. This is a good market to visit and easy to find.
