★★★★★Jay Doster2 months ago

I live in Atlanta, but I drive to Head-Lee twice a spring/summer just for their vast selection. If you're doing a major landscape overhaul, they've got everything you need, and more. If you're looking for seasonal plants, you'll find a much wider selection than at the big-box stores. The great selection at Head-Lee allows me the opportunity to find and try plants I otherwise wouldn't ever have known about.
