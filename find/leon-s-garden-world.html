★★★★★Thomas E Washington8 years ago

Leon's Garden World has everything, Vinca, Marigolds, Perennial Coneflowers, they are Pow wow white, Cheyenne Spirit, and Gemini Pink. For the Celosia collectors, Leon's has a new Celosia called Dracula. I like the Red Twisted Celosia, because they will grow a big head that will drop hundreds of seeds during the winter. As with Intenz Celosia, and other types of Celosia they also will drop hundreds of seeds. There will be so many Celosia sprouting in the last of May and early June, that you will think that they are weeds. But you can tell the difference. The Celosia will have light green leaves with yellow stems. Some will have red leaves with light red stems. You can Google to see the different types of Celosia.
