★★★★★Jenelle Douglas11 months ago

AJ's Berry farm is awesome. We come every year for picking season and it's a 30 min drive for us. Today we picked raspberries and strawberries and they were plentiful and sooo delicious. We were able to fill 2 quarts of each in about 30 min. Their small store always has yummy treats like donuts, jams, candy and drinks. All of the employees are very nice. ★★★★★Bryan Tankersley3 years ago

Went out there to go strawberry picking this morning. The only surprise is that the berries were smaller this year. Boy, dont let that fool you! Those berries are so sweet and juicy! We got two gallon sized buckets full! It took a little bit longer because they were smaller, but I didn't mind. Go picking early in the morning (I believe they open at 6am) before the heat! They encourage you to sample the berries while you are picking! Go into the store, they have all sorts of goodies! Fresh baked strawberry donuts, alreagy picked berries, different jams, dressings, seasonings and more! If they have their chocolate chip cookies available, i recommend you get them! Little piece of heaven right there! AJ and his family are always really nice and super helpful! We will be back for 🎃 in the Fall! Happy picking!
