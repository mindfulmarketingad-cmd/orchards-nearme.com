★★★★★GB Cumings7 years ago

Extensive assortment of indoor, outside garden and container plants. The majority being in great condition. The decorative elements and products offered in the shed have expanded to include many local items (honey, preserves, handmade soaps, art, etc.). Mr. Darnell was very pleasant, knowledgeable and easy to work with: he made himself available without hovering, had a cart appear when it was needed (trays also) and carried those that wouldn't fit (having spinal problems this was greatly appreciated) to the "pile" I was accumulating. He showed me beautiful options for some areas here that are problematic and helped me by choosing the "best of the best" for the selections with which I was unfamiliar. The prices were also good and fair for the quality. Honestly? It was difficult to not get more... but as soon as my back recovers from planting these, I'll definitely be back for more!
