★★☆☆☆Wendy Smith Wardena month ago

Amazing selection and fair prices. I feel strongly about supporting local businesses rather than a big box store. The customer service/interaction skills of some employees here leaves something to be desired. This is the 2nd year my family and I have shopped here for our garden plants, in what should be an enjoyable, fun learning experience. Today my 9 year old son was drawn to the wind chimes and naturally wanted to hear them...they are made to be heard. Granted, he tried them all and they are loud, but he was in no way trying to be rough or break them (we obviously would pay for them if they did get broken) and I was watching to be sure of this, while being aware and understanding he is a little boy. The lady at the counter was quick to be harsh with him and didn't seem to mind one bit to go straight to rude and unkind with him. I did speak to her. We had a similar experience here last year, and are left feeling scolded for just being a young kiddo simply excited about a beautiful garden center. It leaves what should have been an enjoyable experience, we get once per year, tainted and I will most likely visit a different establishment next year.
