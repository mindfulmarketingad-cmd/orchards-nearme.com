★☆☆☆☆Karen Z3 weeks ago

My family and I have been customers of this nursery since 2014, primarily for flowers, shrubs, trees, and landscaping projects. A recent experience left me very disappointed in both the customer service and the guidance I received. I recently began vegetable gardening and purchased two, six foot long raised garden beds. On May 27, during the Memorial Day sale, I visited the nursery to purchase vegetable plants and seeds. Since I am relatively new to vegetable gardening, I specifically asked an employee at checkout about using straw to help retain moisture in my garden beds. I was directed to purchase a bale of straw/hay sold outside. After planting my vegetables and seeds, I spread the straw as recommended. Yesterday, I noticed numerous sprouts emerging throughout the beds. At first, I was excited and assumed they were the seeds I had planted. Unfortunately, I later learned that many of these sprouts appear to be growing from the straw itself. When I contacted the nursery, the manager confirmed that the bale I purchased had not been treated to prevent germination. He also explained that they sell a different straw product inside the store that has been processed specifically to prevent this issue. This was never mentioned when I asked for advice on what to use in my vegetable garden. What I find most frustrating is that I sought guidance from a business that specializes in gardening and landscaping because I was new to this type of gardening. Had I been told there were two options available, and that one could introduce unwanted growth into my garden, I would have purchased the straw. Instead, I am now left trying to identify which sprouts are the vegetables I intentionally planted and which are coming from the straw. What should have been an enjoyable first gardening experience has become a frustrating and time-consuming project. The manager eventually offered a package of the processed straw, because I asked, but that does not resolve the time, effort, and uncertainty already created. More importantly, it does not address the fact that proper information was not provided when I specifically asked for advice. We have supported this nursery for over a decade. However, knowledgeable guidance and customer service are exactly what customers should be able to expect from a business whose expertise is gardening and landscaping. We will not be returning and have shared this experience with others so they are aware.
