★★★★★Garrett Shea5 years ago

Wonderful orchard. Called ahead and the owner gave us excellent suggestions and it was perfect! 1000s of apples to pick yourself and a 1/2bushel bag is provided complimentary to fill up for $15 . They provided some apple picking tools to reach higher on the trees if necessary but most apples were at 3-5 feet which was perfect. Excellent activity in pandemic as we just left money at the bin of the house and grabbed a bag for the orchard. Open dawn to dusk most days. About a 80 minute drive from Knoxville .
