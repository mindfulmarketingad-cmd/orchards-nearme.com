★★★★★Erin McCorda year ago

The Potting Shed has a wide variety of annuals, perennials, roses, and landscape trees and shrubs to choose from. I was extremely pleased to see their flower selection, as it far exceeds the offerings I've seen elsewhere in the area. The quality of their plants is also superior. Their roses, in particular, are a refreshing diversion from the standard fare, and while I love a good Knock Out Rose, I was delighted to see they carry David Austin roses, which are, in my opinion, the prettiest and best-smelling. I highly recommend this small business, which offers off-season discount bucks, to anyone looking for something both traditional or a little different.
