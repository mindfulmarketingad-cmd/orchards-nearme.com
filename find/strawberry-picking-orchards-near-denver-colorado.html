Strawberry Picking Near Denver: What You Need to Know

Strawberry farms within reach of Denver take advantage of the Mountain West's well-drained, high-elevation soil, planting June-bearing and day-neutral varieties that give visitors a few solid weeks of picking each year. Strawberry Picking in the Mountain West

Strawberry farms across the Mountain West grow in well-drained, high-elevation soil, which sets the pace for a season that typically runs June through July. Farms here plant a mix of June-bearing varieties for a concentrated peak and day-neutral varieties that stretch picking out over a longer stretch of the season. Pick-your-own rows tend to open on short notice once the fruit is ready, so checking a farm's current status before visiting is worth the extra step.

Best Time to Go Strawberry Picking Near Denver

June through July, with the earliest berries in Mountain West typically leading the harvest and the window closing quickly once temperatures rise.

Tips for Your Denver Strawberry Picking Trip

Plan a Denver-area strawberry trip for June through July and go early in the day, since the fruit softens quickly once temperatures climb. Many farms near Denver also sell fresh-picked flats at the stand if you would rather skip the fields and still get the same fruit.
