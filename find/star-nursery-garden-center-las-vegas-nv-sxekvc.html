★★★★★Philip Plastinaa month ago

Nice fully stocked nursery. The prices are great but don't get me wrong The quality suffers in lieu of that. They do have all of the plants that you're looking for they're just not the same size and grow quality as the other large competitor. You're also not paying three times as much. So up to you do you want a lesser quality plant for a killer price or do you want to play top dollar for grade A quality? If you're down for more of a bargain price and don't mind getting a lower grade of plant quality than this place is for you. Now onto the supplies. This nursery has more supplies than anyone I've seen in Las Vegas. They have pavers. They have decorative trellises. They have tons of outdoor lighting systems. They have tons of irrigation system products. They have a huge selection of garden and outdoor working tools shovels rakes you name it. I really enjoyed walking around inside the indoor building and looking at all of the products that they have available. They even have the bubblers that I have been looking for all year! So to recap,.I'd come here for products and maybe smaller plants but if I was looking to get larger specimens for my property Id probably choose the other place just based on quality level. I'm glad I came here and discovered what they can offer me.

★★★★☆Thomas B10 months ago

We have been using this nursery for years, so I guess it time to do a review. We have always found this location to be ideal for us, and we believe it is the best Star Nursery in town. Their famous and friendly kitty kat always greets you with warmth and enjoys a quick pat or two. The staff overall have been friendly and helpful, but to be honest, it depends on who you get, we have had a few unsatisfactory visits, but not many. The nursery is well stocked whenever we have been there, and they have probably the widest selections of trees and plants in town. We used to buy most of our plants and trees here because of the one-year warranty, but since that changed a few years ago, we now go to Home Depot or Lowes, since they still do have that one year, hassle free return policy. Bottom line, good location, helpful staff (most of the time) and wide selection of trees and plants. We were very disappointed to hear that Eric Solano received another promotion and is no longer manager of this store.
