★★★★★Tyler Lang von Wellenburg4 months ago

Sky Nursery is easily one of the most well-curated garden centers in the area. Their indoor plant selection is constantly changing with unique finds, and their terracotta collection is a standout—they carry a huge variety of non-red tones, which is perfect if you want the benefits of clay pots without the Southwest aesthetic. ​The seasonal variety here is unmatched. In the spring, they have the largest selection of Coleus I’ve ever seen. I also love their themed displays; they recently featured a 'Night Time' theme with stunning dark foliage and bioluminescent petunias. ​I especially want to shout out their holiday displays. Seeing a massive, prominent PRIDE-themed Christmas tree as a centerpiece was incredible. That kind of visible allyship and inclusivity is so appreciated and makes me even more proud to shop here. Aside from the plants, they have a great coffee shop and a 'Nectar Bar' for bee-friendly gardening. It’s a must-visit for any serious plant enthusiast!
