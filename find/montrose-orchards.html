★★★★☆Samantha Tullar9 months ago

We enjoyed the shop, donuts and cider, and pony rides. The staff was friendly and the hay ride was great. However, we were confused about a lot of things. There were a lot of empty out buildings and we didn't know if we were supposed to go in them. One had hay bales, but benches were piled on them so we don't know what the purpose was. Storage?? No signs or anything. The pony rides were cash only, and you pay for them outside. The hay rides you pay for inside. The animal cages are empty now except for the chickens. Overall, a good time, but could use some more signs or something lol
