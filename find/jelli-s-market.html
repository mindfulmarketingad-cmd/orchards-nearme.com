★★★★★Alex Quail2 years ago

We went for strawberry picking in early June this year and it was a very memorable experience. Parking was easy, they have signs to help navigate the farm. You take a short cart ride to and from the fields. Fields are very well kept and spread out, lots of berries to pick from, not crowded. Workers can help show you spots to pick. After picking you weigh and pay. Bring cash! They do accept cards but 3% service fee. The market area is very small but inviting, they have an area for strawberry sundaes and fresh lemonade. Bathrooms are available. Seating outside is available and some yard games. They also have a small barn area to visit animals as well. This is a small but fun and friendly family farm, you can tell they really value your business.
