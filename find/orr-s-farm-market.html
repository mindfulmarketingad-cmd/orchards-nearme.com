Reviews

★★★★★Jonathan Matiasa month ago

Took my wife and kids to Orr’s Farm again and honestly this place never disappoints. It’s become one of our go-to family spots during the year, especially in the fall. The hayride out to the barnyard is always fun for the kids and gives you that real farm feel right away. Once you get there, there’s a ton for families to do without it feeling overcrowded or overly commercialized. The animals are great and you can tell they’re taken care of well. Most of them will walk right up to you which my kids love every single time. The playground keeps them busy forever, and they’ve added more activities over the years like bubble stations, basketball, and the newer long tube slides where you ride down on a sack/rug. My wife and I even went down a few times ourselves 😂 We haven’t gotten to do all the pick-your-own stuff yet, especially with the frost hurting some of the crops this year, but we did the apple picking last year and had a great time. Even when nature doesn’t cooperate, they still put a lot of effort into making the experience worth coming out for. The market itself is awesome too. Everything always feels fresh, the baked goods are dangerous in the best way, and their monthly sandwiches are seriously underrated. We usually end up leaving with way more than we planned. This is also where we get our Halloween pumpkins every year now. It just feels like one of those genuine family places that’s getting harder to find. You can spend a whole afternoon there without hearing your kids say they’re bored once. Definitely recommend for families looking to make memories without having to drive hours away. 🎃🚜

★★★★★Sam3 months ago

Orr’s Farm Market is one of those places that’s always worth the stop—there’s a reason it’s always packed. The orchard is absolutely beautiful and easily a go-to spot for spring photos. Inside the market, you’ll find some of the best fresh, local products around. From breads and desserts to cider, juices, meats, coffee, and more—it feels like there’s something for everyone. You can really tell they take pride in offering quality, locally sourced goods, which makes supporting them even better. The staff are consistently friendly, and there’s always something new to check out. I also really appreciate how they keep their faith visible in a genuine and respectful way—it adds to the overall charm of the place. Highly recommend stopping by if you’re in the area!
