🍎 A Sweet Stop at Panorama Orchard Sometimes the best travel moments come from spontaneous detours — and today’s was all about apples. We’d been hearing all the chatter about “apple season” in this area, so curiosity (and the smell of cinnamon in the air) led us straight to Panorama Orchard. Let me just say — this place is apple heaven. From the moment you walk in, you’re surrounded by baskets of fresh-picked varieties, warm baked aromas, and the sound of happy munching everywhere. If you go (and trust me, you should), try the fried pies first — flaky, golden perfection with a sweet apple filling that tastes like fall itself. The apple fritters? A short stop turned into a full-blown tasting adventure — and now I totally get the hype. 🍏✨
