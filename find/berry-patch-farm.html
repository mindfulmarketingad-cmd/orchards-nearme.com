Reviews

★★★★★Steph11 months ago

I love the Berry Patch Farm! If you've never been, it's on a U-pick farm on a gravel road outside of Nevada, Iowa, and you only pay for what you decide to take home. Every time I've gone, they've had a variety of fruits to pick, as well as many already picked and packaged fruits/vegetables available to purchase at the kiosk. Their produce is really good quality, and with new ownership, they've expanded to add flowers. They update their Facebook and website frequently, and offer very fair prices. It's a fun quick day trip for fresh food.

★★★★☆Christine H9 months ago

Our family loves coming here. It's a very welcoming and peaceful place. I like how they've increased some things inside the little store. There's something for everyone in there, t-shirts, honey, jam, and I got some plums from the cooler that were delicious. We'll be back before the season ends for a few more apples and pumpkins.
