Strawberry Picking Near Sacramento: What You Need to Know

Sacramento sits within the Pacific Coast's strawberry belt, where farms growing in mild, coastal-influenced soil time their pick-your-own rows to a compact spring window. Families in and around Sacramento treat opening day as a genuine seasonal event, since the best berries disappear from the rows fast.

Strawberry Picking in the Pacific Coast

Strawberry farms across the Pacific Coast grow in mild, coastal-influenced soil, which sets the pace for a season that typically runs April through June. The region's milder spring temperatures push the harvest ahead of most of the country, making it one of the earliest strawberry destinations nationally. Pick-your-own rows tend to open on short notice once the fruit is ready, so checking a farm's current status before visiting is worth the extra step.

Best Time to Go Strawberry Picking Near Sacramento

April through June, with the earliest berries in Pacific Coast typically leading the harvest and the window closing quickly once temperatures rise.

Tips for Your Sacramento Strawberry Picking Trip

Because the season around Sacramento is short (typically April through June), it pays to visit early in the week rather than waiting for the weekend, when popular rows near Sacramento are often stripped by Saturday afternoon. Bring your own containers if the farm allows it — strawberries bruise easily in stacked buckets.
