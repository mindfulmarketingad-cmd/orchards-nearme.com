★★★★★Kelly4 years ago

A local gem in Kinderhook! Samascott’s Orchard continues to be an amazing experience to pick your own food! The crops are high quality, the fields and roadways are well maintained and accessible and the staff is always respectful and kind. The admission fee is per person and you can drive your vehicle into the orchard so you aren’t carrying pounds of produce all the way back to check out. They provide a map of what’s available to pick with prices for the day and you can bring your own container or purchase them on site. When you check out they will inspect your car to confirm no produce has been stolen (sadly people steal from family owned businesses which is just awful). You can also rent a surrey bicycle to ride into the fields to pick for an hour for a fee. The farm stand when you check in/out has produce, ice cream and a really knowledgeable staff to help you with any questions. All around Samascotts is a local pillar, a true working farm, a wonderful experience for kids and adults of all ages.
