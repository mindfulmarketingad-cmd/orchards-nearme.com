Reviews

★★★★★BossHossa year ago

A neat place to go located outside of Athens that is different from anywhere I have been. The staff were friendly and informative about the farm overall. They also have a store located on the property that sells blueberry everything, even blueberry coffee. There are plenty of opportunities to pick blueberries as they have multiple rows of berries. Great experience for kids above the age of 5 or 6 as well as a fun outing for adults. Will be back again. It was a great first-time experience overall.

★★★★★Sarah Yanga year ago

Highly Recommend! Very family oriented. Excellent Blueberry Pastries and there is a Blue Bell Blue Berry Ice Cream you must try. There was so much room and so many blueberries . Don’t rush but go early due to the sun. I recommend long socks and a hat.

★★★★★Merissa Vu12 months ago

Huge farm. Blueberries are very sweet! Happy with what we got! They provide buckets and plastic bags to collect blueberries and blackberries. Wear closed toe shoes unless you like wet feet. There are limited hand washing stations and portable potties available. Decent amount of parking available. There are a few outdoor benches and seating. The mini shop is cute with jams, honey, baked goods and icecream! Please note that there are no pets, or large bags allowed. *Went there for the second year and they expanded! We enjoyed our visit and got a lot of large sweet blueberries by the pound!
