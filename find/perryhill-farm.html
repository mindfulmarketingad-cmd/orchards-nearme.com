★★★★★Susan Hofstad Lewis2 years ago

I have been to this well kept and beautiful farm three times to pick cherries and peaches this summer, and apples this fall. They have a nice selection of varieties and keep their website up to date on what is ready to pick. There is clear signage directing where to pick and large big wheeled wagons to haul your load. The central weighing station, porta-potties and hand washing station are clean and well tended. Three things to know before you go: No dogs allowed. Cash or check only. Some of the trees are on rolling hillsides that may be difficult to navigate if you have limited mobility.
