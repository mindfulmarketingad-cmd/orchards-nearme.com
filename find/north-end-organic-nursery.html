★★☆☆☆Russ Comstocka month ago

I’m having my backyard completely redesigned and some friends from out-of-town were visiting and wanted to gift me a tree. In advance of their visit, I went to several nurseries, including North End, to look at trees to save us all time when they arrived. I settled on a magnolia variety I found at North End; it was small but North End’s price was reasonable. When my friends arrived the following Monday, I took them to North End where we approached an employee, Greg, who was watering. My experience with Greg was that he was inconvenienced by our first question - where the magnolias were. Once we found them again, he left. My friends paid for the tree that we chose but when I told Greg I might need until Friday to pick it up, he was obviously put out and let me know North End usually only holds sold plants for a couple of days. Fine. I made arrangements to borrow a vehicle that would accommodate the tree and picked it up a couple of days later. But, frankly, North End could hardly have been less helpful. My experience at Far West Landscape the prior week was so much different. There I was twice approached by staff in the field who asked if they could help. One spent quite a bit of time showing me tree options. The tree selection was far greater, but the trees are a bit larger and definitely more expensive. Because my friends were gifting me a tree, I chose the smaller less expensive tree at North End. But if there’s a next time and I’m spending my own money, I know where I’ll go, and it won’t be North End.
