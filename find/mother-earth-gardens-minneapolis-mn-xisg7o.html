Reviews

★★★★★H. Freckles6 months ago

An exceptional neighborhood gem that offers everything a plant-lover could want for each season. There’s convenient street parking, friendly and helpful staff, and something for everyone whether a gardener, landscaper, or collector of houseplants. I’m especially impressed with all the potting supplies, giftware, and books. Inventory changes frequently so there’s always something new to discover.

★★★★★Katharine Herman2 weeks ago

My partner and I are here nearly every weekend. They are always happy to answer all our questions and are very patient as we learn. We relied on them to help make our garden beautiful for our wedding and they’ve helped us through squash borers and over wintering perennials. We would marry them if we could.
