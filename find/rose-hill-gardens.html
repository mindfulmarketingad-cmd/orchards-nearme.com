Rose Hill Gardens is situated in a beautiful location. I really enjoyed walking the area and seeing the lovely well kept gardens where plants can be seen in a mature state. They specialize in perennials and you can shop many different greenhouses and an extensive area of hostas. I was here for a class and tour so we got to see the owner demonstrate how to make hypertufa planters. They also have them for sale as this is something they are known for. I had made one of these planters years ago and  never used it. I was inspired and purchased succulents, another specialty item for sale here. I highly recommend you stop if you are looking for plants or if you just want to walk through the lovely gardens.
