★★★★★Karen Sanders3 years ago

Had a wonderful day raspberry picking at this farm yesterday. Wow, they had so many deep-red, large, fresh raspberries! We have tried berry picking in Ann Arbor at other farms and we have only ended up with a small (1/2 a pint) container after an hour of picking. This farm weeds and clears were the berry rows are and this makes it much easier for young kids (and quite frankly me) to reach and find the berries, often hiding under the leaves. Prices are comparable to grocery stores at $4/pint but, obviously these berries are at their peak freshness. The place seems well maintained and the people running the farm are very welcoming and friendly. They even let my kids ring their huge bell hanging from the roof of the barn, which of course the kids were delighted to do! Hoping to return in the spring for some springtime u-picking.
