★★★★★Joanne L11 months ago

What a fun and wonderful experience picking berries at this fabulous farm! 5 of us city dwellers chose Heider’s Berry Farm based on reviews when compared to other farms that offered berry picking, and they were the perfect choice. There weren’t many other customers and we had a delightful time! We picked raspberries and strawberries and also purchased additional already picked veggies including zucchini, cucumbers and eggplant. We thoroughly enjoyed the picking process and everything was delicious and very reasonably priced. The staff was kind, polite and knowledgeable and we had the honor of meeting and chatting with Mrs. Heider who shared the history of the family owned farm and even took our group photo! I highly recommend Heider’s Berry Farm for a great experience with berries to take home!
