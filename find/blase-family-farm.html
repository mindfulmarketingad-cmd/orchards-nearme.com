Reviews

★★★★★Miriam Hernandez3 weeks ago

Very nice place to take your kids! Admission is free, you pay per lb of Blueberries and can purchase keepsake buckets, along with popsicles, fudge, handmade soap and even your own blueberry plants. You do walk a small stretch to fields but it's mostly shaded until the blueberry patch. There is a shaded tree you can sit by the field and also plenty of seating at entrance. Take water! Overall was great and cooled off with popsicles after. You can easily spend 2hrs here. We will be back!

★★★★★Heather Heilmana month ago

Blueberry picking was an awesome experience! Purchase buckets, walk through a short trail in the woods to a beautiful blueberry patch. You can make of day of it too by hanging out near the entrance in comfy chairs and picnic benches. Great for all ages and I recommend looking at the website for pre-trip tips and information. Support our local farmers and families get your blueberries here!
