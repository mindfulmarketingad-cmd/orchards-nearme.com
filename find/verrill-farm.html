Reviews

★★★★★Ahmed3 weeks ago

We were looking for the best strawberry-picking farm near Boston, which we'd never been to, and this place was just perfect. Today was the second day of strawberry picking for this season and we absolutely got some delicious strawberries. And yes, their strawberries are by far the best ones—very juicy, incredibly fragrant, and sweet, far superior to any store-bought strawberries. The price was $12 per person, which I think was very reasonable. They still have plenty of strawberries left to pick, and I wish I had picked more. Parking was easy as well, and we also loved their vegetarian wrap from the cafe. We will definitely go back for more picking. One note for improvement: there were no designated pathways to get to the strawberry-picking area, so it was a bit confusing, and I didn't notice any signs either. The season probably just started, so they are still figuring things out. Highly recommend. 10/10
