What a wonderful hidden gem! We passed by this place while on the way to a birthday party, and just had to stop on our way back home. I put in my Google maps on Friday 07/11/2025 peach orchards in Alabama. Scotts Orchard came up, and they were having a you pick day today, 07/12/2025. We set out on an adventure.
