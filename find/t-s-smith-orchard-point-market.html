★★★★★Justin Brummagea month ago

T.S. Smith Orchard Point Market is a great stop if you’re in the area. The baked goods are delicious, especially the pies and cookies; the pasta salad is also fantastic! They also have a nice selection of fresh produce, olive oils, jams and so much more, all well displayed and high quality. The staff were super friendly and welcoming, which made the whole experience even better.

We stopped here because it was on our way to Ocean City, when we go back to Ocean City in the summer, this will be added to our list of places to stop. The food was amazing, the people were so kind, and the store itself was adorable. The breakfast potatoes were great and my favorite was the apple cider! I am so happy we decided to try TSS!

★★★★★bob schmermunda month ago

This place is AMAZING! Best Apple Ciser Donuts in Delaware if not AMERICA! Topped only by their unbelievably delicious pies, crumble pies,coffee cakes and fresh breads! If you're going to Bethany Beach, Dewey or really any Delaware beaches or northern OC -- after you get off of 404 and make a right at the light onto RT 13, make a left at the very next light onto Redden Road. TS Smith Orchard is on the corner on your left. Go past it about 100 feet and then make a left to backtrack to the parking lot. Great fresh vegetables, wonderful jams and olive oils. Great Place to stop with young kids. Chickens roosters, goats and very kitchy knick knacks. LOVE this place and the people who work there! Delicious milkshakes and sandwiches too!
