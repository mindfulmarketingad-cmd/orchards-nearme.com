★★★★★K. Koleto3 weeks ago

The freshest, most delicious fruit I’ve ever had. My husband works at PCC and I still get the Collins Family Orchard CSA, because their fruit is even fresher and more flavorful than what PCC carries. The summer peaches are my favorite, but I also love how the fall CSA has special heirloom apple varieties that aren’t available anywhere else. Additionally, their customer service is out of this world. I haven’t needed a ton of support, but anytime I’ve emailed them, I’ve heard back so quickly I’m amazed. They have happily helped me with address changes, billing changes, and other questions. I can’t recommend them enough. Check out the CSA— I can almost guarantee there will be a location that’s convenient for you just about anywhere in Seattle, and even on the east side too.
