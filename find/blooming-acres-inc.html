★★★★★Y J2 months ago

This garden center rocks. I absolutely love the wide spacing between the aisles. I went recently on a cool, cloudy day, and it was like therapy walking around in there. While this place has a huge selection, this is definitely not a cookie-cutter garden center. The personal curation is evident. Plus, the inventory was so healthy looking that it really put the big box stores to shame. I actually only do houseplants, and I mainly stopped in to buy some coco coir for my homemade airy mix. I keep telling myself, “absolutely no more plants," but you know, it doesn’t hurt to look. I am a total Hoya maniac, and I saw that they had a beautiful sun-stressed Carnosa with vines that were like 4’ long. Even though I have an entire section of my house filled with Carnosa varieties alone, I couldn’t resist! Another huge find was a healthy Ficus Elastica Tineke, which Blooming Acres had a whole section of. My friend had been complaining of somehow killing hers (lol it’s actually possible, I guess), so I was excited to buy one for her as a gift. She was thrilled to not have to get an inferior, overpriced one off of Etsy. Since the main reason for my visit was the coco coir, I asked the owner if he had any. Fortunately, he had a bunch of the 2 cu ft (I think) bags of the Bush Doctor Coco Loco mix in stock, which I love. In fact, that is a really good mix as-is to use for rooting my hoyas in. I told him that the 2 cu ft was a little too much for me to carry into my house, and he so graciously took a jumbo planter and filled it to the top with the mix and charged me almost nothing for it! I actually needed a planter that size to put my out-of-control Tradescantia in, so it was much appreciated. And speaking of Tradescantia, I saw that Blooming Acres had these gorgeous Purple Hearts in hanging baskets that looked so vibrant and healthy, unlike the crusted mess I have at home. That really gave me the inspiration I needed to chop and restart my plant under better conditions. I also had a blast in the front area of the store, where they were remodeling. I bought some small artisan planters and those little figural planter-hugging accessories (rabbits, squirrels, etc) that I am obsessed with. I also snagged a heavy-duty Buddha head planter for a friend for her String of Pearls! If I were able to actually keep ‘String of’ anything alive, I totally would’ve bought one for myself. The staff members were friendly, and they all greeted me while I was poking around. The owner is highly knowledgeable about basically every plant and gardening topic. My only regret about the whole trip is that I forgot to ask him his name! He is super real and very passionate. Anyway, I can’t wait to go back there!
