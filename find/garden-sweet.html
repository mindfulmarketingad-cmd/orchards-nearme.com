Had the most wonderful time at Garden Sweet today! My SIL wanted local flowers for her wedding, so we stumbled across GS & it was incredible! Even being the end of the season, we spent an hour or so wandering the gardens & filling our two buckets (pictured). The dahlias were GORGEOUS & our two buckets made about 10 small table arrangements & a bouquet!! Can't wait to go back!

★★★★★Kat Bird8 months ago

We loved it! It’s very conveniently located close to the city and has a variety of fresh produce and flowers. We did the U Pick and got strawberries, apples, and flowers! Staff is very friendly and gave us tips on the best spots. We also bought organic produce at the farm stand. Family friendly as we saw several toddlers with their parents, but you definitely don’t need kids to have fun :)

★★★★★Calvin H11 months ago

Beautiful place and fun experience. The admission price plus purchase of the picking items ended up being quite fair. Probably would’ve preferred a half size strawberry basket option as we don’t need $20 worth (and seemed a bit expensive for what it was) but the number of flowers that fit in the cup was surprisingly a lot and made up for it. One $20 cup of stems made 3 good size bouquets. We were worried the summer flower options would be limited but there were still so many options and in great condition. Tons of bumblebees, butterflies, crickets, toads, etc. made for a fun experience. If I could change anything it would be to add some better merch (better graphic design - simple). Would’ve loved to buy some shirts and stickers. Thanks for a great time.

★★★★★Lilly Figueroa9 months ago

Garden Sweet in Fort Collins, Colorado is an absolute must visit! This family farm, located just a few minutes from Old Town, is a great way to get outside and connect to the community. I hope to visit Garden Sweet again and again!
