★★★★★Melissa Jack9 months ago

Champlain Orchard is absolutely fantastic! From the moment you arrive, it’s clear this is more than just an orchard—it’s a full experience. The store is filled with local goods like apple butter, fresh fruit, jellies, jams, and more. The café is cozy and delicious, serving finger foods, soups with bread, pies, and of course, their famous donuts. Behind the store, there’s a wonderful outdoor hangout space with picnic tables, a beautiful sunflower garden, and even a playground for the kids—such a thoughtful touch for families. Their cider flights are excellent, and the staff is super helpful in guiding you to flavors you’ll love. Pick-your-own is also top-notch here. They provide a clear map showing where different fruits are located and what’s in season. You can even drive right up near the orchard rows, making it easy for families. The sheer number of apple trees is incredible, and once you’re up in the orchard, the views of the countryside are breathtaking. Champlain Orchard is the perfect place to spend an afternoon—relaxing, fun, and absolutely delicious. A must-visit!
