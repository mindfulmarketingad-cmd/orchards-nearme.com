★★★★★Jeff Honaker (RealJeffedUP)a year ago

Gritt's Farm is a great spot for families and anyone looking for a day full of fall fun! The food options here are pretty impressive—there are multiple places to grab a bite, and everything we tried was delicious and perfect for the season. The hayride makes it super easy to get around between different spots, and the maze was a big hit with our group, adding a fun challenge to the day. This place is definitely geared toward kids and families, with lots of activities to keep everyone entertained. The only bummer was that some of the balls were missing for the shooting and throwing games, so we had to wait a bit or improvise. But overall, Gritt's Farm delivers a memorable day out and lots of great photo ops for those fall vibes!

★★★★★Bridget McClain Carpentera year ago

We had the best time!! All the staff are amazing! The attractions are for everyone. There are restrooms placed strategically everywhere throughout the farm. There are places to stop and rest. It's located in the country, remember it is a farm. Put on good walking shoes and dress the kids in comfy play clothes. We will go back every year. There are towers of hay, slides, zip lines, heavy equipment to sit in, hay rides, apple cannons, goats, and so much more. Arrive early for good parking.
