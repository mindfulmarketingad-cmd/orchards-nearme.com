★★★★★Tee Ellisa year ago

I recently had the pleasure of visiting Miller Farm for a field trip with my grandson and his class, and it was such a fun and memorable experience! The farm offers a variety of activities that are perfect for kids and families alike. The play area was a big hit with the little ones, providing them with plenty of space to run, climb, and explore. The pumpkin picking was a great hands-on activity, and the kids loved wandering through the field to find their perfect pumpkin. The corn maze added an extra layer of adventure, with plenty of laughs as the kids tried to find their way out. The kids definitely enjoyed the hayride. But let’s talk about the food because Miller Farm’s donuts are absolutely legendary! Their donuts are always fresh, flavorful, and melt-in-your-mouth good. My grandson is obsessed with the sprinkle donuts and keeps asking me when we’re going back just for those. For me, the pumpkin donut with icing on top is an absolute must-try. It’s the perfect fall treat. And if you’re visiting, don’t forget to stop by for one of their chili and cheese hotdogs. It’s hands-down the best I’ve had, and it’s worth the trip alone. Miller Farm is a wonderful spot that offers great family fun and amazing food! Highly recommend!
