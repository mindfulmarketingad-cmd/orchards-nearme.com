Went here towards the end of the season, chilly Monday morning and this place was so inviting. We were greeted by Nancy and Hobo (Chonky Cat). We had all morning to venture through all the Apple varieties, we chose from delicious red, Rome, imperial and golden delicious. Even fortunate to get some peaches that still big and juicy left on the trees. On top of the peaches, we just missed the day they pressed apples for their unpasteurized apple cider, was so good. Still was able to get a gallon. Lovely people, and perfect orchard tucked in the back by pocomoke river.
