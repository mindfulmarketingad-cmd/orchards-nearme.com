★★★★★Nina Swenka month ago

Came in around the middle of March to buy hybrid tea roses. They weren't out yet, but they let us look at them in the greenhouse and then had us view the catalog so we could decide what we wanted. They took our info and marked the ones we wanted so they could call us when they got closer to the planting time (they weren't fully rooted yet). We came back in mid-April and they still weren't quite ready, but they let us pick our specific ones. We paid for them and they tagged them as sold under our name. Last week, they called to tell us they were ready and waiting for us whenever we wanted to come get them. We came the next day and picked them up and they are so beautiful!!! We just planted them a few days ago and the Sugar Moon ones are already blooming and smell AMAZING! Can't wait for the Heavenly Scented to bloom as they were our main choice! Staff were all very friendly and helpful during every interaction!
