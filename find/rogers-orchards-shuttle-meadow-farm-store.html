★★★★☆Tinaa year ago

Beautiful orchard, and their staff was kind despite warm weather for September and being very busy on a Saturday afternoon. We loved our time in the orchard, and their apples are beautiful and they have an abundant amount of apples to choose from even so early in the season. It’s honestly impressive!! My only complaint which is why I’m not rating 5 stars… The apples are covered in a significant amount of pesticide. I know it’s needed for larger orchards like Rogers to keep a good crop and insect population control. But it was incredibly overbearing. Myself, and my son broke out in hives on our hands while apple picking. You can feel the consistency of the pesticide on the apples themselves. I also thought it slightly odd that their store is selling golden delicious apples, but they’re not available to pick at the orchard right now? Just a random thought that crossed my mind… Overall, they have beautiful apples, and it is a beautiful orchard, and very nice staff. I do recommend visiting Roger’s, just be mindful that if you have sensitive skin that the orchard might not be for you. I do wonder if they changed the pesticide they used? We’ve gone apple picking before and this was a first and only time occurrence that we’ve broken out in hives and felt this type of consistency on the surface of freshly picked apples. Have fun, get sweet treats and local products, just be mindful if you have sensitive skin and go into the orchard 🙂
