★★★★☆finnigan916 years ago

This is a fun place for the family. They have a lot of different activities including a slide, giant sling shots, basketball, climbing ropes, a dirt hill for kids, corn hole, pigs and goats, as well as a kid and adult maze. They also had a great pumpkin patch and tons of choices. The maze was not very challenging but was still fun. The location is absolutely beautiful. It's right up against the mountains. We would go back here again.

★★★★★T Fitti7 years ago

Gallagher's corn maze (and slide) is different each year. The tractor/hayride was awesome and the slide and games (bean bag sling shot, basketball, corn hole) were fun. The big corn maze was difficult but the signs with questions and clues helped. The kiddie corn maze was much easier to get out of. Picnic tables with hay bales were around and port-a-potties were right up front. They did have food and drink items you could buy, but we did not purchase anything. There were pigs and goats in gated areas you could look at and pet. We loved the trip out to Clark and to Gallagher's! (CMSLS REVIEWS)
