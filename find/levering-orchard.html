★★★★☆Robbie Deaton5 years ago

While it was the first time we ever picked Apple's and it was fun, the drive back to the orchard was on a single lane dirt road which made it difficult when a car was coming in the other direction. Plus, there were only a very limited amount of places to pull off to let another car get by. On a positive note, the owner was very friendly and the apples tasted great.

★★★★★Christine LeCounta year ago

Basic no frills apple orchard. Don’t go looking for apple cider, hay rides, apple fritters, etc. It’s difficult to find since Apple Maps takes you to a gated drive way. But we found the entrance by a sign we had passed on the way. Once at the orchard, it’s a short drive down a gravel road and a little hike to the orchards. At only $1 per pound we loaded up our buckets even towards the end of the season. The view is gorgeous!! We plan to return next year for cherries and peaches 😋

The apples were great and they introduced us to some new types of apples! We picked 5 bags and are making some great homemade apples sauce!

★★★★★Jamie Simpson2 years ago

I went here the first day of sweet cherry season 2024, as cherries are my favorite fruit. I found Frank and company to be very polite and gracious. My cherries were store-quality and similarly priced. I did not pick my own as the fruit they had already picked was just too inviting, but I plan to explore this fantastic place more in the future. I found their website to be a little clunky, but useful nonetheless. And I strongly recommend the beautiful and scenic drive up from Mt Airy.
