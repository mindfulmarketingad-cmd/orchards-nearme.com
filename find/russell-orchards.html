★★★★★Barry Aleo8 months ago

A Must-Visit New England Gem! Russell Orchards is an absolute delight and the perfect countryside escape for both couples and families alike! From the moment you arrive, you’re surrounded by that warm, rustic charm that makes fall in New England so special. There’s truly something for everyone — from apple picking in their beautiful orchards to scenic hayrides that wind through the farm, and a fun petting zoo that kids absolutely love. The pumpkin patch is picture-perfect and makes for great seasonal photos. The country store is a destination in itself — filled with homemade treats, farm-fresh produce, delicious baked goods, and the most heavenly fresh cider donuts you’ll ever taste. Don’t miss their cakes and jams, all made with love and local ingredients. And for adults, the wine tasting bar is a wonderful surprise! Their Apple Ice Wine is a standout — sweet, crisp, and unlike anything else. Whether you’re visiting for a romantic afternoon, a family adventure, or just to enjoy some fresh air and great food, Russell Orchards captures the best of farm life and fall fun all in one place. Highly recommended!

★★★★★Hannah Lee9 months ago

Ambiance: Market-style store selling a variety of their own goods. There's fresh apples, baked items, fruit wine, and ice cream. Activities: Wine flights, a small pumpkin patch, and of course the apple fields where you can pick your own. Apple Donuts: Incredible! Fun to watch them made fresh, though I wish they came out warm. The sugar and cinnamon is in the inside rather than outside! Strong cinnamon flavor, not overly sweet. Apple Roll-Up: Warm and delicious, just like an apple pie in roll-up form with fresh apples and cinnamon. Apple Cider: The best! Fresh, slightly tart, mostly sweet, and so refreshing. Pure apple flavor.
