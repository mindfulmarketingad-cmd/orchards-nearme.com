★★★★★Christina Ma year ago

We've never been peach picking before and definitely didn't know we could do so in Florida. This farm was a welcome surprise. There are a huge abundance of peach trees, all putting off the most intoxicating smell as you stroll through. The peaches are sweet and delicious. There are also blackberries available to pick and they were quite big on this trip. Then there is a sunflower field across from where the fruit is, which you can be taken to on a short golf cart ride. The sunflower field is so whimsical and lovely. You can pick them and fill a jar or just go in to take some beautiful photos. They also sell a variety of jams, honey and fruit trees back at the main farm entrance. So happy we stumbled across this gem of a farm, we will be making this an annual trip.

★★★★★BJ Ezella year ago

Oh boy, our usual close to our county u-pick peaches location sold out their land to a developer so we were on the hunt for a new location. We’d visited the Graham Farm for U-Pick veggies but found out by calling only the location closed but the original farm was in business and peaches were being picked now!!! And oh what a business! Plenty of parking with quick check-in. We did the bring your own 5 gallon bucket and pick to you hearts content for $35. The restrooms are clean. The family is a great example of why we love and support local growers. They are so modest and humble. Mr Graham took us out on a cart and only upon asking did he identify himself as head of a 6th generation farm family. He dropped us off at just the right spot and showed us how to gently bend the taller branches down to get the sweetest, juiciest peaches ever. Our picking time was a week day between 5 & 7 PM. Our bucket was overflowing and we offered to pay for the overage. We were told a bucket full is a bucketful - $35 only. People don’t believe we have peaches growing in FL let alone sweet juicy ones. We’re going back this week before the season is over as everyone helped us eat that bucket full up in a matter of a few days. Now to pick some for peach cobblers and preserving!! THANK YOU FOR KEEPING AMERICA FED with grace and humility. The king, queen, & princess (Thurman & Dr BJ Ezell along with sister Shirl Dukes) from the city of Hernando in Citrus County FL.
