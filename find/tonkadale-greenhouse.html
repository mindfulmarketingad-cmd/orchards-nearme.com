★★★★★Mirna B.a month ago

Tonkadale is hands down one of the best garden centers around. They have an amazing variety of plants, flowers, and gardening supplies, and everything is always clean, organized, and easy to shop. Even when it’s busy, there’s rarely a long line at checkout. The staff is consistently friendly, knowledgeable, and genuinely helpful. I also love the extra perks like the free water on a hot day, recycling program, and rewards points program. Those little touches really make the experience stand out.

★★★★★Sarah Huntley7 months ago

I drove from Minneapolis to check this greenhouse out. I buy plants from everywhere including Etsy. This greenhouse has a small but reasonably priced selection of carnivorous plants. All of the plants look very healthy and the options were good to either choose a small or large plant based on your expense level. The cactus room was very lovely and I saw a few that I might go back for. For now I just picked up a few small Christmas plants. The varieties of poinsettias was endless. I hope to add a few different colors to my collection since I was able to keep mine alive all year. It’s a beautifully laid out store I hope to go back soon and the drive down Minnetonka blvd is easy if you like backroads.
