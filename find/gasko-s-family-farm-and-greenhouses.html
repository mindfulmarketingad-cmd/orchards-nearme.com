★★★★★Kristen Duska2 months ago

If you love gardening or just want to wander around and get inspired, this is definitely a place worth visiting. This place is a gardener’s dream. The nursery is huge and packed with just about everything you could want with a vast supply of perennials, annuals, shrubs, small trees, herbs and vegetables. They also offer mulch, garden accessories and tools. The prices are unbeatable which is what made me take the trip here. It does get pretty busy on weekends but they keep things moving and checkout goes smooth and it’s an efficient process. It could get overwhelming but the staff are very friendly and will help direct you in the spot you to be. Just a heads up that they only take cash or credit card, so plan accordingly. Overall, an impressive selection, great prices, and plenty to explore.
