I visited Tangerines on Sunday and truly wish I could leave 5 stars because the food was fresh, the atmosphere was beautiful, and the farm view with the crops and fields makes the place feel very peaceful and connected to nature. Unfortunately, my experience with the hostess completely changed the mood of our visit. It was a very hot and sunny day (around 85 degrees), and we were initially given a table with no umbrella. We politely asked if we could move to one of the many shaded tables available, but the hostess — a redhead, I believe her name was Holly (sorry if I spelled it wrong) — refused and gave us several excuses. Her attitude was rude and dismissive from the beginning. While we were eating, we noticed that no one was ever seated at that unshaded table, which confirmed it was clearly not suitable for such a hot day. We eventually asked to speak with a manager, although the hostess chose to speak to the manager herself first. In the end, we were given the shaded table anyway, which made it clear the request could have been handled differently from the start. What was most disappointing was her behavior afterward. She seemed visibly upset and cleared the previous table in a very aggressive manner, practically throwing the silverware while preparing it for us. It felt disrespectful and uncomfortable. Customers are paying to enjoy a pleasant experience and should be treated with courtesy and professionalism. Tangerines is a lovely restaurant with great food and a beautiful farm setting, but interactions like this can really damage the experience. I hope management addresses this, because the restaurant deserves staff who contribute positively to the welcoming atmosphere.
