Reviews

★★★★★Kristen Deibele8 months ago

Went on a calm Thursday morning and had the Orchard to ourselves, it was lovely. the view from the top can't be beat. We really liked the Kindercrisps and the donuts were yummy. There's also a nice corn maze (not too frustrating of going with kids) and a playground and cornhole on the lawn to help stretch your visit. Definitely would recommend and will return in future years too!

★★★★★Mae Zena year ago

Literally the BEST hot apple cider EVER! We have been to a lot of different apple picking farms and this has to be one of the best! We love this place so much, so beautiful and our toddler could have played here all day long. The apples are also delicious and crisp, we just can’t wait to come back!

★★★★★Melba Gotay4 years ago

As soon as you pull in there is an amazing view over looking the mountain scape as far as the eye can see! It's worth the trip for the view alone but they have apple picking, pumpkin picking, a corn maze and an indoor store selling syrup, applesauce, sweatshirts, fresh ( delicious) donuts and much more. It's $5.00 for anyone over 4 to go through the maze all under 4 are free. The apples are so fresh, ripe and delicious!! We had a wonderful day there.
