★★☆☆☆Shelby Herrona year ago

They seem like sweet people but I can't recommend this place. We went there after seeing on Facebook that they were holding a Fall Festivities Event. I figured it would be a great place to take my almost 1yro son for a visit with the animals and a pony ride, especially for only $10 admission for adults. This is where things go sideways. I get the stroller through the gravel driveway to the check-in/pay counter to pay the $10 admission and am told "If paying with cash, it'll be $10." There's no mention of separate prices with cash Vs card online. Luckily, I had cash. I then get my wrist band and am told what it includes, of which isn't feed for animals (NBD as you really don't need feed) but pony rides were completely separate as well. Again, I looked over the post online, among others, nowhere does it say it's a separate fee. It's $5 for that, however, the pony didn't even have a saddle, bareback pad, anything...That mixed with how the horse was acting "hot", just too off-putting. We unfortunately just decided against it. Going off of the original opening weekend posts, they were supposed to have a few vendors. I did not see any, perhaps they were there early on and left or they had to reschedule. There may have been a post about it I missed. Bummer on that. Our son at least experienced the petting zoo, just watch your kids as the critters will try and get a nibble of a stray finger. If you know farm animals, this is pretty normal, just pay attention and you'll be good. I do think the goats should either be dehorned or separated as the one kept ramming (rather aggressively) the other animals, including the miniature horse & calf. I just felt it was all quite misleading, and a bit all over the place in regards to organization. I think they are a newer farm/business, so hiccups are to be expected. Hopefully things get better but for now, with the price vs what you get, I can't recommend it.
