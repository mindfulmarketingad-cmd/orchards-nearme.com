★★★★★Amanda P2 years ago

My family and I are from the Boston area and were visiting the Miami Beach area after a cruise from the Bahamas. We didn’t think we’d find any Asian fruits since Miami is majority Spanish fruit stands and the flea markets didn’t have much. You’d have to travel up north for Asian fruits usually about 3 hrs away. We found another Vietnamese farm nearby but this one was amazing and about 45 minutes away from Miami Beach. It was the day before we left and the owner did her best to accommodate us and I was very thankful. My parents’ favorite part of our entire vacation was visiting her fruit farm! They were so happy! It wasn’t the right season but she did offer us milk fruits and allowed us to pick from her tree, it was quite an experience. She also allowed us to see portions of her fruit farm that were still growing. My parents are already planning their next trip just to see her and her milk fruits were the sweetest, my parents felt like they were back in their home country. You NEED TO CALL/TEXT to make an appointment in case she isn’t there. But she tries her best to accommodate you. My parents were so sad we found her on our last day of vacation, they probably would’ve gone to seen her everyday 😅
