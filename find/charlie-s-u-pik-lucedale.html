Reviews

★★★★★Kerrie Hortona year ago

Love going here to get fresh fruits and veggies! We have made it a yearly tradition, started last year, to go pick their wonderful blackberries! They are huge and on thornless bushes, so it's great to take the kids with you! At their veggie farm, which is a separate location, they have readily available pickings, or you can go pick it yourself for $12/5 gallon bucket. It's best to take an all terrain vehicle or 4-wheel drive vehicle so you can drive and park at each isle. It is also best to wear a long sleeve shirt so that the pesticides don't get on your skin. They do have hand washing stations available to rinse near the checkout area. Overall it's a great place for families to get fresh food!
