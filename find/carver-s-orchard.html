★★★★★Bruce Kessler3 weeks ago

Excellent lunch on Saturday. I had the catfish and my companion had the ground beef steak. Both were cooked to order. We shared an order of apple fritters that came with apple butter. Both were excellent as well as was the service. Large, bright dining room with a nice view of an apple orchard outside of the window. Plenty of parking. Want to come back later and try the chicken and dumplings or maybe the fried chicken? Our server, Robin, was very attentive and did not let my drink get empty, which is not an easy task on a hot Saturday afternoon. Did not have time to visit the apple market next-door. The apple butter brought back fond memories of my childhood in the 1960s. Highly recommended restaurant; you won’t be disappointed!

★★★★★Jewels Newman7 months ago

Beautiful local Smoky Mountain Apple Orchard! I've been coming here for 36 years, Best fresh cider, homemade fried apple pies (come early, they can run out!), lots of apple varieties to choose from in the fall. They have a large Assortment of handmade crafts and a fresh vegetable section as well. October is hopping, lots of visitors, apples of all sorts. You can buy them by the bushel. I got 1/2 bushel of Fuji apples for $13! That's a lot of apples! Check out the handmade chocolate at the candy shop, get a jar of apple butter, a jug of cider and some local honey to take with you.
