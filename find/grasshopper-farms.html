Reviews

★★★★★Katy L2 months ago

Incredible place! Outdoor nursery with great selections and varieties of flowers, hanging baskets, fruits, herbs and spring vegetables. On the other side of the fence is a pay to enter playground which is IMPRESSIVE! Blowup bounce houses and slide. Swings and a sand pit. Gem mining is another add on but pretty fun!

★★★★☆Kelli Burke2 months ago

A cute place to visit! You have to pay for access to the playground and there isn’t a lot of shade over there but the kids enjoyed it! A little underwhelmed at the strawberries but maybe the drought we’re in has something to do with it. Ice cream was delicious and the strawberry lemonade was perfect on such a hot day. But for $5.75 it should be a little more lemonade and a little less ice. The entire cup was filled with ice and there was not much lemonade in it. Ask for light ice! They don’t offer refills. Overall a nice place.

★★★★★John-Eric Jones11 months ago

A True Local Gem! Grasshopper Farms is an absolute delight! The staff are exceptionally nice, pleasant, and knowledgeable, making every visit a pleasure. They specialize in beautiful, locally grown perennials, trees, and bushes, perfect for any garden. Beyond the plants, their charming old-fashioned general store offers a fantastic selection of specialty preserves and fresh, seasonal fruits and vegetables. You'll also find all your gardening needs, from mulch to unique lawn ornaments and plant containers. In season, enjoy fun strawberry picking, delicious locally made ice cream, and great coffee. Plus, their collection of antiques adds a unique touch, making every visit a treasure hunt. It's truly a wonderful local spot with something for everyone!
