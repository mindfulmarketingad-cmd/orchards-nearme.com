★★★★☆Debra Jarzomkowskia week ago

Clean facility, clean bathrooms. Large retail area, selling fresh peaches starting with small bags, to large bushels. There was an employeeat the peach selection explaining that although the peaches were hard, they would soften in 3 days. He was correct, they are now just right, juicy and delicious. Staff is friendly and very helpful. They are Dog friendly, outside and inside... we did not find a dog park which others had mentioned, it was raining of and on when we were there so we didn't mind too much not finding it. There is a restaurant on site with several choices of food, the 4 stars are because of the food, sadly. The hamburgers were very overcooked and dry and fries soft and cold. The cinnamon roll type dessert abs chocolate ice cream were good.. we will return..
