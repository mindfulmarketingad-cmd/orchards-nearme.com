★★★★☆Leisurely Pursuitsa month ago

I came here for strawberry picking in April and had a good time. It was $3 entry plus $2 for the optional bucket, and I had to check the Facebook page in the morning to see whether the fields would be open that day. I was surprised how large the fields were, and although some people seemed to strategize starting at the farthest end, there were plenty of ripe strawberries across all rows. You just have to make sure to lift the leaves all the way up because there were a lot of good hidden strawberries. I really took my time finding the perfectly ripe ones, but other people finished within 30 minutes. Be aware there are random bees, wasps, and other flying insects around the plants — not a lot but I was glad I wore long sleeves. The strawberries weren't especially sweet and they were also on the smaller side, but I think they still tasted good. I also bought other vegetables and fruits inside and ate them for dinner that night. Strawberries came out to $4.99 a pound and the bucket they sell fits about 4-5 pounds. I would go again next season primarily for the experience.
