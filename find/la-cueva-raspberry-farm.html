Reviews

★★★★★Davina Louise7 years ago

This Sunday we went to the raspberry farm in “Mora” and got some delicious treats! We go at least once around this time of the year and are never disappointed! The cafe has local favorites and sandwiches, raspberry sun tea, raspberry slushees, as well as raspberry ice cream that you can get several ways. Our favorite is the raspberry sundae, half vanilla half raspberry, topped with their homemade raspberry topping. Their brisket is a delicious addition to the menu and pairs nicely with their simple yet delicious vinegar style coleslaw. If you walk across the street you can visit the store where you can purchase different kinds of raspberry and local treats. We never leave without some of their raspberry jam. If you stop at the right time of the year, usually late summer/early fall you can pick your own raspberries to take home.
