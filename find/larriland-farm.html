★★★★★Travis Satiritz8 months ago

Larriland has become a fall staple for me and several of my friends. It’s a quick 40-45 minute drive from Baltimore and it’s perfect for either a half or full day trip. Depending on the time of year you go, you’ll be able to pick flowers, herbs, various vegetables, berries, apples, and pumpkins. In the fall there’s ample apple and pumpkin picking which is a perfect way to get in the fall spirit. Always check the website and/or call the hotline before leaving home so that you know what can be picked. I’d recommend getting to the farm early because it will get busy and if you have your heart set on apple picking, it’s possible that if you come too late the fields will be closed. Also, since they have so many different kinds of apples you’ll want to ensure that the ones you want are available for picking on that day. In addition to pick your own, they have a red barn store that is packed with their own fresh produce and other goods such as locally baked bread, local honey, candles, and apple cider (seasonal). Also on the weekends they make and sell fresh apple fritters and warm or cold cider by the cup. There’s ample seating around the red barn to enjoy their goodies or to bring your own picnic.
