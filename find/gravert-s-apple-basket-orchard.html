★★★★★Brad Kamrath3 years ago

It’s the perfect sized orchard to pick your own apples; easy to navigate and make your way around the entire orchard, yet large enough to provide a great variety of apples. The trees were so healthy and productive. I was amazed by how many fresh apples were available to be picked. I went for the honey crisp apples and they were some of the best I’ve ever had. If you prefer not to pick your own, then there’s a nice store with pre-picked apples. The staff was also incredibly helpful in answering all our questions. I would definitely recommend visiting Gravert’s!

★★★★★Nancy Jacobs7 years ago

I was amazed at all of the thought and hospitality at this place. You can pick your own apples or you can buy inside their store. The apples are of #1 quality. I've never gotten a bad one from them. They also sell honey made from local bees, homemade pies (and I know for a fact they make their own!), an occasional different kind of fruit. Very nice. Something you must experience yourself. Family owned and operated too.
