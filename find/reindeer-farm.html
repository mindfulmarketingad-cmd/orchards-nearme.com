★★★★★Maryann M.a month ago

What a wonderful authentic Alaskan experience! The Reindeer Farm in Palmer allowed us to interact with many different animals and even a diverse collection of reptiles! The park is lovingly owned and operated by a family who employs local residents, including students, who are all extremely dedicated to educating visitors and providing care and attention to these animals. We spoke to Denise who owns the farm with her husband. Her family is passionate about their work, especially the rehabilitation and life-saving efforts when animals arrive injured or or are deserted at birth. We benefited so much from up- close encounters with these beautiful animals and their terrific employees. Mike drove us on the tractor ride and shared insights and paid careful attention to young guests as well as adults. Dana 's passion for the Alaska and the animals was outstanding. Will, a new employee, urged us to interact with the four-week old baby reindeer told us of bottle feeding those new arrivals in the farmhouse and his connections with the animals and the owners. Birdie helped us to overcome our jitters in the Reptile House and encouraged us to touch the snake she has coiled around her neck like a necklace! Tristan made certain we had plenty of food for the reindeer and showed us how to approach them gently. Andrea and Chris made our ticket purchases and our bit of shopping for small gifts fun and easy. We had a wonderful afternoon, full of beautifully cared for animals and the people who truly love their work and interacting with the visitors as well! Don't miss this a visit to this exceptional place!
