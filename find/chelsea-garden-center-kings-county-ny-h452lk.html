This will be my go to spot for plants, especially larger pieces. A little out of the way for us, but completely worth the trip. Every plant looked in great condition and well cared for. Everything was well organized. They have great selection of indoor and outdoor plants and pots. The staff is super helpful and friendly. We picked up a small pine and a pot for our balcony and are thrilled with choice.

★★★★★Julie Olivares6 months ago

What a fun gem of a garden center. I always like to search for plant stores when I’m in different areas, and on a side quest from ikea.. stumbled upon here. The staff was helpful. Plants are well taken care of. I was informed they have another location in Williamsburg as well! I look forward to checking them out in the future. I couldn’t leave empty handed, and left with a small Norfolk pine and a poinsettia— along with a few watering trays (can’t really ever have enough of those). Anyway, 10/10 would recommend to any plant lover.. I look forward to visiting again in the Spring.
