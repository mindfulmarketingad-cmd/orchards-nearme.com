★★☆☆☆John Newton9 months ago

I don’t normally leave bad reviews as there is enough negativity in the world. But need to air it out. Bought an arborvitae $55 two months ago. It was troubled but with limited inventory in mid summer I selected one that needed some attention due to a browned branch. Long story short it didn’t survive 1 month. The other three we purchased are still thriving, as well as the two from last year. I understand the policy here at Rosie’s as a 50% credit within a year. This is obviously a plant they shouldn’t have sold me or anyone. I asked for a replacement and was denied. I will not return to work with Rosie’s as this is the second plant in two years that has not made it. We have bought 23 other plants from them that are still thriving in our garden. We have now lost over $300 with Rosie’s plants. Asking for a replacement was too much. Good luck and choose wisely as you could lose a lot of money here. I will go elsewhere.

★★★★★Kewa Jianga month ago

I've been to several nurseries, plant shops, large garden centers in the area and downtown. For individual backyard gardeners, this is the best place for both houseplants and outdoor plants. Pricing I'd say is more in the middle compared to other places I've been in a 30 mile radius, but the varieties here are 10x better and plants feel better cared for. They also rotate a lot of options! I've dialed back on nursery plants this year and started more seeds and bulbs because coming here every week is not financially sustainable 😂. When I do get nursery plants 3/4 times out of 5 it's from here.
