Reviews

★★★★★Stacey Ramsey8 months ago

Super cute little farm! We came mid October picked out a few fantastic pumpkins (very fair pricing!) and they offered wheelbarrows to transport the them back to the car. Great touch because these pumpkins were huge! A cup of feed is included with the admission (another great touch) which you can use for the chickens, cows and/or goats. They had multiple hand washing stations too! The corn maze was fun and there was a smaller one just for the littlest! If you go, you have to try the donuts! They are amazing!

★★★★★Casey Robinson7 months ago

We all had a really good time here! It was really cute and their apple donuts were warm, soft, and amazing!!!! They had this long-haired goat who was hilarious!!! I always love a hay ride! The pumpkins are very affordable and are all the same price so it’s not per pound. I know they are building and expanding it as well so I’m excited to see what more will be added on by next fall.

★★★★★MCheya year ago

We came during the October festival and had a great time! We were skeptical because it seemed more geared towards kids but there are still things for adults to enjoy. The parking situation was easy peasy, you’ll be directed to an open spot. Staff were all very nice and helpful. We loved feeding the animals and finding our way through the corn maze. On our way out we grabbed a very large and reasonably priced pumpkin.

★★★★★Bekah Turbeville8 months ago

Its amazing how much you can do for only $10!! Its a beautiful location. The hay way was the perfect length and beautiful. There are a lot of options for children of different ages.

★★★★★K Herrera2 years ago

We love Cherry Place Farm and have been going each year for the past 4 years!! It doesnt ever seem to be overly crowded and has so many different activities that are all included in the admission price. They have a kids corn maze that has short stalks so you can see the kids and its a single path so they can't get lost. Has corn tables, corn grinders, and a water pump activity, kids feed the goats and cows, hayrides, mini pedal tractors the kids can ride, and a 'cow train'.
