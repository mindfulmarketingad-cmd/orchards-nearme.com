★★★★★JEJa year ago

This family farm is worth the drive. Plenty of space to spread out and enjoy a fall day. This is not a carnival; it’s a working farm, very well run and maintained by an extended farming family. The rolling terrain makes you feel like you’re out in the woods, and they have added so many fun, innovative play features for children over the course of their business. We had a blast with my 3 and 5 year old grandchildren for under $100, including admission to all activities, donuts and cider slushies, and brought home an incredible, locally crafted Boston Cream pie and a pack of u-pick apples. Visit this family farm seasonally and be blessed by this community minded operation.
