★★★★★Melissa Coverdella year ago

This was my first time berry picking, and I will be back many times this season!! The family was SO nice! There are SO many berries, and they're delicious!! I tend to shy away from farms that charge entry fees, so this was a HUGE plus!! Good job, South Barlow! Bring your own buckets/baskets, etc., check in so they can get a weight on your picking equipment (so you're not charged for the weight of your buckets), wash your hands, and head out to the field!! We collected 6.5 lbs. in about 10 minutes (3 people picking)! The fresh squeezed lemonade is AMAZING!!! I don't drink lemonade often, but after trying my husband's, my daughter and I BOTH got one!! So good! They sell fresh jam, cookies, and sticky buns!! Definitely go!! They're great!
