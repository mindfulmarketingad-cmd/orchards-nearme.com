Reviews

★★★★★Mollie Verhagen8 months ago

What a lovely little place! The owners are so nice. There is a wide variety of apples and very full trees as of mid October. No entrance fee and you pay for apples by the pound. Our son loved riding in a complimentary wagon. There is a porta potty available and a few places to sit down in the orchard. No frills, laid back, great place. Definitely an annual tradition for our family!

★★★★★Colleen McCullough9 months ago

Sunflower Orchards is my favorite place to get apples in the KC metro. There are several varieties to choose from and the apples are delicious! If you’re looking to pick fruit in a peaceful, gorgeous setting, this is definitely the place to go.

★★★★★Jeremy Nelson5 years ago

We had a wonderful time here. We got a reservation through Facebook and it was not very crowded even for a Saturday morning. Plenty of good apples to pick from and peaceful and quiet! Our two year old loved picking them and there were plenty within reach. The employees were so kind and there were ladders and wagons and baskets to use!

★★★★★Kelly Esler3 years ago

A complete variety of apples to pick from without an upfront cost and you don't even have to walk far to be at multiple trees to choose from. I missed the blackberries and peaches... but I won't next year.
