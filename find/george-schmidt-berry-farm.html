Reviews

★★★★★J Daltona year ago

A great area for a fun experience. Pick Your Own (PYO) prices are cheap, so you can really get a bang for your buck. Great for individuals, couples, and families 8/2024 - I felt the need to update this review as I have continued to visit this farm over the last several years. I am continued to be impressed with the offerings, including PYO that aren't typically available at other farms like string beans and edamame. Everyone at the farm is so kind and considerate and willing to help. The quality of produce continues to exceed my expectations. I have made several phone calls to reserve large quantities of sweet corn and most recently elderberries, and love that this is an option especially for those interested in canning/preserving. My biggest "complaint" is it being cash only as I typically do not carry cash and prefer other methods of payment. While I know this is my preference, I know that this business is entitled to choose what they accept. Despite this "inconvenience" I will continue to visit and support them!

★★★★★Vicki Severn4 years ago

Love this berry farm. We picked blueberries and blackberries. We brought our own containers from home, and they had to be weighed before we picked so they can accurately calculate what we bought. Signs said they had containers you could buy if you forgot to bring your own. The staff were all so kind, friendly, and helpful and they showed us where we could do our picking. They also have other fruits and veggies like corn, cabbage, peppers, plums, peaches, great beans, corn, cantaloupe... They only accept cash, so come prepared. This is an excellent place to pick berries with your kids, too.
