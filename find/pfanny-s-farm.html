★★★★★Mark Mettler (MPRanger)10 months ago

Ruth is British so the name of this place gave caution to the encounter at first. Traditionally, in England, a ladies privates are not an invitation to discussion. We chose to reside here for the night in addition to the farm tour and the pleasantries of the farm store. Our evening, after dinner down at Jerry’s Hilltop Cafe, was enjoyed with out hosts at the glass wine bottle grain bin. The evening was also the last of the Persieds Meteor shower so we reclined looking upwards over a beverage of choice and talked until nearly midnight with our hosts about the farm and the development of the place. We enjoyed our tour off the greenhouses, the fields of tomato, corn, squash, and harvesting a wide variety of herbs in the large geothermal grow house used through the bitter Nebraska winter. Now for those of you unable to store and process fresh herbs from the farm, it is possible to select the wide variety of them already dried and packaged for use in the farm store. Knowing we would be using the herbs very quickly Ruth choose to take a few cuttings back to prepare meals ion the immediate future. Both of the owners are delightful. Gene will be happy to give you insight on how to construct your very own wine bottle grain bin of you have time to take notes. the accommodations here are choice hideaway venue. The old grey farm dog will love you for a kind pat on the back and you will be befriended for life. The chickens here are escorted about by a very loud goose. If you are new to homesteading or farming, this is a good place to schedule a tour. Looking to escape city life for the weekend, again, a good choice of value too.
