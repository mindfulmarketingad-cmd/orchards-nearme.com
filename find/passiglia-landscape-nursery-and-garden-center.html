★★★★★Jennifer Becker2 years ago

Incredibly unique houseplant selection with rare choices in mature forms. I love that there is a horticulturist on site to answer questions about the care of the more complex plants. I bought an older Thai monstera Deliciosa with incredible variegation at a price that is competitive based upon it size and health. I think their target market is fixated on landscaping and in that market they excel! Selection appears endless with landscaping decor and outdoor furnishings. I felt my educated eye came in handy because the labels in some plants were lacking in detail. I found some beautiful plants that were simply labeled "tropical variety" and a price. I found myself using Google lens to identify. While the staff was friendly they weren't super helpful until I carried the expensive monstera up to the counter. Overall I would return for the quality and selection. I would recommend them with the caveat that you walk in with the independence and education ahead of time. The monstera I acquired at this nursery is currently the love of my indoor jungle!!
