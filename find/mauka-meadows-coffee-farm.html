Reviews

★★★★★Kristi Ng4 months ago

Highly recommend stopping by! This place was recommended to us by an uber driver. You get to take a scenic downhill walk and pick fruits as you go. You can pick up a map with the locations of fruit trees - it was very fun looking for the fruits that were in season, it was like a scavenger hunt! There weren’t many fruits in season when we went, so we picked two different types of citrus. At the bottom of the hill, they will cut the fruit you picked to enjoy with some coffee samples by the pool (not a swimming pool). I liked the extra fancy coffee they had, I would have bought some beans if I had space in my suitcase. The staff were also very nice.

★★★★★Mia Vossa month ago

Incredibly well-maintained property! The infinity pool and coffee tasting area is gorgeous, even on a cloudy day. Very friendly and helpful staff. The walk down is definitely steep so take your time and make sure you are wearing sturdy shoes. They do have a shuttle back up to the top,I would definitely recommend this is you are not used to the elevation. The temperature was about 15 degrees lower than Kona which was a nice break from the heat and humidity. Definitely a must visit when in Kona!

★★★★★SKEFly2 months ago

Beautiful gardens landscape with steep downhill paved paths about 1/2 mile to the pool, coffee sampling, roasting, seating and restrooms, through the fruit, flowers and coffee grounds. Shuttle every 30 minutes back to uphill parking area. $5 per person grounds admission at bottom coffee seating and pool area.

★★★★★G M4 weeks ago

Amazing, very peaceful. Worth the visit. The gardens are well maintained and a joy to walk through. At the bottom of the hill, there is a gorgeous infinity pool and coffee to sample if you wish. While you walk down, you are able to see many different fruit trees (visitors are encouraged to pick ride fruits and sample them as well). We spent about an hour at the farm.

★★★★★Siri6 months ago

$5 per person to enter. Worth every penny. You walk down through gorgeous gardens and orchards, and finally end up at the gift shop which has free coffee samples and beans available for purchase. There’s a free shuttle to the parking lot at the end as well. You can pick ripe fruit from the fruit trees and the staff will clean and cut them for you to enjoy. We picked a couple of star fruits and oranges - they were delightful.
