★★★★★Carlos Snellenberg-Frasera year ago

Best stand/place to stop if you’re driving through the Big Island between Kona and the Volcano NP! We had a blast chatting with Barney and learning all about the dehydration process he does for the macadamia nuts on the farm and how the process enhances the flavor you get when eating them. The stand offers a variety of products and is clearly well loved by the locals as numerous folks who knew the stand stopped by while we were there. A must stop when you’re on the island!
