★★★★★BJ Bender4 years ago

Eat Wyoming is on the Cutting Edge of Local Food! They have helped us get products from both Centennial Grass Finished Beef and Taste of the Wind to become more accessible to our community throughout the state of Wyoming. They generated lots of professional content featuring our products for use on their site as well as our own. Wyoming has struggled with access to local food for a long time and we are very excited for the potential this service has as we gain momentum and people start to realize what a fantastic opportunity this truly is. Eat Wyoming is building stronger community and helping Wyoming hold onto the hard-earned dollars that our own communities generate in the state. Not to mention providing local folks with local, nutritious, and delicious food grown by and for Wyomingites. Thank you to the Eat Wyoming team!
