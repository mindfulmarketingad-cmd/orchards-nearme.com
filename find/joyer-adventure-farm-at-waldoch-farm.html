Reviews

★★★★★Jennifer H.8 months ago

We visited here for the first time last weekend with our 2 kids (3 and 5 years old). They had SO much fun! We had to leave after 2.5 hours so our youngest could nap, but we easily could have stayed a couple more hours without our kids getting bored. There is such a great variety of activities including multiple unique play structures with slides and ropes courses, bikes/trikes, a hay ride to the pumpkin patch, a train ride, old farm equipment to climb on, a really cute petting zoo, a really fun zip line, a corn maze, and more. The admission price may seem a bit high, but essentially everything is included with your admission fee except if you decide to purchase food or pumpkins from the pumpkin patch. It's well worth the price. We were there on the Friday of MEA weekend and even though it was a bit busy, it never felt crowded and we never had to wait more than a few minutes to do anything. The whole property is really beautiful and well maintained. Every single worker we interacted with was incredibly friendly, helpful, and kind. They seemed to genuinely like their jobs. We will for sure be back! Thank you for a great day!

★★★★★Amy Workman3 years ago

Super welcoming, friendly, and knowledgeable staff. There is so much to do here, everything is so well cared for, but the staff is why I'm leaving this review and will be back year after year. Also, the best mini donuts in the state hands down. Don't have life regrets like me and get the bucket. The corn maze is great, you can get pretty lost without the map.
