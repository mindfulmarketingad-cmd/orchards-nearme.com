Reviews

★★★★★Jeff Kane9 months ago

Growing up in upstate NY, apple picking was always a big part of my childhood. Now, having a child of my own, this is an activity I’ve always wanted to share with my daughter. Last year, we discovered Douglas Orchards and farm, and had an amazing experience. This year we decided to visit again, and it did not disappoint. The grounds are beautiful, and well manicured. The animals are healthy, happy, and friendly enough to allow a toddler to respectfully pet them. To top it off, the apples and trees are very well taken care of, and have beautiful natural tasty fruit on them. For the past two years, the staff/owners have been incredibly amazing people to talk with and interact with. You can tell they truly care about their establishment, as well as connecting with every single visitor. The gentleman handing out bags for the apples is always a pleasure talking to, and the woman in the gift shop was so graceful. Well worth the hour drive. I am looking forward to many more years of visiting this orchard, and creating many more memories with my family.
