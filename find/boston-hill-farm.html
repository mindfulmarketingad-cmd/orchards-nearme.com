★★★★★bobbie arrington8 months ago

My friends and I absolutely love this place. We travel up from Cambridge to enjoy a delicious lunch (they have the best soup and sandwiches) and then we amble through the trails, and just take in the beauty of nature while picking apples. We learned today that if you go early in the season (September), the apples might still be a bit sour...sweet, but sour. If you go later, after a cold snap, the apples get a sugar snap and are sweeter. I like the apples sour, I like the apples sweet. This is just information. The shop is delightful, and their cider donuts are LIFE. They are sooo good. And the staff are the salt of the earth. Just good people through and through. It's a family owned, not a big commercialized farm - just good "goodness" all around. Five and a half stars.
