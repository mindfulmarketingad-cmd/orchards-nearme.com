Reviews

★★★★★Dan Lisa Casteel3 weeks ago

Showcase of Citrus is a must see. They have rescue animals you can buy food to feed. (Goats, turtles, bison, camel, and some others) A monster truck school bus ride that takes you pass the orchard and to see the other animals on the property. You can also feed these animals so save some feed. The ride lasts about 45 minutes. They have a store to buy items. We bought salsa, jam and peaches. When we visit again we plan to buy the peach wine and green apple wine, they were very good. The ice cream and orange juice drink was really good. They have bathrooms and handwashing stations. Before we left the kangaroo was moving around with a little joey on the pouch. This is a really cool place. The grandkids enjoyed it too

★★★★★Julie Kloft4 months ago

Love the animals! This place is fabulous around the holidays! Truly great photo ops, so bring a selfie stick or a willing photographer! Buy the big bucket of food and split it into smaller buckets to share. The animals are super friendly! The tortise loves the lettuce! Take the Monster Truck ride! You do not have to climb up in there, it's even wheel chair accessible. Feeding the camel was amazing! Do the Capybara meet and greet! So worth it!

★★★★★Sonia Roelofsena week ago

It's a nice place to take the little ones. They have lots of animals to see and pet. The giant monster truck ride is fun for the kids. The bus driver was very entertaining. The pineapple Dole whip ice cream and the creamsickle drink were delicious. Staff are really friendly. We had a great experience and will be coming back.

★★★★★I’ll Try It6 months ago

What a fun and quaint place to take the kids for to an immersive animal experience. There was no cost to enter, and the buckets of food that you could purchase to feed the animals was fairly priced. There were animal encounters where keepers would showcase various residents, and the staff overall was super friendly! There was plenty for adults as well from the Tractor Bar with a LOT of tasty beers on tap, to the Honey House & General store with free honey samples and plenty of canned goods.
