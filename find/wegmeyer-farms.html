★★★★★Laura Spriggs Graya month ago

This is the home farm for Wegmeyer Farms who grow the most delicious strawberries in Northern Virginia. During strawberry season - still ongoing as of this review - they offer online reservations for u-pick strawberries in their fields (secondary u-pick location at Oatlands) as well as pre-picked buckets of berries for sale. There is a shop at the home farm offering baked goods, preserves and every other item you can make with strawberries. We purchased a pre-picked bucket as well as a fabulous strawberry pie and strawberry syrup. Please note: access to the farm is partially on unpaved roads which are also narrow in places, including a 1 lane bridge.

This is the best pumpkin patch in the area, provided you are up for climbing the big hill! I take my daughter here annually. I love the fairytale pumpkins and gourds. It has not been crowded when we visited, but we usually go on a weekday afternoon. If you have difficulty climbing hills then this may not be the best patch for you. This is a wonderful place to get pictures of your family and they have several fall displays of pumpkins.

Wegmeyer Farms, located in Hamilton, Virginia, is a popular family-owned farm known for its pick-your-own strawberries, pumpkins, and other seasonal produce.
