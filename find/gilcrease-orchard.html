★★★★★Tye gentile2 weeks ago

Early Saturday morning out for a stroll in search of some peaches. What a beautiful place. First time here. I took my walker because I figured it would be a hike. 2.5 miles later I have peaches! I might say that my walker worked out well despite going through the orchard. It was hard to find ripe ones because they were picked over. Hundreds of people must trek through every week. The peaches are better than the store bought ones. We used to have a peach tree at home before the wind destroyed it. Now, those were the sweetest peaches ever. The ones we got at the orchard were okay. They were fresh.
