★★★★★Amy Bird7 years ago

Daisy Chain Farm is a beautiful example of what makes Belfast such a special place. The old barn and house are charmingly surrounded by sunny rolling meadows, strawberry fields, and apple orchards. You pick your own organic berries which taste like an all together different species from the California grocery store variety- sweet and perfumed and warm from the sun. Daisy is lovely, so stay for lemonade, and don’t forget to buy her homemade jam- it makes the most wonderful Christmas gift!
