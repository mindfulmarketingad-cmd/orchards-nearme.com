Nurseries are one of my happy spots. I could just walk through them all day. I've bought outdoor plants and shrubs here. I've picked up fun fairy garden items for my kids. ★★★★★Ben Watson6 months ago

Desert Foothills Gardens Nursery is a standout garden center if you’re looking to enhance your outdoor space with beautiful desert-adapted plants and unique garden features, from vibrant cactus and native shrubs to striking fountains and pottery that give your landscape character and charm; the staff are friendly and clearly love what they do, offering helpful guidance on plant selection and landscape ideas that work in Arizona’s climate.
