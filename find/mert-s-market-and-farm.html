We love Mert’s!! It is such a hidden gem and one that we will absolutely be returning to each year. It’s one of my son’s favorite fall spots - he loves all the fun activities they have for kids. ★★★★★Brianna Petersona year ago

Mert's Market has quickly become a fall favorite for our family! When we visited, they had local vendors on-site, including a donut truck and pop-up petting zoo. It’s clear they care about supporting the community. The family grows, picks, and sells their own pumpkins. I really appreciated that it was free to get in (which is hard to find these days), and the pumpkin prices were reasonable. They had a great selection —carving and baking pumpkins, gourds, and even some fun specialty colored pumpkins. They also have haybales, cornstalks, decorative corn, and fall knickknacks for sale too. The staff were wonderful, and the entire process was a breeze. We will be back this fall to make more memories!

★★★★★Jessica Whitea year ago

Mert’s Market & Farm has been our family’s go-to spot for fall fun since 2020. It’s not too big, not too crowded, and offers an exceptional variety of quality pumpkins. From classic orange to fanciful Cinderella, dramatic green to sunny yellow, smooth to bumpy, tiny to XL, Mert’s has them all—at fair prices and a convenient Venmo payment system. You can pick your own or skip the field to shop the abundant selection of pumpkins, gourds, hay bales, and corn stalks around the building. They also partner with local sellers to provide unique decor and treats. Our kids have fun running through the corn maze and playing yard games. Make this your first stop for all things fall!
