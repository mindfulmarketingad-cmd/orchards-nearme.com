★★★★★Courtney Stanzionea year ago

A must-visit. We knew Madden’s for their unmatched pots and wide selection of plant materials, but didn’t realize it transforms to a winter wonderland for the holidays! ★★★★☆Michele L.5 years ago

I visited Madden Family farms after a friend had recommended I stop by because they were having a 50% off end of season sale. As a serious plant mom, I knew I had to visit to see what treasures I could find. Knowing it was an end of season sale I knew the nursery wouldn't be packed with plants and what would be there wouldn't be in it's prime condition (that's understood for most end of season sales). Yes, I was able to see a lot of succulents that were still in great condition ($1.80) and all hanging baskets of many sizes were only $5! I grabbed a couple hanging baskets and also found two baskets of string of tears as well! I was happy with these finds. I also picked up a couple christmas items and a statue that looks like my dog (why not). Sale at 50% was cash only otherwise it would be 30% with credit card. I did notice a couple plants with some webs (as many nurseries inevitably have some pests like spider mites!) but I always strongly recommend bringing plants home to be isolated and treated/sprayed before you place them with your other plants. I met the owner, Melinda, who was so very nice and friendly to me during check out. She even explained that they would be getting in a new shipment of houseplants in two weeks! There's a small lot for parking and while I was there (and left for cash) I came back to no spots. Not sure what to do if that happens on a busy day. I just waited for someone to pull out of their spot. Not the end of the world but maybe helpful for others it you are planning a trip. BONUS this place is dog friendly! I love, love, when I can bring my fur child along to shop for plant children lol. Will definitely be back!
