This nursery carries great products, which is why I’ve returned several times over the past 10 years. Unfortunately, the customer service has been consistently disappointing. I tend to shop here, have a frustrating experience, stay away for a while, and then come back hoping things have improved—but that hasn’t been the case. In general, staff interactions feel unwelcoming. When I’ve asked questions, I’ve often felt like I was inconveniencing them rather than being helped. Most recently, I purchased bagged mulch and was told to pull up and someone would assist with loading. After waiting for a while with no help, my 9-year-old son and I ended up loading the heavy bags ourselves. Staff could see us from inside, but no one came out to assist. I truly wish the service matched the quality of the products, but based on repeated experiences, I can’t justify more than one star.
