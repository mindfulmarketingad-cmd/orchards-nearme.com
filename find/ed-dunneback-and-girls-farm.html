★☆☆☆☆Patty Wals-Hernandez10 months ago

Lots of charm, but customer service misses the mark. Great concept, but poor execution when it comes to hospitality. I visited Ed Dunneback & Girls Farm with a friend and was excited because the place itself is beautiful. It’s more than just a farm—it’s a café, cider & IPA and full of photo opportunities. On the surface, it feels like a fun spot to spend the day. Unfortunately, the customer service really fell short. The staff seemed very young and inexperienced, and it showed in the way the crowd was handled. With a line of people waiting, there was no sense of urgency, professionalism, or attention to the guest experience. For the amount of money we spent, it was disappointing to feel like customer care wasn’t a priority. This place has a lot of potential, but unless management invests in better training and more mature service, I wouldn’t recommend it for anyone expecting good hospitality to match the atmosphere.

★★★★★Tara Perry2 weeks ago

We visited at the start of the day for strawberry picking. It was a little confusing at first because they were still getting set up, but once everything was running, the experience was wonderful. Parking is available across the street near the strawberry fields. You do need to reserve a time slot in advance, and attendance is limited. This made the experience especially enjoyable because it wasn't crowded and everyone had plenty of space to pick. The staff were friendly and organized, and we were assigned our own row. Our row had a few missing plants, but we were given permission to pick from the row next to us as well. Overall, it was a great experience. My husband and toddler both loved the strawberries and said they were very sweet. Thank you for providing such a fun family activity!
