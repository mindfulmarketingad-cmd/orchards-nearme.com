One of our favorite u-picks in the area, and the place of our annual peach picking trip! The owners and staff are so friendly and helpful. If you dont want to pick, their market is full of fresh fruits and veggies, as well as jams and other local goods. There is a playground for the kiddos, a play yard with bouncy houses and blow up slides. A sandbox with diggers, and pedal John Deer tractors! Also for kids, a cute petting zoo, and a kids train!

★★★★☆Amin3 years ago

I went there about 1 month ago. 🚜 This land has a perfect countryside feel and has domestic animals 🐐 🐔 , 🚜 tractor, cherry trees 🍒 , village children’s toys, and farm and village products. When we enter to farm park my car at the parking. Next went into the red wooden building and picked up some buckets 🪣 and some plastic bags for cherries will be picked. In addition, we paid $3 per person to enter the farm. After that, we moved toward the cherry trees by car, I think, was for carrying fodder. It took us two minutes to reach the trees. The trees were full of cherries, and those were amazing and beautiful. The cherries have a power red color, and they were so shiny. Picked cherry from the tree has a special filling; I had never done it before. Filling the buckets was not easy and took an hour. On the day I went there, the weather was sunny and hot, which made this situation more difficult. After the cherry picking was finished, we came back to the main red building of the farm. There, they weighted our buckets and billed us. By paying a little more money, they can remove the core of cherries by machine. 🌟 All in all, picking cherries and being on the farm is a full fun experience, and I believe everybody that lives in the city and downtown should experience one day living on a farm.
