★★★★★Sean M2 months ago

Absolutely Outstanding Local Nursery For Every Season! Riverbend Nurseries in Franklin is hands-down the best place to buy plants, trees, and shrubs in the Williamson County area. As a longtime customer, I've been shopping here for years, and they never disappoint. The selection is massive -- everything from beautiful perennials and vibrant annuals to healthy trees, shrubs, vegetables, and hard-to-find varieties that you won't see at the big-box stores. They grow so much right on-site, so you know the plants are fresh, strong, and perfectly suited for Middle Tennessee's climate and Zone 7 / 7b conditions. What really sets them apart is the knowledgeable and friendly staff. They're genuinely helpful, take the time to answer questions, offer great planting advice, and even helped me with landscaping ideas that made my yard look amazing. Prices are very reasonable, especially for the quality and size of the plants you get. Whether you're a serious gardener, a weekend warrior, or just starting out, Riverbend Nurseries feels like a true local treasure. Family-owned and operated since 1990, it's clear they care deeply about what they do. If you're anywhere near Franklin or Thompson's Station, do yourself a favor and stop by. You'll leave with healthy plants and great ideas -- and you'll probably be back again soon, just like me!
