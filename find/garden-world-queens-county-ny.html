★★★★☆Bernice Willins8 months ago

My family and I went there for the Fall/Halloween festival...it was fun. The decorations, the kiddie kides, the ponies, the animals were absolutely wonderful. Free entrance, but beware they charge an outrageous price for face painting, pumpkin painting, other stuff. The tickets for the rides were $30.00 for 20 tickets which isn't great because half the rides are 3 or more tickets. The bouncy house is limited to 5 minutes. The pony ride is $9.00 for about a 5 minute walk if lucky. We were all going to do the trackless train ride but no adults were permitted which wasn't right...we saw other adults on the train. They do sell snacks, pies, apple cider, candied apples all expensive. They should charge an entrance fee and include everything else in that price. Trust me... nothing is free in that place
