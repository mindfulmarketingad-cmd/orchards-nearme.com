I finally got everything to the check out to be told there was too many items and how was she going to manage to check all of my items out. Wow! The gal at the register was completely off putting. It was a huge burden to ring up my stuff. Earlier I asked if I could set a few things by the register until I got everything up there and that was a burden too. I asked to speak to a manager before I left. The manager told me she was new. Terrible customer service. If you are a landscaper buying more than I few flats, find somewhere else to go.
