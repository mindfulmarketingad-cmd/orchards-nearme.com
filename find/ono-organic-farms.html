★★★★★Paige Rileya year ago

My daughter and I enjoyed a personal tour of the farms by way of a mule (motorized). Our guide had been working there for 8 years and was very knowledgeable. We made several stops under various fruit trees, many of which I had never heard, and he cut fresh fruit and presented it to us on a cutting board. He provided bug spray, paper towels (needed for the juicy fruit), and hand wash. We spent 2 hours on the tour and finished back at a covered area where we filled a box full of fresh fruit to take with us! We paired this activity with a drive on the Road to Hana. It was a long drive, and treacherous at times, but we are glad we visited Ono!
