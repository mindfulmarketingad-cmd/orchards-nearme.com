This is an excellent specialty nursery. I live about 2 hours away now, and I still find time to make it down here a couple times a year because it's that good. They have wonderful selection of choice plants, especially shade plants and spring ephemerals, and the display gardens are also stunning. I've found the folks working here to be kind and friendly, and prices are fair. I really like they they offer a lot of plants in smaller sizes that are more affordable for grabbing 2 or 3 at a time. Overall, a wonderful place and one of my favorites out of a really good group of nurseries in this area.
