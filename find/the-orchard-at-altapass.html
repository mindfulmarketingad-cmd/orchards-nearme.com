★★★★★Pretty face8 months ago

A Must-Stop Mountain Gem What a great place to bring your family. Even after the impact of Hurricane Helene, this orchard hasn’t lost its heart. You can still feel the spirit of the place the moment you arrive—the laughter of families, the smell of fresh apples in the air, and that small-town kindness that makes you feel instantly welcome. They may be a little weather-worn right now, but you can tell beauty is already returning, and before long they’ll be more breathtaking than ever. The apples are crisp and bursting with flavor, each variety somehow tasting like sunshine and mountain air. The products are equally impressive—thoughtfully made, beautifully displayed, and clearly crafted with pride. You can sense that everyone here truly loves what they do, and that love shows in every detail. And then there’s the food. The apple pie is, without exaggeration, the best you’ll ever eat—warm, buttery, and overflowing with fresh fruit. Pair it with a cup of their hot apple cider, and you’ve got autumn in a mug. It’s comfort in its purest form. On weekends, live music drifts through the orchard, giving the whole place a charming, relaxed vibe. Kids run around with cider donuts in hand while parents tap their feet and soak in the view. It’s simple, wholesome joy—the kind that reminds you what weekends were meant to feel like. If you’re anywhere near the area, don’t just pass through—stop. Take a deep breath of that mountain air, grab a basket of apples, and let this place remind you how sweet life can be.

★★★★★Shana Ridinga year ago

A sweet stop along The Blueridge Parkway. The view is well worth the stop all in itself, but there's so much more to this gem. Each time we've visited, on the drive down the narrow dirt road entrance, our ears have been greeted with a down-home bluegrass band echoing through our windows. As you park, you see a pleasant mountain view that is gorgeous. There is a butterfly garden with photo props for a quick photo op. As you enter the building, you'll see nice little treasures to purchase for souvenirs; shirts, jewelry, jellies, etc. There is also a bathroom; very clean one at that. My kids' favorite section is the ice cream box! It has a good selection and is not super pricey. Also! If you're lucky enough to get there when they have apple pie, buy two slices, and thank me later! We like to take our ice cream and pie out on the back deck; it's decorated with table and chairs to relax and take in the lovely view. There are also a few games to play, and you can also hear the live band playing as well. It's totally worth a stop!
