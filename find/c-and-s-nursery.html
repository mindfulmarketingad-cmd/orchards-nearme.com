★★★★★Thomas Owena year ago

One of my favorite local nurseries with a huge selection of California natives, cacti/succulents, trees, shrubs, edibles, vines and garden tools and more. Plants are very well taken care of & plant prices are very competitive (better than big-box stores) to other privately owned nurseries/garden centers. If you need help, ask for Chris or Danny. The pictures I have included were taken in early November '24. Some noteworthy plants from my visit weeping Blue Atlas Cedars, and even a show-stoppwr serpintine form. I also love all the Australian & S. African native plants here: Leucodendron, Leucodpermum, Gravillia. Lots of healthy Agaves & about 4-5 types of Mangave. I love that they usually have at least 3 varieties of lavender in stock. Highly recommended for home gardeners and landscape professionals.
