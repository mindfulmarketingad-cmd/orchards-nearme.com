Reviews

★★★★★Kate Heekin8 months ago

This is by far the best apple farm I have ever been to. My family and I first visited Isom’s last year and we were impressed with the entire operation. When we arrived we were greeted by the owners and they were very kind. We took a tour of the farm and learned a lot about apples. Then we were able to pick apples ourselves. It was a lot of fun for our entire family. Grandparents all the way down to grandkids had a great time. Now, let’s talk apples. If you haven’t tasted an Isom’s honeycrisp apple, you have truly never tasted the most delicious apple there is. These apples are bright, juicy, crisp, and oh so sweet/tangy. We have not been able to consume any store bought apples since trying Isom’s. Their apples have worked well in apple pie, apple butter, baked treats, dehydrating, and of course dipped in carmel. Speaking of carmel, you MUST try one of their carmel apples. Absolutely divine and very velvety carmel. Jeanne, I hope I got the right, does a great job with these! The fresh pressed apple cider is also so so good. Definitely buy it as soon as you see it for sale. It goes quickly. We are looking forward to trying new treats from Isom’s too. They now have a pumpkin patch and my kids loved being able to cut their pumpkins off the vines. It was really fun. Overall, Isom’s Fruit Farm is a great place to visit for u-pick or store pick up for all your apple needs. I honestly couldn’t tell you how often we visit during apple season to u-pick and pick-up apples. Every experience at the farm has been 5 stars. We love this family and farm! You are doing it right, Isom’s!
