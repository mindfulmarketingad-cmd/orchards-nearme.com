★★★★★Brandon Sims9 months ago

Nice farm providing tractor rides to parts throughout. The apples and pears were tasty, plenty of developed flavor. I would go for the smaller bag next time, you can fit way more into on than I expected. Very nice.

★★★★★Marcus Anderson Sra year ago

Great destination for adult and family activities. We bought the locally made wine with a charcuterie platter and enjoyed lakeside with friends. We also had the absolute best Apple cider donuts(fresh out the grease y'all) I've encountered in a while, as a sweet treat. Although food was available to order (cheese steaks, chicken tenders, hot dogs, fries etc) we got there late and didn't get a chance to order any. The store had all manner of mango habanero salsa products, warm and chilled wine(needed more chilled in my opinion), fresh produce and baked goods. Had a real country store vibe. Tractor rides were available to go apple picking and little tractor carts for the littles to ride in as well. A huge inflatable slide deal was going on as well as a handful of vendors. Ample parking but ladies beware if you're wearing open toe shoes like my lady was you're definitely going to want to avoid the rock driveway. Also the playground behind the store was quite popular for the littles to blow off some steam. I forgot to mention the staff were pleasant and helpful, bathrooms(porta johns with hand washing stations) were available as well. I live 15 minutes away and I've never been but we're definitely coming back to hang out.

★★★☆☆Stephanie Lucasa year ago

I was pleasantly pleased with our apple and pumpkin picking experience here in October 2024. We arrived pretty early in the day and it wasn't outrageously crowded. There is a fee per vehicle to go picking and it's cheaper to pay it online before arriving. There were still apples on the trees in October and a great selection of flowers and vegetables. Apples were reasonably priced. You are taken around on a hayride which is fun for everyone. There's a store with canned goods and pies. Food trucks onsite were expensive and seemed to have a huge issue with hornets. Great outing for families.

He usually eats an apple every morning. Now he will have apples that he picked. It is great that he could see were apples came from and they are not just from a shelf in a big super market. It was wonderful getting out in the country and seeing were our food comes from.
