★★★★★Jade Klugh11 months ago

We pick blueberries here every summer as a family activity. They grow 12 varieties of blueberries to enjoy, and you pick by the gallon bucket. Filling your bucket means heaping because they don't level it off, plus you get to taste test as many as you like while you're picking! You pay $24 per bucket, cash or card, but cash is preferred. There are also other delectable goodies to purchase like honey, syrups, or handcrafted soaps. It's all great. Oklahoma summers are sunny and HOT, so bring sun protection and water. If you forget to bring water, they've usually got ice cold water for customers. They also have bug spray if you forget to bring some of your own. They're friendly and their product is excellent. I can't recommend them highly enough. If you need an activity in June or July, head to Outback Farm.
