★★★★★Ian Martin11 months ago

At first, I visited to just get my Wine Passport stamped but what I found is that they grow their own grapes and have other local wineries turn it into wine. The owner is very nice and I talked with her for a bit about the vineyard and wines. She pointed out where which grapes are grown where which is pretty cool! I tasted the St. Vincent which is so excellent that I ended up buying a bottle! St. Vincent was vinted by Whiskey Run Creek. They also have Edelweiss and Frontenac Blanc white wines. Which I will try next time I visit this orchard. They also have some really good smelling foods which I will buy next time. They have music throughout the summer and on those days they are open later than normal. I highly recommend this place to everyone.
