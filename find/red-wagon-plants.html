★★★★★Carlanne Herzoga month ago

Lovely collection of various greenhouses, very well maintained. Helpful staff. It's a bit of a slog pushing your cart around because of sloping terrain and gravel pathways. Like all greenhouses, expensive, especially the $40 hanging baskets. Bedding plants more reasonable @ 4/$5.

★★★★★Mieko A Ozekia year ago

We have planted starts in our garden from Red Wagon Plants, but this visit was our first time at their farm and we intentionally visited for the bake shop. From the moment you step in this new building, there long tables to sit and enjoy your coffee and baked goods. The display of baked goods are varied from scones to cakes. It is hard to choose, but be prepared to pick something for eating on site and some treats to bring home. Coffee and tea selection are nice along with beverages in their cooler. There are also prepared sides that are ready to go. We enjoyed eating our pastries outside with views of Camel’s Hump and gardens. Afterward we took a walk into the green houses to check out the plants they had. Madame Cutler, a small business that sharpens knives, was setting up for a couple hour on that day. Highly recommend finding this gem in Hinesburg.
