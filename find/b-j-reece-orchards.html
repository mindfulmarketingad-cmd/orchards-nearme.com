Reviews

★★★★★Vinay Patel8 months ago

Great family experience! We visited B J Reece Orchards over the weekend and had a wonderful time. It’s a great place to visit with family — there were plenty of apples to pick, and the orchard views were beautiful. The marketplace was large and had a great selection of local goods, treats, and apple-themed items. Lines to checkout were so long tht many left without buying anything. They have 3 U-pick areas so be prepared to walk. There are lots of fun activities for kids, including animal petting, the giant hamster wheel, corn pit, apple cannon, and slides — definitely something for everyone! The general admission tickets are reasonably priced at $13 and expensive one was $36 that includes all activities. Their apple bags start from $10, $20 and $40 I believe. Be prepared for lot of walking and a bit of a wait in lines, especially on weekends, but it’s absolutely worth it for the overall experience. We’ll definitely be back next season! 🍎

There are some activities for kids as well. As this is a huge farm, it would be good to have few volunteers to guide the visitors. Store products priced at a higher side.

Traveling through Georgia, we spotted a sign for the “world’s best fried apple pie” at B.J. Reece Orchards — and obviously, we had to slam on the brakes and investigate. Priorities, right? When we do, you better believe I’ll be back with an update! If you’re passing through, do yourself a favor and stop. Your taste buds will thank you. 🍎🥧
