★★★★☆William McLaughlin4 weeks ago

Rustic farm with lots of different areas to explore. The main barn has numerous take-home eats - preserves, cider, honey and more. There’s a smaller adjacent building with fast food choices and yet another similar one a few hundred yards away. A kid “village” also is nearby, as is a covered bridge, a pond, and a waterfall. All in all, there’s enough to entertain for an hour or more.
