★★★★★HVACR Mana year ago

Aspen Grove Farm is the ultimate destination for all your berry cravings. They offer a wide variety of fresh berries, including honeyberries, strawberries, raspberries, saskatoons, currants, gooseberries, and elderberries. Plus, they have frozen berries available for out-of-season needs, as well as a selection of preserved goods that you can purchase directly from their website. Last year, I took my wife and kids to their u-pick days, and we had an amazing time! We enjoyed adding the fresh berries to yogurt, cottage cheese, and ice cream—delicious! Support local farmers and treat yourself to Aspen Grove’s fantastic berries. You won’t regret it!
