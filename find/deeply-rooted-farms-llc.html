Reviews

★★★★★Mary Dake Ryerson3 years ago

So many beautiful big pumpkins, colorful gourds, and yummy winter squash to fill our pantry for the winter! Use the detour on Locust Road to get to the farm on Route 72 in Harwinton. There is construction on the road just past the farm entrance. Look for the orange PYO pumpkin signs, and the field filled with adorable “pumpkin people” scarecrows that the kids have made.

★★★★★Heather Hanscom3 years ago

I go here yearly for the strawberry crop and they never disappoint! The staff is super friendly and helpful and the berries are delicious. The asparagus was phenomenal this year and I cannot wait to visit again!!

★★★★★Jilliana year ago
