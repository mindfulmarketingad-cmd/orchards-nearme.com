Reviews

★★★★☆Ashley Morikawa9 months ago

Great outside atmosphere at this place. Many tables near the restaurant and down below. We stopped here multiple times during our trip as many things close in this area at the beginning of the week. We were able to find multiple gluten-free options. The gluten-free burger buns were extremely fluffy like a real bun. Loved the sweet potato fries. The chili fries were loaded too much with chili and couldn’t eat all of it. The tiny tacos were definitely not tiny. They were overflowing with meat. The meat was delicious. Be careful with some of their hot sauces 🔥. The apple ginger juice was tasty; lightly sweetened. Their ice cream is a must have. My wife really enjoyed the unique flavors. There’s music outside even when there’s not a band.

★★★★★Jorge R8 months ago

What an experience this was! This place was recommended by someone in Marble, and we’re so glad we took their advice—they were absolutely right. We went on a weekday, so it wasn’t busy at all, which worked out perfectly. It felt like we had the entire place to ourselves. The property is large and full of things to explore: there’s an indoor market, outdoor seating with a stage and activities, and quirky art and items scattered throughout. Nearby, there’s also a campground, an orchard you can wander through, and even a vineyard. It was a truly magical experience, made even better by visiting on a quiet day.

★★★★★Laura Bevan11 months ago

The cafe had great food and a really fun atmosphere. We will definitely plan a stay again next year and maybe even again this fall.
