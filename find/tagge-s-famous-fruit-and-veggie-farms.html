★★★★★D R Lewis10 months ago

Great fruit stand with a multigenerational family commitment to local produce and homemade preserved foods. They've committed to keeping their land under agricultural production and selling their own (and other local) fruit and vegetables direct to the public. They're carrying on a historic tradition on the old Hwy 89 Fruit Way, offering fresh produce at good prices. Melons, corn, peaches, tomatoes (among other produce) not to be missed. Not the biggest stand, but the best one to visit.
