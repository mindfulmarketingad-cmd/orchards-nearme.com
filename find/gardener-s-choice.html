★★★★★Jenny Walker3 months ago

Gardener’s Choice is, without question, one of the best nurseries I’ve ever visited. Every time I walk in, I’m immediately impressed by the variety and the quality. I’ve purchased everything from magnolia trees to roses to stunning red honeysuckle, and every single plant has been healthy, vibrant, and clearly well cared for. You can feel the difference—it’s not just inventory, it’s thoughtfully nurtured greenery that actually thrives once you bring it home. What I especially appreciate is the consistency. It doesn’t matter when I stop by, the plants always look incredible—lush foliage, strong roots, and no signs of neglect. That level of care is rare, and it makes Gardener’s Choice a place I trust completely when I’m adding to my garden. And beyond the plants, they have this wonderful boutique-style shop that’s honestly a gem on its own. It’s filled with everything from beautiful pots and planters to candles and unique home and garden décor. It’s one of those spaces where you go in for one thing and end up finding five more you didn’t know you needed. It adds such a creative, curated feel to the whole experience. And then… there’s the cat. Their resident cat has clearly decided the catnip section is the place to be and can often be found happily lounging right on top of it. It’s both hilarious and the best possible endorsement—if the cat approves, you know it’s good. It gives the entire place a warmth and personality that you just don’t find everywhere. Gardener’s Choice isn’t just a nursery—it’s an experience. Beautiful, healthy plants, a thoughtfully curated boutique, and a charming little cat who runs quality control. I’ll absolutely keep coming back.
