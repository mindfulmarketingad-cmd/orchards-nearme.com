Reviews

★★★★★Julie Querido3 years ago

What a beautifully scenic space to pick cherries in the rolling hills above the Columbia River. We had a lovely breeze keeping us cool on a hot day which made the picking seem faster. We were welcomed with smiles and information on the current cherries. They have buckets that hold about ten pounds picked and sturdy high wagons at the ready to borrow while we picked. They have clean port-o-potties and hand washing stations available as well. The trees were heavy with cherries so the three of us had just over 47 pounds to share with friends and family in an our and twelve minutes! I highly recommend the Early Season farm and will be back for their other farm to pick Rainier.
