★★★★★Makayla Walker3 weeks ago

Drove down from North Austin for a quick day trip to Fredericksburg and Jenschke Orchards was at the top of our to-do list. Husband and I had a blast, even with the off and on showers throughout the morning (just make sure you don't make the same mistake as me and wear shoes you don't mind getting muddy on the off chance it does rain!) The peach ice cream is delicious and we've already decided we'll bring an ice chest next time to bring some home with us! As of 6/4/26, there are TONS of strawberries for picking. We already knew before going, but they aren't currently offering pick your own peaches, as they had a bad growing season this year. However, they do have select amounts in their store for purchase, which are fairly priced and still taste great. We also brought home some preserved peaches, fresh honey, and peach cider, all which are yummy. We cannot wait to visit this farm again later this year for the pumpkin patch and Christmas trees. I hope this family owned farm continues to thrive for many, many years, as it is truly a gem!
