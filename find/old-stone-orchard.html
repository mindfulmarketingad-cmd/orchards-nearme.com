This is a lovely orchard with a most friendly and engaging owner. The fact that it’s a dog-friendly farm is a big plus! My dog and I enjoyed making friends with the resident pig and goat, as well as the turkeys, ducks and chickens! The apples are absolutely delicious!

★★★★★Jen9 years ago

Nice place, not too crowded and very picturesque. We go apple picking here every year and my kids love it! Their apples are much better than store bought and cheaper than other orchards in the area. They have a good variety of pumpkins too for 50¢ a lb. Also chickens for the kids to see, pigs and a sweet old barn dog that roams around greeting everyone.
