★★★★★o / a9 months ago

We’ve been coming to this farm every season, but this visit stood out. While blueberry picking, my son got stung by a bee for the first time. The kind lady, possibly the owner, with lovely white hair, immediately helped him, taking care of the sting, bringing an ice pack and external anesthetic, and going out of her way to lift his spirits. We didn’t get a chance to ask her name, but everyone there is just wonderful. We are so grateful for her care and kindness. She turned a scary moment into a sweet, memorable experience. The farm itself is wonderful. The berries are delicious, and their homemade popsicles and honey are a treat. Thank you for always making our visits so special!
