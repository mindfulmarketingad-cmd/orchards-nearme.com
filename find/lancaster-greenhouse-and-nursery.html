★★★★★Jordyna year ago

Store is sort of like if a greenhouse and a gift shop had a baby. They carry a lot of local items, and have a good selection of succulents and house plants. They also have a wider range of native plants than other local shops. Their selection of annuals and vegetables is a little thinner than other greenhouses this time of year, and they are pricier than other local options. However, I still recommend you stop in and see what you can find. From a sweet treat, to a bottle of wine, to a new plant baby, you will definitely find something to take home. The young ladies they have working are very kind and courteous as well. They also carry the a selection of the best salsa in all of Ohio: Jose Madrid!
