★★★★★Laura McConnell2 months ago

My daughter and I drove out to Daisy Creek Farm last Friday to pick blueberries and we had a super fun time! We were served by Jezzy and Zelly who gave us buckets and showed us the way. Wow- these sweet fat blueberries were easy to pick: the brushes loaded with ripe blueberries- and easy to reach. The walk ways were wide enough for comfort and the atmosphere was so easy and casual. As we picked we talked to, and met, other folks who were there to pick. We didn't "eat as you pick": but there is an option to do just that, and some children were and I think it made it fun for them- and easier for their parents (🙂). We also were able to pick strawberries, boisenberries, and lavender. We got to meet Jag (the owner) and discovered that this organic farm provides produce to the Natural Food Co-op (where my daughter works) in downtown Sacramento. Jag told us we can also pick, when in season, cherries, apricots, peaches, plums, pluots, raspberries, and blackberries. Everyone was super friendly and we recommend this farm for a great U-Pick experience.
