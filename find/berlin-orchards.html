★★★★★E L8 months ago

Beautiful orchards, perfect for apple picking on a warm sunny fall day. The apples were delicious, it was our second visit to Berlin Orchards. They are conveniently located close to Rt. 495. There is a store where you can buy apples if you are not in the mood for apple picking. There are also cinnamon donuts, warm apple crisp, assorted pumpkins, and more. There is also a petting zoo, and you can buy feed for the animals at the store. I highly recommend Berlin Orchards.

★★★★★jake vallante10 months ago

Berlin Orchards is hands down my favorite orchard experience in Massachusetts. The staff here are genuinely kinder and more welcoming than at any other orchard I’ve visited—you immediately feel like a guest, not just a customer. There aren’t a bunch of unnecessary rules or restrictions, which makes the whole trip feel relaxed and enjoyable. Instead of being micromanaged, you’re trusted to just explore, pick at your own pace, and enjoy the day. The atmosphere is laid-back, the trees are well-kept and loaded with fruit, and the overall vibe is exactly what you want in a fall orchard outing: authentic, friendly, and stress-free. If you’re looking for a place where the people make the experience just as great as the apples, Berlin Orchards should be at the top of your list.

★★★★☆L Toba10 months ago

We visited the orchard for apple picking. Their website states clearly for the prices & we didn't have any surprise / additional charge. We paid for $30/peck (~10lbs) for 4 pax into the orchard + wagon ride. We had a great time and relaxing at the farm. It was a beautiful farm with a short ride to apple picking area. The staff were friendly & reminded us to walk further to the end because of end of season. We managed to fill up the plastic with various types of apples including Macintosh, golden supreme, Cortland, Jona Gold, etc. After apple picking, we sat down outside the store & enjoyed the warm apple cider & apple cider donut. The cider was a bit sour & the donut was soft with sweet apple taste (yummy & must try👍). We also spent time at the farm feeding lovely donkeys, sheep & imu. There were also small colorful houses for phototaking.
