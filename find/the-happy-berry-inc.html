★★★★★Donna Toney2 years ago

Very scenic drive there. I recommend going early and be prepared to walk alot on very hilly areas. These blueberry bushes were LOADED with luscious berries! We picked about ALOT! Bring your own containers for picking, although they do have some available to use. Wear a hat,sunscreen,tennis shoes, sunglasses,and dress very comfortably. Very good prices and friendly helpful staff. We missed out on the blackberries, others had already got most. But there are others that will be ripe very soon. Only regret was not coming sooner!! We will be back!!

★★★★★Katelyn Miller3 years ago

Such a fun experience for all ages! Great family activity. Everyone loved it from great-grandparents to grandkids. Perfect activity for an overcast morning. Staff was helpful but laid back. Property was beautiful. No crowds. Blueberries were delicious. I enjoyed this more than apple picking because it took the kids longer to fill their blueberry baskets.
