★★★★★Robert Ripper2 years ago

Wide selection of threes and shrubs. The facility has a large enough parking lot and even bathrooms. When you come in you grab a cart. It is kind of heavy duty (but relatively small), all-terrain cart that you pull behind yourself. Then you walk through a web of well-maintained paths to pick your trees and shrubs. The check out is in a small building (or an oversized shed) that you enter from the rear. The door is big enough to fit the cart with your trees. I had loaded two 7-gallon Green Giants on my cart, which filled its capacity. The prices of my trees were comparable with the big box stores. This nursery has a considerably larger selection than their ever-present competitors, as well as better customer service. They even offered to help me load the trees in my car.
