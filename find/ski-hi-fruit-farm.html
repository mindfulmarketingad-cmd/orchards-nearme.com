★★★★★wmonkey33a month ago

Okay, WOW. We just got back from a wedding hosted at Ski-Hi and it was absolutely fantastic. The attention to detail and care from the staff throughout the day and into the night was exceptional. I could tell all the staff were dedicated to making the evening go smoothly, and it did!! Everything was so well organized and fantastically executed. The dance floor was very inviting and also slightly removed from the main hall area, making it easy to meander from one spot to another. People who wanted to chat and munch on delicious donuts or cider slushies away from the music could go ahead while dancers cut a rug on the dance floor. There was an Airbnb onsite for the wedding party to use, which was very convenient. The only issue was the line for the restroom, but that could be remedied by the use of port-potties in the future. There was an opportunity to feed animals at the petting zoo. The whole grounds were so picturesque, it was perfect!!! I would highly recommend this location to anyone interested in having an epic party. Way to go, Ski-Hi!!
