★★★★★Chris Flores3 years ago

I love trees and plants. I’ve very very particular with the materials I use with my landscape and the plants and trees I pick out. That being said I found a place I can almost call home. Nestled in the heart of Clovis is Willow Gardens Nursery. First thing I noticed is how warm and attentive the staffs are. They will help you answer any questions you may have. I also noticed how the staff are all smiles, that tells you they are in a good environment. They have everything you need from mulch, bark, soil, rocks…. You name it they got it. The quality isn’t compromised at all unlike other nurseries. They have decorations, vases and fountains as well for both indoor and outdoor. Let’s talk about the plants and trees now. They are all well taken care for. You can find a lot of plants that is already growing nicely on a trellis, those took my breath away if I’m being honest. Trees here are super healthy and the root system of each one is well planted. Yes I did notice and observed these things because I work in the same field. I am beyond pleased with this nursery and I HIGHLY RECOMMEND this place both to pro and non pro landscapers. I’ll be back again and again. Oh I forgot to mention about their topiaries?!? I have never seen a bunny and bear topiary before and they have it all!!! Thank me later 😊
