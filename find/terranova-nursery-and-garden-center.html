★★★★★Daphne4 years ago

Located just south of Rosery on Clearwater- Largo just above 8th Ave. There is a flashing crosswalk right near the entrance. It is nestled, you might pass it. It is right next door to the Coastal Market and Design ( you can park on the lot in the rear as well as inside the garden center) It is a local nursery. They carry the normal assortment of garden plants as well as a few really unique specimens (unique bromeliads, variegated Confederate Jasmine, a variety of Salvia in different colors, dwarf Xanadu, specialty cactus and Orchids so pretty they look fake etc.)The plants you find will keep you going back. The plants are healthy and competitively priced. The decor arrangements had changed to a htf selection, accordingly priced. The service was welcoming. They have an Agave as large as can be found in a botanical gardens. It appears to be ready to bloom.
