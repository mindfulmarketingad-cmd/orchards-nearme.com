★★★★★Aron2 years ago

Great place for for a Sunday afternoon. The have strawberry and blueberry picking that are pesticide free. As well as fresh eggs and some vegetables for sale depending on availability. The owner is super kind. Check their Facebook page to see if they are open. We went in May, and the strawberries where delicious. There were plenty of berries to pick. It was raining when we went, but we still had a good time. It takes around 20 minutes to fill a bucket up, so don't expect the visit to be longer than 40 minutes. They charge per $5.50 per lb. There is around 5 lb in a bucket. There is a porta potty with a washing station right by the picking field. Will definitely come back again for strawberry picking.
