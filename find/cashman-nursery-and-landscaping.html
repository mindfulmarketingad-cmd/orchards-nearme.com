★★★★☆Christina I2 years ago

We went to a Christmas tree branch arrangement class. When you walk into the classroom, you are greeted by the Cashman family. A plate of grandma Cashman’s fantastic homemade cookies are there to help get you into the Christmas spirit. The whole family loved it. Class cost included planter, soil, branches, and a ribbon for decoration. Lots of other decorations available for purchase including: an array of ornaments, decorative Burch sticks, bows, and more! The class starts with an instructor giving a step-by-step demonstration while you put your own planter together. Assistants walk around for questions or to help the class make their arrangements to each person’s liking. It is a little on the pricey side, but they all turned out cute and lasted all winter on the front porch.
