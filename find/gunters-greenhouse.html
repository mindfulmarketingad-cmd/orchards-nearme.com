★★★★★Mariah Bromley5 months ago

This is by far my favorite garden center in the triangle. They always have a great selection and everything is so well organized, making shopping so much easier. But most of all, I’ve purchased probably 10-15 houseplants here so far and not a single one has had any pests. Of all the garden centers I’ve shopped in the area, this is one of very few that I can say that for. On top of that, they are so welcoming and have a great rewards program that always tracks automatically with my Apple Pay. Very highly recommend! Added a picture of the stunning Peperomia Marble I got at my last visit
