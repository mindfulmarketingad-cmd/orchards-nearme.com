Reviews

★★★★★Chontida Pijanchota year ago

I recently visited this charming farm for some Pick-Your-Own peaches, and I couldn’t be more pleased with the experience. Although the farm is typically open on weekends, they offer flexible hours upon request, which was a huge plus for us. I simply texted them to arrange a Monday visit, and they were incredibly accommodating. The orchard itself is beautifully maintained—spotlessly clean and well-organized. The peaches we picked were absolutely delicious, and I was excited to see that apple and pear trees are coming into season soon. The staff was incredibly friendly and welcoming, adding to the overall delightful experience. If you’re looking for a fantastic place to pick fresh fruit and enjoy some great hospitality, this farm is definitely worth a visit!

★★★★★Rose Leea year ago

It’s the 3rd week of August. I go peach and apple picking annually at various orchards, but today was the best peach picking of all time. It was so enjoyable! I’ve been here 3 times now over the last few years for peaches, and this orchard is very quiet and well maintained - a very idyllic and peaceful location to pick fruit to your heart’s content. $3.25/lb for pick your own peaches, which, to me, is a very fair price for beautiful, delicious, fresh fruit straight off the tree. Both the yellow and white peaches were HUGE today - picked one white peach that was the size of a small pumpkin. I’d highly recommend this orchard and will definitely be back for apples in a month or so. Officially my favorite orchard now for peach picking! October 5th update: tons of apples, farm fresh eggs, and honey from hives on the farm. Another successful visit to the orchard! I’m not a huge fan of Macintosh style apples, which is what seems to mostly be ripe this week, but there were some really gorgeous golden delicious apples out there and some variety I don’t know with some more blush on them….Crisp, crunchy, and juicy. I’m pretty sure we picked more than a hundred pounds of apples haha. I love how peaceful and quiet the orchard is, and the owners are so lovely. Worth the trek from NYC.
