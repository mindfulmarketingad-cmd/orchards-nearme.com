★★☆☆☆Nara Ba month ago

Love the lay out of the place and the concept. I’ve spent $600+ this year alone and have to throw away $300 worth of plants at this point. One is infested with scale, a type of fungus gnat I’ve never seen before, and some sort of aphid or weevil. The other is infested with scale, thrips, and wood boring beetles. I’ve seen evidence of spider mites and potentially mealy bugs on a third, but haven’t been able to locate any just yet. I understand that a huge plant shop may be bound to have pests, which is why I quarantine and treat when they first come home. But at this point it seems like there’s something new coming out of the soil every other month. The entire store is heavily infested. It’s kind of irresponsible to not be regularly treating plants when they’re all close like that. Some of these pests are preventable with simple care, but now two are almost dead due to infestations. I was so sad that they’re closing, but honestly, I wouldn’t buy from here again anyway. It’s not worth the headache and putting my other plants at risk. I have never had an issue greater than basic fungus gnats shopping with Home Depot.
