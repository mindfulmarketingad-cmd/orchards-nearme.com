★★★★★Kerry Wilmerding4 months ago

We took the full Honoli’i orchard “Farm and Hawaiian Craft Chocolate Tasting Tour” recently and I have to say it was an incredible experience! Colin and Jude were so personable and passionate about educating our group and sharing their love of the cacao bean. We had no idea the effort and scientific processes that go into producing a single bar of chocolate, but after this tour we feel enlightened! The Honoli’i orchard grows the cacao beans, which is a very lengthy labor of love…..planting, pollinating, harvesting, fermenting and drying the beans before they are even at the stage of actually producing the chocolate. So interesting and educational! We sampled 14 different kinds of chocolate…... it was amazing the different flavor's you could detect….honey…..coconut…passion fruit……citrus blossoms…… creamy butterscotch and caramel and in complete contrast some chocolates were Smokey and spicy ….so many layers! We would definitely recommend this tour, no matter where you’re staying on the island, and no matter the weather! Best wishes Sheri and Kerry
