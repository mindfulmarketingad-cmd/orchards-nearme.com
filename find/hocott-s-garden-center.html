Reviews

★★★★★William Lindseya year ago

Hocott's is a little gem in the historic neighborhood of Hillcrest. Unlike larger and more commercially-minded plant vendors, it offers an idiosyncratic collection of plants, not a few of them chosen with an eye to the local climate and to local tastes. I find, for instance, a variety of mint plants at Hocott's that I don't spot at other larger nurseries in the city. In addition to its interesting assortment of garden plants and house plants — and the plants are well taken care of and healthy — Hocott's sells seeds, garden ornaments, gift items, etc. The staff are always helpful and cordial. On one occasion when we bought plants there, the person staffing the desk — perhaps the owner — offered us as a gift fresh eggs from her chickens and invited us to return at any time and get more eggs. When my spirits are glum, I like stopping into Hocott's and just walking around. I never fail to spot something interesting there or some item my garden can't do without, like a wonderful fountain I got at Hocott's at a bargain-basement price last year, which now sits in front of my house next to the Boston ferns that mark the entrance to my porch.
