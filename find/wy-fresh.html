★★★★★Nelly Genisson5 months ago

Living in northern Colorado, I’ve had to deal with the disappointment of raw milk being illegal. However, my passion for the incredible benefits of raw milk has led me to discover a fantastic solution just over the state line. I happily make the 90-minute drive to Cheyenne to stock up on an amazing selection of high-quality raw dairy products (grassfed A2). From creamy milk and yogurt to sumptuous butter and kefir, this farmstand has everything I’ve ever dreamed of! And that’s not all—they also offer delicious meat, fresh eggs, and a variety of pickled and fresh vegetables. The farmer is genuinely welcoming and takes immense pride in showcasing the work of local partner farms. It’s more than worth the trip for the quality and variety of these nutritious options!
