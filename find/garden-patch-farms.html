Reviews

★★★☆☆Jacqueline Bernardo3 years ago

There is no more picking of anything. It's pretty much a fresh produce market. There was a sign for pie and cupcakes, but it wasn't something they had in stock during our visit. They do not do donuts anymore. They also offer some handmade wooden home decor items. They have enough pumpkins to pick from, but this year they didn't do a formal patch to pick from. It's something that *might* be re-explored in the future. But for now, if you want to grab some fresh fruit and veggies, as well as honey without dealing with crowds or lines or a fuss, you can pop in. The owners are very friendly. The picnic tables outside have nice pumpkin arrangements for that quick photo op. So, if you don't have time to do a full fall picking experience and still wanna get some photos with pumpkins that isn't inside a supermarket, this is a good quiet spot to pop into coz there's no crowd at all. Probably a nice 10-15 minute stop, buy some fruits and veggies, take pix with the pumpkins, before you return to your busy day. Or you can turn it into a longer visit if you wanna talk D&D with the owner :) Their google pages/websites need to be updated, but they can't get a hold of the former webmasters, so hopefully this helps.
