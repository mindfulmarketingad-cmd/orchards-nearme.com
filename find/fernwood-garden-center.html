★★★★☆Monty R.3 months ago

This is a big garden center in Columbia, Tennessee. It's along James Cambell boulevard and has two access points/parking places on either side of the establishment. This place is beautiful and had innumerable trees, shrubs and indoor plants. The perennial/annual selection was very limited in my visit. Staff were extremely friendly and very helpful, but the place is quite pricey. You'll pay big $$$ for things, but it'll be worth it for the quality. Pretty great place!
