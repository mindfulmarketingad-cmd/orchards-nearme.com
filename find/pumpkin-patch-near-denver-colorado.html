Mountain West Pumpkin Patches

Mountain West pumpkin patches across Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, and Wyoming run a slightly compressed season from mid-September into October, shaped by the region's earlier-arriving fall frosts at altitude.
