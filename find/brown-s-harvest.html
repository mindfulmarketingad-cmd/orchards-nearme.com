★★★★☆Kimbodian Speaks11 months ago

Brown's Harvest is a farm located in Windsor and was established in 1978. They're known for their produce and pick you own fruits. There is a farm stand with some goodies in there. We bought some honey sticks and popcorn seasoning. On this visit in June, we wanted to do pick your own blueberries. We were early in the season but there were a few ready to pick. They also have strawberries, sunflowers, and more, throughout the season. They also host a ton events. We enjoyed walking around. There was a playground, maze, and garden. My kids definitely enjoyed the activities. I definitely recommend blueberry picking and can't wait to visit in the fall.
