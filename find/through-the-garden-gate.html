★★★★★Kirsten Woostera month ago

Friday, I embarked on a thrilling adventure to find the elusive snowball bush, scouring every local greenhouse I could think of. Although disappointment followed me repeatedly, I refused to give up. My determination led me online, where my sister's discovery of tons of them at the Navy exchange in Va. Beach left me smiling. Lol… Yet, it wasn't until I recalled a hidden gem of a greenhouse in Corinna, conveniently located on my route to pick up Gage and Hannah, that hope was rekindled. With optimism coursing through my veins, I anxiously drove to the greenhouse, envisioning the perfect snowball bush. Thankfully, this haven boasted an exquisite selection of flowers and multiple variations of the coveted bush. I was overjoyed to find the Korean spice viburnum, a stunning specimen with an intoxicating spring aroma, at an unbeatable price. The staff's kindness and expertise surpassed my expectations; the young lady assisting me went above and beyond, showcasing various types and helping me select the best one. She even loaded the heavy bush into my Jeep with ease! Their exceptional service and friendly demeanor left an indelible mark on my visit. Through The Garden Gate truly exceeded my expectations, and I wholeheartedly recommend it to anyone seeking an unforgettable experience.
