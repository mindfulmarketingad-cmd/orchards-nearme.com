Reviews

★★★★★Maria Oisheea year ago

In the midst of our busy lives, this place offers a touch of serenity—a truly wonderful spot to enjoy beautiful moments with the whole family. Picking fruits directly from the field is a rare and delightful experience. Not only adults, but I believe children will especially enjoy it and have the chance to learn a lot about farming and cultivation. We visited in May, so we had the opportunity to pick strawberries. The lady who provided the baskets and guided us through the process was incredibly kind and welcoming. The sweet aroma of fresh strawberries during the picking was simply enchanting. However, I unknowingly wore a pair of white sneakers, and since the soil was wet due to irrigation, my feet sank slightly and the shoes got muddy, which made exploring a bit difficult. So, I recommend wearing appropriate footwear. The place also has adorable cats and dogs. While paying the bill, I discovered they also sell organic honey, goat milk soaps, souvenirs, t-shirts, bags, and more. They even offer two non-alcoholic drink options. All in all, it was a delightful and memorable day.

Lovely farm with warm country hospitality. We went strawberry picking last year and apple picking twice this year. There's a lovely shaded picnic area with tables for eating food (you can bring your own - they don't really have any meals, just snacks and produce from their farm and neighboring farms) or drinking their hard/sparkling cider or lemonade. There's also a nice mostly shaded playground for kids as well.
