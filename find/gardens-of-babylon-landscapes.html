★★★★★Yaminah Mitchella month ago

I’ve now been to Botanical Bingo twice with Gardens of Babylon at Garage Sale Vintage in East Nashville, and it has been such a fun experience every single time. Neil O’Neil brings so much energy, personality, and genuine joy to the event that he somehow makes bingo and plant time the perfect combination. One of my favorite parts is that it really feels like a community event. There’s even been a couple of times where a kid helped call the bingo numbers, which makes the whole night even more fun and memorable. Also, special thanks to “Vanna Black” for helping pass out plants during last night’s bingo session. She understood the assignment. I’ve also attended one of the veggie workshops through Gardens of Babylon, and it’s clear that Neil is not only incredibly knowledgeable about plants, but also just a really personable and welcoming human being. If you ever get the chance, definitely visit Gardens of Babylon at the Nashville Farmers Market on Rosa Parks. Their space is beautiful, they offer classes where you can actually learn something useful, and they host events that are just plain fun. Highly recommend supporting this business. And when you go, ask for Neil. Also, check out his music too!
