If you’re heading up/down I-65 and you appreciate good produce, look no further. The peaches and watermelons are as sweet as can be, and they have some tasty homemade offerings such as peach cake and pickles. I highly recommend at least one of everything on your way through Clanton (Chilton County), AL. It’s a guaranteed stop for my family on our way to/from Florida. It’s a little pricey but par for the course - convenience. Very easy and quick access from the highway.
