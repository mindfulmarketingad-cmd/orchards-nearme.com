★★★★★Nicole Offenheiser4 months ago

One of the best plant stores/ nurseries I've ever been to! They have a huge section for outdoor plants/ bushes but I always focus on their indoor plant section which is one of the largest I've seen. They have a wide variety of aroids (pothos, antherium, philodendron, etc.) as well as succulents and cacti. I do want to emphasize that they carry antheriums because besides the "normal" flamingo antheriums I rarely see antheriums at local plant stores. Every plant I've purchased their has done amazing as soon as I bring it home. I have over 100 house plants so I have lots of experience with acclimating plants into my home. Naturally some plants are going to need more of a recovery period but I can tell that the plants they sell are healthy and well cared for and that cuts down on the acclimation period.
