★★★★★Seasoned Perspective10 months ago

Oh wow, that white flesh peach is so juicy and sweet! This place is just a fantastic local farm that you should enjoy. Their produce is excellent and it's really important to support your local farmers! They always have great pumpkin patch rides in the fall. We just picked up a yellow flesh watermelon too and it's something you should try if you've never, good eats for sure. Really like the old farm feel of this place. Beautiful!

★★★★☆David Josia month ago

Me and my family had a lovely time at Thistledown Farm. We really enjoyed petting the goats, exploring the hay maze, playing on the toy train, and seeing all the beautiful plants around the farm. It was a fun and memorable family outing. One downside was the strict no-sampling policy for the strawberries. While we were checking out and about to pay for a flat of strawberries along with other vegetables and items, an employee kind of scolded us for trying a couple or strawberries. It came across as odd and a bit overbearing, especially since we were literally purchasing the fruit at that moment. It unfortunately dampened the overall experience a little. My recommendation would be to have a small sampling bowl available, or at least approach situations like that with a little more friendliness and flexibility. Overall though, we still had a great time and appreciated everything the farm has to offer.
