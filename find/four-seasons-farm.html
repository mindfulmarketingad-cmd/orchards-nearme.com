★★★★★Robert Abstena year ago

My wife and daughter went a couple of Sundays back and absolutely loved it. They came home with about a gallon each of just picked (by them) blueberries and blackberries. Andnlet me tell ya, they were delicious. The blueberries beat the Kroger produce section berries like a rented mule. And the blackberries were large, firm examples of the blackberries I grew up with: sweet and tart simultaneously, with a light tanning note adding complexity. Store-bought blackberries are fine, good for smoothies or mixing with yogurt. THESE are the ones you want in your pancakes, scones, biscuits, jams and jellies, and dropped into that keg of Baltic Porter you and your brewing pals put down after big brew day. Bottom line: Good, solid West Virginia food and fun on the farm. Well worth a Saturday afternoon this summer.
