Fun excursion to pick your own fruits. We went to both locations. The strawberry fields were a much easier walk than the farm with 3 fruits for sure. If you are sensitive to heat, don't go in the afternoons because the walk is almost fully in the sun. We did not make it to the raspberries because starting with blueberries at the bottom, followed by what felt like a half mile to the cherries up and around then down again was too much in one day. Fruits are tasty and we all have been enjoying them since.

We had a great time with our 2 kids (3&5). The location is beautiful and maintained well and clean. No entrance fee but each should buy minimum $5 fruits. It’s not posted as don’t eat while picking and I guess it is okay to eat as anyway we see lot of fruits are fallen after spoiling in the tree itself. You might feel the fruits are little expensive than the market value, but don’t forget the worth of time you spend enjoying the pick your own 😊 They have plenty of options as blue berries, raspberries, variety of peaches, green apples, currants, etc Also they have fresh vegetables in their shop.
