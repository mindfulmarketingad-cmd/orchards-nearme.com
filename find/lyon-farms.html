Another successful visit! The strawberries are very ripe this time of the year. The picking fields are in the back this year since they are rotating crops. As soon as you drive in, a plesant scent of a farm greets you (haven't smelled that in ages). You can check in with the cashier who will provide you with baskets ($1 each). The strawberries are $2 per pound when we went. Late April seems like it is a great time to pick. There were fruits that were just coming out and other were ripe (but not too ripe where they are too soft). The strawberries were juicy and delicious. Excited to return for the other berries later in the year!
