★★★★★Ashley Gray4 months ago

We went to Bishop’s Orchards in Guilford during their Construction Time event and it was such a fun day out. Tons of hands-on activities.... mini diggers, pretend bulldozers and excavators, giant tires to climb through- if your kid likes dirt and machines, this is basically paradise. Only heads up: everyone has to pay to get in, whether you’re playing or just supervising. I get it, but if you’re rolling in with a bigger group or multiple adults, it adds up fast. There’s also a tractor ride that pulls carts... and it goes way faster than you’d expect 😂 The kids screamed-laughed the whole time. There’s a massive slide (my daughter was just shy of the height requirement ), tons of space to run around, and picnic tables everywhere. The unexpected MVP: the corn kernel barn. I cannot explain how weirdly satisfying it was to jump into a giant barn full of kernels. It’s heavy, cozy, messy, and you WILL be finding corn in your pockets, shoes, and hair two days later. Totally worth it. Food truck on-site, plenty of shady seating, bathrooms nearby, and the little market/store is great if you want treats for the ride home. We also happened to go when you could pick flowers — the bouquets came out gorgeous. All in all, 10/10 day trip. Great for kids, fun for adults who still have a little kid in them, and totally worth catching while the event is running. We’ll definitely be watching for it next year!
