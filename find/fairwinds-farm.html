★★★★★James Stephen Shaw6 months ago

I love the folks from Fairwinds Farm at the Portland Farmer's market. Good people. I'll highlight one purchase I made this summer – their cornmeal. I had four varieties to choose from Nothstine Dent Corn, Kennebec Dent Corn, Calais Flint Corn, and Floriani Red Corn. Each one, I am told, has a distinct flavor/cooking profile. I chose the Kennebec Dent Corn. I've had fun experimenting with it. The thing I love making the best is cornbread. I "grind" the cornmeal a little bit finer (in a regular electric coffee grinder) and use the recipe from Indian Head cornmeal 1 cup Corn Meal 1 teaspoon salt ¼ cup oil 1 cup flour ¼ cup sugar 1 cup milk.(or ¼ cream+¾ water or probably just water) 3 teaspoons baking powder 1 egg, beaten Corn Bread: Preheat oven to 425°. Combine com meal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl. Mix oil, egg, and milk together and add to dry ingredients Mix oil, egg, and milk together and add to dry ingredients mixing just until batter is uniform. Bake in a greased round 9" cake pan (or 9x9x2 pan) for 20 to 25 minutes. It's not sweet but it's absolutely delicious and has a nice slightly coarse texture. Friends I've served it to love it, especially my 9 year old grandson! In the unlikely event there is any leftover, I make croutons out of it by cubing it, drying it and then a very quick bake in the oven (or toast over a wood stove when I have access to one).
