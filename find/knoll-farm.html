Reviews

★★★★★Irene Fa year ago

Knoll Farm is a treasure. From u-pick blueberries to their farm stand with Icelandic wool yarn and tasty farm fresh goodies like local honey and jams, they also raise sheep, have a summer concert series and run retreats for creative people. Situated on a gorgeous hillside with walking trails and views over the mad river valley.

★★★★★Kim Wecht8 months ago

A relaxing, go at your own pace, farm with Icelandic sheep grazing, placed to view the gorgeous views around and to meditate and reflect. A store sells local and fresh items with a convenient self-checkout ability. It was a nice and reflective place to visit. I’m so appreciative of the owners quiet and gracious hospitality to allow people to walk around.

★★★★★Heather Wood11 months ago

Knoll Farm is magic incarnate. My husband and I have attended most of their summer concerts for the past two years. The music is consistently excellent ….. but it’s the venue - the farm itself - that is the star of the show. Nearly 360 degree views of the Mad River Valley and surrounding hills. “Stunning” as a descriptor does not do it sufficient justice. Plus the farm’s produce is amazing, its staff is dedicated and it’s over all mission of environmental stewardship and social justice does the heart good. If every community had a Knoll Farm in its midst, we’d be much happier & healthier world.
