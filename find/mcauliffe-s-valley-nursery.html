★★★★★Nipun Prashar2 months ago

I can’t recommend McAuliffe’s Valley Nursery in Snohomish enough! This family-owned gem has one of the greatest selections I’ve seen at any local nursery—beautiful trees, shrubs, perennials, Japanese maples, conifers, and so much more, all grown right on their fertile Snohomish Valley fields. What truly sets them apart is the best service around. The staff is incredibly knowledgeable, friendly, and genuinely helpful. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced gardener, they take the time to offer practical advice tailored to our Pacific Northwest conditions. No pushy sales, just real expertise and care. On top of that, the quality of the products is excellent—healthy, vigorous plants that establish well and thrive in the garden. Prices feel fair, especially considering how much stock is field-grown on-site. If you’re in the Snohomish area and looking for plants, pots, soil amendments, or just some gardening inspiration, make the trip to McAuliffe’s. It’s worth every minute. I’ll definitely be back again and again!
