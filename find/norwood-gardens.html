★★★★★Grant Hall2 months ago

I’ve been to Norwood Gardens a few times now and it’s easily one of my favorite spots. It doesn’t feel like your typical garden center at all—it’s super well put together and everything actually looks like it belongs together. What I like most is how helpful everyone is without being pushy. They’ll actually take the time to explain what works, what doesn’t, and why, which makes a big difference if you don’t know a ton about plants. You can tell they care more about setting you up right than just making a quick sale. Also just a really cool place to walk around. Grab a coffee, look at everything, get some ideas—it’s a whole vibe. Definitely recommend if you want your yard to look good and not just thrown together.
