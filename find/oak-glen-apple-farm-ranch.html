Reviews

★★★★☆Justin8 months ago

I've never visited the the Apple Orchard but heard a lot about it. We went on a Saturday morning right when they opened and thank goodness we did because it gets crazy busy! It's a very large farm and you have a lot of stuff to see and a lot of money to spend, picking 7 apples cost me close to $30, they charge per pound. We stopped at the shops and purchased some Caramel Apples of course. They BBQ and have a handful of food options on the property. Parking can be crazy so again arrive early. They have an area where you can picnic as well. It was a fun experience and I'll go back again most definitely.
