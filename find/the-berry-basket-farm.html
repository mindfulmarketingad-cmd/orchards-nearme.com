★★★★★Allison Goertz8 years ago

My family and I had such a pleasant time at the Berry Basket Farm last week. The attractive signage and the walk down the winding mulch path through the trees to the berry patch made for a great first impression. We were welcomed and set up with a row of berries ready for the picking. Even my 2-year-old got in on the fun as she plopped ripe berries into her small tin pail (and a few straight into her mouth)! The experience was terrific, and the fact that the berries were incredibly juicy and beyond flavorful was almost just icing on the cake. We will be back next year!
