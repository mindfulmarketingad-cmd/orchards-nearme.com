★★★★★Tony Pa year ago

The greenhouses are located approximately halfway between Clemmons and Winston -Salem and is accessible from both East and Westbound Interstate 40 and North and Southbound US Hwy 421 by using Stratford Road ( US Hwy 158 ). ★★★☆☆Shelly Skeen, ServSafe Instr2 months ago

I'm so glad this place came up in my reviewers feed!!!! I hate doing this to places I like, but sometimes the places we love to frequent doesnt always love us back as patrons. I discovered this place by my Master Gardner in 2023. I remembered how great the visit was back then, so I had to remember where it was to come back and get mint plants. If you looking for direct one on one service, you gotta wait and be patient in this greenhouse. They are small and the place is too big for them to keep up with the customers. They give attention to you once they are with you!!! I called on the phone; asked all the questions; got prices and headed that way after work. Awesome price $2.50 per!! I bought 6 and would return again as soon as the other mint was available. WHY 3 STARS? I arrived... found the lady to locate the mint. Found it... checked out...$3.00 per plant!!! I mentioned to her that I was the one who called. She was acting like it wasn't her that took my call. luckily I kept notes in my phone while talking to her showing the $2.50 quoted price (it's a small family owned greenhouse so I know it was her voice). But she stumbled a second, and said no I'm sorry thats not the price. My heart sank and my stomach turned! I called my current grower on the phone to tell him of the price change. While she was listening, I said, "we just gotta get our next 20+ plants from that other greenhouse. Because the price doesnt change over there." -- I wanted her to be sure and hear me! Sad but I won't return! Dang it!! We really do need 20+ more and ongoing supply, but I for sure gotta find some else farm or greenhouse. I'm also going to tell my former Master Gardener what happened as well.
