Reviews

★★★★★Leah Grabert5 years ago

I love this place! I drove an hour from New Orleans to check it out and I’m so happy I did. They had every plant I was looking for and then some. Their prices are awesome too. They have newer, smaller plants at lower prices and the bigger version for you to choose from. This was what I was looking for, since most nurseries you can only get mature plants and I don’t have the funds or room to purchase a $40 large, mature plant. The staff was sooo helpful too!! Jessica and her father answered my millions of questions and taught me a lot, plus gave me great recommendations on plants for my lighting. Super excited for my purchases and will be back! Just remember it is a green house so it’s HOT while you’re looking. It was tolerable for me, but I was sweating a lot. They have fans, but just be warned and dress properly if you’re going in the summer.
