★★★★★Jordan Smith9 months ago

Great cider from actual cider apples that were actually ripe (not heat pasteurized). Fantastic heirloom apple varities. They have red fleshed apples that i love and also zodiac apples. It has that sweet small town charm i remember from orchards as a child. Plus you pick pumpkins. Will definitely be back!

★★★★★Scott Forsythe (Forssa's personal account)4 years ago

This small orchard and shop are the true pinnacle of the fall experience! Anyone who finds themselves in rural northeastern Indiana. The facility is gorgeous, the apples and cider are superior, and the pastries (seemingly mostly available on weekends) are like little bites of autumn. It more than deserves its five star rating.
