★★★★★B T (Montana)10 months ago

The best produce, great atmosphere and wonderful staff! Hard to get to eastbound on I-90 compared to Thorps, but well worth the effort - watch the exits carefully and use Navigation Aids to prevent long turnaround trips. Easy to get to off the highway, the red tent is visible from the road & exit. The location is clean and charming with the old school farm stand vibe, spacious and well maintained with blooming plants and authentic farm equipment on site. In farming, harvest can be unpredictable and it takes as long as it takes, this is not a grocery store but a direct line of tree-ripe, fresh produce from farm to table. You don't see this kind of stand very often, with realistic prices and seasonal variety, and for those of us seeking a genuine and family friendly local produce stop, this is it.
