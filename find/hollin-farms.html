★★★★★Y.C. Wua year ago

We came here around the end of June for peach picking, and the experience was fun! Their peaches had great quality and was ready for picking. The apricot and plums were still a bit far from ready. There was no cherry anymore during that time. Overall, it’s a nice place for peach picking - just need to make sure that you stay hydrated when it’s hot out there.

★★★★★Talon Konkel8 months ago

My partner and I have been making Hollin Farms an annual trip. Only about an hour from Arlington and a great place to get away from the city and get into the mode of fall festivities. Beautiful views of the farm-filled countryside and excellent farm goods like apple cider donuts, honey, and other fresh produce.

★★★★★sophie williams3 years ago

Such a fun fall activity! We were pleasantly surprised they still had apple picking this late in the season (Oct 29). The options were limited but it was still fun! The pumpkin selection was gorgeous, and the apple cider and apple cider donuts were amazing. The staff was incredibly kind and the experience overall was a great one. We wish we had come a bit earlier in the season for prime time leaves, but it was still really stunning even this late!

★★★★★Diana Cervantes2 years ago

What a fun day we had at Hollín farms! It was a nice warm 80 degree day! My son had fun picking out some lettuce and apples. We got some good apples from trees but don’t forget to check the floor, we got some good ones. We got a variety of apples including the green Granny Smith apples. I know $25 for the bag might seem pricey for some but you’re helping keep the farm running. And you’re paying for the experience. My husband bought the Apple cider donuts 6 for $8.99. We all loved them! They do have limited “lunch” options if you’re hungry. They also sell water, soda, sparking water and apple cider slushy.
