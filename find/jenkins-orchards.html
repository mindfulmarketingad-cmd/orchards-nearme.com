Reviews

★★★★★Bea Gallardo-Lacourt3 years ago

Jenkins Orchards is awesome. They have a cute little market with great quality produce and an amazing variety of preserves. We went apple picking and they had two varieties, Roma and Fuji. Both of them were perfect, delicious, and really big. This is a family owned and operated business. They gave us great tips to select our apples!! Highly recommended! Also, the place is good for kids! Remember to bring cash as this place doesn’t take cards

★★★★★ARG Report8 months ago

Not sure what's up these reviews regarding "rude staff". Staff was helpful with me and my fiance when we asked how the apple picking works. Got a pick of apples, lots of fixings out of the store, and had a nice time on the lovely property. If you're city folk coming out of NOVA expecting a bougie fluffed up experience, this isn't the place for you. It's an quiet family farm, if that's what you're looking for you'll get what you came for! Cash only, know ahead of time and save yourself the stress.

★★★★★Mara Ithildinstar2 years ago

Love Jenkins Orchard. I go several times a year for their seasonal produce. Cherries, berries, peaches, apricots, apples, pumpkins, plus vegetables and canned jellies & jams, and their own honey. It’s necessary to keep honey bees in their orchard, it’s how the fruit trees are pollinated. Make sure to bring cash, they also accept personal checks. They do not take credit cards or other payment methods. Staff is friendly and helpful.
