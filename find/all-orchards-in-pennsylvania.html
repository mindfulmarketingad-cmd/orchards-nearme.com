Orchards in Pennsylvania: What You Need to Know

Pennsylvania sits within the Mid-Atlantic, and visitors across the state have access to orchards reflecting the area's growing conditions and seasonal calendar. Whether you're near Harrisburg or elsewhere in Pennsylvania, use the map below to find the closest location and check current ratings before you go.

Mid-Atlantic Orchards

New Jersey and Pennsylvania anchor a strong mid-Atlantic orchard tradition spanning apples, peaches, and cherries, complemented by farms throughout Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, and West Virginia.

Best Time to Visit a Orchard in Pennsylvania

Peaches and cherries in summer, building to a well-known apple season from September through October.

Tips for Visiting Orchards in Pennsylvania

Many orchards here grow several fruits across the season, so ask what else is ready when you call about one crop in particular.
