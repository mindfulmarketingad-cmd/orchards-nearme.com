Reviews

★☆☆☆☆Courtney Murraya year ago

My family and I attended the photo opportunity that the farm advertised. The ad stated that photos could be taken in a red truck, from 10 AM-4 PM, either for free with your own camera or for a very reasonable $50 with their photographer. It stated the event was first come/first serve and if there was a line it would move quickly. Of course, the farm owners have no real control over the length of the line or wait time and I was prepared to wait, and hoped to have a professional photo of my child taken but also was prepared to take my own. I checked the social media posts several times before going to the event in case of updates and was surprised when we got in line at 1:30 PM to hear that the professional photographer had stopped taking photos. It was disappointing but we chose to stay in line to take our own photos. What I am most disappointed in is that the professional photographer did occasionally come outside and photograph some families but not others. When it was our turn, after about an hour of waiting, the photographer actually stepped in front of me about to put my 4 year old on the truck and said he had a booking and had to photograph them ahead of us - I expressed my frustration that they were no longer doing professional pictures but somehow were still doing them for some people, and were cutting ahead of people who had been waiting, and it seemed if it was not being offered anymore that it would be frustrating and very confusing to everyone else. The photographer said it was posted on social media to book ahead and all the posts I saw specifically said no booking was available or required. Most frustratingly is that I posted in the comments on the farm FB page about my disappointment and apparently my comments (which were not replied to) have actually been deleted. I was surprised to see that there were no other comments on social media sharing my opinions since there seemed to be others who were frustrated at the event, but seeing how my comments were deleted I imagine any others were as well. Those comments would have been helpful if seen beforehand as I would have elected not to go had I known the professional photos had stopped so early. I do understand that it must have been busier than anticipated, and I do believe there is kindness in the event being offered, but the lack of organization and professionalism in the event was frustrating and even more frustrating to see the lack of professionalism where any comment that is less than complimentary is deleted from the public page.
