This review is a hard one to write however it needs to be shared for awareness especially for those like myself who really don’t have the extra money to spend on flowers but choose to do so anyways. I love visiting Churchills, it always makes me happy, I love walking the grounds. We decided to pick up some hanging plants, veggies and some David Austin roses. Roses are amazing and I’m excited for them to grow! Hanging plants are $40.00 each which is a lot to begin with so when purchasing I know I’m getting quantity and quality. $80.00 later, one completely died in 2 days and the other is hanging on. At their price tag I decided to reach out via Instagram, they answered promptly (thank you) however I know to water my flowers. I followed advice and followed up this week, they are still dead and I am so sad! I see that’s it’s been happening to others too, so just something to be aware of. It’s a lot of money wasted and it’s disappointing.
