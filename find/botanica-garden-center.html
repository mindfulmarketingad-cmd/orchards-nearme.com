I came back from a 1.5-week trip to a full-blown fungus gnat infestation in that room; dozens of small gnats scattered across the walls and window, ranging from very tiny to slightly larger. The only thing that entered that space was the plants from this store (where I had noticed a couple of bugs flying around the store during my visit but assumed it was incidental since the doors are often left open). I'll post the video, so anyone interested can judge for themselves. Infestations happen, and I understand that.
