★★★★★Ginny Mazzaa month ago

This place is MAGICAL and sprawling. It is the perfect nursery to go to get into the spirit of any season! Their selection and quality are both fantastic and there always seem to be enough farmers/gardeners around to answer questions. They have a few buildings amongst the plants with gifts and trinkets for sale too- perfect things for the whimsical people in your life! +they have 2 centrally located port-a-potties which are well-maintained. If you are bringing young children, any season is great to visit, but fall at the Farmer's Daughter feels straight out of a Hallmark movie. They have so many things to do for people of all ages. I took my son for the first time when he was 14 months old and he loved it just as much as he does now at almost 5. (Hint: If you have a sensory-sensitive family member, you might opt to visit on a week day in the fall, as the weekend festivities are very popular. There are less activities (tickets are cheaper), but still plenty for a whole morning of fun (and WAY less people).
