★★☆☆☆Phillip P5 months ago

I purchased several discounted jade plants here that initially seemed like good deals. After bringing them home and inspecting them more closely, it became clear they were recovering from a prior infestation that had killed off new growth. About a week later, I found active mealy bugs emerging on the previously damaged areas as the plants attempted to recover. This indicates the plants had an infestation that was only partially treated before being sold at a discount. Selling infested plants, even discounted ones, poses a serious risk to customers’ existing plants and home gardens. I want to support local plant stores, but I cannot continue to shop here after seeing plants presented as healthy when they were not. This is how infestations spread between collections. After I left this review, the store responded with what appeared to be a generic reply referencing a merger of multiple market locations and a newly opened location. As a customer, that response was confusing and did not align with my understanding that there is only one market location. It also did not meaningfully address the issue of selling infested plants. Fortunately, I caught the problem early and was able to isolate the plants before it spread further. Still, this was disappointing, and I expected better quality control from this store.
