Very limited on apple selection. Great bakery though. The handicapped access to the store was very far away. Bathrooms are down hill from the store. This is a good place for family fun. The apple cannon was fun for all. Be sure to get your ticket before walking back to the cannons.

★★★★★H R8 months ago

After exploring the various orchards over the years we can confidently say this one is our favorite. We love the property, food from the bakery, and how nice everyone is. We missed the u pick season this year, but we enjoyed still roaming around. And we really appreciate how pet friendly this property is. Our pup made friends and happily enjoyed all the good sniffs!

★★★★★Wattanachai CH8 months ago

Perfect family-friendly apple orchard. Peaceful fields and lovely mountain views with plenty of photo spots. U-pick apples straight from the trees crisp and sweet. There are play areas and space for kids to run around. Before you leave, stop by the farm store for fresh apples and tasty local goodies; they make great souvenirs. Relaxed vibe, fun day out—we’ll be back!

★★★★★Michael Doherty8 months ago

Great visit to our local apple orchard. I even chatted with one of the Justus family who was working who was super nice. The place is so much more than an orchard because there are lots of preserves and apples, plus the bakery, plus the bar (lots of beers on tap, games for kids, and a playground. Plenty of seating in different areas and plenty of parking. We didn’t get a chance to pick our own apples, but it’s because we came at the end of the weekend. We tried SO MANY apple goodies. I think my favorite was the fried apple pie and the apple pie slushie. I did like the empanada too. The apple cider donuts were nice too. I was a little sad they had run out of fritters but I came late in the day. My only critique is that each child activity is $5. I’d rather just pay $10 and let them do whatever they want. We will definitely be back
