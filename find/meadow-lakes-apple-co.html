Reviews

★★★★★Heather8 months ago

Overall had a great experience here. Staff was friendly and informative. Scenery was beautiful. Apples and everything they sold in the shop was delicious. They offered free coffee and apple cider samples. It was a little disorganized due to it being a very busy Saturday morning. Some sections the apple trees seemed pretty picked clean and you had to really hunt for them. Other varieties had plenty. There were buckets of each variety in the shop you could pick from if you didn't find what you wanted picking. Price was $4.50 a pound so a bit pricey but they are all organic and taste amazing so we didn't mind the splurge. Especially since picking your own is a fun activity in of itself. We were not charged any entry fees, just the price of the apples themselves. Lots of other products available in the shop as well.

★★★★★Nick8 months ago

If you want to do some Apple picking, this is the place to come. It is seasonal so before you head up check and make sure they're open and that Apple picking is going on. They have a small bakery that offers cookies and other pastries you can choose from. Staff is friendly and informative. Also have fresh Apple juice you can buy as well.

Owners are so generous with pricing. $4.50 a pound for organic apples, no entrance/picking fee and so much freedom to wander and enjoy.
