5/5 Stars: A must-visit for families! ​We had the absolute best time at Tanaka Farms today! If you have kids, this place is a total win. My little ones were so entertained by the "bumblebee" train ride, and we spent a good chunk of time just exploring all the cute photo ops around the property. ​What really stood out was how educational they made it—they actually named all the different produce growing in the fields, which kept the kids engaged the whole time. We finished the day shopping at the produce stands, and I have to say, the strawberries are out of this world. Seriously, they’re the best I’ve ever had. Highly recommend for a fun, fresh Sunday out!

★★★★★Martin Brighta month ago

A fun gem in Irvine. Fruit picking tours, strawberries in our case, a tractor tour plus they have a great little shop and animal area. As a bonus the strawberry picking ticket included a bonus "crop" and we got take home a brussel sprouts we got to pick. Theres a cafe but we were too early for it to be open. They also do other fruit picking tours or you can just come by to pick up farm fresh items. They mentioned melons in the Summer and pumpkins in the Fall. Highly recommended to book ahead of time and to arrive early - the tours were sold out for the day when we got there at 10:30am.

★★★★★Nancy Khai3 months ago

The tractor tour was fun. I took a lot of pictures of the fields. They grow a lot of local produce and it's a very child friendly place to spend time with your young ones. I went strawberry picking, but there's other U-picks and tours throughout the year for their seasonal crops. I took home a lot of celery and a box of strawberries.
