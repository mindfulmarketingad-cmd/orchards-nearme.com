This was our 1st time here and the farm didn't disappoint. There was a great selection of pumpkins to pick. You could take the hay ride or walk. The walk was wonderful as you walk past the pond. The pumpkin patch was large with a variety of pumpkins to choose from. We met the chickens & roosters, the ducks, turkey and barn cat. The gift shop had a wonderful variety of local products. The staff was helpful and friendly. We will definitely be back!

Had a great time visiting! We picked apples in the orchard and walked around the grounds. Loved how much there is to see and do, and it wasn’t too crowded or chaotic as other places are in the fall.
