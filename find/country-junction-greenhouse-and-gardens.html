Just beautiful! We all just loved it. There were two maze options when we went as well. The family that owns this farm was very kind too. There was some food options and even a small petting zoo. The sunflowers were just gorgeous. They provided funny little area for photos as well.

★★★★★kimberly Bridges5 years ago

Small rural sunflower maze which is raising money for ALS victim, plus another maze with Mother Goose stops for kids, attached to nice greenhouse with gorgeous plants and little rocky brook. Goats for kids to pet and dirt pile with little riding toys to play on. Also can book for photography shoots. They have thought of everything to make their business sustainable.

★★★★★Mia OLD ACCOUNT5 years ago

Really wonderful place to go for the day with family, and a nice drive through the country on the way there. There was a photographer named Desiree Bouiler who was doing mini shoots at the time. She's a wonderful photographer and have made friends with her since. Everyone at the sunflower farm was so friendly; and if you're looking for a good place to get some perennials, this is the place! So many different plants to choose from. Foxgloves to peach trees, lilac varieties and more. I look forward to going again next year!
