★★★★★kelly bradburya year ago

I recently hosted a birthday party for 50+ guests of all ages at Digs Garden Center, and I couldn't be happier with the experience! From start to finish, the team at Digs was incredibly helpful, easy to communicate with, and went above and beyond to make sure everything was perfect. They provided a detailed walkthrough of the event space and a useful checklist, which made planning smooth and stress-free. The layout is incredibly flexible. We could easily arrange both the indoor and outdoor spaces to fit our needs, making it a great venue regardless of weather. The indoor space has a warm, rustic ambiance that features wood accents, exposed wooden beams, lush green plants, and a large farmhouse style table. The outdoor space has cocktail style tables, as well as longer family style seating, string lights overhead, and is decorated with the garden center's seasonal flowers. If you're looking to work with generous, helpful people and for a picturesque and hassle-free setting, I highly recommend renting Digs Garden Center for your next event!

★★★★★Audrey Pattena year ago

We just had our wedding reception here, and I cannot say enough good things about DIGS! It was the perfect place for us to celebrate with our friends and family. The ambiance is eclectic and fun, and when the sun goes down the feeling is warm and inviting. Do not let the location to the highway turn you away- once the evening hits, traffic lessens and the sound is drowned out by guests and music. It's very kid friendly, as there's plenty of space for them to run around and explore. We didn't have too many kids at our reception, but the ones that were there were fully occupied with their surroundings. Parking is very ample- we had about 75 guests and there was plenty of parking right outside of DIGS. There's 2 brand new restrooms inside for guest use- they were nice and clean. Employees and Karen (the event and marketing director there) are friendly, accommodating, and knowledgeable. Special shout out to Lucy, the in-house cat who works hard doing pest control. She made an appearance at our reception, and was a big favorite with our guests. If you are looking for an outdoor venue for your reception (or event), I cannot recommend DIGS enough. I believe they have plans on expanding to offer food and drink, as well as an indoor event space. So if outdoor isn't your vibe, keep your eyes peeled on their development. Thank you, DIGS! (and Lucy).
