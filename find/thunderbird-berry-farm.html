★★★★★Grace Caughern2 weeks ago

I LOVE this place! My family and I have gone every summer for the past 7years I believe. We always end up picking more blueberries than we intended. But it's $5 a pound and absolutely worth it! The place feels very authentic, there can be a lot of different animals. I've seen a donkey, horses, and chickens. They have blackberries, raspberries, blueberries and you can even pick your own bouquet of wildflowers for $12 I highly recommend coming her for a lovely nature experience, social activity or friend activity.
