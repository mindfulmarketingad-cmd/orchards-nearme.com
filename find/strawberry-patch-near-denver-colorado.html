Mountain West Strawberry Patches

High altitude and cool nights give Mountain West strawberry patches a later, shorter season—typically late June into July—across Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, and Wyoming. The region's intense daytime sun combined with cold nights actually concentrates sugars in the berries, and local growers are often happy to talk about their high-altitude growing techniques.
