★★★★★Janelle Schwarz2 years ago

Just got a bulk delivery of Garden Mix and it looks fantastic! It’s a combo of roughly 65% sandy loam top soil and 35% compost. When I called they were extremely helpful and friendly. The Garden Mix compost component is made with dairy cow compost, although they said later in the year it is usually the black tea compost. I’m really happy with the texture of this mix, it seems to be screened from large chunks. I had previously ordered a raised bed mix from a landscape company and was very disappointed with the amount of large mulch and some stones that were in it. Very happy to have found quality at Jared’s!
