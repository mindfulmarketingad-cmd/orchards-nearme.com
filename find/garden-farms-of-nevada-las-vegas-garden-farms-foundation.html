★★★★★amy swindlea year ago

“Learn to grow with the pros” — that’s what they promise when you sign up for their Seasonal Education Course, and they absolutely deliver! When I moved to Nevada in 2016, I tried for six years to keep up the gardening tradition my grandmother passed down to me, hoping to grow my own food. Unfortunately, the extreme heat always caused everything to die in the summer, and I eventually gave up. But then I found Garden Farms, and I am beyond thankful. They send a professional farmer to my house every other week, and I get to learn alongside them in my own backyard. It’s been a game-changer! Thanks to Garden Farms, I’m finally able to grow my own food again, and I couldn’t be more grateful for their expertise and support. Highly recommend their course to anyone looking to learn how to grow successfully in Nevada!
