★★★★★Caroline Shurig Galloway3 weeks ago

North Haven Gardens in Dallas is the best garden center in the city. Their selection of native plants, pollinator‑friendly flowers, herbs, vegetables, and organic gardening supplies is great. Everything is healthy, beautifully organized, and clearly labeled, which makes shopping actually enjoyable. You can tell they take real pride in the quality of their plants. I also love that North Haven offers classes, workshops, and tons of helpful guidance — it’s the kind of place where you walk in with questions and walk out feeling confident and inspired. If you’re looking for a Dallas garden center that truly cares about its customers and the community, this is the one.

I didn’t end up finding exactly what I was looking for, and it was surprisingly difficult to get assistance.
