Reviews

★★★★★zincink11 months ago

If you are looking for roses this place has a nice selection. Their prices are excellent and they have a great variety for real gardeners. They have perennials, begonias, coleus, and many annuals to fill up your garden. They have huge buckets of Jersey tomatoes for a great price. I purchased some coral phlox and a king coleus. I love their atmosphere in their wooden greenhouse where the plants are stored and their friendly nature. The colored sheds nearby are visually adorable. I'd like to thank them for going the extra mile and gifting us some bottled water in this 90 degree heat.

★★★★☆Jason Caponetto2 months ago

Good selection and helpful staff. Prices this year seem higher than prior years. Some stuff is about average with most potted or hanging seeming on the higher end. Places like this used to be cheaper than home depot or lowes, that gap is closing.

★★★★★AP SAI5 years ago

Visited recently for the first time and was excited to see the great collection of different flower and potting plants along with a wide range of gardening related stuff. To be frank this is a one stop for all kind of stuff for the garden. Right from soil to the decorative pieces in the garden you will find a large selection of items for your garden.. Interestingly my eye caught few fresh produce as well in this place. Very accessible for those on US-130. It is in a huge area. It was like a maze while moving from one end to another end in the garden. There are small plants of different spices. There are a lot of Bonsai plants too. We visited during summer and in the afternoon. It was very hot. We will visit again and to have a very detailed look of the farm. Plan on spending at least about an hour here.
