Reviews

★★★★★Lana H10 months ago

Great orchard ! Definitely worth a visit. We are here early in the season picking apples and although some trees were not ready for the picking , there are still summer apples that you can pick. There are blueberries, raspberries and cut your own flowers areas. There is a wonderful corn maze if that is something you want to do. There is a cafe where you can get pizza, ice cream , apple cider donuts etc. There is a driver with a tractor that you can hitch a ride to areas of the orchard . Overall a great day to support your local Maine business.
