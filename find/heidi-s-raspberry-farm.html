I recently visited Heidi’s Ice Cream and tried the Burnt Honey S’mores flavor, and it was absolutely delicious! The flavor was rich and unique, with the perfect combination of sweet honey, toasted marshmallow, and chocolate. Every bite tasted fresh and creamy, and the texture was smooth and satisfying.
