★★★★★Robin Gerber9 months ago

Finally decided to stop in to see Royal Oak Farm Apple Orchard. What a hidden gem this place is. Deep in the heart of Harvard Illinois farm country just south of Lake Geneva, Wisconsin is one of the best apple orchards in the region. Huge 160 acres of orchard and all kinds of things for entertainment like shops, a bakery, restaurant, flower garden, carousel etc. They take you out to the orchard in tractor pulled wagons so you can pick literally 30 different kinds of apples. They had a wide variety of baked goods including all things apple duh and cookies, pies (which I got the cherry crumble which had 3 different kinds of cherries) and even carrot cake fudge. The highlight for me was the maze. An apple tree maze that is the largest one in America and searching the internet maybe in the World. The maze covers over 4 acres and features 1.5 miles of walking trails with 3,000 trees. At the center of it is a tower you can climb and see it all and the beautiful countryside. I’m thinkin’ a great place to pop the question. We were asked to solve a word puzzle with areas of the maze that had word clues. Thankfully with my companion, the internet, phone a friend and ask the audience we were able to solve it and get one of the best free apple cider donuts I’ve ever had. Only August 22nd through October 31st. You got to get one of their donuts!
