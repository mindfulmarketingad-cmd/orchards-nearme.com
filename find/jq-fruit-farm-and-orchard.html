Reviews

★★★★★Margie Church9 months ago

I love this place! We pick gallons of blueberries every summer. They have three acres of high bush berries bursting with flavor. And they’re huge! They are varieties cultivated from wild Minnesota blueberries so they have the tang of wild berries and subtle other sweet flavors. They are reasonably priced. Fill out a postcard to be reminded when the berries are ripe next season and you’ll get $2 off your first bucket next year. Their apples are fabulous. They have 15 varieties and the trees are trimmed to enable picking without ladders. They encourage groups to pick for projects and community pantries. We pick early and late season to take advantage of the varieties that ripen later. Wagons are provided to put the supplied baskets in. They also have a small amount of raspberries. Children are welcome to come pick. Eating is encouraged. 😉 The owners offer plenty of tips for freezing and canning the blueberries and apples.
