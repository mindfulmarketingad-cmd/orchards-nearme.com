Reviews

★★★★★Travis Garrena month ago

We had a fantastic stay at B Berry Farms. It's a very quiet, family-friendly farm. Sadly, their strawberry crop was destroyed by hail this season, but it is still absolutely worth the visit! They have tons of homemade activities for kids and adults scattered around the property, and it gets incredibly dark and peaceful at night. A few notes for RVers: Site details: We had a grass and gravel pull-through site that was surprisingly perfectly level. We opted for the single site that offers 50-amp service (there is a $15 electric fee). Rig size: We had plenty of room to maneuver our Ram 1500 and 27-foot trailer, but if your rig is much bigger, you will want to keep an eye out for low tree branches. Utilities: You can fill your fresh water tank before parking, but there is no dump station on the property. AT&T cell service was strong enough to stream a show. Must do: Order their baked goods in advance! They deliver them right to your campsite fresh and warm. The farm is also very dog-friendly with plenty of dirt paths for walking. Highly recommend this relaxing getaway!

★★★★★Casie Clarka year ago

We had the most amazing stay at B Berry Farms!! The owners were incredibly nice and even brought us warm homemade cookies when we arrived. It was a relaxing and simple weekend where our kids had tons to keep them busy on site. They had an obstacle course, zip lines, ax throwing, frisbee golf, hiking trails, a suspended bridge, a water hole (not included in base price), and so much more all included with our site. The amenities were a little primitive but it was well worth it!!! The bathroom is just a simple outhouse but for what it was, it was fairly clean! The shower house was decent for also being outhouse style. Cold showers are free (which was amazing for the hot summer) but hot showers costed a few dollars. Our only complaint for the entire weekend was that the shower houses are locked at all times (I assume due to hot showers costing money). We came back from swimming and weren’t able to get into the showers for about an hour. However, the owners were incredibly friendly and accommodating. We are so glad we stayed here and will definitely be back!!
