★★★★★Cody Pham7 months ago

After a long day of performative apple picking, we made it to Snow-Line Orchard right before closing. The building itself is the very picture of autumn - a rustic mountain farm barn-style shed with red painted walls worn down over time. The line stretched out of the large open doors all the way to the peach trees in the back, but moved relatively quickly. Inside, the air was filled with a jovial atmosphere as workers laughed and bantered with local customers. Unfortunately, the hot apple cider was all sold out as it was later in the day, but the apple cider slush was still available. The apple cider slush was cold and refreshing. In hindsight, I think I would’ve preferred the slush over the hot cider anyways because it was a relatively hot day in October. The bright side of coming late, however, was getting extra apple cider donuts since they were trying to get rid of them. We took our bag of mini-donuts to the outdoor picnic area and tore them apart. They were delicious! The donuts were hot and fresh out of the oil and dusted lightly with a layer of sugar. The most remarkable part was how they tasted just like apples. I can definitely understand why people stop by year after year.
