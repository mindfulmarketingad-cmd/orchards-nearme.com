This place was great! So many different options for picking fruit, plus helpful people to guide those of us who don’t know much about the whole process. I was happy to pay the entrance fee because I didn’t have to worry about my kids tasting new things while out in the field - it’s encouraged here! ★★★★★Skylar Thompson8 months ago

We went out for some apple picking between rains, were expecting lots of apples at the farm, and were not disappointed. It took a little foraging but despite being a little late in the season, came away with 15lbs after an hour of work. The staff are super knowledgeable about all the fruit they grow, plus there's at least one orchard cat Tiger Lily who will at least check the humans out occasionally. There are some low-star reviews here, which is surprising, and I hope people can keep in mind that this is a working farm, not an amusement park. The fruit won't look perfect (just listen to the staff about what imperfections are cosmetic and what affects the taste) but will be delicious, and certainly expect to get a little muddy and wet. We're looking forward to going back for some more apples next year!
