Reviews

★★★★☆Raychael Chavez Coombesa year ago

Not much to look at from the outside, but an absolute wonderland on the inside! They had SO many healthy gorgeous plants. Extremely affordable. Excellent selection. There were houseplants in there I had never seen before so I was super excited. They also had the same plant in several different sizes, which I absolutely LOVE! Amazing selection of fairy garden plants too. Huge selection of succulents. The prices were incredible. Everything seemed to be one standard price per pot size. The staff were friendly and greeted us right when we walked in. The only thing I didn't like was that most of the plants didn't have tags on them so you really need to know your plants. All in all, if you have the opportunity, definitely worth a stop in.
