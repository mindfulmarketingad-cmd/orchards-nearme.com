★★★★☆J P3 months ago

I recently visited Kiel Ranch Historic Park and was genuinely impressed by the experience. This park offers a unique blend of natural beauty and historical charm, making it a standout destination in North Las Vegas. It’s conveniently located just five minutes from my home, which makes it even more enjoyable to visit. The park features a variety of elements that create a rich and relaxing environment, including an artesian spring, a gazebo, picnic areas, and diverse wildlife such as frogs, fish, ducks, and even wild cats. The combination of greenery, water features, and open space provides a peaceful setting that is perfect for families or anyone looking to unwind outdoors. My dogs also thoroughly enjoyed the visit, which made the experience even better. While my overall experience was very positive, there are a few areas that could be improved. I noticed some trash in the water, and additional trash cans—especially near the water—would help maintain the park’s cleanliness. Regular maintenance and cleanup efforts would go a long way in preserving the park’s natural appeal. Additionally, there was one individual experiencing homelessness in the area, which may be a concern for some visitors. Overall, this park is a great addition to the community and a step in the right direction for North Las Vegas. With a bit more attention to upkeep, it has the potential to be an exceptional local destination.

★★★★☆Blackbeard The Biker2 years ago

This hidden gem is just 3 miles north of Fremont Street, in downtown Las Vegas. It's a historic site, and a well-maintained park. Kiel Ranch Historic Site, in the City of North Las Vegas, was one of the earliest ranches established in North Las Vegas Valley. The adobe structure is one of the oldest buildings in the region. The site is also home to an artesian well and a small wetlands. There is a picnic area and as far as I can tell, BBQ grills that have never even been used!
