My family and I stopped by on a weekend getaway and had such a good time. My daughter (5) really enjoyed herself and loved looking for the ribbons hanging off the trees with ripe apples. It was such a good easy set up to find different types of apples. The location was so peaceful and beautiful all around. The gentleman working let us know he’s 6th generation there at the orchard which just made everything even better. The best part? We bought 3 types of apples and all are absolutely delicious!
