Reviews

★★★★★Anne Trotter9 months ago

Oh my goodness the apples and pickles. I got a quart of pickles on a whim last weekend and they are *gone*. The apples are spectacular. I've had them every day all week and could eat them every day all year. They have *flavor*. Nothing mealy about these! Been chopping them up and mixing them with blue cheese and mayo, but they're good in a walnut salad too. I could easily eat a couple more bags of those apples. The trees are absolutely loaded, and so gorgeously pruned you can easily reach them. This orchard is a thing of beauty for pick your own.

★★★★★Kelly Hall4 years ago

Beautiful apple orchard! Perfect corn maze for the family. We enjoyed picking some honeycrisps this late in the season. Friendly staff who were very welcoming. Went at 5 (they close at 6) and we got some beautiful golden hour photos. Grabbed some donuts and apple cider to go as well.

★★★★★Charity Tompkins4 years ago

Always love spending an autumn afternoon at Lemieux's! The apples are easy to pick and delicious, the pumpkins are plentiful and the people are super friendly. They have a corn maze, apple cider, treats and tractor rides the kids will love, the fall scenery is beautiful and you can even bring the family dog. Look forward to this visit every fall!
