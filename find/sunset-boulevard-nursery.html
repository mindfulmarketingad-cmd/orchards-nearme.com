★★☆☆☆Jarret Spino3 months ago

I’ve shopped at Sunset Nursery several times. They have an incredible selection of succulents, a strong variety of California native species, and one of the best selections of planters I’ve seen in all sizes. If you’re looking for something specific or more unique, they’re likely to have it. Their prices are generally higher than big box stores, but that can be expected from a local nursery with a more specialized inventory. Unfortunately, the customer service has been a recurring issue. The staff are rarely warm, and during my most recent visit the experience crossed a line. About ten minutes before closing, multiple employees lined up near us and began pressuring us to leave. One employee directly asked, “Are you even going to buy something?” — which I was, until that interaction. They also started turning off the lights while we were still browsing. I understand that no one wants customers lingering past closing time, but there’s a professional and respectful way to communicate that. This felt coordinated, aggressive, and unnecessarily demeaning. I generally prefer supporting local businesses over big box retailers like Home Depot or Lowe’s. However, those stores have been improving their plant selection, and while their staff may not always be as knowledgeable, they are consistently polite. Because Sunset Nursery is a local business, I’m not writing them off completely. I hope this feedback is taken seriously. A great selection is important, but customers should be treated with basic courtesy and respect.
