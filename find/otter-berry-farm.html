★★★★★Amy Germolus9 months ago

Hands down our favorite fall activity spot! Lots of new features this season too. Our 4 kids ages range 10 months to 9 years and everyone had a great time. The mechanical goat feeder was a favorite for the 4 year old, 9 year old loved the paintball target area, 7 year old burned TONS of energy on the zip line, and baby had plenty to see - never cried the whole 3+ hours we were there! Our whole family enjoyed the corn maze, especially the 3 kids who got to take turns leading the way. The bale sculptures are awesome, and the bale slide is a hit each year. Many kinds of animals that are all friendly, some more curious than others. We got to meet the infamous “Baby Goat”, what a hoot! Corn pit is HUGE, I didn’t see a kid without a smile in there. The food trailers were great too - we enjoyed kettle corn, lemonade slushies, and freshly made deep fried cheese curds. We hope to come back for the night maze (the kids really want to test their flashlights in the maze). Something to do everywhere you look. Very reasonable price for all of the activities! We drive an hour and a half for this - totally worth the drive. Super nice family that owns it too - we were the last people there before closing and they never rushed us. They are really nice to answer questions about their farm and made sure we had a great experience! Highly recommend adding this to your seasonal outings schedule! Photo ops galore! 10/10
