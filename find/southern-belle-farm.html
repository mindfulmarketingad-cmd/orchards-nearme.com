★★★★★Lacey Chapman2 months ago

Southern Belle Farms was AMAZING! There are so many activities—seriously, something for everyone. It’s perfect for kids and adults, and everything is spread out nicely so you can enjoy the whole experience without feeling crowded. The strawberry picking is a must—so fun and the strawberries are great quality. Pro tip: don’t feel like you have to buy their bucket. You can bring your own bag. If you’re going with kids, definitely get the punch cards for activities. You don’t need the $12 one—the $7 option is more than enough and gives you plenty to enjoy. That said, I highly recommend paying the $17 for the full activity access. It includes slides (which are HUGE and so much fun), car racing, a train ride, multiple playground areas, and even a bee education walkthrough. We got there around 10 AM and didn’t leave until after 3–4 PM, so this is truly a full-day activity. You also get access to the farm, which adds even more to the experience. Overall, this is a great place to spend the day with family or friends. I definitely recommend it—just come ready to have fun and stay awhile! 🌞🍓
