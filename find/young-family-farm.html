★★★★★Andrew Zampariolo10 months ago

Stopped at Young’s Family Farm while passing through for some tomatoes and lettuce and was struck by the freshness and quality of their produce. What stood out even more, though, was the kindness of the people working there, including the owners. They had samples of their delicious yellow Sun Sugar tomatoes, but the stand had sold out. I didn’t realize it at the time, but the woman at the register let the owner know. When I returned to my car, I found a pint of freshly picked yellow tomatoes waiting for me on the roof, along with a cheerful “Merry Christmas” from Mr. Young, who refused to let me pay for them. That small act of kindness and generosity made the visit unforgettable. I’m now a lifelong customer. Thank you, Mr. and Mrs. Young!
