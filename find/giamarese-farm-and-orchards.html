★★★★★Lok Yee Cheunga month ago

May 31st 2026--5 stars, excellent parking and services despite all the bad reviews. This year's weather was terrible but the crops came out decent! They were honest about thinner crops so they only allowed one carton per person for strawberries. There are two separate fields for strawberries; there were sugar snow peas, and a variety of lettuce to choose from ($1.99 per head). The tractor ride was short, but it is cheaper than Alstede Farm by a lot. It is better for adults that do not care about the mazes and animals. The store was really nice as well. It is worth revisiting!

★★★★☆Yan La year ago

small cute farm. we went on a weekday so it was not too busy. didn't have the hayride or corn maze at the time we visited, but we had a good time picking the strawberries. we paid $3 per person for the entrance which I think it's fair, cheaper than some of the farms that we went to. the strawberries and the snow peas are $4.99 per lb. those are the sweetest strawberries I've never have! taste 100x better than the one you buy from the supermarkets. we got about 9 lbs. and some jams and snacks from the shop. ★★★★★mimi o9 months ago

Went here on Sept. Saturday 20th, w/ my 2 daughters & my granddaughter & grandson. We paid $5 a person to get in, which I thought was a fair price. We purchased tickets for the Hayride, they gave the kids coloring books of farm life. We went on the Hay Ride to a Corn maze, we were in there for a while. I thought we'd never get out 😉. We had a blast, then you can go over to a field to pick Sunflowers 🌻, which were free. On the way back the driver stopped where there were animals & he was telling us about them. We then went & picked apples & we also picked some eggplants 🍆 & watermelons🍉, they were free & the price for the apples & vegetables were a fair price, better then the store prices. They have 🎃 pumpkin fields to pick your pumpkins. They sell food, drinks, snacks & ice cream. We went into both stores. Left w/homemade preserves, cakes, cookies, chocolate lolipops & candles. I bought one of their 1/2 gallon jugs of apple cider, it is delicious. We had a great time here, the kids loved the Farm. I highly recommend this place if you're looking for a wonderful place to enjoy nature & what she offers us. Plenty of parking & fair prices which is going right back into the farm. Help support Our local farmers.
