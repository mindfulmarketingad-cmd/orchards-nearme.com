★★★★★Allison Scott4 years ago

We recently went to Lanni Orchards Columbus Weekend Festival to try their “Amazing Bee Corn Maze” and their “Jumping Pillow”. We had a great time, totally got lost in the maze for a good 30 minutes, just me and my 6 year old! They have you watch a video in the store before you head out and they give you a little packet to use during your maze adventure, it’s very cute! The jumping pillow was fun too, they allow adults(went on with the kiddo), and there is a little “snack shack” where you can get anything from caramel apples to popcorn and cider! Reasonable prices for both the maze and the pillow. FAVORITES - the maze was really big with a watchtower and activity boards to find along the way. The snack shack was perfect for a snack after the maze! TAKE AWAY - Great place for a fun fall adventure either with the kids or on a date! Plenty of other activities to do both inside and out as well as fresh produce, frozen meals, and now craft cider/beer! HEADS UP - Save a few bucks by buying your corn maze ticket/s on their website ahead of time. Also, bring some cash to be able to get snacks from the snack shack.
