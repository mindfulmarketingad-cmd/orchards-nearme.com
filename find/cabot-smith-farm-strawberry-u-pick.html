★★★★★Jessica Della Pepa5 years ago

What a wonderful place to pick berries! They also sell pre-picked, but those usually aren't ready until 10 am. Please check out their info before heading over. With COVID-19 changing the world, it has trickled down to small farms, too. You will.want to schedule a time to pick, to space out people in the fields plus spread the wealth of berries, bring your masks, and no children under 8 this year. That last one might be the saddest part, but it's all to keep people safe, and the farm is really looking forward to having kids back next year!! I filled a tray quite quickly, and I loved that the berries weren't all perfect shapes. I found some fabulous shapes, including one that looks like a bunny (image 1 in my post). I plan to go back before the season is over to stock up on their gumminess! You better head over and grab yours before the season is over!
