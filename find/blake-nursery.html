★★★★★Annie Buchwalda year ago

I don't even know where to start! The plant selection is fabulous! I've never seen the typical nursery plants like petunias, pansies and violas, etc. The plants they have are natives and non-natives that fit with our habitat naturally. They carry flowering perennials, bushes, trees, and plants with a variety of lighting and watering needs. I have come in with terrible conditions for growing natives and get amazing knowledgeable advice and coaching and they have so much patience! AND my native plants gardens (that have horrible light conditions) are thriving and beautiful! People drive from hours away and over challenging mountain passes to come to these folks and it is all worth it! As a bonus, they also sell their all natural, grass-fed and grass-finished beef! I love these guys! They have earned my top recommendation.
