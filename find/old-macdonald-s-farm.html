Reviews

★★★★★Em GM10 months ago

This is a fun place to see and actually pet some animals. We got to bottle feed the sheep babies and the calves. We did the pony ride and the train ride. We had my grandson who is 9 and my granddaughter who is 1 yr old. They had a blast. We bought 1 cup of food per person that lasted us feeding most of the animals in the farm. The baby chicks were so cute and they had some for sale. The gift shop offered an array of toys, shirts, jackets, blankets and more. After our exploring time we got ice cream in the store and headed home.
