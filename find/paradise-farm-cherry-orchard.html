★★★★★Cory Groshek4 years ago

My wife and I visited this wonderful farm a few months ago to pick tart cherries for making Cherry Bounce with and everyone there was very nice, especially the man who was in charge of transporting the cherry pickers by truck. There were a LOT of cherry trees to pick from and more cherries than the eye could see. We picked about six buckets worth of cherries and had a wonderful time. There was nothing fancy about how this Pick Your Own situation worked, and that was the beauty of it. We just showed up, picked our buckets, were driven to a spot full of cherries on the back of a truck that resembled a bench, picked our cherries, were taken back to the front of the farm, paid, and went home. Easy! I would highly recommend the Pick Your Own Cherries experience at Paradise!
