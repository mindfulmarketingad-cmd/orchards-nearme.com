★★★★★Julie Carter Dance7 months ago

Ken, Mandy, and the team are incredible. I came to them with my ideas for a few landscaping projects at our house about a month ago, and I can't recommend them enough. The process was quick and painless, from getting a free quote to the finished product. They really brought my vision to life. The pricing was competitive to other quotes, and they were very willing to make any changes I needed. They knew which plants would do well in the many different locations around my house and answered all of my questions, making great suggestions to add to the design. They even incorporated some of my existing outdoor and potted plants into the design. The team was professional, time-efficient, and had excellent communication throughout the whole project. I'm so happy with my landscaping. It really elevated our fixer-upper. They love what they do, and it shows! Thank you, Green Valley! Keep in mind, this project was done in the fall, so a few of my existing plants had started to go dormant.
