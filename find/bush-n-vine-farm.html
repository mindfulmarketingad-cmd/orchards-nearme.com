★★★★★Charley8 months ago

Absolutely loved our visit to Bush-N-Vine Farm! This place is everything you could want from a true local farm experience. The market inside is full of fresh, high-quality products — from juicy fruits and crisp vegetables to locally sourced meats, fresh-baked breads, and jars of golden South Carolina honey. You can really tell everything is grown and made with care. The staff were kind and welcoming, happy to answer questions and share information about their produce. Outside, the farm is picture-perfect — buckets of bright strawberries, colorful pumpkins of all shapes and sizes, and beautiful fall displays that make it such a great stop for photos. I also loved their food area. Hot fresh donuts, cold drinks, yummy ice cream and more — the slushie was cold, refreshing, and tasted like pure sunshine in a cup! It’s the perfect place to shop local, support South Carolina farmers, and enjoy a peaceful day surrounded by fresh air and friendly faces. You can feel the pride they take in what they do from the moment you arrive. I’ll definitely be back again soon for more fresh produce and homemade goodies! 🍓🎃🌻
