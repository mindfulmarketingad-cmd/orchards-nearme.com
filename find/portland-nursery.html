★★★★★Shiloah Lairda month ago

In my search for plants that are native to the pacific northwest, I have visited many nurseries and purchased an embarassing amount of plants. Searches online kept directing me to this place. I went a few days ago and WOW. The selection is fantastic. Where a lot of nurseries will have a sad little table of natives, this place had ALL of the hard to find natives I have been looking for at reasonable prices. When I took the plants home, NOT one of them were root bound. Every plant has been cared for and the layout of the nursery is easy to navigate. They also have every other type of plant, though I didn't look closely at the others, because I was on a mission! I now can say there are TWO top notch nurseries that I will drive an hour to get to.. The other is mentioned in another review and I cannot decide which one I like the best.
