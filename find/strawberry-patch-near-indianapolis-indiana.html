Midwest strawberry patches have a compact June season across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, Ohio, North Dakota, South Dakota, and Wisconsin, squeezed between the last spring frost and the region's hot midsummer stretch.
