★★★★★Austin Yochus4 years ago

Had a great time picking strawberries! Bring your own container to pick and its $2.50 per pound which is the cheapest I have seen anywhere. No reservations needed. Take a container with a hard bottom. Take it slow and start in the back or the middle of the rows! They also have a market where you can buy other produce. It can get muddy after rainy weather so bring boots if it rained recently!
