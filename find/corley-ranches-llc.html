Gotta give it to this family who really goes all out every year to make this gem of a local experience shine. Corn (and also less spooky) mazes, hay rides, pig races, a quaint little sweet petting zoo with plenty of goats, etc.. Great way to spend a few hours with the family and leave with some super cool pumpkins. Just being your own shade. Or raincoat, depending. It is Nevada, after all.
