Reviews

★★★★★Carrie Lopez8 years ago

This place is amazing! The place is HUGE. I just spent an hour there and only got half way through! I pass signs for it on my way to work and I'm so glad I stopped! I've got a long wish list now based on only half of what they have! Perrenials, annuals, succulents, ground cover... you name it, they are bound to have it. Plants are in excellent condition and most pretty well established in their containers. The rows, sections are labeled for ease of knowing what you're looking at. Little information cards are also posted for specific information on the plants (such as "attracts butterflies", etc). I'm a horrible gardner and don't often find myself in nurseries but price points seem quite fair for plants in such great shape! I am definitely planning a field trip back and plan to bring my good camera because its such a beautiful place to photograph! I also made friends with the sweetest garden kitty! She's so darling.
