★★★★★Ray W2 months ago

Have been shopping here a couple of years. I don't think there is any other business in the area with prices as low for the quality of flowers and seedlings. A guy had a gripe in the reviews about not taking cards. How do you think their prices are this low?? Fussing abt service? This is busy season where the weather just broke and people are rushing to purchase flowers and plants and you want pampered service? Give me a break. Please come and support (just walk with your cash lol). I used to fuss with growing seeds at home and now just come here and buy seedlings. You may get a container with 3 or 4 seedlings where you can break them apart and have multiple plants for $2 or $3. The workers are friendly and if your timing is right, you can get great nuggets of info. Since the frost passed, I will be popping by this week to snag my tomatoes. Happy planting!

★★★★★JB Lighter10 months ago

For the best bargains in annuals, you'll not believe the size & choices at MD Flower & Foliage. Take the street directly across from the front gateway of Richardsons, for half a mile. The prices of small annuals are better than anything we've seen elsewhere. They do not take credit cards. You have to pay with a personal check or in cash--but it's worth it! We've been buying our annuals & tropical plants there for over ten years. Every single thing we've bought has leapt up in size. Generally x3 or x4 within 3-4 weeks. (The place is mobbed around Mother's Day.) Go early in the morning, not around lunch or afternoon. Parking can be hard to find. Be patient. This nursery is big, really big.
