★★☆☆☆Morgan Bertison3 weeks ago

Generally unimpressed. I want to stress that I’m talking about their online ordering which may have nothing to do with the in person experience. I received my order exactly one month after it was submitted (submitted in May, so this was not for seasonal delay). The plants are very small with 8 crammed into a very small overstuffed box that fit in my mailbox. The included insert states not to worry about any damage/wilting of the plants, because they have healthy root systems and will rebound. In the same insert, it states they refuse to take responsibility for anything that goes wrong after 72 hours. None of my plants are overtly dead, but only about half look healthy enough I’m confident they’ll live. I’m including photos of the box and size/quality of plants I received.

★★★★★Evo Rachev2 months ago

This is not just a nursery. This is the NYBG meets an English style farmhouse. Amazing stone walls, barn quilts and a giant cairn that puts an accent on the great vistas in the back. Very tidy and organized place. I visited in early April. I can only imagine the explosion of colors this place provides in spring and summer. A true gem that makes me love Maine even more. This was the only place that had the tree I wanted (European copper beech) and I had called more than 50 nurseries in New York, Connecticut, Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Maine. The owner, Steve, is knowledgeable and a great guy to talk horticulture with. Rarely do you see someone this passionate about plants. Very impressed and proud that there are still people as passionate about gardening as Steve.

★★★★★Shayna Piedimonte11 months ago

This garden center is amazing. It has a different vibe compared to the commercial gardens around. When you drive up the driveway, it feels like you are heading up to someone's house, which really you are. The owners live on-site and have been on this land for generations. The plants are lovingly tended to by great staff. Definitely seek one of them out so they can help you find what you are looking for. They are knowledgeable about the plants and great at giving tips. They suggested I come back every few weeks to see what is blooming. They have a great selection from trees, hanging baskets, herbs as well as annuals and perennials. Their hosta selection is amazing! They have them arranged by size and even carry giant hostas. I will definitely be returning.
